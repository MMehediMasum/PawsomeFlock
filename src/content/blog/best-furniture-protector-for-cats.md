## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Furniture Protector For Cats?



The best protector is durable, easy to apply, and safe for pets. FixZilla and Secopad offer heavy-duty, transparent options that prevent cat scratches effectively without damaging furniture.



### How Do Self-adhesive Cat Scratch Protectors Work?



Self-adhesive protectors stick directly to furniture surfaces, creating a tough barrier. They deter cats from scratching by feeling unpleasant under their claws but cause no damage to furniture.



### Are Twist Pins Necessary For Furniture Protectors?



Twist pins enhance protection by securing the shield firmly. They prevent cats from lifting edges and damaging furniture, ensuring long-lasting defense without harming your sofa or walls.



### Can Furniture Protectors Damage Leather Sofas?



No, specially designed protectors for leather sofas like Panther Armor protect without leaving residue or marks. They are easy to remove and maintain the leather’s natural look.



### How Long Do Cat Scratch Furniture Protectors Last?



Quality protectors typically last several months to a year. Longevity depends on cat activity and protector type, with heavy-duty and pin-secured options offering extended use.



### Are Transparent Furniture Protectors Effective Against Cat Scratches?



Yes, transparent protectors like JINVOGREEN provide discreet defense. They blend with furniture aesthetics while delivering strong scratch resistance to maintain your sofa’s appearance.
