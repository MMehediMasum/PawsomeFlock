## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Portuguese Water Dog Shampoos Special?



Portuguese Water Dog shampoos are designed for their unique coat. These shampoos often include oatmeal and aloe to soothe skin. They help maintain a healthy, shiny coat while reducing irritation. They are gentle and suitable for regular use, ensuring your dog stays clean and comfortable.



### How Can I Give Pills To My Portuguese Water Dog?



Use treat wraps designed for Portuguese Water Dogs. These wraps, made from real human-grade peanut butter, help mask pills. They are moldable and non-sticky, making it easy to conceal medication. This method ensures your dog willingly takes their medicine without stress.



### Are Multivitamins Necessary For Portuguese Water Dogs?



Multivitamins support your dog's overall health and well-being. They provide essential nutrients that might be missing from their diet. These supplements can improve coat condition, boost immunity, and support joint health. They are especially useful for active breeds like Portuguese Water Dogs.



### What Are The Benefits Of Dog Dental Care Treats?



Dental care treats help maintain your dog’s oral health. They reduce plaque and tartar buildup, promoting fresh breath. Made with natural ingredients, they are safe and effective for Portuguese Water Dogs. Regular use supports healthy gums and teeth, contributing to overall well-being.



### Is Air-dried Jerky Safe For Portuguese Water Dogs?



Yes, air-dried jerky is safe and nutritious. Made from single ingredients like emu, it’s a high-protein treat. It’s suitable for all breeds, including Portuguese Water Dogs. Air-drying preserves nutrients and flavor while being free from additives, making it a healthy snack option.
