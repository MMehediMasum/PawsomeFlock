## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Are Best For Cat Hairball Supplements?



Effective hairball supplements often include pumpkin, psyllium, omega-3 fatty acids, and vitamins. These ingredients support digestion, skin health, and furball elimination. Chicken flavor and soft chew forms improve palatability, making it easier to administer to cats regularly.



### How Do Hairball Supplements Help Cats?



Hairball supplements aid digestion and promote natural hairball elimination. They contain fiber and vitamins that reduce hair ingestion and improve gut health. This helps prevent furball formation and supports a healthier coat and skin.



### Are Chewy Or Creamy Hairball Treats Better For Cats?



Both chewy and creamy treats can be effective. Chewy treats provide vitamins and fiber, while creamy lickables like INABA Churu offer hydration and nutrients. Choice depends on your cat’s preference and ease of administration.



### How Long Does It Take For Hairball Supplements To Work?



Most supplements show results within 2 to 4 weeks. Consistent daily use improves digestion and reduces hairball frequency. Some products offer quick relief, but long-term use ensures better hairball control.



### Can Hairball Supplements Improve My Cat’s Skin And Coat?



Yes, many supplements contain omega-3s, biotin, and zinc, which promote healthy skin and a shiny coat. Improved digestion also enhances nutrient absorption, benefiting overall fur quality and reducing shedding.



### Are Hairball Supplements Safe For Kittens And Adult Cats?



Most hairball supplements are formulated for both kittens and adult cats. Always check product labels for age recommendations. Consult your veterinarian before starting any new supplement to ensure safety and proper dosage.
