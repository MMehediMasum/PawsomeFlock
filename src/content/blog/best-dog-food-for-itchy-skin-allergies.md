## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Soothe Itchy Skin In Dog Food?



Ingredients like salmon, omega-3 fatty acids, and hydrolyzed proteins reduce inflammation and soothe itchy skin effectively. These nutrients support skin health and improve coat condition. Choosing dog food with these ingredients can relieve itching caused by allergies or sensitivities.



### How Does Hydrolyzed Protein Benefit Dogs With Allergies?



Hydrolyzed protein is broken down into smaller pieces, reducing allergic reactions. It helps dogs with food sensitivities by preventing immune responses. This protein supports skin health and minimizes itching, making it ideal for dogs with skin allergies or sensitive stomachs.



### Are Grain-free Dog Foods Better For Itchy Skin?



Grain-free dog foods often reduce allergens that trigger itching. They focus on protein and healthy fats, which improve skin and coat health. However, consult your vet before switching, as some dogs may benefit more from specific formulations like hydrolyzed protein or limited ingredient diets.



### Can Probiotics Reduce Dog Skin Allergies And Itching?



Yes, probiotics support gut health, which influences the immune system and skin condition. They help balance bacteria and reduce inflammation, easing itching and allergy symptoms. Supplements like Zesty Paws Dog Allergy Relief combine probiotics with other ingredients for effective skin allergy relief.



### Which Dog Foods Are Best For Sensitive Stomachs And Itchy Skin?



Foods like Hill's Science Diet Sensitive Stomach & Skin and Purina ONE Sensitive Stomach formulas combine gentle digestion with skin-supporting nutrients. These options improve nutrient absorption and reduce allergic reactions, helping dogs with both sensitive stomachs and itchy skin feel better.



### How Often Should I Feed Allergy-specific Dog Food For Best Results?



Feed allergy-specific dog food consistently, usually twice a day, for at least 4 to 6 weeks. This allows skin to heal and itching to subside. Regular feeding ensures sustained nutrient intake, improving your dog’s skin health over time.
