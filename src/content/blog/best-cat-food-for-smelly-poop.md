Foods rich in real meat and low in fillers help reduce waste odor. Supplements can also improve intestinal function and litter box smell. This guide covers top cat foods and supplements that target smelly poop. Each option promotes cleaner litter boxes and happier cats. Find the best choice to keep your home fresh and your cat healthy.
