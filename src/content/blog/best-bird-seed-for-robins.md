## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Bird Seed Is Best For Attracting Robins?



Kaytee Wild Bird Food Mealworms and Kaytee Nut & Fruit Wild Bird Seed are excellent for robins. These blends offer high protein and fruit content, mimicking robins' natural diet. They attract robins effectively to your backyard feeders.



### Are Mealworms Good For Feeding Robins?



Yes, mealworms are highly nutritious and favored by robins. Kaytee Wild Bird Food Mealworms provide essential protein, boosting robins' energy and health. Including mealworms in seed mixes attracts robins and supports their dietary needs.



### Which Seed Blends Support Robin Health And Nutrition?



Pennington Pride Songbird Nut & Fruit Blend and Wagner's Farmer's Delight with Cherry Flavor offer balanced nutrition. These blends include nuts, fruits, and seeds beneficial for robin vitality and feather condition.



### How Much Bird Seed Should I Buy For Robins?



Start with a 5 to 10-pound bag like Cole's Special Feeder Bird Seed. This amount ensures fresh supply and reduces waste. Adjust based on robin activity and feeder visits.



### Can Robins Eat Fruit-flavored Bird Seed Mixes?



Yes, robins enjoy fruit-flavored mixes like Wagner's Farmer's Delight with Cherry Flavor. These blends mimic natural fruit consumption and attract robins effectively to feeders.



### What Is The Best Feeder Type For Robin Seed Blends?



Use platform or tray feeders to offer robin seed blends. These feeders accommodate robins' ground-feeding habits and provide easy access to seeds and mealworms.
