## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Relieve Dog Skin Allergies In Shampoos?



Medicated dog shampoos often contain chlorhexidine, oatmeal, hydrocortisone, and antifungal agents. These ingredients soothe itching, reduce inflammation, and fight infections. They also help restore the skin’s natural barrier and improve coat health, providing effective relief from allergies and hot spots.



### How Often Should I Use Medicated Shampoo For My Dog?



Use medicated shampoos 2-3 times a week initially, then reduce frequency as symptoms improve. Follow product instructions or vet advice closely. Overuse can dry the skin, while proper use controls itching, redness, and infections effectively, promoting faster healing and comfort.



### Are Medicated Shampoos Safe For Puppies And Cats?



Many medicated shampoos are formulated to be safe for puppies and cats, like TropiClean and Veterinary Formula brands. Always check product labels for age and species recommendations. When in doubt, consult your veterinarian to ensure safety for your pet’s specific needs.



### Can Medicated Shampoos Treat Hot Spots And Yeast Infections?



Yes, medicated shampoos with antifungal and antibacterial properties effectively treat hot spots and yeast infections. Ingredients like chlorhexidine and hydrocortisone reduce inflammation and control microbial growth. Regular use supports healing and prevents recurrence of these common skin issues.



### What Should I Avoid In Medicated Dog Shampoos?



Avoid shampoos with parabens, dyes, and harsh soaps, which can irritate sensitive skin. Opt for soap-free, fragrance-free, and hypoallergenic formulas for allergy-prone dogs. Gentle ingredients ensure effective cleansing without worsening skin conditions or causing additional discomfort.
