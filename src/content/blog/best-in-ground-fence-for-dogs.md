**Choosing the best in-ground fence keeps your dog safe and secure inside your yard. These fences offer reliable boundaries without harsh physical barriers.**



An in-ground fence uses buried wires to create an invisible boundary your dog learns not to cross. Many systems include waterproof collars that give a gentle warning or correction if your pet nears the edge. Brands like PetSafe and SportDOG provide options for different yard sizes and dog behaviors.



Some models suit stubborn dogs, while others are easy to install and weather-resistant. Rechargeable collars and adjustable settings help customize training for your pet’s needs. Whether you have one dog or multiple, these fences offer flexible solutions to keep pets safe outdoors. Understanding features such as boundary length, collar type, and expandability helps you pick the right fence for your dog and yard.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best In-ground Fence For Stubborn Dogs?



The PetSafe Stubborn Dog In-Ground Pet Fence is ideal. It covers 500 feet and handles persistent pets well. The system uses a 20 gauge wire for durability and consistent containment. It helps train even the most determined dogs safely indoors.



### How Do Rechargeable In-ground Dog Fences Work?



Rechargeable fences like SportDOG and Cawjug use boundary wires to create safe zones. Collars receive signals and deliver tone or static corrections. Rechargeable collars ensure continuous use without frequent battery changes. These systems provide reliable containment and are waterproof for outdoor use.



### Can In-ground Fences Contain Multiple Dogs?



Yes, many kits like PetSafe Basic and Cawjug support multiple dogs. They come with extra collars and adjustable settings for different pets. These systems allow simultaneous containment and training of two or more dogs in one yard.



### Are In-ground Fences Safe For Cats And Small Dogs?



PetSafe offers specialized models for cats and dogs over 5 lbs. These fences provide gentle static correction and tone-only modes. The collars are lightweight, waterproof, and designed for smaller pets, ensuring safety and comfort during containment.



### How Large An Area Can In-ground Fences Cover?



Coverage varies by model: PetSafe YardMax covers up to 10 acres, SportDOG up to 100 acres. Smaller kits cover 1/3 acre or less. Choose a system based on your yard size and number of pets to ensure effective containment.



### Is Installation Difficult For In-ground Dog Fences?



Installation requires burying boundary wire around your yard perimeter. Most kits include detailed instructions and tools. Wireless models like PetSafe Stay & Play require no digging, making them portable and easier to set up for travel or rental homes.
