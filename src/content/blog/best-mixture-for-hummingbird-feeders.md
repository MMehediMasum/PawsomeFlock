## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Mixture For Hummingbird Feeders?



The best mixture is a 4:1 water-to-sugar ratio using pure cane sugar. Avoid dyes or additives. Ready-to-use and powder nectars like Kaytee or BIRDBROS offer convenience and freshness for hummingbirds.



### How Long Does Hummingbird Nectar Last In Feeders?



Nectar typically lasts 3 to 5 days before fermenting. Products with preservatives, like Sapphire Labs Nectar Defender, can extend freshness up to 7 days, keeping feeders safe and attractive for hummingbirds longer.



### Can I Use Store-bought Hummingbird Nectar Safely?



Yes, ready-to-use nectars like Kaytee and Pennington are safe and formulated for hummingbirds. They save time and ensure proper sugar concentration without boiling or mixing.



### Should Hummingbird Nectar Be Boiled Before Use?



Boiling is not necessary if using pure cane sugar and clean water. Powder concentrates like Perky-Pet and BIRDBROS only require mixing with water, making preparation quick and safe.



### Are Artificial Dyes Safe In Hummingbird Nectar?



No, artificial dyes can harm hummingbirds. Choose dye-free products like BIRDBROS or Happy Wings. Natural red coloring in feeders attracts birds without risking their health.



### How Do Nectar Powders Compare To Liquid Nectar?



Powders like Perky-Pet and Happy Wings offer longer shelf life and easy mixing. Liquid nectars provide immediate use but may spoil faster. Both types support healthy feeding when prepared correctly.
