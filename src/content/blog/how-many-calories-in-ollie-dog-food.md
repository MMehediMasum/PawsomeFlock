## Factors Affecting Calorie Needs

Understanding how many calories your dog needs is important for their health. Ollie dog food provides balanced nutrition, but calorie needs can change.

Several factors affect how many calories your dog should eat daily. Knowing these helps you feed the right amount.

### Dog Size And Breed

Large dogs need more calories than small dogs. Different breeds have different energy needs. For example, active breeds burn more calories.

* Small breeds need fewer calories.
* Large breeds require more calories.
* Working breeds often need extra energy.

### Activity Level

Active dogs burn more calories each day. Dogs who exercise or work need more food. Less active dogs need fewer calories to stay healthy.

* High activity dogs need more calories.
* Moderate activity dogs need balanced calories.
* Low activity dogs need fewer calories.

### Age And Health

Young puppies and older dogs have different calorie needs. Puppies need more calories to grow. Older dogs may need fewer calories due to slower metabolism.

Health conditions can also change calorie needs. Dogs with illnesses may need special diets with adjusted calories.

### Daily Calorie Intake Guidelines

Dogs need different calories based on size, age, and activity. Ollie dog food labels show calories per serving.

Most adult dogs require between 30 and 50 calories per pound each day. Puppies and active dogs need more.

* Small dogs (under 20 lbs): 600 to 1,000 calories daily
* Medium dogs (20-50 lbs): 1,000 to 1,500 calories daily
* Large dogs (50-100 lbs): 1,500 to 2,500 calories daily

### Adjusting Portions For Weight Goals

Change meal sizes to help your dog gain, lose, or maintain weight. Track your dog's weight and adjust calories regularly.

For weight loss, feed 10-20% fewer calories than usual. For weight gain, add 10-20% more calories each day.

| Weight Goal | Calorie Adjustment |
| --- | --- |
| Maintain Weight | Feed daily calories based on dog's weight |
| Lose Weight | Reduce calories by 10-20% |
| Gain Weight | Increase calories by 10-20% |
