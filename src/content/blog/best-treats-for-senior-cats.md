## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Treats For Senior Cats With Joint Issues?



Treats like Nutramax Cosequin and Cat Hip & Joint Health Chews support senior cats' joints. They contain glucosamine, chondroitin, and omega-3s to improve mobility and reduce discomfort effectively.



### Are Lickable Treats Good For Senior Cats?



Yes, lickable treats like INABA Churu and Hartz Delectables Bisque are gentle and hydrating. They are easy for senior cats to consume and provide essential vitamins like taurine and vitamin E.



### Which Grain-free Treats Suit Senior Cats Best?



Blue Buffalo Wilderness Grain Free Soft-Moist Treats are ideal for senior cats with grain sensitivities. They offer high protein from chicken and turkey without fillers, promoting healthy digestion and energy.



### How Do Wet Treats Benefit Senior Cats?



Wet treats such as Hartz Delectables Stew and Chowder provide hydration and are easier to chew. They help maintain moisture balance and support oral health in older cats.



### Can Treats Improve Senior Cats’ Skin And Coat Health?



Yes, supplements like Nutramax Cosequin contain omega-3s that enhance skin and coat condition. Regular treats with these nutrients help senior cats maintain a shiny, healthy coat.



### Are Crunchy Treats Suitable For Senior Cats?



Crunchy treats like Temptations Classic are tasty but may be hard for some senior cats to chew. Soft or lickable treats are often better for aging cats with dental issues.
