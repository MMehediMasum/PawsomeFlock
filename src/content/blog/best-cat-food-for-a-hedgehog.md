**Choosing the best cat food for a hedgehog can keep your pet healthy and active. Not all cat foods suit hedgehogs, so select carefully.**



Hedgehogs need a diet rich in protein and low in fat. Some cat foods meet these needs and contain ingredients hedgehogs enjoy. Brands like Mazuri, Exotic Nutrition, and Vitakraft offer specially made hedgehog foods with balanced nutrition. These foods often include mealworms, poultry, and vitamins.



Some cat foods, like Purina ONE, provide real chicken and essential nutrients. Always check labels for protein content and avoid foods with too many fillers or artificial ingredients. Feeding the right cat food helps maintain your hedgehog’s energy and supports a strong immune system. Choosing quality food ensures your pet stays happy and healthy every day.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Cat Food For Hedgehogs?



Mazuri and Exotic Nutrition Hedgehog Essential are top choices. They provide balanced nutrition with protein and vitamins. These foods meet hedgehogs' dietary needs for health and energy.



### Can Purina One Cat Food Be Fed To Hedgehogs?



Purina ONE is formulated for cats and lacks some nutrients hedgehogs need. It’s not recommended as a primary diet for hedgehogs. Specialized hedgehog food is better for their health.



### Are Mealworms Included In Quality Hedgehog Food?



Yes, many premium hedgehog foods, like Hedgehog Complete and Vitakraft, include mealworms. Mealworms supply essential protein and mimic natural insect diets for hedgehogs.



### How Important Is Protein In Hedgehog Food?



High protein is crucial for hedgehogs’ muscle and energy needs. Foods like Sunseed Vita Prima and Berries & Bugs have high protein content to support their active lifestyle.



### Is Suncoast Sugar Gliders Spike's Delite Suitable For Hedgehogs?



Yes, it’s designed for insectivores like hedgehogs. This food contains balanced protein, fiber, and vitamins to keep hedgehogs healthy and happy.



### Do Hedgehog Foods Contain Vitamins For Overall Health?



Most quality brands, such as Atlantic Rush and Pretty Bird International, are vitamin-fortified. They ensure hedgehogs get essential nutrients for immune support and vitality.
