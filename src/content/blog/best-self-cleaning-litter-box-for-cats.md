## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make These Litter Boxes Self-cleaning?



These litter boxes use automatic rakes or rotating mechanisms to remove waste. They often include app control for easy monitoring and waste disposal bags for convenience.



### Are These Litter Boxes Suitable For Multiple Cats?



Yes, most models have large capacities and are designed to handle waste from multiple cats efficiently without frequent emptying.



### How Do Safety Sensors Protect My Cats?



Safety sensors stop the cleaning cycle if a cat is detected inside. This prevents accidents and ensures your cat’s safety during operation.



### Can I Control The Litter Box Remotely Via An App?



Many models offer app control, allowing you to monitor, schedule cleaning, and receive alerts remotely for added convenience.



### Do These Litter Boxes Help With Odor Control?



Yes, features like sealed waste compartments, odor-free disposal systems, and negative ion sterilization reduce unpleasant smells effectively.



### Are Open-top Designs Better For Senior Or Large Cats?



Open-top designs provide easy entry and exit, making them ideal for senior or larger cats who need more space and comfort.
