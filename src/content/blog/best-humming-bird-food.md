## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Ready-to-use Hummingbird Food?



Kaytee and Natural Springs offer high-quality ready-to-use hummingbird food. They require no mixing and attract hummingbirds effectively. These options are convenient for quick feeder refills and contain no harmful additives, ensuring safe feeding for your local hummingbirds.



### How To Mix Hummer’s Galore Hummingbird Nectar?



Hummer’s Galore nectar comes in packets that dissolve in water. Simply mix one packet with the recommended water amount for fresh, all-natural nectar. It contains no dyes or preservatives, providing a healthy feeding option for hummingbirds.



### Which Hummingbird Food Has No Preservatives Or Dyes?



Hummer’s Galore nectar packets are free from preservatives and artificial dyes. This all-natural formula keeps hummingbirds healthy and happy. Avoiding dyes helps prevent harm to birds and keeps your feeder clean longer.



### How Long Does Ready-to-use Hummingbird Nectar Last?



Ready-to-use nectar like Kaytee or Perky-Pet typically lasts 3-5 days in warm weather. Store it in a cool place and clean feeders regularly to prevent mold and bacteria growth, ensuring safe feeding.



### Can I Use Powdered Hummingbird Nectar Concentrate?



Yes, powdered nectar like Happy Wings or Perky-Pet concentrate is easy to store and mix. Follow package instructions to dilute properly with water. It provides a cost-effective and fresh feeding option for hummingbirds.



### What Size Hummingbird Food Packs Are Best For Feeders?



Choose pack sizes based on feeder size and hummingbird activity. Large packs (64 oz or more) like Kaytee or Perky-Pet suit frequent feeders. Smaller pre-measured packets like Audubon Park are ideal for occasional use or travel.
