These foods often include chicken, rice, pumpkin, or salmon, which are easy on the stomach. Wet dog food also adds moisture, aiding digestion and hydration. Finding a balanced diet with limited ingredients reduces allergy risks and promotes better nutrient absorption. This guide covers top wet dog foods designed to keep sensitive stomachs calm and dogs happy.
