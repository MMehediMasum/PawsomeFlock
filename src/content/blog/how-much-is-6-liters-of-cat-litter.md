## Cat Litter Types

Choosing the right cat litter matters for both cats and owners. Different types suit different needs. Understanding options helps pick the best one. Each type has unique features and benefits. This guide breaks down common cat litter types to help you decide.

### Clumping Vs Non-clumping

Clumping litter forms solid clumps when wet. This makes cleaning easier and faster. Non-clumping litter absorbs moisture but does not clump. It may require more frequent changes. Clumping litter is popular for odor control. Non-clumping options tend to be less dusty.

### Natural Vs Synthetic

Natural litter comes from materials like wood, corn, or wheat. It is biodegradable and eco-friendly. Synthetic litter is made from clay or other minerals. It often lasts longer and controls odor well. Natural litter is softer on paws. Synthetic litter may create more dust.

### Silica Gel Options

Silica gel litter uses crystals to absorb moisture. It traps odors effectively and stays dry longer. This type requires less frequent changing. Crystals can feel rough but are lightweight. Silica gel litter is low dust and good for sensitive cats.
