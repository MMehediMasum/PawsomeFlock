## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Dog Food For Maltese With Allergies?



Royal Canin Maltese Adult and Hill's Science Diet Sensitive formulas are top choices. They target skin and stomach sensitivities effectively. Limited ingredient and grain-free options like Natural Balance also reduce allergy risks. These foods promote healthy skin and reduce itching in Maltese dogs.



### Are Grain-free Dog Foods Better For Allergic Maltese?



Grain-free dog foods can help Maltese with allergies, especially if grains trigger reactions. Options like Natural Balance and Victor offer grain-free recipes. However, always consult your vet to ensure the diet meets your dog's nutritional needs and allergy profile.



### Which Ingredients Should Be Avoided For Allergic Maltese Dogs?



Avoid common allergens such as chicken, beef, wheat, soy, and corn. Foods like Forza10 Dermo Allergy and Victor exclude these ingredients. Opt for limited ingredient diets with novel proteins like fish or salmon. This helps reduce allergic reactions and supports sensitive skin.



### How Does Dog Food Support Maltese Skin And Coat Health?



Foods rich in omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids, like Blue Buffalo and Diamond Naturals, nourish skin and coat. These nutrients reduce inflammation and itching. Specialized diets also include vitamins and antioxidants to promote a healthy, shiny coat and soothe irritated skin.



### Can Small Breed Formulas Help Maltese With Allergies?



Yes, small breed formulas like Royal Canin Maltese Adult and Hill's Science Diet Small & Mini Breeds cater to Maltese nutritional needs. They have smaller kibble size and tailored nutrients that support sensitive stomachs and skin, improving overall health and allergy management.



### How To Transition Maltese To Allergy-friendly Dog Food Safely?



Gradually mix the new allergy-friendly food with the old over 7-10 days. This reduces digestive upset and helps your Maltese adjust. Monitor for any allergy symptoms during the transition. Consult your vet for personalized advice and to choose the best allergy diet for your dog.

Choosing the right dog food for your Maltese with allergies takes time and care. The brands listed offer options that support sensitive skin and stomach health.
