## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Best For Non-slip Stair Treads For Dogs?



Non-slip stair treads for dogs are best made from polyester, TPR, rubber, or TPE backing. These materials provide strong grip and durability, preventing slips on wooden steps. They are also pet-friendly and easy to clean, ensuring safety for dogs and household members alike.



### How Do Adhesive Stair Treads Work For Pet Safety?



Adhesive stair treads stick firmly to wooden steps, offering a secure, non-slip surface. Reusable adhesives allow easy installation and repositioning without residue. This prevents dogs from slipping and falling on stairs, enhancing overall pet safety indoors.



### Are Clear Stair Treads Effective For Wooden Stairs?



Yes, clear stair treads provide excellent traction while maintaining the natural look of wooden stairs. They are anti-slip, waterproof, and easy to clean, making them ideal for homes with dogs and elderly family members. Their transparency blends well with any décor.



### How Many Stair Treads Come In A Typical Pack?



Most non-slip stair tread packs include 14 to 15 pieces, enough to cover a full staircase. Sizes commonly range around 8"x30" or 6"x30", suitable for standard wooden steps. This quantity ensures consistent safety and uniform appearance across all stairs.



### Can Stair Treads Be Used For Kids And Elders Too?



Yes, these stair treads are designed for kids, elders, and pets. Their non-slip surfaces reduce fall risks for all age groups, providing added safety on slippery wooden stairs. They are soft yet durable, catering to diverse household needs.
