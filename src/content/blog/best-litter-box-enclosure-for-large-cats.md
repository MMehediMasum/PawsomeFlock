## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Litter Box Enclosure Suits Large Cats Best?



Extra-large enclosures with dimensions around 30"x20"x20" fit large cats comfortably. Look for models with wide entries and tall walls to prevent litter scatter. Products like unipaws and Feandrea offer spacious designs ideal for big cats.



### How Does A Top-opening Litter Box Enclosure Help Cats?



Top-opening enclosures provide privacy and easy access for cats. They reduce litter tracking and prevent dogs or children from interfering. Models like IRIS USA and unipaws feature top-entry designs that keep litter contained and cats comfortable.



### Are Removable Dividers Useful In Cat Litter Enclosures?



Yes, removable dividers allow customization of space inside the enclosure. They help separate multiple cats' areas or adjust the enclosure size. Products like unipaws and HOOBRO include dividers for flexible, convenient use.



### Which Materials Are Best For Litter Box Enclosures?



Durable materials like wood, metal, and high-quality plastic are ideal. Wood offers aesthetic appeal, metal provides sturdiness, and plastic is lightweight and easy to clean. Brands like Homhedy and MEEXPAWS use these materials effectively.



### Can Litter Box Enclosures Reduce Litter Tracking?



Yes, enclosures with high sides, lids, or mats minimize litter scatter. Features like footboards and litter-catching lids help keep floors clean. IRIS USA and Amazon Basics models excel in reducing mess around litter boxes.



### Do Litter Box Enclosures Double As Furniture?



Many enclosures serve as end tables or benches, blending with home decor. Products like Feandrea and HOOBRO offer stylish designs that hide litter boxes while providing functional furniture. This dual purpose saves space and looks neat.
