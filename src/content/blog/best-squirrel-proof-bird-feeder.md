## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Truly Squirrel Proof?



A squirrel-proof bird feeder uses weight-activated perches or cages to block squirrels. It ensures only birds can access the seed. Durable metal or chew-proof designs add extra protection. This prevents seed waste and keeps your bird feeding experience enjoyable and squirrel-free.



### How Do Weight-activated Perches Protect Bird Feeders?



Weight-activated perches close access ports when heavy animals land. This mechanism stops squirrels from reaching the seed. Birds, being lighter, can feed without triggering the closure. It’s a smart and effective feature found in many top squirrel-proof feeders.



### Which Bird Feeder Has The Largest Seed Capacity?



The Wellavich and Wildpark feeders offer a large 4 to 5-pound seed capacity. This reduces the need for frequent refilling. Ideal for avid bird watchers who want continuous feeding with minimal maintenance. Larger capacity supports a variety of wild birds comfortably.



### Are Metal Mesh Feeders Better For Outdoor Use?



Yes, metal mesh feeders are durable and weather-resistant. They withstand chewing attempts by squirrels and other pests. Mesh design allows birds to cling and feed easily. They also provide good ventilation to keep seeds fresh longer.



### How Easy Is It To Clean Squirrel-proof Bird Feeders?



Many squirrel-proof feeders feature removable trays or parts for easy cleaning. Regular cleaning prevents mold and seed spoilage. Clean feeders promote bird health and attract more visitors. Look for models with simple disassembly for hassle-free maintenance.
