Outdoor cats face dirt, rain, and strong smells. The right litter should clump well and prevent tracking. It also needs to be safe and easy to scoop. Some litters use natural ingredients to reduce dust and keep paws clean. Others offer strong odor control for multiple cats.



Lightweight options make handling easier. Crystal litters reduce moisture and last longer outdoors. Hooded litter boxes protect litter from rain and wind. This guide covers top products like Dr. Elsey’s Ultra UnScented Clay, Purina Tidy Cats, Fresh Step, and World’s Best Cat Litter. Find the best choice to keep your outdoor cat happy and your space fresh.
