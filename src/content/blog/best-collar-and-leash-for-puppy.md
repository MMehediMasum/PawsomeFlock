## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Collar Material For Puppies?



The best collar material for puppies is soft and breathable, like padded neoprene or cotton hemp. These materials avoid skin irritation while ensuring durability. Reflective collars also enhance safety during walks, especially in low light conditions.



### How Do I Choose The Right Collar Size For My Puppy?



Measure your puppy’s neck with a soft tape measure. Choose collars labeled adjustable to fit small, medium, or large sizes. Make sure you can fit two fingers between the collar and neck for comfort and security.



### Are Reflective Collars Important For Puppy Safety?



Yes, reflective collars improve visibility during early morning or evening walks. They help drivers and pedestrians see your puppy clearly, reducing the risk of accidents. Many collars and leash sets like Ladoogo or Maiiu include reflective features for added safety.



### Can I Use A No-pull Harness For My Puppy?



Yes, no-pull harnesses like YIMEIS or rabbitgoo help train puppies to walk without pulling. They distribute pressure evenly and reduce choking risks. These harnesses are usually adjustable and padded for comfort during walks.



### What Leash Length Is Ideal For Puppies?



A 4 to 6-foot leash is ideal for puppies to maintain control while allowing some freedom. Most sets like Olahibi and EXPAWLORER come with 5-foot leashes, balancing safety and comfort during walks.



### How Often Should I Replace My Puppy’s Collar And Leash?



Replace collars and leashes when they show signs of wear or if your puppy outgrows them. Regularly check for fraying or damage to ensure safety. Adjustable sets make it easier to update as your puppy grows.
