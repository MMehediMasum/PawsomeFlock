## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Training Treats For Shih Tzu Puppies?



The best training treats for Shih Tzu puppies are soft, natural, and low-calorie. Options like Blue Buffalo Baby BLUE and Wellness Puppy Treats are ideal. They offer gentle ingredients that support growth and are easy to chew during training sessions.



### Are Grain-free Treats Good For Shih Tzu Puppies?



Yes, grain-free treats like Wellness Puppy Treats help reduce allergies and digestive issues. They contain high-quality proteins and natural ingredients, making them safe and healthy for Shih Tzu puppies during training.



### Which Treats Support Joint Health For Shih Tzu Puppies?



Zesty Paws Training Treats are excellent for joint and muscle health. These treats contain ingredients that promote hip and joint support, perfect for active Shih Tzu puppies during their training phases.



### How To Choose Soft Treats For Shih Tzu Training?



Choose soft treats like Blue Buffalo Baby BLUE Soft Biscuits or Pupford Soft & Chewy Treats. These are easy to chew and swallow, perfect for small mouths and frequent rewards during training.



### Are Freeze-dried Treats Good For Shih Tzu Puppies?



Yes, freeze-dried treats such as Pupford Freeze Dried Training Treats retain nutrients and flavor. They are lightweight, healthy, and highly motivating for Shih Tzu puppies during training sessions.



### Can Peanut Butter Treats Be Safe For Shih Tzu Puppies?



Yes, Zuke’s Mini Naturals with real peanut butter are safe in moderation. They provide protein and flavor that puppies love, but always check for allergies before regular use.
