* **Black-capped Chickadee:**Small and black-capped with white cheeks, but lacks a white wing stripe.

* **Dark-eyed Junco:**Often black or dark gray with white outer tail feathers, not a stripe on the wing.

These birds might seem similar at first glance, but their markings and behaviors vary. Have you ever mistaken one for the other during a quick glance? Paying attention to small details can make all the difference.
