## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Non-rawhide Dog Bones?



Non-rawhide dog bones are safer and easier to digest. They reduce choking risks and avoid digestive blockages. These treats, like DreamBone and SmartBones, use real chicken or peanut butter for flavor. They provide long-lasting chewing enjoyment without harmful chemicals.



### Which Non-rawhide Bones Suit Aggressive Chewers Best?



Pork Chomps and Earth Animal No Hide chews are ideal for aggressive chewers. They are durable, natural, and long-lasting. These options prevent damage to teeth and keep dogs engaged. Their tough texture satisfies strong chewing instincts safely.



### Are Non-rawhide Chews Healthy For Dogs?



Yes, non-rawhide chews are healthy when made from natural ingredients. Products like NutriChomps and Canine Naturals use real chicken and simple recipes. They support digestion and provide protein without additives. Always choose treats labeled rawhide-free for safer chewing.



### How Long Do Rawhide-free Chews Typically Last?



Rawhide-free chews like DreamBone Spirals and SmartBones last between 15 to 45 minutes. Longevity depends on your dog’s chewing strength and size. Harder chews like Pork Chomps last longer. These treats provide extended chewing satisfaction without harmful residues.



### Are Peanut Butter Flavored Non-rawhide Bones Safe?



Yes, peanut butter flavored bones like SmartBones Medium are safe for most dogs. They contain real peanut butter without xylitol or harmful additives. Always check the ingredients for allergens. These treats offer a tasty and safe chewing experience.



### Can Small Dogs Enjoy Non-rawhide Chews Safely?



Small dogs can enjoy mini-sized rawhide-free chews like SmartBones Mini Bones. These are sized appropriately to prevent choking. They use digestible ingredients like chicken for gentle digestion. Always supervise small dogs while chewing to ensure safety.
