### How Does Activated Charcoal Help Control Odors?



Activated charcoal traps and absorbs odor-causing molecules effectively. It prevents litter box smells from spreading in your home. Fresh Step’s multi-cat litter uses this to maintain a fresh environment longer.



### Which Litter Lasts Longest For Odor Control?



Fresh Step Heavy Duty Advanced litter provides up to 30 days of odor control. ARM & HAMMER Clump & Seal Platinum lasts 14 days. Both are great choices for extended freshness in multi-cat households.



### Are Scented Litters Safe For Multiple Cats?



Yes, but choose formulas with natural essential oils like Arm & Hammer Lavender. Some cats prefer unscented options, such as Dr. Elsey’s Ultra UnScented, to avoid irritation or allergies.



### How Do Litter Deodorizers Improve Odor Control?



Litter deodorizers like Arm & Hammer Baking Soda neutralize smells without masking them. They enhance the effectiveness of clumping litters and keep your home smelling clean longer.



### What Is The Best Low-dust Cat Litter Option?



Fresh Step Multi-Cat Litter has a low dust formula, reducing respiratory issues for cats and owners. Dr. Elsey’s Ultra UnScented clay litter is also low dust and highly recommended.
