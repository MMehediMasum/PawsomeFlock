Regular brushing also helps control dandruff and improves blood flow to the skin. Selecting a brush with comfortable handles makes grooming easier for both you and your cat. This guide covers top options like the Aumuca Cat Brush, Depets Self Cleaning Slicker Brush, and Menesia Pet Soft Brush.
