A well-designed box encourages cats to use it consistently. Large or extra-large boxes work better for big cats or multiple cats at home. Some models include odor control filters and scoops for convenience. Finding the best litter box can improve your cat’s habits and keep your home clean. This guide covers top options with features that help cats who pee outside the box.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Litter Box Prevents Cats From Peeing Outside?



Litter boxes with high sides or pee shields, like QNBCAA 3Pcs Pee Shields, prevent spills. Enclosed or hooded boxes also help contain urine and reduce mess.



### How Do Odor Control Litter Boxes Work?



Boxes with built-in charcoal filters, such as Nature’s Miracle Hooded Litter Box, absorb and neutralize odors. This keeps your home smelling fresh and clean longer.



### Which Litter Box Suits Large Or Multiple Cats?



XL or extra-large boxes like IRIS USA XX-Large and Stainless Steel Cat Litter Box provide ample space. They reduce crowding and help manage multiple cats’ needs easily.



### Are Stainless Steel Litter Boxes Better For Odor Control?



Yes, stainless steel boxes like WoofiGo don’t absorb odors or urine. They are easy to clean and more hygienic than plastic options.



### What Features Help Reduce Litter Scatter Effectively?



High-sided boxes with scatter shields, like IRIS USA Large Open Top, keep litter contained. These designs minimize mess and make cleaning faster and simpler.



### Why Choose A Top-entry Litter Box For Cats?



Top-entry boxes prevent dogs from accessing litter and reduce scatter. They also offer privacy and help contain odors effectively.
