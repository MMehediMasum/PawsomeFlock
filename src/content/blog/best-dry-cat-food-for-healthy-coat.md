## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Improve A Cat's Coat Health In Dry Food?



High-quality proteins like salmon and turkey support coat health. Omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids nourish skin and fur. Vitamins such as biotin and zinc also enhance coat shine and reduce shedding.



### How Does Dry Cat Food Support Sensitive Skin?



Formulas with limited ingredients and added probiotics soothe sensitive skin. Ingredients like turkey and salmon reduce allergy risks. These foods balance digestion and improve nutrient absorption, promoting healthier skin.



### Can Dry Cat Food Help Reduce Hairballs?



Yes, fiber-rich dry foods like Purina Cat Chow Hairball aid digestion. They reduce hairball formation by helping cats pass ingested hair naturally. Regular feeding improves coat condition and overall digestive health.



### Is Grain-free Dry Cat Food Better For Coat Health?



Grain-free options like Stella & Chewy's focus on protein and healthy fats. They avoid fillers that may irritate skin. Grain-free diets can improve coat shine and reduce allergies in sensitive cats.



### How Do Probiotics In Dry Cat Food Benefit Cats?



Probiotics promote healthy digestion and nutrient absorption. They support skin and coat by reducing inflammation and allergies. Probiotic-rich foods like IAMS Proactive Health enhance overall immune function and coat quality.
