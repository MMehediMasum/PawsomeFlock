## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Litter Box For High Spraying Cats?



The best litter box for high spraying cats has high sides and an enclosed design. Stainless steel options prevent odor absorption and are easy to clean. Look for extra-large sizes to accommodate big cats and prevent urine leakage effectively.



### Why Choose Stainless Steel Litter Boxes For Cats?



Stainless steel litter boxes are durable, rust-resistant, and odor-free. They don’t absorb urine or smells, making them ideal for high spraying cats. These boxes are easy to clean and prevent bacteria buildup, promoting better hygiene for your cat and home.



### How Do High-sided Litter Boxes Help With Spraying?



High-sided litter boxes contain urine and litter scatter, reducing mess. They prevent spray from escaping, keeping floors clean. This design is especially helpful for cats that spray or mark territory frequently indoors.



### Are Enclosed Litter Boxes Better For Multi-cat Households?



Yes, enclosed litter boxes offer privacy and help contain odors. They reduce litter scatter and prevent urine leakage. Enclosed models with lids and high sides work well for multi-cat homes to maintain cleanliness.



### Can Litter Box Pee Shields Reduce Spraying Mess?



Pee shields add extra protection by blocking urine sprays outside the box. They are easy to install on open top litter boxes. These shields help keep surrounding areas cleaner and reduce litter tracking.



### What Size Litter Box Suits Big Or Multiple Cats?



Extra-large or XXL litter boxes provide enough space for big or multiple cats. Larger boxes reduce crowding and spraying outside the box. Choose models with high sides or lids for better odor and mess control.
