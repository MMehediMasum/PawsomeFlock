## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Probiotics For Dogs With Yeast?



Probiotics help balance yeast levels by promoting healthy gut bacteria. They reduce yeast infections, itchy skin, and improve immunity. Regular use supports overall digestive health and allergy relief in dogs prone to yeast overgrowth.



### How Do Probiotics Relieve Dog Allergies And Itchy Skin?



Probiotics strengthen the immune system and reduce inflammation. They help calm allergic reactions and soothe itchy skin. This supports healthier skin by balancing gut bacteria linked to allergy symptoms in dogs.



### Can Probiotics Improve Dog Digestive Health And Reduce Diarrhea?



Yes, probiotics restore gut flora balance and aid digestion. They reduce diarrhea, gas, and constipation by supporting healthy digestion. This improves nutrient absorption and overall gut function in dogs.



### Are Prebiotics Important In Dog Probiotic Supplements?



Prebiotics feed beneficial gut bacteria, enhancing probiotic effectiveness. They promote growth of good bacteria, improving digestion and immunity. Combining prebiotics with probiotics supports long-term gut health in dogs.



### Which Dog Probiotic Flavors Are Most Popular?



Common flavors include chicken, lamb, pork, and salmon. These flavors encourage dogs to chew supplements easily. Choosing a tasty probiotic improves compliance and health benefits for dogs with yeast and allergies.



### How Many Probiotic Chews Should I Give My Dog Daily?



Dosage depends on dog size and product instructions. Typically, 1-2 chews daily support gut and skin health. Always follow manufacturer guidelines or consult your vet for best results.
