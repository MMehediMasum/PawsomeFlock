## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Plants To Attract Birds Outdoors?



Bird of Paradise plants and Mexican Birds of Paradise bushes attract various bird species. Their bright flowers provide nectar and shelter, making them ideal for bird-friendly gardens.



### How Do Bird Of Paradise Plants Benefit Birds?



Bird of Paradise plants offer nectar-rich flowers that attract hummingbirds and other nectar-feeding birds. Their dense foliage also provides shelter and nesting spots for small birds.



### Can Bird Of Paradise Plants Grow Indoors?



Yes, Bird of Paradise plants can thrive indoors with enough sunlight. They need bright, indirect light and regular watering to stay healthy and vibrant inside your home.



### How Do I Protect My Garden From Pests Without Harming Birds?



Use bird netting with drawstrings to protect plants from pests like cicadas. This barrier keeps pests out while allowing birds to visit safely and feed on insects.



### What Accessories Help With Hanging Bird Feeders And Plants?



Shepherd hooks and metal plant hangers with decorative bird designs are perfect for hanging bird feeders and plants. They are sturdy, weather-resistant, and enhance garden aesthetics.



### Are Artificial Bird Of Paradise Plants Good For Bird-friendly Decor?



Artificial Bird of Paradise plants add vibrant decor but don’t provide nectar or shelter. They’re great for decoration but real plants better support bird habitats.
