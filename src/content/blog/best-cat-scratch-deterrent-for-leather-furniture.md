## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Cat Scratch Deterrent For Leather Furniture?



The best deterrents include adhesive furniture protectors and sprays like Nature's Miracle. They prevent damage without residue or harm to leather. Choose products designed specifically for leather to ensure strong adhesion and easy removal.



### How Do Adhesive Cat Scratch Protectors Work On Leather?



These protectors create a tough barrier cats dislike scratching. They stick firmly to leather, preventing claw damage. Most are transparent, reusable, and leave no residue. Some include optional pins for extra hold on corners.



### Are Cat Scratch Protectors Safe For Leather Couches?



Yes, protectors designed for leather are safe. They avoid damage by using gentle adhesives and materials. They are easy to remove without leaving marks or residue. Always choose products labeled specifically for leather furniture.



### Can Sprays Help Stop Cats From Scratching Furniture?



Yes, sprays like Nature's Miracle discourage scratching with safe, natural ingredients. They do not damage leather and are easy to apply. Sprays work best combined with physical protectors for maximum effect.



### How Long Do Cat Scratch Furniture Protectors Last?



Most protectors last several months to a year depending on usage. Durable, heavy-duty options resist wear and tear well. Replace or reposition them when adhesive weakens or they show signs of damage.



### Is It Easy To Install And Remove Cat Scratch Protectors?



Yes, most protectors are designed for easy application and removal. They use strong but gentle adhesive tapes or pins for corners. Removal leaves no residue or damage, making them perfect for renters or temporary use.
