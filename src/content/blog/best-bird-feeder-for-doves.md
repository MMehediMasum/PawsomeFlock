**Choosing the best bird feeder for doves helps bring these gentle birds to your yard. A good feeder offers easy access to food and keeps seeds fresh and clean.**



Doves prefer feeders that sit low or on platforms where they can feed comfortably. Tray feeders with mesh bottoms work well because they let seeds drain and stay dry. Cedar wood and recycled plastic feeders resist weather damage and last longer.



Seed mixes made for doves provide the right nutrition to keep them healthy and visiting often. Large, sturdy trays attract more birds and prevent spills. Metal mesh or stainless steel trays offer durability and easy cleaning. Selecting a feeder with an adjustable dome helps protect seeds from rain and squirrels. Using the right feeder creates a welcoming space for doves to visit your garden regularly.
