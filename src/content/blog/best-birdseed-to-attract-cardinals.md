**Cardinals are bright, beautiful birds that many people enjoy watching. Choosing the right birdseed helps attract these colorful visitors to your feeder.**



Cardinals prefer seeds that are easy to eat and full of nutrition. Black oil sunflower seeds are a favorite because they have thin shells and high oil content. Safflower seeds also attract cardinals and keep away unwanted birds like squirrels.



Blended mixes designed for cardinals combine these seeds with nuts and fruits to provide variety. Using fresh, high-quality birdseed ensures cardinals return often. Clean feeders regularly to keep birds safe and healthy. With the right seed and care, your backyard can become a lively spot full of cardinals all year.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Birdseed Attracts Cardinals The Most?



Cardinals prefer seeds like sunflower, safflower, and peanuts. Blends such as Audubon Park Cardinal Blend and Kaytee Cardinal Wild Bird Seed work well to attract them.



### How To Choose The Best Cardinal Birdseed Blend?



Look for blends with high sunflower and safflower seed content. Products like Wagner's Cardinal Blend and Happy Wings Cardinal Mix are ideal for cardinals.



### Can Flavored Birdseed Attract Cardinals Better?



Yes, flavored seeds like Wagner's Farmer's Delight with cherry flavor can entice cardinals by adding appealing scent and taste.



### Is Safflower Seed Good For Cardinals?



Yes, cardinals enjoy safflower seed. It’s a favorite in mixes like Happy Wings Cardinal Blend and helps attract these birds effectively.



### How Much Cardinal Birdseed Should I Buy?



Buy based on feeder size and bird activity. Bags from 4 to 10 pounds, like Kaytee’s 7 lb or Wagner’s 10-pound blends, offer good value.



### Are No-grow Seeds Better For Cardinal Feeders?



Yes, no-grow seeds prevent sprouting and mess. Happy Wings Cardinal Blend uses no-grow seed to keep feeders cleaner and birds healthier.
