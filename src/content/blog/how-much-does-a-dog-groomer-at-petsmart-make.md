## Petsmart Groomer Salary Range

The salary range for a Petsmart dog groomer varies by experience and location. Understanding this range helps set clear expectations for those interested in this job. Pay depends on skills and time spent in the role. Let's explore the typical earnings for groomers at different stages.

### Entry-level Pay

New groomers at Petsmart usually start with a lower wage. The entry-level pay often falls between $10 and $13 per hour. This rate allows beginners to gain experience while earning a fair wage. The focus is on learning grooming techniques and customer service skills.

### Experienced Groomer Earnings

Experienced groomers earn more due to their skill and speed. Their hourly pay can rise to $15 or more. Some can make up to $20 per hour in busy locations. Skilled groomers often receive tips, increasing their total income. Experience also opens doors to bonuses or raises.

### Factors Influencing Salary

* Location: Pay rates differ across cities and states.
* Store Performance: High-traffic stores may offer better wages.
* Skill Level: Advanced grooming skills boost earnings.
* Customer Reviews: Positive feedback can lead to raises.
* Work Hours: Full-time groomers usually make more than part-time.

## Work Schedule And Hours

Understanding the work schedule and hours is important for anyone interested in a dog groomer job at Petsmart. The hours can vary based on the position and store location. Full-time dog groomers usually work around 35 to 40 hours a week. Their schedules often include weekdays and weekends. Part-time groomers have fewer hours, typically under 30 per week. This option suits those balancing other commitments or starting in grooming. Both schedules require good time management to handle pets efficiently.

### Overtime And Holiday Pay

Petsmart may offer overtime pay for hours worked beyond standard schedules. Overtime rates often increase the hourly wage, rewarding extra work. Holiday shifts can pay higher rates too, reflecting the busy times. Working holidays means more income but also more demanding work. Groomers should check with their manager about specific pay policies.
