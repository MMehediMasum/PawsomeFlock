Brands like Purina, IAMS, Nutrish, and Blue Buffalo provide options that promote healthy weight, improve digestion, and reduce hairballs. Selecting the right food can also help with cleaner litter boxes and better coat health. This guide reviews top dry cat foods that meet indoor cats’ unique needs, making feeding easier and healthier for your pet.
