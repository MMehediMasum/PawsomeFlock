### How Do Cranberry Chews Support Dog Bladder Health?



Cranberry chews contain antioxidants that strengthen the urinary tract lining. They reduce bacterial adhesion to the bladder walls. This helps prevent infections and bladder stones. The chews also improve immune response and support gut health, making them a convenient and tasty option for dogs.



### Are Cranberry Supplements Safe For Dogs With Bladder Stones?



Yes, cranberry supplements are generally safe for dogs with bladder stones. They help dissolve small stones and prevent new ones from forming. However, always consult your vet before starting any supplement, especially if your dog has existing bladder conditions or is on medication.



### How Long Does It Take For Cranberry Supplements To Work?



Cranberry supplements usually show effects within 1 to 2 weeks. Consistent daily use is important for best results. Some dogs may take longer depending on severity of infection or bladder issues. Always follow dosage instructions and consult your vet if symptoms persist.



### Can Cranberry Supplements Prevent Urinary Incontinence In Dogs?



Yes, cranberry supplements can help reduce urinary incontinence. They strengthen bladder control and reduce leakage frequency. These supplements support overall urinary tract health, which helps manage incontinence symptoms. For best results, combine cranberry with proper diet and vet care.



### What Ingredients Enhance Cranberry Supplements For Dog Uti Treatment?



Many cranberry supplements include D-Mannose, fish oil, and vitamins. D-Mannose helps prevent bacterial adhesion in the urinary tract. Fish oil supports kidney function and reduces inflammation. Vitamins boost immune health, making the supplement more effective in treating and preventing UTIs.
