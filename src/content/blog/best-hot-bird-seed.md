**Choosing the best hot bird seed helps attract vibrant birds to your feeder. These spicy blends keep squirrels away and provide energy for wild birds.**



Hot bird seeds mix traditional seeds with spicy ingredients to deter pests and encourage birds to visit. Birds enjoy the heat, but squirrels and other animals often avoid it. This means less waste and more feeding time for birds. Brands like Cole’s, Wild Delight, and COOL BIRDS offer various blends with chili oil, cayenne pepper, and sunflower hearts.



These mixes suit different feeder types and bird species. Whether you want a small 5-pound bag or a large 20-pound supply, hot bird seed options fit your needs. This guide covers popular, effective choices to keep your garden lively and pest-free.
