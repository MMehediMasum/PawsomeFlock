Switching to Fancy Feast dry food should be gradual. Sudden changes can upset your cat’s stomach. Follow these tips to help your cat adjust:

* Mix 25% new food with 75% old food for 3 days.
* Increase new food to 50% for the next 3 days.
* Raise new food to 75% for another 3 days.
* Feed 100% new food after about 10 to 12 days.

Watch for any signs of digestive upset. If problems occur, slow down the transition.
