## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Hummingbird Window Feeder Leak Proof?



Leak proof feeders have sealed feeding ports and sturdy construction. This prevents nectar spills and keeps your window clean. Products like Kingsyard and Aspects use advanced sealing to ensure no leaks during outdoor use.



### How Do Window Feeders Keep Wasps And Bees Away?



Bee and wasp proof feeders use special port designs or moats. These barriers prevent insects from reaching the nectar. Models like Kingsyard and Perky-Pet include these features for uninterrupted hummingbird feeding.



### Are Suction Cup Mounts Strong Enough For Outdoor Feeders?



High-quality suction cups provide strong, secure attachment to glass surfaces. Feeders from Dreamdrawer and Redboat offer durable suction cups that hold feeders firmly, even in windy conditions.



### How Easy Is It To Clean And Refill Window Feeders?



Most window feeders are designed for easy cleaning and refilling. Features include wide openings and removable parts. Brands like HCGADON and Skweeky Hitch emphasize hassle-free maintenance to keep nectar fresh.



### What Size Feeder Is Best For Attracting Hummingbirds?



Feeders ranging from 3 to 10 ounces suit different needs. Smaller feeders like iBorn are portable, while larger ones like Skweeky Hitch support more birds. Choose based on your yard size and hummingbird activity.



### Can Window Feeders Provide A Close-up Bird Watching Experience?



Yes, window feeders mount directly on glass, allowing up-close views. Hand-blown glass models like Pozfun enhance this experience with clear visibility and aesthetic appeal. Perfect for bird lovers wanting close encounters.
