## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Height Is Best For A Retractable Dog Fence?



A height of 33 to 42 inches is ideal for most dogs. Taller fences prevent jumping and escape. Retractable fences in this range offer secure containment without being bulky.



### How Wide Can Retractable Dog Fences Expand?



Widths vary from 55 inches to over 200 inches. Choose based on your doorway or outdoor space size. Extra-wide fences cover large openings effectively for outdoor use.



### Are Retractable Dog Gates Suitable For Outdoor Use?



Yes, many models feature weather-resistant materials and sturdy mesh. Outdoor gates withstand elements and provide safe containment. Look for reinforced rods and durable fabric for longevity.



### Can Retractable Fences Prevent Small Pets From Crawling Under?



Models with reinforced support rods and strips effectively block crawling. The design tightens mesh to the floor, enhancing safety. This prevents escapes for small dogs or pets.



### How Easy Is Installation For Retractable Pet Gates?



Most retractable gates offer simple, tool-free installation. One-hand operation and adjustable widths add convenience. They fit various doorways, stairs, and outdoor areas quickly.



### Are Retractable Dog Fences Portable And Space-saving?



Yes, they roll up compactly for storage and transport. Portable designs suit travel, apartments, and temporary use. Their lightweight frame and mesh ensure easy handling.
