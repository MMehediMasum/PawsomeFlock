## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do Ultrasonic Rodent Repellents Protect Car Engines?



Ultrasonic repellents emit high-frequency sounds that rodents find uncomfortable. This deters rats, mice, and squirrels from nesting under the hood. They are safe, non-toxic, and effective in preventing damage to wires and engine components without using chemicals or traps.



### Are Natural Granular Repellents Safe For Pets And People?



Yes, all-natural granular repellents like Skunk Scram are safe for pets and humans. They use natural ingredients that repel animals without harmful chemicals. These granules effectively keep cats, rodents, and other pests away from your car and surrounding areas without risk.



### Can Ultrasonic Repellents Deter Both Rodents And Cats?



Ultrasonic repellents primarily target rodents like mice and rats. However, some products also help deter cats by emitting strobe lights or sounds unpleasant to them. Combining ultrasonic waves with visual deterrents increases protection against various animals under your car.



### How Do Strobe Lights Improve Rodent Repellent Effectiveness?



Strobe lights add a visual deterrent that startles rodents and cats. When combined with ultrasonic sounds, the flashing lights enhance the repellent’s ability to keep animals away. This dual-action approach is more effective than sound alone in protecting vehicles from pests.



### Is Battery-powered Repellent Better For Car Engine Protection?



Battery-powered repellents offer portability and easy installation under the hood. They work independently of the car’s electrical system, ensuring continuous operation even when the engine is off. This makes them reliable for long-term rodent and animal deterrence in vehicles.



### What Maintenance Is Needed For Car Rodent Repellents?



Most rodent repellents require minimal maintenance. Ultrasonic devices need occasional battery checks or replacements. Granular repellents should be replenished as they disperse. Regular inspection ensures the repellent remains effective and your car engine stays protected from pests.
