Cranberry supplements support urinary tract health by preventing bacterial adhesion. They help reduce urinary tract infections, bladder stones, and incontinence in dogs. These chews also boost immune and gut health, ensuring overall well-being.



### How Do Urinary Tract Supplements Help Dogs With Bladder Control?



Urinary tract supplements strengthen bladder function and reduce inflammation. Ingredients like D-Mannose and cranberry extract prevent bacterial buildup. This improves bladder control and reduces episodes of incontinence in dogs.



### Are These Supplements Safe For All Dog Breeds And Ages?



Most urinary tract supplements are safe for all breeds and ages. Always check product labels and consult your vet before starting new supplements. Proper dosage and vet guidance ensure safety and effectiveness.



### Can These Supplements Prevent Recurring Urinary Tract Infections?



Yes, cranberry and other urinary supplements help prevent recurring infections. They create an environment hostile to bacteria in the urinary tract. Regular use supports bladder health and lowers infection risk.



### How Long Does It Take To See Results From These Supplements?



Results vary but typically appear within 2 to 4 weeks. Consistent daily use is essential for effectiveness. If symptoms persist, consult your veterinarian for further treatment options.



### What Additional Ingredients Support Urinary Health In These Supplements?



Many supplements include echinacea, fish oil, and vitamins. These ingredients boost immune response and reduce inflammation. Combined with cranberry, they provide comprehensive urinary and kidney health support.
