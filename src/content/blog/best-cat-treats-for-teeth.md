## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Cat Treats For Dental Health?



Greenies and Purina DentaLife treats are top choices. They help reduce plaque and tartar while freshening breath. These treats are vet-recommended and made with safe, tasty ingredients cats love.



### How Do Dental Treats Improve Cat Teeth?



Dental treats clean teeth by scrubbing plaque during chewing. They also support gum health and reduce bad breath. Regular use helps maintain strong teeth and prevents dental diseases.



### Are Natural Ingredients Important In Cat Dental Treats?



Yes, natural ingredients like chicken, salmon, and catnip are safer and more appealing. They reduce risk of allergies and promote overall oral health. Products like WHIMZEES use wholesome, natural flavors for dental benefits.



### Can Dental Treats Replace Brushing My Cat’s Teeth?



Dental treats complement but don’t replace brushing. Brushing remains the most effective way to clean teeth. Treats help maintain dental hygiene between brushings and make oral care easier.



### Which Flavors Do Cats Prefer In Dental Treats?



Popular flavors include oven-roasted chicken, salmon, and catnip. Cats enjoy these tastes, encouraging consistent chewing. Flavor variety helps keep cats interested in daily dental care.



### How Often Should I Give Dental Treats To My Cat?



Daily use is recommended for best results. Consistent treats help control plaque buildup and maintain fresh breath. Follow package guidelines to avoid overfeeding and ensure balanced nutrition.
