Opt for these trusted brands to ensure your dog enjoys a healthy, allergy-free life.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Are Best For Dogs With Allergies?



Limited ingredient recipes like sweet potato, salmon, and turkey reduce allergy risks. Grain-free options also help sensitive dogs avoid common allergens such as wheat and corn, promoting better digestion and skin health.



### How Does Grain-free Canned Dog Food Help Allergies?



Grain-free canned foods eliminate common allergens like wheat and corn. This reduces allergic reactions and supports sensitive stomachs, improving your dog’s overall skin and coat condition.



### Are Limited Ingredient Diets Effective For Allergic Dogs?



Yes, limited ingredient diets simplify your dog's meals. They minimize exposure to allergens and reduce allergic symptoms, promoting healthier skin and digestion.



### Can Wet Food Improve My Dog's Allergy Symptoms?



Wet food adds moisture and uses hypoallergenic ingredients. This supports hydration and eases digestion, which can help reduce allergy-related discomfort and improve skin health.



### Which Protein Sources Are Best For Allergic Dogs?



Novel proteins like venison, lamb, and salmon are less likely to cause allergies. These proteins provide essential nutrients without triggering common food sensitivities.



### Is It Safe To Switch My Dog To Allergy-friendly Canned Food?



Yes, switch gradually over 7-10 days to avoid digestive upset. Monitor your dog’s reaction to ensure the new food improves allergy symptoms effectively.
