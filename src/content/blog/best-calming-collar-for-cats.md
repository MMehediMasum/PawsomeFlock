### How Long Do Calming Collars For Cats Last?



Most calming collars last between 30 to 60 days. Some products offer extended effects up to 120 days with multi-pack options.



### Are Calming Collars Safe For All Cats?



Yes, calming collars use natural pheromones and are safe for cats of all ages and breeds when used as directed.



### Can Calming Collars Reduce Cat Anxiety And Stress?



Yes, these collars help reduce anxiety, stress, loud noises, and separation issues by releasing calming pheromones around your cat.



### How Adjustable And Waterproof Are Calming Collars?



Most calming collars are adjustable to fit various neck sizes and waterproof to maintain effectiveness even if your cat gets wet.



### Do Calming Collars Help With Aggressive Or Meowing Cats?



Yes, pheromone collars can reduce aggression and excessive meowing by promoting calm behavior and relaxation in stressed cats.



### Can Calming Collars Be Used Indoors And Outdoors?



Yes, calming collars are designed for both indoor and outdoor use to help cats stay calm in various environments.
