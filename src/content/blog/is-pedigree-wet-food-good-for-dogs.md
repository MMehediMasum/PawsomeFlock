## Tips For Feeding Wet Food

Feeding wet food to your dog can be healthy and tasty. It adds moisture and variety to their diet.

To keep your dog happy and healthy, follow some simple tips when feeding wet food like Pedigree.

### Transitioning Your Dog

Switch your dog to wet food slowly. Mixing wet food with dry food helps them adjust.

Start by adding a small amount of wet food. Increase it over several days until fully switched.

* Mix 25% wet with 75% dry food on day one
* Increase wet food to 50% by day three
* Use 75% wet food by day five
* Feed only wet food by day seven

### Portion Control

Feed the right amount of wet food to avoid weight problems. Follow feeding guidelines on the package.

Adjust the portion based on your dog's size, age, and activity level to keep them healthy.

* Small dogs need smaller portions
* Active dogs may need more food
* Older dogs often require less food
* Consult your vet for exact amounts

### Storage And Shelf Life

Store wet food properly to keep it fresh and safe for your dog. Use airtight containers after opening.

Keep opened cans or pouches in the fridge and use them within a few days to avoid spoilage.

* Refrigerate opened wet food
* Use within 2 to 3 days after opening
* Do not leave wet food out for more than 2 hours
* Check expiration dates before feeding
