## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Are Best For Hummingbird Food?



The best hummingbird food includes pure cane sugar and water in a 1:4 ratio. Avoid red dye and additives. Natural nectar mixes like Kaytee and Hummer's Galore use safe, all-natural ingredients suitable for hummingbirds.



### How Long Does Ready-to-use Hummingbird Nectar Last?



Ready-to-use nectar like Kaytee and Natural Springs lasts about one week in warm weather. Cooler temperatures can extend freshness up to two weeks. Always replace old nectar to prevent mold and harmful bacteria.



### Can I Use Powdered Nectar For Hummingbirds?



Yes, powdered nectar like Sapphire Labs and Perky-Pet concentrate is convenient. Mix with water as per instructions for fresh, clear nectar. Powdered forms often last longer and are easy to store.



### Are Preservatives Safe In Hummingbird Nectar?



Most commercial nectars avoid harmful preservatives. Products like Hummer's Galore use no preservatives or dyes. Natural formulas are safest to protect hummingbird health and encourage feeding.



### How Much Hummingbird Nectar Should I Prepare?



Prepare nectar based on feeder size and hummingbird activity. A 64-ounce bottle or mix is ideal for frequent refills. Avoid overfilling to prevent fermentation and spoilage.



### Why Avoid Red Dye In Hummingbird Food?



Red dye is unnecessary and may harm hummingbirds. Most feeders are red, attracting birds naturally. Choose clear, natural nectar like Pennington or Perky-Pet to keep birds healthy.
