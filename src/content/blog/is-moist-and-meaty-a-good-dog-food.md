### Portion Sizes

Determining the right portion size is key to keeping your dog healthy. Moist And Meaty often comes in small, pre-portioned sachets, which makes measuring easier. However, the correct amount depends on your dog's weight, age, and activity level.

Here’s a simple guide to help you:

| 10 - 20 kg | 400 - 600 | 4 - 6 |
| 20 - 30 kg | 700 - 900 | 7 - 9 |

Adjust portions based on your dog's appetite and energy. Watch their weight and body condition closely. If you notice weight gain or loss, tweak the portions accordingly.

### Transitioning To Moist And Meaty

Switching your dog’s food too quickly can cause stomach upset. Instead, introduce Moist And Meaty gradually over 7 to 10 days. This helps your dog’s digestive system adjust without discomfort.

Try mixing increasing amounts of Moist And Meaty with your dog’s current food daily. Start with 25% Moist And Meaty and 75% old food. Then move to 50/50, then 75/25, until you feed only Moist And Meaty.

Have you noticed your dog’s reaction to new food? Some dogs love the taste right away, while others need more time. Keeping a close eye on your dog during this period helps catch any signs of allergies or digestive issues early.
