Glass feeders often last longer and look nice in your yard. Plastic feeders can be lighter and easier to handle. This list covers top feeders with different designs and sizes. Each one helps you enjoy hummingbirds safely and comfortably. Whether for your garden or as a gift, these feeders suit many needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do I Choose The Best Hummingbird Feeder?



Choose a feeder with multiple feeding ports and bee guards. Look for easy-to-clean designs and consider capacity based on hummingbird activity. Opt for feeders with ant moats and leak-proof features to ensure nectar stays fresh.



### Are Glass Hummingbird Feeders Better Than Plastic?



Glass feeders are durable and easier to clean. They maintain nectar freshness better than plastic. However, plastic feeders are lightweight and less prone to breakage. Choose based on your preference and feeder location.



### How Often Should I Clean My Hummingbird Feeder?



Clean your hummingbird feeder every 3 to 5 days. In hot weather, clean more frequently to prevent mold and bacteria. Regular cleaning ensures the health of visiting hummingbirds.



### What Features Prevent Ants And Bees In Feeders?



Look for feeders with built-in ant moats and bee guards. These features keep pests away from the nectar. Ant moats create a barrier, while bee guards prevent bees from accessing feeding ports.



### How Much Nectar Do Hummingbird Feeders Hold?



Nectar capacity varies; most feeders hold between 10 to 32 ounces. Choose based on the number of visiting hummingbirds. More birds require larger capacity feeders to ensure enough nectar is available.
