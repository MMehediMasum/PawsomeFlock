### How Does Grain-free Cat Food Benefit Senior Cats?



Grain-free options such as Wellness Complete Health reduce allergies and improve digestion. They focus on high protein and natural ingredients, helping seniors maintain energy and weight.



### Which Cat Food Supports Senior Cats’ Immune Health?

### Are High-protein Diets Good For Senior Cats?



Yes, high-protein diets like Purina ONE High Protein support muscle maintenance. Seniors need quality protein to stay strong and healthy as they age.



### How To Choose Dry Food For Indoor Senior Cats?



Look for formulas like Purina ONE Indoor Advantage Senior+ with controlled calories. These help prevent weight gain and support joint health in less active indoor cats.
