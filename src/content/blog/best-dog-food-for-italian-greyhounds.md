## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Dog Food For Italian Greyhounds?



The best dog food for Italian Greyhounds is high-quality, protein-rich, and easily digestible. Look for recipes with chicken or beef, like Healthy Breeds Jerky Bites. Avoid fillers and artificial additives to support their sensitive digestive system and maintain a lean, active body.



### Are Healthy Breeds Treats Good For Italian Greyhounds?



Yes, Healthy Breeds treats, such as Chicken & Rice or Beef Jerky Bites, are great. They provide premium protein and natural ingredients. These treats support healthy skin, coat, and energy levels, making them ideal rewards for Italian Greyhounds.



### How To Maintain Dental Health In Italian Greyhounds?



Use dental care snacks like UrbanX HealthyBones Mint Snacks. They reduce plaque and freshen breath. Regular brushing and vet checkups also help maintain oral hygiene, preventing gum disease common in small breeds like Italian Greyhounds.



### What Type Of Clothing Suits Italian Greyhounds?



Soft, stretchy, and lightweight clothes like D Doggy Star’s refined stripe onesie fit well. Italian Greyhounds have thin skin and low body fat, so breathable fabrics protect them from cold and sun, ensuring comfort and style.



### Can Puppy Shampoo Be Used For Italian Greyhounds?



Yes, tearless puppy shampoos like Healthy Breeds Italian Greyhound Puppy Shampoo are gentle and safe. They cleanse without irritating the skin or eyes. This shampoo supports the sensitive coat and skin of Italian Greyhound puppies.



### Are There Special Treats For Senior Italian Greyhounds?



Healthy Breeds Senior Dog Care Soft Chews are designed for older Italian Greyhounds. They support joint health, mobility, and overall wellness. These treats contain essential nutrients tailored to aging dogs’ needs.
