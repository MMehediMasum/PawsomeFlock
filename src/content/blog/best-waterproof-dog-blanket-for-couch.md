## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Dog Blanket Truly Waterproof?



A waterproof dog blanket has a special membrane blocking liquids. It prevents spills, urine, and moisture from reaching furniture. This keeps couches dry and clean. Most use leakproof materials and sealed seams. It’s ideal for pets prone to accidents or outdoor use.



### How Do I Choose The Right Size For My Dog Blanket?



Measure your dog or furniture surface first. Choose blankets labeled XL or XXL for large breeds. Ensure the blanket covers the couch or bed fully. Oversized blankets protect better from fur and dirt. Check product dimensions like 60x80 inches for accuracy.



### Are These Waterproof Dog Blankets Machine Washable?



Yes, most waterproof dog blankets are machine washable. They use durable fabrics that withstand frequent washing. Always follow care instructions on the label. Washing keeps the blanket fresh and odor-free. It also maintains waterproof quality and softness over time.



### Can Waterproof Dog Blankets Protect Furniture From Scratches?



Many blankets, like gogobunny, offer scratch-resistant layers. They shield sofas and beds from pet claws and rough play. This extends furniture life and reduces damage costs. Look for blankets labeled scratch proof or extra strong. Reversible designs add versatility and style.



### Do Waterproof Dog Blankets Also Provide Comfort To Pets?



Yes, these blankets often have soft fleece or plush tops. They offer warmth and coziness for dogs and cats. Materials like sherpa fleece soothe anxious or senior pets. Comfort features ensure pets enjoy resting spots without discomfort. Dual-sided blankets give extra softness and warmth.
