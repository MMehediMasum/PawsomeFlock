### Mountain Chickadee

The Mountain Chickadee shares a similar color pattern but adds a white stripe above its eye. This subtle difference helps separate it from the Black-capped Chickadee when you see them in mountainous or forested areas. Its gray body and black-and-white head make it a striking little bird.
