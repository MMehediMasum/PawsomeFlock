## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Anti-inflammatory Foods For Dogs?



Best anti-inflammatory foods for dogs include salmon, turmeric, green-lipped mussel, and pumpkin. These foods reduce joint pain and inflammation naturally, improving mobility and comfort in dogs with arthritis or allergies.



### How Does Turmeric Help Reduce Dog Inflammation?



Turmeric contains curcumin, a powerful anti-inflammatory compound. It helps reduce joint pain, swelling, and stiffness in dogs. Turmeric also supports overall health by boosting antioxidant levels and improving cardiovascular and digestive functions.



### Can Omega-3 Fish Oil Reduce Dog Joint Inflammation?



Yes, omega-3 fish oil from sources like wild-caught salmon lowers inflammation in dogs. It supports joint health, improves skin and coat condition, and enhances heart function by providing essential fatty acids.



### Are Glucosamine And Msm Effective For Dog Joint Support?



Glucosamine and MSM help repair cartilage and reduce inflammation in dog joints. Together, they improve mobility, ease stiffness, and support overall joint health in aging or active dogs.



### How Do Green-lipped Mussels Benefit Dog Joint Health?



Green-lipped mussels contain omega-3s and antioxidants that reduce inflammation and joint pain. They improve flexibility and support cartilage repair, making them ideal for dogs with arthritis or mobility issues.



### What Natural Supplements Help With Dog Pain And Inflammation?



Natural supplements like turmeric, glucosamine, MSM, salmon oil, and collagen provide effective anti-inflammatory relief. They promote joint health, reduce pain, and support overall wellness in active and senior dogs.
