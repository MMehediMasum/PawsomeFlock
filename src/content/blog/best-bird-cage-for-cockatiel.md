## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Bird Cage Is Ideal For A Cockatiel?



A cage at least 24 inches wide and 24 inches tall is ideal for cockatiels. Larger cages like the Yaheetech 52-inch provide ample space for flight and play, ensuring your bird’s comfort and exercise needs are met.



### Which Bird Cage Features Are Best For Cockatiel Safety?



Look for wrought iron bars with non-toxic powder coating. Secure locks, rounded edges, and sturdy construction prevent injuries and escapes. Cages like VIVOHOME 54 Inch offer these safety features, ensuring a secure environment for your cockatiel.



### Are Rolling Stands Necessary For Cockatiel Cages?



Rolling stands increase mobility, making cleaning and relocation easier. Many cages, such as the Yaheetech 64-inch and YITAHOME 62-inch, come with detachable rolling stands for convenient movement and maintenance, enhancing usability.



### How Important Is An Open Playtop For Cockatiels?



Open playtops encourage interaction and exercise outside the cage. Models like Yaheetech 61-inch offer open playtops, allowing your cockatiel to safely enjoy supervised playtime, boosting mental and physical health.



### Can These Cages Accommodate Multiple Bird Species?



Yes, cages like Yaheetech 69-inch and VIVOHOME 30 Inch are spacious enough for cockatiels, lovebirds, conures, and small parrots. They provide versatile space and accessories suitable for multiple birds living together comfortably.



### What Maintenance Features Help Keep Cockatiel Cages Clean?



Removable trays, easy-open doors, and detachable stands simplify cleaning. Most cages listed, including Yaheetech and VIVOHOME models, have these features, allowing quick waste removal and maintaining a hygienic habitat for your cockatiel.
