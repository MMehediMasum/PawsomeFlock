## Feeding Recommendations

Feeding your dog the right amount of food helps keep them healthy. Pure Harmony dog food offers balanced nutrition for dogs of all sizes.

It is important to follow feeding guidelines based on your dog's weight and activity level. This ensures they get enough energy without overeating.

### Portion Sizes

Portion sizes depend on your dog's age, weight, and activity. Check the feeding chart on the Pure Harmony packaging for guidance.

Adjust portions if your dog gains or loses weight. Smaller dogs usually need less food, while active dogs may need more.

* Small dogs (up to 20 lbs): ½ to 1½ cups per day
* Medium dogs (21-50 lbs): 1½ to 3 cups per day
* Large dogs (51-100 lbs): 3 to 5 cups per day
* Very active dogs may need 10-20% more food

### Transitioning Tips

Switching dog food should be done slowly. This helps avoid stomach upset and allows your dog to adjust to the new taste.

Mix Pure Harmony with your dog's old food over several days. Gradually increase the new food amount each day.

* Day 1-2: 25% new food, 75% old food
* Day 3-4: 50% new food, 50% old food
* Day 5-6: 75% new food, 25% old food
* Day 7: 100% new food
