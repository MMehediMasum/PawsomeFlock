## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do Top Fill Hummingbird Feeders Work?



Top fill hummingbird feeders have a wide opening for easy filling. Simply remove the lid and pour in nectar. These feeders are designed to prevent spills and make cleaning easier. This user-friendly design is ideal for beginners and experienced bird enthusiasts alike.



### Why Choose A Glass Hummingbird Feeder?



Glass hummingbird feeders are durable and resistant to discoloration. They offer clear visibility of nectar levels, making refilling easy. Glass material is also less likely to harbor bacteria compared to plastic, ensuring a safer feeding environment for birds.



### Are Built-in Bee Guards Effective?



Built-in bee guards are designed to keep bees and wasps away. They allow hummingbirds to access nectar while preventing larger insects from entering. This feature helps maintain the feeder's hygiene and ensures more nectar is available for the birds.



### What Is The Benefit Of Removable Perches?



Removable perches offer flexibility in feeder design. They allow you to cater to different bird preferences. Some hummingbirds prefer to hover, while others like to perch while feeding. This feature enhances the feeder's versatility and attractiveness.



### How Do I Clean My Hummingbird Feeder?



To clean a hummingbird feeder, disassemble the parts and soak them in warm, soapy water. Use a small brush to scrub hard-to-reach areas. Rinse thoroughly and allow the parts to dry completely before refilling with fresh nectar. Regular cleaning prevents mold and bacterial growth.
