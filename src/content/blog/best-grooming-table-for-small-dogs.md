## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Grooming Table Suits Small Dogs Best?



A grooming table around 30 to 36 inches is ideal for small dogs. It provides enough space without overwhelming them. Tables like Go Pet Club 30 Inch or Yaheetech 32-inch are perfect for home grooming and easy storage.



### Are Foldable Grooming Tables Good For Small Dogs?



Yes, foldable grooming tables are great for small dogs. They save space and are portable. Models like Go Pet Club and Yaheetech offer foldable options with adjustable arms, making grooming convenient and safe at home or on the go.



### How Important Is The Adjustable Arm On Grooming Tables?



Adjustable arms are crucial for small dog grooming. They hold your pet safely in place and prevent movement. Many tables, like Lyromix 46'' or Yaheetech 36'', feature adjustable arms with nooses for secure, comfortable grooming sessions.



### What Weight Capacity Is Needed For Small Dog Grooming Tables?



A weight capacity of 220 to 330 pounds is more than enough for small dogs. This ensures stability and safety during grooming. Tables like Yaheetech and Go Pet Club offer high weight capacities for durability and peace of mind.



### Can Grooming Tables Be Used For Cats And Small Dogs?



Yes, many grooming tables suit both cats and small dogs. Adjustable height and nooses accommodate different pets. For example, Lyromix and Yaheetech tables work well for small dogs and cats, making them versatile grooming stations.



### Do Grooming Tables Include Storage Features?



Some grooming tables come with mesh trays or shelves for grooming tools. This helps keep everything organized and within reach. Models like Yaheetech 46'' include mesh trays, making grooming efficient and clutter-free at home.
