## Common Feeding Mistakes

Feeding your dog Freshpet roll sounds simple, but many pet owners stumble on common mistakes that can affect their furry friend’s health. Understanding these errors helps you avoid unnecessary problems and keeps your dog thriving. Let’s look at what to watch out for when deciding how much Freshpet roll to feed per pound of your dog's weight.

### Overfeeding Risks

Giving your dog too much Freshpet roll can quickly lead to weight gain and digestive upset. Dogs fed beyond their calorie needs may develop obesity, which increases the risk of joint pain and heart problems.

Watch for signs like panting after mild activity or difficulty jumping. It’s easy to think your dog wants more food, but extra rolls won’t always make them happier—sometimes, it just stresses their body.

### Underfeeding Signs

On the flip side, feeding too little can cause your dog to lose energy and appear lethargic. If your dog seems unusually hungry, loses weight, or has a dull coat, you may need to adjust the portion size.

Check your dog’s body condition regularly—ribs should be felt but not seen prominently. If you’re unsure, ask your vet to help you find the right amount of Freshpet roll based on your dog’s activity level and size.

### Balancing Treats And Meals

Treats are great for training and bonding, but they add extra calories that can throw off your dog’s diet. Many dog owners forget to reduce meal portions when giving treats, leading to overfeeding without realizing it.

Think about all the snacks your dog gets throughout the day: table scraps, training rewards, and even a few bites from your plate. Adjust your dog’s Freshpet roll servings accordingly to keep a healthy balance.

## Transitioning To Freshpet Roll

Switching your dog to Freshpet Roll requires care and patience. Sudden changes in diet can upset your dog's stomach. A smooth transition helps avoid digestive issues and encourages acceptance of the new food.

### Gradual Introduction Plan

Start by mixing a small amount of Freshpet Roll with your dog's current food. Increase the Freshpet portion over 7 to 10 days. Watch how your dog responds to each change. A slow shift helps your dog's body adjust easily.

### Monitoring Digestive Health

Check your dog’s stool for changes in consistency and frequency. Look for signs like diarrhea, vomiting, or gas. If your dog seems uncomfortable, slow down the transition. Keep track of any unusual behavior or appetite changes.

### When To Consult A Vet

Contact your vet if your dog shows persistent digestive problems. Sudden weight loss or lethargy needs immediate attention. Your vet can offer advice tailored to your dog’s health needs. Early care prevents bigger health issues later.
