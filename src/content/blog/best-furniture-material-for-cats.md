## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Furniture Material For Cats To Scratch?



Carpet and sisal materials are ideal for cats to scratch. They offer texture cats love and protect furniture from damage. Self-adhesive carpet mats are great for easy application and replacement on couches or cat trees.



### How Do Self-adhesive Cat Scratch Mats Protect Furniture?



These mats stick firmly to furniture surfaces, creating a barrier against cat claws. They absorb scratching impact, preventing tears and scratches. Plus, they are trimmable to fit different furniture shapes and sizes perfectly.



### Can Cat Scratch Mats Be Customized For Different Furniture?



Yes, most cat scratch mats are trimmable and flexible. You can cut them to fit couches, walls, cat trees, or stairs. This DIY feature ensures full coverage and better protection tailored to your home setup.



### Are Transparent Couch Protectors Effective Against Cat Scratches?



Transparent protectors provide discreet protection without changing furniture appearance. They are self-adhesive and easy to install. These protectors deter cats from scratching while maintaining your sofa’s original look.



### How Long Do Adhesive Cat Scratch Mats Typically Last?



Adhesive mats usually last several months depending on scratching frequency. They are replaceable and designed for durability. Regularly check and replace worn mats to keep furniture fully protected from cat claws.
