Ingredients like salmon, brown rice, pumpkin, and probiotics support digestion. These reduce inflammation and promote gut health in senior dogs with sensitive stomachs.



### How Does Purina Pro Plan Benefit Senior Dogs' Digestion?



Purina Pro Plan includes salmon and rice, which are gentle on sensitive stomachs. It also supports skin health and digestion in senior dogs aged 7 and older.



### Are Grain-free Options Better For Sensitive Senior Dogs?



Grain-free foods like Solid Gold and Blue Buffalo Basics reduce allergens. They help dogs with sensitive stomachs avoid digestive issues linked to grains.



### Can Hill’s Science Diet Improve Senior Dog Digestion?



Yes, Hill’s Science Diet Perfect Digestion contains brown rice and whole oats. These ingredients provide fiber and nutrients, aiding digestion in senior dogs.



### Is Wet Dog Food Better For Sensitive Senior Dog Stomachs?



Wet dog food like Blue Buffalo Basics offers higher moisture content. This helps hydration and is easier to digest for senior dogs with sensitive stomachs.



### What Role Do Probiotics Play In Senior Dog Food?



Probiotics in foods improve gut flora balance and digestion. They reduce stomach upset and enhance nutrient absorption in senior dogs with sensitive stomachs.
