Self-cleaning brushes like Swihauk and Dipoo have retractable bristles. After grooming, press a button to remove hair easily. This feature saves time and keeps the brush clean for the next use, making grooming efficient and hassle-free.



### Which Brush Suits Long And Short-haired Cats?



Soft silicone brushes like the Aumuca and Pecute are ideal. They work well on both long and short hair, removing loose fur gently. These brushes prevent skin irritation and promote healthy grooming for all coat types.
