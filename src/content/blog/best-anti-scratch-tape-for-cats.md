## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Tape To Stop Cats From Scratching Furniture?



The best tape is double-sided, transparent, and non-toxic. Sofisti-Cat and Panther Armor tapes are highly effective. They create an unpleasant surface that discourages cats without harming them or furniture.



### How Does Anti-scratch Tape Protect My Furniture?



Anti-scratch tape creates a sticky barrier on furniture surfaces. Cats dislike the texture and avoid scratching. This protects couches, doors, and carpets from claw damage while training cats to stay away.



### Is Double-sided Tape Safe For Pets And Kids?



Yes, most anti-scratch tapes like Karaseno and Chuangseed are pet and kid safe. They use non-toxic adhesives and materials that won’t harm animals or children when applied properly.



### Can Anti-scratch Tape Be Used On All Furniture Types?



Yes, tapes like FixZilla and Petslucent work well on couches, chairs, doors, and carpets. They adhere to various surfaces and protect different furniture without leaving residue.



### How Long Does Anti-cat Scratch Tape Last On Furniture?



Anti-scratch tapes typically last from several weeks to months. Longevity depends on tape quality, surface type, and cat activity. Regular replacement ensures continued protection and effectiveness.



### Does Anti-scratch Tape Leave Residue Or Damage Furniture?



High-quality tapes like Panther Armor and Sofisti-Cat usually do not leave residue. They are designed for easy removal without damaging furniture finishes or fabrics. Always follow manufacturer removal instructions.
