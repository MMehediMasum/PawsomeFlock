## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Electric Fence Charger For Dogs?



The best electric fence charger for dogs balances power, range, and safety. Models like PE2 0. 10 Joule and FenceMate offer reliable containment and protection. Choose one with appropriate joule output and coverage for your yard size to keep dogs safe and secure.



### How Far Can These Electric Fence Chargers Cover?



Coverage varies by model, typically from 2 miles (8 acres) up to 20 miles. For example, the PE2 covers 2 miles, while the Andmon MINI400 reaches 20 miles. Select based on your property size and animal containment needs.



### Are Solar-powered Electric Fence Chargers Effective?



Yes, solar-powered chargers like the 0. 18J Portable Energizer provide eco-friendly, continuous power. They work well in sunny areas and reduce electricity costs. Solar models often include rechargeable batteries for night and cloudy day use.



### Can Electric Fence Chargers Protect Against Predators?



Absolutely. Chargers like Happy Henhouse and Briidea models deter raccoons, skunks, and other predators. They deliver safe shocks that discourage intrusion, protecting livestock, poultry, and pets without causing harm.



### How Do I Choose The Right Joule Rating For My Fence?



Choose joule rating based on fence length and animal type. Small pets need 0. 1–0. 25 joules, while larger livestock require higher output. Proper joule ensures effective containment and predator control without overloading your fence system.
