Products like Wagner’s Farmer’s Delight and Kaytee’s Nut & Berry Blend provide balanced nutrition. Sunflower-flavored mixes attract many bird species.



Waste-free foods reduce mess and protect the environment. High-protein blends support strong muscles and energy. Offering a mix of these foods encourages more birds to visit. Feeding wild birds also helps you learn about different species. Simple, quality bird food makes your yard a safe place for birds to eat and rest.
