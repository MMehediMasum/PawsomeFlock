## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Retractable Leash Ideal For Chewers?



Retractable leashes made with durable materials resist chewing damage. Strong nylon cords and reinforced handles enhance longevity. Models like flexi Cinturino and Croci Flexi New Classic offer chew-resistant designs. Choosing these ensures safety and durability during walks with aggressive chewers.



### How Do Retractable Leashes Help With Dog Training?



Retractable leashes provide controlled freedom, aiding obedience training. They allow gradual distance adjustment, encouraging recall skills. Brands like Edipets and WinGluge offer smooth extension and retraction. This flexibility supports effective training while keeping dogs safe and responsive.



### Which Retractable Leash Suits Large Dogs Up To 50 Kg?



Leashes such as flexi Cinturino New Classic L and Croci Flexi New Classic fit dogs up to 50 kg. These feature strong cords and ergonomic handles. Their lengths, typically 5 to 8 meters, provide ample movement while ensuring control over bigger dogs.



### Can Retractable Leashes Prevent Leash Tangling?



Certain models like TUG’s 360° swivel leash reduce tangling. The design allows leash rotation without twisting the cord. This feature improves walk comfort and prevents leash damage, especially useful for active dogs prone to pulling or spinning.



### Are Retractable Leashes Suitable For Small Dogs And Cats?



Yes, models like WinGluge and Flexi Nastro Strap are designed for small dogs and cats. They support weights up to 15-20 kg. These leashes offer lightweight, flexible, and safe walking options for smaller pets needing controlled freedom.
