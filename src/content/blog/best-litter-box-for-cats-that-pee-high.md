Extra-large sizes suit big cats or multiple cats in one home. Adding splash guards or lids can give extra protection. This guide covers the best litter boxes designed for cats that pee high. You will find options with high walls, anti-leak features, and easy maintenance. These products help keep your space fresh and your cat happy. Say goodbye to litter scatter and wet floors with the right litter box choice.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Stainless Steel Litter Boxes Ideal For High Pee Cats?



Stainless steel litter boxes resist urine stains and odors effectively. Their non-stick surface ensures easy cleaning. High sides prevent urine splashes, keeping the area tidy. Durable and hygienic, they are perfect for cats that pee high.



### How Do High-sided Litter Boxes Prevent Urine Leakage?



High-sided litter boxes create a barrier that contains urine splashes. This prevents messes outside the box. Enclosed designs with lids add extra protection. They keep floors clean and reduce frequent litter area cleaning.



### Are Xl Litter Boxes Better For Big Cats That Pee High?



Yes, XL litter boxes provide ample space for large cats. They reduce chances of urine escaping the box. Extra room helps cats feel comfortable and avoid accidents. High sides in XL boxes further contain urine splashes.



### Can Litter Box Pee Shields Help With Cats That Pee High?



Pee shields attach to litter boxes to block urine sprays. They protect walls and floors from stains. Transparent shields maintain visibility and are easy to clean. Pee shields are an affordable solution for high pee problems.



### What Features Make A Litter Box Easy To Clean For High Pee Cats?



Non-stick surfaces and stainless steel materials simplify cleaning. Boxes with removable lids and smooth edges help access every corner. Included scoops and mats make maintenance faster. Easy cleaning prevents odor buildup and keeps cats happy.



### How Do Enclosed Litter Boxes Help With Odor Control?



Enclosed litter boxes trap odors inside with sealed lids. High walls reduce litter scatter and urine splash. They maintain a cleaner, fresher environment for pets and owners. Enclosures are especially useful for multi-cat households.
