## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Window Perch Suits Large Cats Best?



A large window perch should support cats over 15 pounds comfortably. Look for sturdy frames and ample space, like 18x24 inches or bigger, for large cats to relax without feeling cramped.



### Are Suction Cup Window Perches Safe For Heavy Cats?



High-quality suction cup perches with strong cups and metal frames can hold large cats securely. Check weight limits and installation instructions carefully to ensure safety.



### How Do Orthopedic Window Perches Benefit Large Cats?



Orthopedic perches provide extra support with bolsters and cushions. They help relieve joint pressure, ideal for older or arthritic large cats, enhancing comfort during rest.



### Can Window Perches Fit Different Window Types Easily?



Many window perches offer adjustable designs to fit various sill widths. Some models include screw anchors or multiple installation options for secure mounting on different windows.



### What Materials Make The Best Durable Cat Window Perches?



Hardwood combined with metal frames offers durability and stability. Cushions with washable covers add comfort and easy maintenance, making these materials ideal for large cats’ perches.



### Is Assembly Difficult For Large Cat Window Perches?



Most window perches come with simple instructions and require minimal tools. Adjustable features and foldable designs make assembly quick and user-friendly for pet owners.
