## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Cage Is Best For Budgies?



A cage at least 18x18x18 inches is ideal for budgies. Larger cages offer more flight space. The Yaheetech 54-inch and 53-inch cages provide ample room for active budgies to fly and play comfortably.



### Are Rolling Stands Necessary For Bird Cages?



Rolling stands make cage mobility easier for cleaning and relocation. Many Yaheetech bird cages come with rolling stands. This feature enhances convenience without compromising cage stability or safety for your budgie.



### Which Cage Features Ensure Budgie Safety?



Look for sturdy wrought iron bars, secure locks, and smooth finishes. Yaheetech cages offer these features to prevent escapes and injuries. Proper bar spacing also avoids budgie getting stuck or harmed.



### Can Travel Bird Cages Keep Budgies Comfortable?



Yes, travel cages like the Yaheetech Open Play Top are compact and ventilated. They provide safety and comfort during trips. Handles and lightweight design improve portability for budgie owners on the go.



### What Toys And Accessories Suit Budgie Cages?



Toys like swings, ladders, and chewable perches stimulate budgies mentally and physically. Some Yaheetech cages come with built-in toys for enrichment. Adding these accessories enhances your budgie’s well-being and reduces boredom.
