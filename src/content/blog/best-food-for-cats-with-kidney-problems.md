## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Support Kidney Health In Cat Food?



Kidney-friendly cat foods contain low phosphorus, controlled protein, and added omega-3 fatty acids. These ingredients reduce kidney strain and inflammation, promoting better kidney function and overall health for cats with kidney problems.



### How Does Hill's Prescription Diet K/d Benefit Kidney Health?



Hill's k/d formula is designed to reduce kidney workload. It has controlled protein and phosphorus levels. This supports kidney function and helps manage chronic kidney disease in cats effectively.



### Are Wet Or Dry Foods Better For Cats With Kidney Issues?



Wet foods provide more hydration, which helps kidney function. Dry foods offer dental benefits but less moisture. A mix or mainly wet food is often best for kidney-compromised cats.



### Can Supplements Like Homeopet Feline Kidney Rescue Help?



Yes, HomeoPet Feline Kidney Rescue offers natural support for kidney health. It contains herbs that promote detoxification and kidney function, complementing a kidney-friendly diet for cats.



### Is Prescription Diet Necessary For Kidney Problems In Cats?



Prescription diets like Hill’s k/d or Blue Buffalo K+M are formulated specifically for kidney support. They provide balanced nutrition tailored to manage kidney disease and slow progression effectively.



### How Do Forza10 Renal Cat Foods Support Kidney Health?



Forza10 renal formulas use limited ingredients and fish or lamb protein. They offer low phosphorus and antioxidants, supporting kidney function and reducing oxidative stress in cats with kidney issues.
