## Order Processing Time

This phase begins right after you place your order and ends when the package is handed over to the shipping carrier. Knowing how long this takes can save you from last-minute worries about your pet’s meals.

### Typical Processing Duration

Chewy usually processes orders within 1 to 2 business days. This means your order is picked, packed, and prepared for shipment during this time. If you’ve ever ordered dog food and noticed it shipped the next day, that’s the processing time working efficiently.

Keep in mind weekends and holidays can add extra days. For instance, an order placed late Friday might not start processing until Monday. This small delay can affect when you get your delivery.

### Factors Affecting Processing Speed

Several things influence how fast your order moves through processing:

* **Product availability:**If the dog food is in stock, it’s packed quickly. Backordered items slow things down.
* **Order size:**Large orders or multiple items may require extra handling time.
* **Special requests:**Custom or subscription orders sometimes need extra checks.
* **Warehouse location:**Orders processed closer to your delivery address often ship faster.

Have you ever waited longer because a favorite brand was out of stock? It’s a common experience that shows how stock levels matter. If you want to avoid delays, check if your item is available before ordering.
