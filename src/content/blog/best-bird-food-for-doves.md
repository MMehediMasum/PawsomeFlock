## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Bird Food For Doves?



The best bird food for doves is a mix of seeds like millet, cracked corn, and safflower. It provides balanced nutrition and mimics their natural diet, promoting health and vitality.



### Can Doves Eat Sunflower Seeds Safely?



Yes, doves can safely eat sunflower seeds. These seeds are rich in nutrients and help improve their feather quality and energy levels.



### How Often Should I Feed Doves Bird Food?



Feed doves fresh bird food daily, offering small amounts twice a day. Regular feeding supports their digestion and overall well-being.



### Are Commercial Dove Food Mixes Better Than Homemade?



Commercial dove food mixes are convenient and nutritionally balanced. However, homemade mixes allow customization based on your dove’s preferences and dietary needs.



### Should I Add Supplements To Doves' Diet?



Supplements like calcium and grit are beneficial for doves. They aid digestion and strengthen bones, especially during breeding or molting seasons.
