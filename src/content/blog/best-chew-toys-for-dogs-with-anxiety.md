## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Chew Toys Good For Dogs With Anxiety?



Chew toys for anxious dogs are durable and calming. They help reduce stress and boredom by providing mental stimulation. Ingredients like hemp or bacon flavor add soothing effects. Toys designed for aggressive chewers last longer and keep dogs engaged safely.



### Which Chew Toys Are Best For Aggressive Chewers?



Tough nylon and rubber chew toys like Benebone and Fuufome are ideal. They withstand strong chewing and help clean teeth. These toys also reduce anxiety and boredom by keeping dogs busy and focused on play.



### How Do Chew Toys Help Reduce Dog Anxiety?



Chew toys provide a healthy outlet for nervous energy. They stimulate the dog's mind and distract from anxiety triggers. Interactive and flavored toys improve engagement, promoting calmness and reducing destructive behaviors linked to stress.



### Are Flavored Chew Toys Safe For Dogs?



Yes, reputable brands like Benebone use real bacon flavor that is safe. Always choose non-toxic, vet-approved chew toys. Flavored toys can increase interest, encouraging longer playtime and anxiety relief without harmful ingredients.



### Can Chew Toys Help With Dental Health?



Durable chew toys like SIHRMIU and Apasiri help clean teeth and gums. Chewing reduces plaque buildup and massages gums, supporting oral hygiene. Toys designed for aggressive chewers are especially effective for medium and large breeds.



### What Size Chew Toy Suits My Dog’s Breed?



Choose size based on your dog's breed and chewing strength. Medium toys fit small to medium dogs, while large toys suit bigger breeds. Proper sizing ensures safety and maximizes chewing benefits without choking risks.
