## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Dog Chews For Small Dogs?



The best chews for small dogs include Nature Gnaws Bully Sticks, Nylabone Healthy Edibles, and Pur Luv Chicken Jerky. These chews are sized for small mouths, safe, and made from natural ingredients that support dental health and satisfy chewing urges.



### Are Bully Sticks Safe For Small Dogs?



Yes, bully sticks like Nature Gnaws Extra Thin Bully Sticks are safe for small dogs. They are natural, digestible, and long-lasting, ideal for light chewers and puppies. Always supervise chewing to prevent choking or ingestion of large pieces.



### Which Dental Chews Help Small Dogs’ Oral Health?



ORAVET Dental Chews and Milk-Bone Original Brushing Chews effectively clean teeth and freshen breath. These treats reduce plaque and tartar buildup, promoting better oral hygiene for small dogs weighing 10-24 pounds.



### How Long Do Dog Chews Last For Small Dogs?



Chew longevity varies by product and dog. Bully sticks and DreamBone Twist Sticks typically last longer for light chewers. Dental chews like Hartz Chew 'n Clean are shorter but provide effective cleaning benefits.



### Are Rawhide-free Chews Better For Small Dogs?



Rawhide-free chews like DreamBone Twist Sticks are safer for small dogs as they reduce choking risks. They are made with real chicken and digest easily, making them a healthier choice for aggressive or sensitive chewers.



### What Flavors Do Small Dog Chews Come In?



Small dog chews come in flavors like beef, chicken, bacon, apple, and liver. Products like Nylabone Healthy Edibles and Good ‘n’ Fun Triple Flavor Kabobs offer assorted flavors to keep dogs interested and satisfied.
