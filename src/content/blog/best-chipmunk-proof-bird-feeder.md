## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Chipmunk Proof?



A chipmunk-proof bird feeder uses weight-activated perches or gravity protection. These features block chipmunks from accessing seeds. Durable metal mesh and baffles prevent chewing and climbing. This design ensures only birds can feed, keeping chipmunks away effectively.



### How Do Weight-activated Perches Work On Feeders?



Weight-activated perches close seed ports when heavy animals land. Birds are light enough to access seeds freely. Chipmunks and squirrels trigger the perch to block feeding. This smart mechanism protects seeds and reduces unwanted pest visits.



### Are Metal Mesh Feeders Better For Chipmunk Proofing?



Yes, metal mesh feeders are durable and resistant to chewing. They prevent chipmunks from gnawing holes and stealing seeds. Mesh also allows good seed ventilation, reducing mold. Metal feeders last longer in outdoor conditions than plastic.



### Can Squirrel Baffles Stop Chipmunks Too?



Squirrel baffles placed on poles or hooks block chipmunks from climbing. Their smooth, wide design prevents chipmunks from reaching feeders. Baffles add an extra layer of protection, complementing chipmunk-proof feeder features. They are effective for outdoor bird feeding setups.



### What Seed Capacity Is Ideal For Chipmunk Proof Feeders?



A seed capacity of 3 to 5 pounds balances frequent refills and bird needs. Larger feeders reduce the need for constant filling. This size supports cardinals, finches, and chickadees well. It also helps maintain fresh seed supply while deterring chipmunks.



### Which Birds Commonly Use Chipmunk Proof Feeders?



Chipmunk-proof feeders attract cardinals, chickadees, finches, and blue jays. These birds comfortably feed using weight-activated or gravity-protected feeders. The feeder designs keep chipmunks away while providing birds safe access to seeds.
