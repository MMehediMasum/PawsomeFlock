Catnip toys stimulate cats' natural hunting instincts and encourage active play. Toys like kickers and crinkly pillows promote movement, reducing boredom and obesity. Regular play with these toys helps cats stay fit and mentally engaged indoors.



### Are Refillable Catnip Toys Better For Cats?



Yes, refillable catnip toys like Potaroma Chew Ropes allow fresh catnip use, maintaining scent and interest. They provide long-lasting play value and keep cats engaged without needing frequent replacements, making them economical and environmentally friendly.

Toys with textured surfaces, like Potaroma Chew Ropes and Catstages Crunchy Pickle, help clean teeth and massage gums. They reduce plaque buildup while satisfying cats’ chewing instincts. These toys are safe and promote dental health during play.

### How Do Crinkle Sound Catnip Toys Benefit Cats?



Crinkle sound toys simulate prey noises, stimulating cats’ hunting instincts. Products like Potaroma Pillows and Saury Fish engage cats with sound and texture. This interactive play enhances mental alertness and physical activity, improving overall feline well-being.
