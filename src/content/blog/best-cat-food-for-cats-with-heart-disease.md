**Cats with heart disease need special diets to support their health and energy. Choosing the right food helps manage symptoms and improves quality of life.**



Heart disease in cats affects how well the heart pumps blood. This condition often requires foods low in sodium and rich in taurine, an essential amino acid. Foods with kidney support also benefit many cats, as heart and kidney problems often occur together.



Balanced nutrition helps maintain muscle strength and reduces fluid buildup. Products like Forza10 and Hill’s Prescription Diet offer formulas designed for cats with heart or kidney issues. Supplements such as taurine and L-carnitine support heart function and overall wellness. Selecting vet-formulated diets and supplements ensures your cat gets the nutrients needed to stay healthy and active despite their condition.
