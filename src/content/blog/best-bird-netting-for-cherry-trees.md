## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Bird Netting Is Ideal For Cherry Trees?



A 10x10 ft netting is ideal for most cherry trees. It provides ample coverage and easy installation, protecting fruits from birds and pests effectively.



### How Does Zipper Bird Netting Benefit Fruit Tree Protection?



Zipper bird netting allows easy access for pruning and harvesting. It ensures secure closure, preventing birds and insects from entering and damaging the fruit.



### Can Bird Netting Protect Cherry Trees From Cicadas?



Yes, bird netting with fine mesh can block cicadas. It prevents these insects from harming leaves and fruits, promoting healthier tree growth.



### Is Heavy-duty Nylon Bird Netting Durable For Gardens?



Heavy-duty nylon mesh is strong and long-lasting. It withstands weather conditions, resists tearing, and provides reliable protection for fruit trees and plants.



### How Do Drawstrings Improve Fruit Tree Netting Usability?



Drawstrings secure netting tightly around trees, preventing gaps. This feature enhances protection by keeping out birds, squirrels, and other pests effectively.



### Can Bird Netting Also Protect Other Fruit Plants?



Yes, bird netting is versatile and protects various plants like blueberries, apples, peaches, and tomatoes. It keeps multiple crops safe from pests.
