## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Are Best For Dogs With Kidney Failure?



Low protein and phosphorus ingredients like fish, lamb, and herbal supplements support kidney health. These reduce kidney strain and promote overall well-being. Vet-formulated foods such as Forza10 and Hill's Prescription Diet use these ingredients effectively.



### How Does Low Phosphorus Benefit Dogs With Renal Issues?



Low phosphorus helps slow kidney damage progression and maintains kidney function. It reduces toxin buildup in the bloodstream. Kidney support dog foods like Dave’s Pet Food and Purina Pro Plan use low phosphorus formulas to protect kidney health.



### Can Herbal Supplements Aid Kidney Support In Dogs?



Yes, herbal supplements like Rehmannia and Dong Quai enhance kidney function and fluid balance. Products like Pet Wellbeing Kidney Support Gold provide natural kidney support, improving energy and vitality in dogs with kidney issues.



### Are Wet Or Dry Dog Foods Better For Kidney Failure?



Both wet and dry foods can support kidney health. Wet foods like Forza10 Kidney Care provide hydration, while dry foods offer convenience and dental benefits. Choose based on your dog's preference and vet recommendations.



### What Treats Are Safe For Dogs With Kidney Problems?



Low protein, renal-friendly treats like Kidney Restore Bacon Flavor and Choolip Vita Sticks are safe. They support kidney health without overloading protein or phosphorus, making them ideal for dogs with kidney failure.



### Do Veterinary Prescription Diets Improve Kidney Function?



Yes, prescription diets like Blue Buffalo KS and Hill’s k/d are specially formulated to slow kidney disease progression. They balance nutrients to reduce kidney workload and enhance overall health in affected dogs.
