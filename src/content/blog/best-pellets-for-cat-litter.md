**Choosing the best pellets for cat litter helps keep your home clean and your cat happy. Quality pellets absorb moisture well and control odors effectively.**



Cat litter pellets come in many types, including pine, paper, walnut shells, and zeolite. Pine pellets like Nature's Logic and Feline Pine absorb moisture and reduce dust. Paper pellets such as Fresh News offer a soft, eco-friendly option. Walnut shell pellets provide natural odor control and sustainability.



Zeolite pellets, found in Honeycare and Purina Tidy Cats Breeze, trap smells and keep litter fresh longer. Some pellets clump for easy cleaning, while others prevent dust and tracking, like ökocat and Breeze Litter Pellets. Picking the right pellet depends on your cat’s needs and your litter box system. This guide covers top pellet options to help you choose wisely.
