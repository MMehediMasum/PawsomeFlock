## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Hummingbird Feeder Bee-proof?



Bee-proof feeders have built-in bee guards and ant moats. These prevent bees and ants from accessing nectar. The FEED GARDEN and Perky-Pet models include these features, ensuring hummingbirds feed without competition or contamination. This keeps nectar fresh and feeders clean longer.



### How Do I Clean And Refill Hummingbird Feeders Easily?



Choose feeders with wide mouths and included cleaning brushes. Wide openings simplify filling and washing. The FEED GARDEN and WOSIBO feeders come with easy-clean designs and brushes, making maintenance quick. Regular cleaning prevents mold and keeps hummingbirds healthy.



### Are Glass Or Plastic Hummingbird Feeders Better For Outdoors?



Glass feeders are durable, leak-proof, and easy to clean. They resist fading and offer vibrant garden decor. Plastic feeders are lightweight but may degrade faster. Many top models, like Jealoeur and Perky-Pet, use glass to combine beauty and longevity.



### How Do Ant Moats Protect Hummingbird Feeders?



Ant moats are water-filled barriers above feeding ports. They stop ants from reaching nectar by creating a water trap. Most feeders listed, such as FEED GARDEN and Jealoeur, include built-in ant moats, ensuring hummingbirds enjoy uncontaminated nectar without ant interference.



### What Is The Ideal Nectar Capacity For Hummingbird Feeders?



Ideal feeders hold between 16 to 28 ounces of nectar. This size balances frequent refilling and attracting multiple hummingbirds. Feeders like LUJII and WOSIBO offer larger capacities (26–32 oz), perfect for busy gardens or longer feeding intervals.
