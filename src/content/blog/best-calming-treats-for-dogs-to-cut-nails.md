### What Ingredients Help Calm Dogs During Nail Trimming?



Ingredients like melatonin, chamomile, valerian root, L-theanine, and hemp oil help reduce anxiety in dogs during nail trimming. These natural components soothe nerves and promote relaxation, making grooming less stressful for your pet.



### How Do Calming Chews Reduce Dog Anxiety?



Calming chews contain natural supplements that lower stress hormones and promote calmness. They work by balancing brain chemicals, easing nerves, and improving overall mood, which helps dogs stay relaxed during stressful events like vet visits or storms.



### Are Calming Treats Safe For All Dog Breeds?



Yes, most calming treats are formulated to be safe for all dog breeds. Always check dosage instructions based on your dog's size and consult your vet if unsure about specific ingredients or health conditions.



### How Soon Do Calming Treats Work Before Nail Clipping?



Calming treats typically take 30 to 60 minutes to start working. Administer them before nail clipping to allow time for the calming effects to set in and help your dog stay relaxed.



### Can Calming Chews Help With Other Anxiety Triggers?



Yes, calming chews also help with anxiety from thunderstorms, fireworks, travel, and separation. Their natural ingredients support overall stress relief in various stressful situations beyond nail trimming.



### What Flavors Are Available For Calming Dog Chews?



Popular flavors include bacon, chicken, and natural hemp. These flavors make chews more appealing and easier to administer, ensuring your dog willingly takes them during stressful times.
