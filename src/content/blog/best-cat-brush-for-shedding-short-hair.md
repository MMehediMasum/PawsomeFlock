Yes, most brushes listed, like MIU COLOR and Depets, suit both long and short hair cats. They effectively remove loose fur and undercoat without damaging the coat. This versatility makes them ideal for multi-pet households with varied fur types.

For best results, groom short-haired cats daily or every other day. Regular brushing, using tools like Freshly Bailey Deshedding Brush, reduces shedding significantly. Consistent grooming removes loose fur before it spreads around your home.
