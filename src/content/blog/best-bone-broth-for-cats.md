## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Bone Broth For Cats?



Bone broth supports joint health, improves hydration, and boosts immunity. It also aids digestion and enhances skin and coat condition. Adding it to meals increases nutrient intake for overall cat wellness.



### How Do I Choose The Best Bone Broth For My Cat?



Look for bone broths with natural, high-quality ingredients. Avoid artificial flavors, preservatives, and grains. Select options with collagen and protein to support joints and digestion.



### Can Bone Broth Help Picky Eaters Eat More?



Yes, bone broth adds flavor and moisture, enticing picky cats to eat. It also provides extra nutrition, making meals more appealing and balanced.



### Is Bone Broth Safe For Cats With Allergies?



Choose limited-ingredient bone broths free from common allergens like grains and artificial additives. Always check ingredient lists and consult your vet before introducing new foods.



### How Should Bone Broth Be Served To Cats?



Serve bone broth as a topper on dry or wet food. It can be warmed slightly but should never be hot. Use it daily for best results.



### Are Powdered Bone Broths As Effective As Liquid Ones?



Powdered bone broths are convenient and retain essential nutrients. They dissolve easily in food and offer similar joint and immune support benefits as liquid broths.
