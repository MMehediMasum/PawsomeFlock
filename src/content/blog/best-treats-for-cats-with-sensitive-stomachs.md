## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Treats Are Best For Cats With Sensitive Stomachs?



Treats with probiotics and natural ingredients support digestion in sensitive cats. Options like Get Naked Digestive Health Crunchy and I AND LOVE AND YOU Treat Meow are ideal. These treats aid gut health and reduce digestive upset effectively.



### Are Grain-free Treats Better For Sensitive Cats?



Yes, grain-free treats reduce the risk of digestive irritation. Brands like Get Naked and Vital Essentials offer grain-free options. These treats are easier to digest and help maintain healthy stomach function in sensitive cats.



### How Do Probiotics In Cat Treats Help Digestion?



Probiotics balance gut bacteria, improving digestion and immune health. Treats like Whole Life Cat Living and Get Naked Biteables contain human-grade probiotics. They support nutrient absorption and reduce stomach discomfort in cats with sensitive digestion.



### Can Freeze-dried Treats Benefit Cats With Sensitive Stomachs?



Freeze-dried treats like Vital Essentials Salmon Bites preserve nutrients and are easy to digest. They contain single proteins and no fillers, reducing allergy risks. These treats are excellent for cats prone to digestive issues.



### What Ingredients Should I Avoid In Treats For Sensitive Cats?



Avoid artificial additives, grains, fillers, and common allergens. Ingredients like corn, soy, and wheat can trigger stomach upset. Opt for natural, single-protein treats with added probiotics for safer digestion.



### Are Soft Or Crunchy Treats Better For Sensitive Stomach Cats?



Both can be suitable if they contain digestive support ingredients. Soft treats like I AND LOVE AND YOU Meow Lickable offer hydration benefits. Crunchy options like Wellness Kittles aid dental health while supporting digestion. Choose based on your cat’s preference.
