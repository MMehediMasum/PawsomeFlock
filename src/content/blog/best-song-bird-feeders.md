## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Metal Bird Feeders?



Metal bird feeders are durable and weather-resistant, making them ideal for outdoor use. They are often chew-proof, ensuring longevity. Additionally, metal feeders offer aesthetic appeal with designs like brushed copper or bronze finishes. These features attract various birds while enhancing garden decor.



### How Does A Solar Bird Feeder Work?



Solar bird feeders use solar panels to store energy during the day. This energy powers LED lights, illuminating the feeder at night. This feature enhances nighttime garden aesthetics. The lights do not disturb birds and can attract more species, providing a unique garden experience.



### How Do Squirrel-proof Bird Feeders Work?



Squirrel-proof bird feeders use mechanisms that prevent squirrels from accessing the seeds. Features include weight-sensitive perches that close seed ports when heavier animals land. This ensures birds get the food while deterring squirrels. Some designs also have locking lids for added security.



### Why Choose A Bird Feeder With A Locking Lid?



A locking lid prevents squirrels and other animals from accessing bird seed. It ensures seeds remain fresh and dry, reducing waste. This feature also makes refilling easier and keeps the feeder clean. Locking lids offer peace of mind and attract more birds by providing consistent food.



### What Birds Are Attracted To Hanging Bird Feeders?



Hanging bird feeders attract a variety of birds, including cardinals, chickadees, and finches. Their elevated position makes them accessible and visible, enticing more species. Birds are drawn to feeders that offer a reliable food source, making hanging feeders a popular choice among bird enthusiasts.
