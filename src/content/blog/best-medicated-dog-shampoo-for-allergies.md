## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Make Medicated Dog Shampoos Effective For Allergies?



Medicated dog shampoos often contain chlorhexidine, hydrocortisone, and oatmeal. These ingredients reduce itching, inflammation, and bacterial growth. They soothe irritated skin and help manage allergies effectively. Paraben and dye-free formulas minimize further skin irritation, making them safe for sensitive dogs.



### How Often Should I Use Medicated Shampoo For My Allergic Dog?



Use medicated shampoo once or twice a week, or as directed by your vet. Overuse can dry out your dog’s skin. Regular bathing helps control itching, hot spots, and redness caused by allergies. Always follow product instructions for best results.



### Can Medicated Shampoos Treat Hot Spots And Yeast Infections?



Yes, medicated shampoos with antifungal and antibacterial properties can treat hot spots and yeast infections. Ingredients like chlorhexidine and hydrocortisone reduce inflammation and kill infection-causing microbes. Consistent use helps heal irritated areas and prevents further flare-ups.



### Are These Shampoos Safe For Puppies And Cats?



Many medicated shampoos, like Veterinary Formula Clinical Care and TropiClean, are safe for puppies and cats. Always check the label and consult your vet before use. Some formulas are specifically designed to be gentle yet effective on young or sensitive pets.



### Do Medicated Shampoos Help Reduce Shedding In Allergic Dogs?



Yes, shampoos formulated with skin and coat supplements can help reduce shedding. They nourish the skin and improve coat health. Ingredients like oatmeal and vitamins soothe irritation and promote a healthy, less flaky coat, reducing excessive shedding caused by allergies.
