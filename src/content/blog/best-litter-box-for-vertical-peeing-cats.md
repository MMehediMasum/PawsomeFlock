**Cats that pee on vertical surfaces need special litter boxes to keep your home clean. Choosing the best litter box helps control mess and odor effectively.**



Vertical peeing is common among some cats, especially males marking territory or feeling stressed. Regular litter boxes may not have high enough sides to contain sprays. High-sided or enclosed boxes prevent urine from escaping and make cleanup easier. Stainless steel or plastic materials offer durability and simple cleaning.



Some boxes also include lids, scoops, and odor control features. Picking the right size and design matters for your cat’s comfort and hygiene. This guide reviews top litter boxes designed to handle vertical spraying. Protect your floors and walls while keeping your cat happy with the best litter box for vertical peeing cats.

### How Do Stainless Steel Litter Boxes Help With Odor Control?



Stainless steel boxes like L7 Pro resist odor absorption and are easy to clean. They also include deodorizer options for freshness.
