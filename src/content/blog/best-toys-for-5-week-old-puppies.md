These toys also help prevent boredom and destructive behavior as puppies learn. Choosing the right size is important to avoid choking hazards. The right toys support puppies’ teeth, muscles, and minds. This guide covers top safe, fun, and affordable toys suited for tiny puppies under 15 pounds. Keep your new furry friend happy and healthy with the perfect puppy toys.
