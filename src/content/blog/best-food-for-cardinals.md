## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Seeds Attract Cardinals The Most?



Cardinals prefer sunflower seeds, safflower seeds, and peanuts. These seeds provide essential nutrients and energy. Products like Wagner's Cardinal Blend and Happy Wings mix contain these favorites. Using these seeds in feeders increases cardinal visits effectively.



### How To Choose The Best Cardinal Bird Food?



Select bird food with high-quality seeds like sunflower and safflower. Avoid fillers like millet and cracked corn. Look for blends from trusted brands like Kaytee or Wagner's for balanced nutrition and better cardinal attraction.



### Can Cardinals Eat Fruit-flavored Bird Seed Blends?



Yes, cardinals enjoy fruit-flavored blends, especially cherry flavors. Wagner's Farmer’s Delight with cherry flavor is popular. Fruit adds variety and extra nutrients, encouraging cardinals to visit your feeder more often.



### Are Safflower Seeds Good For Cardinal Feeding?



Safflower seeds are excellent for cardinals. They are high in fat and protein. Wagner's 57075 Safflower Seed is a great choice. Safflower also deters squirrels, ensuring cardinals get most of the food.



### How Often Should I Refill Cardinal Feeders?



Refill feeders every 2-3 days, depending on bird activity. Fresh seed attracts more cardinals and prevents mold. Regular refilling keeps cardinals returning and maintains their health with fresh nutrition.



### Which Cardinal Food Blend Suits Multiple Wild Birds?



Kaytee Wild Bird Ultimate Birder’s Blend feeds cardinals, grosbeaks, woodpeckers, and more. It contains a mix of seeds catering to various species. This blend supports a diverse backyard bird population while attracting cardinals.
