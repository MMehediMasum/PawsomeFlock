## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Tapeworm Dewormer For Cats?



Elanco Praziquantel tablets are highly effective for cats and kittens 6 weeks and older. They quickly eliminate tapeworms safely. These tablets are veterinarian-recommended and easy to administer, ensuring fast parasite control and lasting relief for your cat’s health.



### Can I Use Liquid Dewormers For Cats And Dogs?



Yes, liquid dewormers like Durvet WormEze work well for both cats and dogs. They paralyze and expel roundworms and tapeworms effectively. Liquid forms are easy to dose for puppies and kittens, making treatment stress-free and accurate.



### Are Natural Dewormers Effective For Cats?



Natural dewormers with wormwood and black walnut promote gut health and parasite control. They support flora balance and detoxification. While gentle, they work best combined with vet-approved treatments for broad-spectrum parasite defense in cats.



### How Fast Do Tapeworm Tablets Work In Cats?



Tapeworm tablets like Elanco Praziquantel start working within hours to kill tapeworms. Complete expulsion usually occurs within 24-48 hours. Fast action helps reduce symptoms and prevents reinfestation, ensuring your cat recovers quickly and comfortably.



### Are Broad Spectrum Dewormers Safe For All Cat Breeds?



Broad spectrum dewormers like Elanco Drontal are safe for all cat breeds and sizes. They target multiple parasites including tapeworms, roundworms, and hookworms. Always follow dosage instructions and consult your vet for the best treatment plan.



### Can Tapeworm Dewormers Also Control Fleas?



Some products, like PetArmor CAPACTION, target fleas quickly but do not treat tapeworms directly. Flea control helps prevent tapeworm reinfestation since fleas carry tapeworm larvae. Use combined treatments for comprehensive parasite and flea management.
