**Winter is tough for wild birds. Providing the best food helps them stay healthy and strong during cold months.**



Birds need extra energy in winter to keep warm. High-fat seeds and nuts give them this energy. Special blends like Wagner’s Farmer's Delight and Kaytee Nut & Fruit offer tasty, nutritious options. These mixes attract many birds such as cardinals, finches, chickadees, and woodpeckers.



No-mess seeds reduce waste and keep feeders clean. Regional blends like Wagner’s Eastern and Midwest blends suit local bird species well. Adding suet or peanut treats also supports birds in harsh weather. Feeding birds in winter supports their survival and brings beautiful wildlife close to home. Choosing the right bird food makes a big difference for feathered friends.
