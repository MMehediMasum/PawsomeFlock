Kidney-supporting cat foods often include low protein, controlled phosphorus, and added antioxidants. Herbal supplements like Rehmannia and Dong Quai also promote kidney function and fluid balance. These ingredients help reduce kidney workload and improve energy in cats with kidney failure.



### How Does Hill’s K/d Diet Help Cats With Kidney Failure?



Hill’s K/D diet lowers protein and phosphorus to ease kidney stress. It includes essential nutrients to support renal health and maintain hydration. This prescription diet helps slow kidney disease progression and improves cats’ quality of life.



### Are Wet Foods Better For Cats With Kidney Failure?



Wet foods provide more moisture, aiding hydration in cats with kidney issues. They often have balanced nutrients tailored for renal health. Products like Hill’s K/D wet food and Weruva Phos Focused cans are ideal for maintaining kidney function and fluid balance.



### Can Herbal Supplements Improve Kidney Function In Cats?



Yes, veterinarian-formulated supplements with herbs like Rehmannia and Dong Quai can support kidney health. They help regulate fluid balance, boost energy, and reduce inflammation. Such supplements complement prescription diets for better kidney support.



### What Is The Benefit Of Low Phosphorus In Cat Kidney Diets?



Low phosphorus diets reduce kidney damage by limiting toxin buildup. They help slow disease progression and protect remaining kidney function. This is crucial in products like Hill’s K/D and Weruva Phos Focused formulas.
