## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Calm Dogs During Fireworks?



Calming treats often include melatonin, hemp, chamomile, L-tryptophan, and thiamine. These ingredients reduce anxiety and promote relaxation. They help dogs stay calm during stressful events like fireworks, thunderstorms, and travel, improving overall comfort and behavior.



### How Do Calming Chews Reduce Dog Anxiety?



Calming chews work by combining natural supplements like melatonin and hemp. These ingredients support the nervous system and reduce stress hormones. They help ease fear and hyperactivity, making dogs feel more relaxed during loud noises or separation.



### Are Calming Treats Safe For All Dog Breeds?



Most calming treats are formulated for all breeds and sizes. Always check dosage instructions based on your dog's weight. Consult your vet if your dog has health issues or is on medication to ensure safe use.



### How Soon Before Fireworks Should I Give Calming Treats?



Administer calming treats 30 to 60 minutes before fireworks or stressful events. This timing allows ingredients like melatonin and hemp to take effect. Consistent use over several days may improve results for highly anxious dogs.



### Can Calming Chews Improve Sleep During Noisy Nights?



Yes, calming chews often include melatonin, which helps regulate sleep cycles. They promote restful sleep by reducing anxiety and agitation caused by loud noises like fireworks or thunderstorms. This leads to better overnight rest for your dog.



### What Should I Look For In A Calming Dog Treat?



Look for natural, vet-recommended ingredients like hemp, melatonin, chamomile, and L-tryptophan. Choose treats with clear dosage guidelines and positive user reviews. Quality, safety, and effectiveness are key factors for calming treats during fireworks or storms.
