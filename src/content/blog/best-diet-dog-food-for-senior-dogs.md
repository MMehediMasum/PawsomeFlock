Protein helps maintain muscle strength and vitality. Senior dog food also focuses on healthy weight management to prevent obesity.



Many trusted brands offer formulas tailored for different sizes and ages of senior dogs. Selecting the right diet can improve your dog’s quality of life. This guide covers top options like Hill’s Science Diet, Blue Buffalo, and IAMS, known for quality ingredients and balanced nutrition. Helping your senior dog stay active and healthy starts with choosing the right food.
