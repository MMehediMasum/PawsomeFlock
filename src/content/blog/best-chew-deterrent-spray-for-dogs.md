## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Make Chew Deterrent Sprays Effective?



Chew deterrent sprays use bitter substances like bitter apple or natural extracts. These ingredients create an unpleasant taste dogs dislike. Some sprays also include soothing agents to protect skin and wounds. Alcohol-free formulas are common to ensure safety and prevent irritation.



### Are Chew Deterrent Sprays Safe For Dogs And Furniture?



Yes, most chew deterrent sprays are safe for dogs and furniture. They use non-toxic, natural ingredients that discourage chewing without harming skin or surfaces. Always follow product instructions to avoid any adverse reactions and test sprays on a small area first.



### How Do Chew Deterrent Sprays Help With Puppy Training?



Chew deterrent sprays discourage puppies from chewing on furniture and belongings. The unpleasant taste teaches them to avoid certain items. Consistent use alongside positive reinforcement helps puppies learn appropriate chewing behavior quickly and effectively.



### Can Chew Deterrent Sprays Stop Dogs From Licking Wounds?



Yes, sprays like Bodhi Dog Bitter 2-in-1 soothe wounds and deter licking. These sprays protect healing areas while preventing further irritation. They are safe for skin and wounds, promoting faster recovery and reducing infection risk.



### How Often Should I Apply Chew Deterrent Spray?



Apply chew deterrent spray as directed, usually once or twice daily. Reapply after cleaning or if the taste fades. Consistent use is key to training dogs to avoid chewing and licking unwanted items.



### Do Chew Deterrent Sprays Work On All Dog Breeds?



Most chew deterrent sprays work on all dog breeds. However, effectiveness can vary based on individual dog preferences and chewing habits. Combining sprays with training improves results regardless of breed or size.
