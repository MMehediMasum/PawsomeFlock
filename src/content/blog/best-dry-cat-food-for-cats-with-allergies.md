## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Dry Cat Food Is Best For Cats With Allergies?



IAMS Proactive Health and Purina Pro Plan Allergen Reducing formulas are top choices. They reduce allergens and support sensitive skin and digestion. These foods contain limited ingredients to minimize allergic reactions and promote overall cat health.



### How Does Purina Liveclear Reduce Cat Allergens?



Purina LiveClear contains a patented ingredient that reduces the allergen Fel d 1 in cat hair. This helps lower allergen exposure for allergy sufferers. It supports sensitive skin and digestion, making it ideal for cats with allergies.



### Are Grain-free Dry Cat Foods Good For Allergic Cats?



Yes, grain-free options like Natural Balance Limited Ingredient Salmon & Green Pea are excellent. They avoid common allergens like wheat and corn. Grain-free diets often improve digestion and reduce skin irritations in allergic cats.



### Can Sensitive Stomach Cat Foods Help With Allergies?



Sensitive stomach formulas, such as Hill’s Science Diet Sensitive Stomach & Skin, aid digestion and reduce inflammation. These diets are gentle and help manage allergic symptoms by supporting skin and gut health.



### What Ingredients Should I Avoid In Dry Cat Food For Allergies?



Avoid common allergens like corn, wheat, soy, and artificial additives. Look for limited ingredient diets with novel proteins like turkey or salmon. This helps reduce allergic reactions and supports skin and digestive health.
