## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Bird Cage Is Best For Parrotlets?



A medium-sized cage around 30 to 64 inches suits parrotlets well. It offers ample space for movement. Models like Yaheetech 64-inch cages provide comfort and play areas. Always ensure the cage has horizontal bars for climbing and enough room for toys.



### Are Rolling Stands Beneficial For Parrotlet Cages?



Yes, rolling stands add mobility and convenience. They allow easy cage relocation for cleaning or sunlight. Many Yaheetech models include detachable rolling stands. This feature enhances flexibility in bird care and cage placement, improving your parrotlet’s environment.



### How Important Is Cage Bar Spacing For Parrotlets?



Bar spacing between 1/2 to 5/8 inches is ideal. It prevents escape or injury to small parrotlets. Most recommended cages maintain this safe spacing. Proper spacing ensures safety and comfort, crucial for your bird’s health and happiness.



### Can Parrotlets Use Travel Cages Safely?



Yes, travel cages like Yaheetech’s 25. 5-inch wrought iron carrier are designed for safety. They include secure handles and seed guards. These cages offer comfort during trips, minimizing stress. Always monitor your parrotlet during travel for their well-being.



### What Features Make A Cage Suitable For Parrotlets?



Important features include open-top play areas, sturdy construction, and easy cleaning trays. Toys and perches enhance mental stimulation. Cages like ZENY 61-inch or SUPER DEAL PRO 61-inch offer these benefits. These features promote physical activity and happiness for your parrotlet.



### How To Maintain Cleanliness In A Parrotlet Cage?



Use slide-out trays for quick waste removal. Clean cage bars and perches weekly with bird-safe disinfectants. Rolling stands help move cages for thorough cleaning. Regular maintenance prevents diseases and keeps your parrotlet healthy and comfortable.
