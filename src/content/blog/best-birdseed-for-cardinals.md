**Cardinals need the right birdseed to stay healthy and visit your feeder often. Choosing the best seed blend helps attract these bright, beautiful birds.**



Feeding cardinals is simple with the right mix. They love seeds like sunflower, safflower, and millet. These seeds provide energy and nutrients cardinals need. Many birdseed blends combine these favorites to keep cardinals coming back. Some blends also include nuts and fruit for extra nutrition.



Different brands offer various mixes, like Audubon Park Cardinal Blend and Kaytee’s Cardinal Wild Bird Seed. Quality seed prevents waste and keeps birds safe from fillers or harmful ingredients. Offering fresh, clean seed in a good feeder helps cardinals feed comfortably. This guide covers top birdseed options perfect for cardinals to enjoy year-round.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Birdseed For Cardinals?



Cardinals love seeds rich in oil and nutrients. Audubon Park Cardinal Blend and Kaytee Cardinal Wild Bird Seed are top choices. These blends contain sunflower and safflower seeds, which are cardinal favorites. They provide essential nutrients and attract these vibrant birds to your feeders.



### Are Specific Seeds Better For Cardinals?



Yes, certain seeds are ideal for cardinals. Sunflower and safflower seeds are preferred due to their high oil content. Blends like Wagner's Cardinal Blend and Happy Wings Cardinal Blend include these seeds. They provide essential nutrition and help maintain the cardinals’ vibrant plumage and health.



### How Do I Attract Cardinals To My Feeder?



To attract cardinals, use a feeder with suitable seeds. Fill it with blends like Kaytee Nut & Fruit Wild Bird Seed. This blend attracts cardinals with its mix of sunflower, safflower, and fruit. Place the feeder in a quiet, elevated area for best results.



### Can Cardinals Eat Fruit And Nuts?



Yes, cardinals enjoy fruit and nuts. Blends like Kaytee Nut & Fruit include these ingredients. These mixes provide variety and essential nutrients. Cardinals are attracted to feeders offering a diverse diet, ensuring they visit your garden frequently.



### Do Cardinals Prefer Specific Feeder Types?



Cardinals prefer feeders with wide perches and larger ports. This accommodates their size and feeding style. Hopper or platform feeders work well. Using these feeders with blends like Wagner's Farmer's Delight ensures cardinals can easily access their favorite seeds.



### Is No-grow Seed Better For Cardinals?



No-grow seeds, like those in Happy Wings Cardinal Blend, are beneficial. They prevent unwanted plant growth under feeders. This keeps your garden tidy while providing cardinals with their preferred seeds. They are a practical choice for maintaining a clean feeding area.
