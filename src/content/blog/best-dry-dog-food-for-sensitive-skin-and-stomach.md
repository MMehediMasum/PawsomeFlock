## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Sensitive Skin In Dry Dog Food?



Ingredients like salmon, rice, and natural oils soothe sensitive skin. They provide essential fatty acids for healthy coats and reduce irritation. These ingredients are found in Purina Pro Plan and Wellness Complete Health formulas, supporting both skin and stomach health effectively.



### How Does Dry Dog Food Aid Sensitive Stomachs?



Dry dog foods for sensitive stomachs contain easily digestible ingredients. They include prebiotics and probiotics to improve digestion and nutrient absorption. Brands like Purina ONE and Hill’s Science Diet focus on gentle formulas to minimize stomach upset and promote digestive health.



### Are Small Breed Formulas Better For Sensitive Dogs?



Small breed formulas have appropriately sized kibble for smaller mouths. They are often tailored with balanced nutrients to support sensitive skin and stomach needs. Hill’s Science Diet and Purina Pro Plan offer small breed options that cater to these specific requirements effectively.



### Can Dry Dog Food Improve My Dog's Coat Condition?



Yes, dry dog foods with omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids enhance coat shine. Ingredients like salmon oil nourish skin and reduce dryness. Formulas such as Purina ONE +Plus Skin & Coat help maintain a healthy, glossy coat while supporting digestive health.



### Is Grain-free Better For Dogs With Sensitive Stomachs?



Not always. Some sensitive dogs benefit from wholesome grains like rice for easier digestion. Wellness Complete Health includes wholesome grains that support sensitive stomachs. Choosing the right formula depends on your dog’s specific dietary needs and vet recommendations.



### How To Choose The Best Dry Dog Food For Sensitivity?



Look for formulas with limited, natural ingredients and added probiotics. Check for high-quality proteins like salmon and gentle carbohydrates like rice. Consider brand reputation and reviews. Purina Pro Plan, Hill’s Science Diet, and Wellness offer trusted options designed for sensitive skin and stomach.
