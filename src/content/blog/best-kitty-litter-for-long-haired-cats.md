## Frequently Asked Questions



### Which Cat Litter Is Best For Long-haired Cats?



WORLD'S BEST CAT LITTER and Dr. Elsey's Ultra Unscented are great for long-haired cats. They clump well and reduce tracking. Their low dust formulas keep fur clean and prevent matting, making scooping easier and odor control long-lasting.



### How To Reduce Litter Tracking With Long-haired Cats?



Use crystal litters like Dr. Elsey's Crystal Attract or PetSafe ScoopFree Premium. These litters minimize dust and tracking. Their lightweight, non-clumping nature keeps litter off your cat’s fur, helping to maintain cleanliness around your home.



### Are Scented Litters Safe For Long-haired Cats?



Scented litters like Fresh Step with Febreze or Arm & Hammer Cedarwood can be safe if your cat shows no allergies. However, unscented options like WORLD'S BEST CAT LITTER Comfort Care are better to avoid irritation or stress in sensitive cats.



### What Litter Controls Odor Best For Long-haired Cats?



WORLD'S BEST CAT LITTER and Arm & Hammer Ultra Last offer excellent odor control. They use natural ingredients and baking soda or activated charcoal to keep odors at bay for up to 30 days, ideal for multi-cat households with long-haired cats.



### Is Clay Or Crystal Litter Better For Long-haired Cats?



Clay litters like Dr. Elsey's Ultra Unscented clump tightly but can stick to fur. Crystal litters like PetSafe ScoopFree attract moisture without clumping, reducing fur contact. Crystal litter often results in less tracking and easier maintenance for long-haired cats.



### How Important Is Dust-free Litter For Long-haired Cats?



Dust-free litters like Cat's Pride Max Power and WORLD'S BEST reduce airborne particles. This prevents respiratory issues and keeps long fur cleaner. Low dust formulas help maintain a healthy environment for both cats and owners.
