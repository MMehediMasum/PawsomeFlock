## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Comb For Long Hair Cats?



The best comb for long hair cats has both long and short stainless steel teeth. It detangles, removes mats, and reduces shedding gently. Products like Docrok Pet Grooming Combo are ideal for safe, effective grooming at home.



### How Do Deshedding Brushes Help Long-haired Cats?



Deshedding brushes remove loose fur and reduce hairballs. They reach the undercoat to prevent matting and tangles. Brushes like Maxpower Planet Pet Grooming Rake work well for long-haired cats by minimizing shedding up to 95%.



### Can I Use One Comb For Dogs And Cats?



Yes, many combs like Cafhelp 2 Pack Dog Combs suit both cats and dogs. They have rounded stainless steel teeth to safely remove knots, mats, and loose fur from different coat types.



### How Often Should I Comb My Long-haired Cat?



Combing your long-haired cat daily or at least 3-4 times a week prevents mats and tangles. Regular grooming also reduces shedding and keeps fur healthy and smooth.



### Are Silicone Brushes Effective For Long-haired Cats?



Yes, soft silicone brushes are gentle and great for shedding control. They massage the skin and remove loose fur without irritating sensitive skin, making them perfect for indoor long-haired cats.
