## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Reduce Dog Shedding And Dry Skin?



Ingredients like wild-caught salmon, omega-3 fatty acids, flaxseed, and vitamin E are essential. They nourish the skin, reduce inflammation, and promote a healthy, shiny coat, effectively minimizing shedding and dryness in dogs.



### How Does Salmon Benefit My Dog's Skin And Coat?



Salmon provides rich omega-3 fatty acids that hydrate the skin and reduce irritation. It supports a glossy coat and helps relieve dry, itchy skin, making it ideal for dogs prone to shedding and skin issues.



### Are Fish Oil Supplements Necessary For Skin Health?



Fish oil supplements, like those containing EPA and DHA, enhance skin hydration and reduce itching. They complement dog food and support overall skin and coat health, especially in dogs with sensitive or dry skin.



### Can Dry Dog Food Improve Skin And Coat Condition?



Yes, dry dog foods enriched with real meat, omega fatty acids, and vitamins promote healthy skin and coat. They help manage shedding and dryness when given consistently as part of a balanced diet.



### What Should I Look For In Dog Food For Sensitive Skin?



Look for high-quality protein sources, omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids, and limited fillers. Avoid foods with corn, wheat, or artificial additives that may irritate sensitive skin or cause allergies.



### How Often Should I Give My Dog Skin And Coat Supplements?



Most supplements are given daily to maintain skin hydration and coat health. Follow product directions and consult your vet to ensure the right dosage for your dog’s specific needs.
