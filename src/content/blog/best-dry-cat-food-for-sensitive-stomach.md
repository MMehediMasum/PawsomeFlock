## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Cats With Sensitive Stomachs?



Ingredients like chicken, rice, pumpkin, and probiotics aid digestion. Grain-free and gluten-free options reduce irritation. These ingredients soothe sensitive stomachs and improve nutrient absorption, promoting better gut health for cats prone to digestive issues.



### Which Dry Cat Food Is Best For Sensitive Skin?



Purina Cat Chow Gentle and Purina ONE Sensitive Skin formulas are top choices. They contain skin-supporting nutrients and avoid common allergens. These foods help reduce itching and promote a healthy coat in cats with sensitive skin.



### Are Grain-free Cat Foods Better For Sensitive Digestion?



Grain-free foods often reduce digestive upset in sensitive cats. They avoid common grains that may trigger allergies or intolerance. Choosing grain-free options like Solid Gold Sensitive Stomach can improve digestion and overall comfort.



### How Does Probiotics Benefit Cats With Digestive Issues?



Probiotics support healthy gut bacteria balance, improving digestion. They reduce inflammation and help nutrient absorption. Including probiotics in cat food, like in Solid Gold Sensitive Stomach, promotes better stool quality and reduces stomach discomfort.



### Can Sensitive Stomach Cat Food Support Senior Cats?



Yes, formulas like Hill’s Science Diet Perfect Digestion cater to seniors. They include gentle ingredients and digestive support nutrients. These foods help maintain digestive health and nutrient uptake in older cats with sensitive stomachs.



### Is Lamb And Rice A Good Combo For Sensitive Cats?



Lamb and rice are gentle, easily digestible ingredients. Purina Pro Plan Sensitive Skin and Stomach uses this combo to minimize allergies. It provides essential nutrients while reducing stomach irritation in sensitive cats.
