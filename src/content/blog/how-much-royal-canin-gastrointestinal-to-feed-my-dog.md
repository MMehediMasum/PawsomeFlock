## Factors Influencing Feeding Amount

Determining the right amount of Royal Canin Gastrointestinal to feed your dog depends on several key factors. These factors ensure your dog gets the proper nutrition without overeating or underfeeding. Understanding these elements helps maintain your dog's health and supports their digestive system effectively.

### Dog's Weight

Your dog’s weight is the most important factor in deciding the food quantity. Larger dogs need more calories and food than smaller dogs. Feeding guidelines on the package often use weight ranges to suggest portions. Regularly weighing your dog helps adjust the amount as their weight changes.

### Age And Activity Level

Puppies and young dogs require more food for growth and energy. Older dogs usually need fewer calories because they move less. Active dogs burn more energy and need larger portions to stay healthy. Sedentary dogs should have smaller meals to avoid weight gain.

### Health Conditions

Some health issues affect how much your dog should eat. Dogs with digestive problems may need smaller, more frequent meals. Your vet might recommend specific amounts based on your dog’s condition. Always follow veterinary advice for feeding amounts during illness or recovery.

## When To Consult A Veterinarian

Knowing when to consult a veterinarian is important for your dog's health. Feeding Royal Canin Gastrointestinal requires careful attention to your pet’s response. Watch your dog closely for any unusual signs or symptoms. Early consultation can prevent serious health problems.

### Signs Of Overfeeding

* Weight gain beyond normal limits
* Excessive thirst or urination
* Frequent vomiting or regurgitation
* Restlessness or discomfort after meals

These signs may indicate that your dog is eating too much Royal Canin Gastrointestinal. Adjusting the portion size should be done with professional advice.

### Digestive Issues

* Diarrhea lasting more than two days
* Constipation or difficulty passing stool
* Excessive gas or bloating
* Loss of appetite or sudden refusal to eat

Digestive problems can signal that the diet is not suitable or the quantity is off. A veterinarian can help diagnose and suggest changes to avoid worsening conditions.

### Adjusting Diet For Special Needs

Dogs with health conditions need personalized feeding plans. Puppies, seniors, or dogs with allergies require special attention. Consult your vet to tailor the Royal Canin Gastrointestinal amount. This ensures your dog receives balanced nutrition without risks.
