## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Natural Oils Are Effective In Tick Repellents For Dogs?



Natural oils like lemongrass, cedarwood, and peppermint are proven tick repellents. They safely deter ticks without harsh chemicals. Products like Wondercide use these oils to kill and repel ticks while being gentle on dogs' skin.



### How Long Does Natural Flea And Tick Protection Last?



Protection duration varies by product. Collars like Seresto last up to 8 months. Sprays and spot-ons typically protect for 3 to 4 weeks. Always check product instructions for exact protection time and reapply as needed.



### Are Natural Tick Repellents Safe For Puppies And Small Dogs?



Yes, most natural repellents use gentle ingredients safe for puppies and small dogs. However, always follow dosage guidelines and consult your vet before use. Products like Allyeah collars offer adjustable sizes suitable for small breeds.



### Can Natural Repellents Also Kill Fleas And Mosquitoes?



Many natural repellents, such as Wondercide and Richard’s Organics sprays, target fleas, ticks, and mosquitoes. They use essential oils that repel and kill multiple pests, providing comprehensive protection for your dog and home.



### How Often Should I Apply Natural Flea And Tick Sprays?



Most natural sprays require reapplication every 1 to 4 weeks. Frequency depends on product strength and exposure level. Regular use ensures continuous protection, especially during peak tick seasons or outdoor activities with your dog.



### Do Natural Flea And Tick Collars Repel Ticks Effectively?



Yes, collars like Seresto and Wondercide use natural oils to repel ticks effectively. They provide long-lasting protection, often lasting several months, and are waterproof, making them convenient for active dogs.
