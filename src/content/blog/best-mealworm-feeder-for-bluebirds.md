## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Mealworm Feeder Ideal For Bluebirds?



An ideal mealworm feeder offers easy access, squirrel-proof design, and weather resistance. It should attract bluebirds while keeping food fresh and safe from pests. Metal and recycled plastic feeders with cages or domes work best to meet these needs effectively.



### How Do Squirrel-proof Bluebird Feeders Work?



Squirrel-proof feeders use cages, metal trays, or dome tops to block squirrels. These barriers prevent squirrels from reaching mealworms while allowing bluebirds to feed comfortably. This design protects food and ensures bluebirds get consistent access to mealworms without interference.



### Can Mealworm Feeders Attract Other Small Birds Besides Bluebirds?



Yes, mealworm feeders also attract robins, wrens, titmice, and cardinals. These birds enjoy live or dried mealworms and can feed safely on squirrel-proof designs. Offering mealworms helps diversify backyard birdwatching opportunities beyond just bluebirds.



### How Do I Clean And Refill A Bluebird Mealworm Feeder?



Most feeders feature easy-to-open lids or trays for quick refilling and cleaning. Use mild soap and water to clean trays regularly. Proper maintenance keeps mealworms fresh and prevents mold or bacteria buildup, promoting healthy feeding conditions.



### Are Metal Mealworm Feeders More Durable Than Wooden Ones?



Metal feeders are generally more durable, weatherproof, and easier to clean than wooden feeders. They resist mold and insect damage better and often include squirrel-proof features. However, wooden feeders like cedar offer natural aesthetics and insulation but may require more upkeep.
