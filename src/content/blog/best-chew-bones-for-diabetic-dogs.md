## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Chew Bones For Diabetic Dogs?



The best chew bones for diabetic dogs are low glycemic and rawhide-free. Options like Old Dog Cookie Company treats help maintain healthy blood sugar. Natural, easily digestible chews such as Canine Naturals Chicken Recipe are also ideal for diabetic dogs.



### Are Rawhide-free Chews Better For Diabetic Dogs?



Yes, rawhide-free chews are safer and easier to digest. They reduce the risk of digestive issues and help maintain stable blood sugar. Products like Pur Luv Chicken & Sweet Potato Jerky Wraps are rawhide-free and diabetic-friendly.



### Which Chew Treats Support Diabetic Dogs' Dental Health?



Chew bones like Cadet Stuffed Shin Bone support dental health while being digestible. These treats help clean teeth and gums without spiking blood sugar. Look for options with high protein and natural ingredients.



### Can Diabetic Dogs Safely Enjoy High-protein Chews?



High-protein chews, like NutriChomps Braids, are safe for diabetic dogs when low in carbs. They satisfy chewing urges and support muscle maintenance. Always choose treats that are vet-approved and designed for diabetic needs.



### Do Chew Treats For Diabetic Dogs Improve Joint Health?



Yes, some chews like Pawstruck Premium Beef Jerky contain glucosamine and chondroitin. These ingredients promote hip and joint health while being low in sugar. Such treats are suitable for diabetic dogs needing joint support.



### How To Choose Chew Bones For Diabetic Dogs?



Select chew bones that are low glycemic, rawhide-free, and vet-approved. Prioritize natural, digestible ingredients like real chicken or beef. Avoid treats with added sugars or preservatives to keep your diabetic dog healthy.
