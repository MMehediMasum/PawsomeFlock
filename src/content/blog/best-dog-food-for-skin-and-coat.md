## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Improve Dog Skin And Coat Health?



High-quality proteins like wild-caught salmon and chicken provide essential amino acids. Omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids nourish skin and coat. Vitamins, antioxidants, and minerals support overall skin health and reduce itching or dryness.



### How Does Salmon Oil Benefit A Dog's Coat?



Salmon oil is rich in omega-3 fatty acids. These reduce inflammation and promote a shiny, healthy coat. It also helps soothe sensitive skin and supports immune function.



### Can Sensitive Stomach Dog Food Improve Coat Condition?



Yes, formulas like Purina ONE Sensitive Stomach include gentle ingredients. They aid digestion and nutrient absorption, improving skin and coat health over time.



### Are Wet Dog Foods Effective For Skin And Coat?



Wet foods provide hydration and often contain high-quality proteins. They can enhance skin moisture and coat shine, especially in picky eaters or dogs with dry skin.



### What Supplements Support Dog Skin And Coat Health?



Omega-3 blends, fish oil chews, and antioxidant-rich supplements like Zesty Paws help. These support skin elasticity, reduce shedding, and improve coat texture naturally.



### How Does Freeze-dried Raw Food Help Itchy Skin?



Freeze-dried raw food offers natural nutrients and enzymes. It reduces allergic reactions and promotes a healthy skin barrier, easing itchiness and improving coat appearance.
