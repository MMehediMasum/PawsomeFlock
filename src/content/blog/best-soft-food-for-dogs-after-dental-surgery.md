### Can Soft Chews Replace Regular Dog Food After Surgery?



Soft chews can supplement but not replace balanced meals. They help with dental care but lack complete nutrition. Always combine soft chews with a vet-recommended diet for full recovery.



### How Long Should Dogs Eat Soft Food Post Dental Surgery?



Typically, soft food is recommended for 7 to 14 days after surgery. This allows gums to heal without irritation. Follow your vet’s advice based on your dog’s specific needs.



### Are Natural Flavor Soft Chews Safe For Sensitive Dogs?



Yes, natural flavors like hickory smoke in Nutri-Vet chews are safe and appealing. They minimize allergic reactions and encourage dogs to chew gently, aiding recovery without added chemicals.
