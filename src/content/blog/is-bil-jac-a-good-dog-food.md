## Tips For Transitioning To Bil Jac

Switching your dog to a new food like Bil Jac needs care. A smooth change helps your dog adjust well.

Take time and watch how your dog reacts to avoid stomach problems and picky eating.

### Gradual Introduction

Start by mixing a small amount of Bil Jac with your dog's current food. Increase Bil Jac slowly each day.

This slow change helps your dog's digestive system get used to the new food. It also lets your dog accept the taste.

* Mix 25% Bil Jac with 75% old food on day one
* Change to 50% Bil Jac and 50% old food on day three
* Use 75% Bil Jac and 25% old food on day five
* Feed 100% Bil Jac by day seven or later

### Monitoring Dog’s Reaction

Watch your dog for signs of discomfort or allergies during the switch. Check for vomiting, diarrhea, or itching.

If you see any problems, slow the transition or talk to your vet. Keep track of your dog's energy and appetite too.

* Note any changes in stool quality
* Observe appetite and eating habits
* Look for skin or coat changes
* Keep an eye on overall activity and mood
