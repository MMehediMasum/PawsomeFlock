## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Suet For Attracting Woodpeckers?



Kaytee Suet & Seed High Energy Suet is excellent for woodpeckers. It is enriched with seeds and provides the necessary energy. This suet is specially formulated to attract woodpeckers, among other wild bird species.



### How Do Suet Cakes Benefit Wild Birds?



Suet cakes provide essential fat and energy needed by wild birds. They are particularly beneficial during colder months. Birds like woodpeckers, cardinals, and sparrows thrive on the high-energy nutrients found in suet cakes.



### Are Suet Cakes Safe For All Bird Species?



Yes, suet cakes are safe and beneficial for most wild bird species. They are designed to cater to a variety of birds. Each suet type may attract different species, providing essential nutrients and energy.



### Can Suet Cakes Melt In Warm Weather?



Some suet cakes, like C&S Hot Pepper Delight No Melt Suet Dough, are designed not to melt. They are perfect for warmer climates. These suet cakes retain their shape and texture, ensuring birds can enjoy them year-round.



### How Should I Store Suet Cakes?



Store suet cakes in a cool, dry place to maintain freshness. Ensure they are kept in airtight containers. This prevents moisture and pests from affecting the suet's quality, ensuring birds receive the best nutrition.



### How Often Should I Replace Suet Cakes?



Replace suet cakes every one to two weeks or when depleted. Frequent replacement ensures the suet is fresh and appealing to birds. Regularly check and clean the feeder to maintain a healthy feeding environment.
