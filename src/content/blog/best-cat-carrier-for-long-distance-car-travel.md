## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Cat Carrier Is Best For Long-distance Travel?



Choose a large carrier with enough space for your cat to move comfortably. Look for dimensions around 24"x17"x17". Ensure it has ventilation, a litter box, and secure zipper locks for safety during long trips.



### Are Soft-sided Carriers Better For Car Travel?



Yes, soft-sided carriers offer flexibility and comfort. They fit well in car spaces and often include expandable sections. They also provide good ventilation and are lightweight, making them ideal for long-distance car travel with cats.



### Do Carriers With Litter Boxes Improve Travel Comfort?



Carriers with built-in litter boxes help maintain hygiene. They allow cats to relieve themselves during long trips. This feature reduces stress and mess, making travel more comfortable for both pets and owners.



### Can I Carry Two Cats In One Carrier Safely?



Yes, some large carriers are designed for two cats. Ensure the carrier is spacious and has separate compartments or enough room. Look for models that support up to 55 lbs and include multiple access points.



### Are Expandable Cat Carriers Useful For Road Trips?



Expandable carriers increase space when needed, providing extra comfort. They allow cats to stretch during breaks. This feature is practical for long-distance travel, camping, or hiking trips with your pet.



### What Features Ensure A Carrier Is Airline Approved?



Airline-approved carriers are soft-sided, lightweight, and fit under airplane seats. They have secure zippers, ventilation on multiple sides, and a comfortable fleece pad. Check specific airline requirements before travel to ensure compliance.
