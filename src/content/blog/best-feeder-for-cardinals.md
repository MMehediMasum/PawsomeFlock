## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Bird Feeder Ideal For Cardinals?



An ideal cardinal feeder has a sturdy metal mesh, weatherproof design, and a capacity around 2. 5 to 4 lbs. It should accommodate sunflower seeds and have a roof to protect seeds from rain. Easy hanging and squirrel-proof features help attract and protect cardinals.



### How Does Squirrel-proofing Work In Cardinal Feeders?



Squirrel-proof feeders use weight-activated perches or metal cages to block squirrels. When a squirrel lands, the feeder closes access to seeds. This ensures cardinals and smaller birds can feed without interference. Durable metal builds also prevent squirrels from chewing through the feeder.



### Why Choose A Metal Mesh Feeder For Cardinals?



Metal mesh feeders offer durability and good ventilation, keeping seeds fresh longer. They are weatherproof and resist rust, ideal for outdoor use. Cardinals can easily cling to the mesh while feeding, making metal mesh feeders popular for attracting these birds.



### How Much Seed Capacity Is Best For Cardinal Feeders?



A seed capacity between 2. 5 and 4 pounds suits cardinals well. This size provides enough food to attract them without frequent refills. Larger capacity feeders are ideal for busy birdwatching areas or multiple feeding sessions.



### Can Solar Bird Feeders Attract Cardinals Effectively?



Yes, solar bird feeders with hanging designs and metal construction attract cardinals. The solar lantern feature adds decorative lighting for bird lovers. However, ensure the feeder offers proper seed access and protection from weather for best results.



### What Seed Types Are Recommended For Cardinal Feeders?



Black oil sunflower seeds are the top choice for cardinals. They are nutritious and easy for cardinals to crack. Mixes including safflower seeds and peanuts also attract cardinals but avoid cheap seed blends with fillers.
