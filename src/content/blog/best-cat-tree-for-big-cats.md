## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Cat Tree Is Best For Large Cats?



Choose a cat tree at least 50 inches tall with wide platforms and sturdy posts. This ensures stability and comfort for big cats.



### Are Sisal-covered Scratching Posts Good For Big Cats?



Yes, sisal posts are durable and satisfy large cats' scratching needs. They help protect your furniture and provide exercise.



### How Many Levels Should A Cat Tree Have For Big Cats?



A multi-level cat tree with 3 to 6 levels offers ample climbing space. It keeps large cats active and entertained indoors.



### What Features Make A Cat Tree Suitable For Maine Coons?



Look for extra-strong construction, wide perches, and large condos. Maine Coons need heavy-duty cat trees to support their weight.



### Can Cat Trees With Hammocks Support Heavy Cats?



Yes, high-quality hammocks made with sturdy materials can support big cats comfortably. Verify weight limits before purchasing to ensure safety.



### Is Easy Assembly Important For Large Cat Trees?



Definitely. Large cat trees can be complex; easy assembly saves time and ensures the tree is stable for your big cat.
