Some dogs have allergies to certain ingredients in treats. Hartz Chew 'N Clean may contain:

* Wheat or gluten, which can cause itching or digestion issues.
* Artificial colors or flavors, triggering skin reactions.
* Preservatives that may upset sensitive stomachs.

Overfeeding these chews can lead to problems. Large amounts might cause:

* Digestive upset, including diarrhea or vomiting.

Always follow feeding guidelines. Consult a vet for dogs with health issues.
