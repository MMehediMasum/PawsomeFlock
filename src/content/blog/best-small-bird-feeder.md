## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Squirrel Proof?



A squirrel proof feeder includes gravity protection or locking lids. These features block squirrels from accessing seeds while allowing birds to feed easily. Metal roofs and chew-proof materials also prevent damage from squirrels. This keeps seeds safe and attracts wild birds without interruptions.



### How Do Metal Mesh Bird Feeders Benefit Outdoor Use?



Metal mesh feeders allow air circulation, keeping seeds fresh and dry. Their durable design withstands weather and chewing by squirrels. Hanging metal mesh feeders are easy to refill and clean. They attract small birds like finches and chickadees efficiently.



### Why Choose A Window Bird Feeder For Bird Watching?



Window feeders attach with strong suction cups for close viewing. Transparent acrylic offers an unobstructed view of wild birds feeding. They are easy to clean and refill. Perfect for indoor bird watching and connecting with nature from home.



### What Capacity Is Ideal For A Small Bird Feeder?



A 1 to 3-pound seed capacity suits small bird feeders well. It provides enough food without frequent refilling. Larger capacities like 3 lbs suit high-traffic feeding areas. Choose capacity based on bird population and feeder location.



### How Important Is Weather Resistance In Bird Feeders?



Weather resistance ensures feeders last through rain, wind, and sun. Materials like metal and durable plastics prevent rust and wear. Weather-resistant feeders protect seeds from moisture and spoilage. This maintains seed quality and feeder longevity outdoors.



### Can Bird Feeders Attract Specific Bird Species?



Yes, feeder design and seed choice attract certain birds. Finch and cardinal feeders often use mesh and specific seed blends. Perch size and feeder ports influence which birds can feed. Choose feeders tailored to your local wild bird species for best results.
