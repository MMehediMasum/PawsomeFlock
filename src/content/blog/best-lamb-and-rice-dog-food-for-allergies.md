## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Lamb And Rice Dog Food Good For Allergies?



Lamb and rice dog food contains limited ingredients, reducing allergens. Lamb is a novel protein for many dogs. Rice is gentle on the stomach. Together, they minimize allergic reactions and support sensitive digestion effectively.



### Which Lamb And Rice Dog Food Is Best For Sensitive Skin?



Purina Pro Plan Sensitive Skin and Stomach with Salmon & Rice is ideal. It targets skin and digestive health. It contains omega fatty acids that soothe irritation. The formula is designed for dogs with allergies and sensitive skin.



### Are Limited Ingredient Lamb And Rice Formulas Beneficial?



Yes, limited ingredient diets like Natural Balance’s Lamb & Brown Rice reduce allergens. They contain fewer proteins and grains, simplifying digestion. This helps dogs with food sensitivities avoid reactions and maintain better overall health.



### Can Small Breed Dogs Eat Lamb And Rice Formulas?



Yes, Blue Buffalo Life Protection Small Breed Lamb & Brown Rice suits small dogs. It provides balanced nutrition with smaller kibble size. It supports energy levels and digestive health, perfect for small breeds with allergies.



### How Does Lamb Meal Differ From Fresh Lamb In Dog Food?



Lamb meal is concentrated, dried lamb with moisture removed. It contains more protein per serving than fresh lamb. Both provide essential amino acids, but lamb meal offers higher nutrient density for active or allergic dogs.



### Is Lamb And Rice Dog Food Suitable For All Allergy Types?



Lamb and rice formulas help many allergy types but not all. They work best for food allergies to common proteins like chicken or beef. Always consult a vet to identify specific allergens before switching diets.
