## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Yak Chews For Small Dogs?



Yak chews provide long-lasting entertainment and promote dental health. They are natural, high in protein, and free from gluten and lactose. These treats help reduce plaque and tartar buildup, making them ideal for aggressive chewers and small breeds under 15 lbs.



### Are Yak Chews Safe For Dogs With Allergies?



Yes, most yak chews are grain-free, lactose-free, and made from all-natural ingredients. This makes them suitable for dogs with common allergies or dietary sensitivities. Always check specific product labels for ingredient details to ensure safety for your dog’s needs.



### How Long Do Himalayan Yak Chews Typically Last?



Himalayan yak chews are known for their durability and can last several hours to days. Their hard texture makes them ideal for aggressive chewers, providing extended chewing sessions that satisfy your dog’s natural chewing instincts.



### Can Yak Chews Improve My Dog’s Oral Health?



Yes, yak chews help clean teeth by reducing plaque and tartar buildup. Chewing these treats stimulates saliva production, which naturally cleans the mouth and freshens breath. They are excellent for maintaining healthy gums and teeth.



### Which Yak Chews Are Best For Aggressive Chewers?



Products like Wag Yak Cheese and Native Pet Yak Chews are designed for aggressive chewers. They are dense, long-lasting, and high in protein, ensuring durability and extended chewing satisfaction for powerful jaws in small dogs.



### Are Yak Chews Suitable For Puppies And Senior Dogs?



Yes, yak chews with limited ingredients and smaller sizes are suitable for puppies and senior dogs. They provide a natural, safe chewing option that supports dental health without harsh chemicals or additives. Always supervise chewing sessions for safety.
