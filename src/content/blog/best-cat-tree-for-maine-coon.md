## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Cat Tree Suits A Maine Coon Best?



A Maine Coon needs a large, sturdy cat tree. Choose one with wide perches, tall height, and strong posts. Trees over 60 inches tall and designed for cats 20+ lbs are ideal.



### How Many Scratching Posts Should A Maine Coon Tree Have?



Look for a cat tree with multiple scratching posts. Trees with 10 to 15 thick sisal posts help satisfy scratching instincts. This protects your furniture and keeps claws healthy.



### Are Hammocks Beneficial For Maine Coon Cat Trees?



Yes, hammocks provide cozy resting spots. They offer comfort and support for large cats. Hammocks also add variety to climbing and lounging areas.



### Which Materials Are Best For Heavy-duty Maine Coon Cat Trees?



Opt for durable wood or engineered wood frames. Sisal-covered posts and soft plush fabric are ideal. These materials withstand heavy use and are easy to clean.



### Can Maine Coons Use Multi-level Cat Trees Safely?



Absolutely. Multi-level trees encourage exercise and mental stimulation. Ensure platforms are wide and stable to support their weight and size safely.



### How To Choose A Maine Coon Cat Tree For Indoor Use?



Choose trees with a stable, wide base and tall height. Look for features like condos, perches, and scratching posts. Indoor trees should fit your space and lifestyle.
