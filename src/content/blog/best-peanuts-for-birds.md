Some brands offer raw, unprocessed peanuts that attract a variety of wild birds like woodpeckers, chickadees, and cardinals. Offering peanuts can also feed squirrels and other wildlife safely. Selecting the right type depends on your local birds and feeding preferences. This guide reviews top peanut options that balance nutrition, safety, and bird attraction effectively.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Type Of Peanuts Are Best For Wild Birds?



Shelled, raw, and unsalted peanuts are best for wild birds. They provide essential protein and fats. Options like Kaytee Shelled Peanuts or Old Potters Wildlife Peanuts are great choices for feeding birds safely.



### Are Shelled Or In-shell Peanuts Better For Birds?



Both shelled and in-shell peanuts are good for birds. Shelled peanuts offer easier access, while in-shell peanuts provide natural foraging fun. Choose based on bird species and feeding preferences.



### Can Peanuts Attract Specific Wild Bird Species?



Yes, peanuts attract titmice, woodpeckers, chickadees, and jays. High-protein peanuts like Lyric Peanut Pieces are designed to draw these species to your feeder.



### Are Organic Peanuts Safe For Wild Birds?



Organic, non-GMO peanuts are safe and healthy for birds. They contain no harmful pesticides. Brands like Old Potters Wildlife offer organic, farm-grown options ideal for bird feeding.



### How Much Peanut Food Do Birds Typically Eat Daily?



Birds usually consume 1-2 tablespoons of peanuts daily. Providing fresh, moderate amounts prevents waste and supports their nutritional needs. Large bags like Kaytee’s 11-pound offer long-term supply.



### Can Squirrels Eat The Same Peanuts As Birds?



Yes, squirrels can eat the same raw, unsalted peanuts as birds. Products like Wildlife Elements Peanut Party cater to both wildlife types, providing a nutritious food source.
