## Potential Risks And Considerations

Choosing between Greenie and Mil Bone dental chews means looking beyond flavor and texture. You need to consider possible risks that could affect your dog's health. Understanding these risks helps you pick a chew that not only cleans teeth but also keeps your dog safe and happy.

### Allergy Concerns

Both Greenie and Mil Bone contain ingredients that might trigger allergies in sensitive dogs. Greenies often include wheat and soy, common allergens for some pets. Mil Bone chews may have corn or artificial additives that can also cause reactions.

If your dog has a history of food allergies, check the ingredient list carefully. Watch for symptoms like itching, swelling, or digestive upset after chewing. Have you noticed any unusual behavior or skin issues after your dog tries new treats?

### Choking Hazards

Dental chews are designed to be chewed, but pieces can break off and pose choking risks. Greenies tend to be softer, reducing the chance of large chunks breaking away. Mil Bone chews are firmer and can sometimes splinter, especially if your dog is a heavy chewer.

Always supervise your dog while they enjoy a chew. If your dog is aggressive with chews or gulps pieces quickly, consider breaking the chew into smaller parts. Could a safer chew type help you feel more confident about your dog’s safety?

### Caloric Intake Impact

Dental chews add extra calories to your dog’s daily diet. Greenie chews typically have fewer calories per piece compared to Mil Bone. If your dog is on a weight management plan, these differences matter.

Be mindful of how many chews you give and factor them into your dog’s total calorie allowance. Overfeeding treats can lead to weight gain and related health problems. Are you tracking your dog’s treat calories alongside their meals?
