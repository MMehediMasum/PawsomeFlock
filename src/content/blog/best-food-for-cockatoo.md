## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Parrot Food For Large Cockatoos?



Vitakraft Menu Premium and F. M. Brown's Tropical Carnival are ideal for large cockatoos. Both offer vitamin-fortified, balanced nutrition for big birds, supporting their health and vitality.



### Are Pellet Foods Better Than Seed Mixes For Cockatoos?



Yes, pellet foods like ZuPreem and Kaytee Fiesta provide balanced nutrition. They reduce selective eating and ensure essential vitamins and minerals for cockatoos’ overall health.



### How Often Should I Feed My Cockatoo Premium Bird Food?



Feed cockatoos a measured portion twice daily. Use products like Kaytee Nutri Soft or Hari Tropimix for a nutritious, varied diet that supports their wellbeing and energy.



### Can I Mix Fruits And Nuts With Commercial Cockatoo Pellets?



Absolutely. Combining Hari Tropimix or Wild Harvest pellets with fresh fruits and nuts enriches your cockatoo’s diet, providing natural vitamins and stimulating their natural foraging behavior.



### Which Bird Food Brands Support Cockatoo Feather Health?



Kaytee Fiesta and ZuPreem FruitBlend include nutrients for skin and feather health. These brands promote strong feathers and vibrant plumage in cockatoos through added vitamins and minerals.
