## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Treat Yeast Infections In Dog Ear Cleaners?



Effective dog ear cleaners contain antifungal agents like ketoconazole, chlorhexidine, and hydrocortisone. These ingredients reduce yeast growth and inflammation. They soothe itching and clear infection-causing debris. Products like Vetnique Oticbliss and Pet MD wipes use these to treat and prevent ear infections effectively.



### How Often Should I Clean My Dog's Ears With Infection?



Clean your dog’s ears 2 to 3 times weekly during infection treatment. Over-cleaning may irritate the ear further. Use medicated solutions like Zymox Otic Plus or VetWELL Ear Cleaner. Follow product instructions and consult your vet for specific cleaning frequency.



### Are Wipes Effective For Cleaning Dogs' Ears With Yeast Infections?



Yes, wipes like Pet MD and Curaseb are convenient for daily cleaning. They remove debris and soothe itching with aloe and eucalyptus. Wipes help maintain ear hygiene and reduce infection risk but may need to be combined with medicated solutions for severe cases.



### Can Hydrocortisone In Ear Cleaners Reduce Ear Inflammation?



Hydrocortisone is an anti-inflammatory that helps reduce redness and swelling. It relieves itching and discomfort in infected ears. Products like Vetnique Oticbliss Drops and Zymox Otic Plus include hydrocortisone for faster relief and healing of yeast infections in dogs.



### What Is The Best Ear Cleaner Scent For Dogs With Infections?



Mild scents like cucumber melon in VetWELL Ear Cleaner are dog-friendly and soothing. Strong fragrances can irritate sensitive ears. Choose gentle, vet-approved formulas that clean effectively without causing discomfort or allergic reactions in dogs with yeast infections.



### Can Ear Cleaners Prevent Future Yeast Infections In Dogs?



Yes, regular use of antiseptic and antifungal ear cleaners helps prevent yeast buildup. Consistent cleaning removes excess wax, debris, and moisture. Products like Pet MD Veterinary Tris Flush provide ongoing protection against infections when used as part of routine ear care.
