Some products contain ingredients like praziquantel and pyrantel pamoate, effective against various worms. Herbal remedies also support gut health and parasite control naturally. Always pick a product that fits your dog’s weight and age. Consult your vet for the best choice and dosage. Regular deworming keeps your dog healthy and happy. This guide covers popular, trusted worm medicines for dogs.
