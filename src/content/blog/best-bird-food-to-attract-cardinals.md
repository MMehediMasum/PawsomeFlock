**Cardinals are bright, beautiful birds that bring life to any backyard. Feeding them the right food helps attract and keep them nearby.**



Choosing the best bird food for cardinals makes a big difference. Cardinals prefer seeds like sunflower and safflower because they are easy to eat and full of energy. Special blends, such as Audubon Park Cardinal Blend and Wagner’s Cardinal Blend, mix seeds and fruits that cardinals love.



Some foods also avoid filler seeds that cardinals usually ignore. Offering fresh, high-quality bird food increases the chances of seeing these lovely birds daily. This guide covers top bird seed options, including Kaytee Nut & Fruit and Happy Wings Cardinal Blend, to help you create a welcoming feeding spot for cardinals in your yard.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Seeds Attract Cardinals Most Effectively?



Sunflower seeds, especially black oil sunflower, attract cardinals best. Mixes like Audubon Park Cardinal Blend include these seeds. Cardinals prefer large, nutritious seeds for energy.



### Which Cardinal Bird Food Mix Is Best For Outside Feeders?



Wagner's 62032 Cardinal Blend Wild Bird Food is ideal for outside feeders. It contains a balanced mix of seeds cardinals love. Its 6-pound bag offers great value for attracting cardinals.



### Can Flavored Bird Food Attract More Cardinals?



Yes, flavored foods like Wagner's Farmer’s Delight with cherry flavor can attract cardinals. The added aroma and taste entice cardinals to visit feeders more often.



### Are No-mess Cardinal Bird Foods Worth Buying?



No-mess foods like Kaytee Wild Bird Ultimate reduce waste and attract cardinals. They contain hulled seeds, minimizing shell debris. This keeps your yard cleaner and birds happier.



### What Bird Food Mix Supports Various Songbirds Including Cardinals?



Wagner's 82042 Songbird Banquet Wild Bird Food supports cardinals and other songbirds. It offers a diverse seed mix that attracts finches, chickadees, and grosbeaks alongside cardinals.



### How Much Cardinal Bird Seed Should I Buy For Regular Feeding?



Buying 4 to 10 pounds, like the 4-pound Audubon or 10-pound Wagner’s Farmer’s Delight, ensures consistent feeding. Larger bags save money and keep feeders stocked longer.
