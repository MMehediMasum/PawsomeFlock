## Feeding Tips

Feeding your cat the right amount and making smooth changes to their diet are key to keeping them happy and healthy. Even with a quality food like I And Love And You, how you feed your cat matters just as much as what you feed them. Paying attention to portion sizes and transitioning foods carefully can prevent health issues and keep your cat eager for mealtime.

### Portion Control

Determining the right portion size for your cat can be tricky, but it’s essential to avoid overfeeding or underfeeding. The feeding guidelines on I And Love And You packaging are a great starting point, but remember that your cat’s age, weight, and activity level affect how much they need.

Try measuring your cat’s food with a standard cup or scale to keep portions consistent. If your cat starts gaining weight, reduce the amount slightly and watch for changes. Have you noticed your cat begging for more even after a full meal? Sometimes, it’s more about boredom than hunger.

### Transitioning Foods

Switching your cat to I And Love And You food requires patience. Sudden changes can upset their stomach or cause them to refuse the new food altogether. Gradually mix increasing amounts of the new food with their current food over 7 to 10 days.

* Start with 25% new food and 75% old food for a few days.
* Then move to 50/50 for the next few days.
* Next, 75% new food and 25% old food.
* Finally, 100% new food.

Watch your cat’s reaction during this period. Are they eating eagerly or pushing the bowl away? Adjust the pace accordingly to keep their appetite strong and digestion smooth.
