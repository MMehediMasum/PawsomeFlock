Fish oil supplements provide Omega-3 fatty acids that improve skin hydration and reduce inflammation. This leads to less itching and shedding. Products like Shed-X Liquid Dog Supplement and Natural Dog Company Wild Alaskan Salmon Oil enhance coat quality and support overall skin health.

### Can Dry Dog Food Improve A Lab’s Skin And Coat Health?

### What Ingredients Should I Look For In Anti-shedding Dog Supplements?



Look for Omega-3 fatty acids like EPA and DHA, vitamins, and minerals. Fish oils from salmon or wild-caught sources are best. These ingredients reduce itching, inflammation, and dry skin, which are common causes of shedding in Labs. Always check for natural, vet-recommended formulas.



### How Long Does It Take For Shedding Supplements To Work?



Most shedding supplements show visible results within 4 to 6 weeks. Consistent use improves skin moisture and coat shine, reducing hair loss over time. For best results, combine supplements with a balanced diet and regular grooming tailored to your Lab’s needs.
