Some Friskies wet food varieties contain artificial colors, flavors, and preservatives. Not all cats enjoy the taste of Friskies wet food. Some find the texture or flavor unappealing. This can cause picky eating or refusal to eat.

Try offering small portions first to see your cat’s reaction. Variety in flavors may encourage better acceptance.

## Feeding Tips And Recommendations

Feeding cats the right food in the right way supports their health and happiness. Knowing how much to feed and how to mix foods matters. Switching foods smoothly helps avoid tummy troubles. These tips help you feed Friskies wet cat food properly and safely.

### Proper Serving Sizes

Serve the right amount to keep your cat healthy. Follow the guidelines on the Friskies package. Cats need different portions based on age, weight, and activity.

* Adult cats usually eat 1/3 to 1 can per day.
* Kittens need smaller, more frequent meals.
* Adjust portions if your cat gains or loses weight.

Use a measuring cup or kitchen scale to be accurate. Overfeeding can cause obesity and health problems.

### Combining Wet And Dry Food

Mixing wet and dry food can balance nutrition and texture. Wet food adds moisture to your cat’s diet. Dry food helps keep teeth clean.

* Offer wet food once or twice daily.
* Dry food can be left out for grazing.
* Watch your cat’s preference and digestion.

Keep fresh water available at all times. Avoid feeding too much dry food to prevent dehydration.

### Transitioning To Friskies

Change your cat’s food slowly to avoid upset stomachs. Start by mixing a small amount of Friskies with the current food.

* Day 1-2: 25% Friskies, 75% old food.
* Day 3-4: 50% Friskies, 50% old food.
* Day 5-6: 75% Friskies, 25% old food.
* Day 7: 100% Friskies.

Watch for signs of allergies or refusal to eat. Consult a vet if problems arise.
