## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Waterproof Airtag Case Ideal For Dogs?



A waterproof Airtag case ensures your dog's tracker remains functional in wet conditions. It protects the Airtag from rain, mud, and water splashes. This is essential for active dogs who love outdoor adventures. It ensures peace of mind for pet owners, knowing their pets are always trackable.



### How To Install An Airtag Holder On A Dog Collar?



Installing an Airtag holder is simple and quick. Most holders feature a loop or clip design for easy attachment. Slide the holder onto the collar, ensuring it's secure. Some designs include a screw or buckle for added security. Always check the holder is tightly fitted to prevent it from falling off.



### Is A Silicone Airtag Case Durable For Dogs?



Yes, silicone Airtag cases are highly durable for dogs. They are designed to withstand daily wear and tear. Silicone provides a soft, flexible yet robust protective layer. It's resistant to bites, scratches, and impacts. This makes it perfect for active pets who enjoy running and playing.



### Can Airtag Holders Fit All Dog Collar Sizes?



Most Airtag holders are designed to fit various collar sizes. They accommodate collars up to 1. 5 inches wide. Flexible materials like silicone ensure a snug fit on different collars. Always check the product specifications to ensure compatibility with your dog's collar size.



### Do Airtag Cases Affect Gps Tracking Accuracy?



Airtag cases typically do not affect GPS tracking accuracy. They are designed to protect the Airtag without interfering with its signal. High-quality cases maintain the Airtag’s functionality. They ensure reliable tracking, whether your pet is indoors or outdoors, providing peace of mind for pet owners.
