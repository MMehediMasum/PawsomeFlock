## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hill's Science Diet Good For Sensitive Poodles?



Hill's Science Diet uses high-quality ingredients that support digestion and skin health. It includes chicken or salmon recipes with balanced nutrients. The formula is gentle on sensitive stomachs and helps reduce skin irritation in poodles, making it an ideal choice for their dietary needs.



### How Does Purina Pro Plan Help Poodles With Sensitive Stomachs?



Purina Pro Plan offers salmon and rice formulas designed for digestive health. These recipes are rich in omega fatty acids to soothe skin and improve coat condition. The small breed options ensure easy digestion and nutrient absorption for poodles with sensitive stomachs.



### Are Wet Dog Foods Better For Sensitive Poodles?



Wet dog foods like Hill's Science Diet Chicken & Vegetables Loaf provide extra moisture and are easier to digest. They can help poodles with sensitive stomachs stay hydrated and reduce digestive discomfort. Wet food is a great supplement or alternative to dry kibble.



### What Ingredients Should I Avoid For Sensitive Poodles?



Avoid foods with artificial colors, flavors, and preservatives. Also, limit grains like corn, wheat, and soy, which can trigger allergies. Look for limited ingredient diets with quality proteins like chicken or salmon, and easily digestible carbohydrates such as brown rice.



### Can Small Breed Formulas Benefit Poodles With Sensitivity?



Yes, small breed formulas have appropriately sized kibble for poodles. They contain balanced nutrients tailored to smaller dogs' metabolism. These formulas support sensitive stomachs and skin by providing gentle digestion and essential vitamins for healthy coat and overall wellness.



### How Often Should I Feed Sensitive Stomach Dog Food To Poodles?



Feed sensitive stomach dog food twice daily in measured portions. Consistent feeding times help regulate digestion and reduce stomach upset. Avoid overfeeding to prevent digestive issues. Always provide fresh water and consult your vet for personalized feeding guidance.
