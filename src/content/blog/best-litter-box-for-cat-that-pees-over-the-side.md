## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Litter Box Prevents Cats Peeing Over The Side?



High-sided litter boxes like IRIS USA and WoofiGo prevent side peeing. They have tall walls that contain spills effectively.



### How Do Cat Litter Box Pee Shields Work?



Pee shields attach to open top boxes to block urine splashes. They help keep floors and surrounding areas clean.



### Are Stainless Steel Litter Boxes Better For Odor Control?



Yes, stainless steel boxes like Powools and Suzzipaws resist odors. They are non-porous and easy to clean, preventing odor buildup.



### Can Large Litter Boxes Help With Side Peeing Issues?



Large boxes give cats more space to move and reduce accidents. Models like Amazon Basics and Suzzipaws offer ample room and high sides.



### Is Top Entry Litter Box Good For Cats That Pee Over The Side?



Top entry boxes like Amazon Basics reduce litter scatter and prevent side peeing. Cats enter from above, limiting mess outside the box.
