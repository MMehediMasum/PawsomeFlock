## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is Air Dried Dog Food?



Air dried dog food is made by gently removing moisture from raw ingredients. This process retains nutrients and flavor. It offers a natural, highly digestible option for pets. Ideal for small breeds, it provides concentrated nutrition without fillers. Air dried food often features high-quality proteins and limited ingredients.



### Is Air Dried Dog Food Good For Small Breeds?



Yes, air dried dog food is excellent for small breeds. It is nutrient-dense and easy to digest. The concentrated nutrition supports their energy needs. It also promotes healthy skin and coat. With limited ingredients, it's suitable for dogs with sensitivities.



Small, flavorful pieces make it appealing to picky eaters.



### How Does Air Dried Dog Food Compare To Kibble?



Air dried dog food retains more nutrients and flavor than kibble. Unlike kibble, it doesn't contain artificial preservatives or fillers. It offers a higher protein content and better digestibility. The natural ingredients provide essential nutrients for dogs. This makes it a healthier option for small breeds.



### Can Air Dried Dog Food Be Mixed With Kibble?



Yes, air dried dog food can be mixed with kibble. It enhances flavor and nutritional value. Mixing provides variety in your dog's diet. It encourages picky eaters to enjoy their meals. Ensure the combination meets your dog's dietary needs. Always consult a vet for tailored feeding advice.



### What Are The Benefits Of Grain-free Air Dried Dog Food?



Grain-free air dried dog food is ideal for dogs with sensitivities. It reduces the risk of allergies and digestive issues. High-quality proteins and natural ingredients provide balanced nutrition. It supports healthy skin, coat, and energy levels. This type of food is often recommended by vets for its benefits.
