## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Bird Feeder For Mealworms?



The best bird feeder for mealworms offers durability, easy cleaning, and squirrel-proof features. Models like Kingsyard and Jacobi Jayne® Pearl Feeder Mini™ attract wild birds effectively. They provide ample capacity and protect mealworms from weather and pests, ensuring birds enjoy fresh food.



### How Do Squirrel-proof Mealworm Bird Feeders Work?



Squirrel-proof feeders use cages, metal mesh, or weight-activated mechanisms to block squirrels. These features prevent squirrels from accessing mealworms while allowing small birds to feed safely. Products like Kingsyard Squirrel Proof Platform Feeder are designed specifically to keep squirrels out.



### Which Birds Prefer Mealworm Feeders?



Mealworm feeders attract bluebirds, robins, wrens, chickadees, woodpeckers, and cardinals. These birds seek high-protein food, especially during breeding season. Using specialized feeders like DutchCrafters Hanging Bluebird Feeder increases chances of attracting these species to your backyard.



### Can Mealworm Feeders Hold Live And Dried Mealworms?



Yes, most feeders, including Jacobi Jayne® Pearl Feeder Mini™ and Kingsyard Upgraded Bluebird Feeder, accommodate live and dried mealworms. Ensure the feeder has proper ventilation and drainage to keep mealworms fresh and prevent spoilage.



### How Much Mealworm Capacity Do These Feeders Offer?



Mealworm feeders typically hold between 1. 5 to 3. 5 pounds of mealworms. For example, Kingsyard Platform Feeder offers a large 3. 5 lbs capacity, reducing the need for frequent refills. Choose a size based on your bird activity and feeding frequency.



### Are Recycled Plastic Bird Feeders Safe For Mealworms?



Yes, recycled plastic feeders like Kingsyard Platform are safe and eco-friendly. They resist weather and decay, ensuring mealworms stay fresh longer. These feeders combine sustainability with durability, making them ideal for outdoor use.
