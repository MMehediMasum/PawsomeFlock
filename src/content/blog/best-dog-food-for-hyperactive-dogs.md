## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help With Hyperactive Dog Food?



Ingredients like fish, lamb, and brown rice support calm energy. They provide balanced nutrients and avoid fillers causing hyperactivity. Hydrolyzed proteins and limited ingredient diets reduce allergies and improve digestion. These foods help regulate energy and soothe sensitive stomachs in hyperactive dogs.



### How Does Sensitive Stomach Dog Food Reduce Hyperactivity?



Sensitive stomach dog food improves digestion and nutrient absorption. It avoids artificial additives and fillers that may trigger hyperactivity. Proper digestion stabilizes energy levels. Ingredients like salmon, rice, and probiotics support gut health, calming hyperactive dogs naturally.



### Can Hypoallergenic Dog Food Calm Hyperactive Dogs?



Yes, hypoallergenic dog food reduces allergies causing restlessness. It uses limited, gentle ingredients like hydrolyzed proteins and no fillers. This minimizes itching and discomfort that can increase hyperactivity. Hypoallergenic diets promote overall well-being and balanced behavior in sensitive dogs.



### Why Choose Grain-free Dog Food For Hyperactive Dogs?



Grain-free dog food removes common allergens like corn and wheat. This reduces digestive upset and inflammation linked to hyperactivity. Ingredients such as sweet potato and vegetables provide steady energy. Grain-free diets can help calm hyperactive dogs by avoiding spikes in blood sugar.



### Which Dog Food Supports Skin And Digestive Health?



Dog foods with fish oils, probiotics, and limited ingredients support skin and digestion. Forza10 and Wellness Complete Health offer formulas targeting itching and sensitive stomachs. These foods promote healthy skin, reduce hot spots, and improve gut function, calming hyperactive dogs effectively.
