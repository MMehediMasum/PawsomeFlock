## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Make Cat Repellent Sprays Safe For Furniture?



Most sprays use natural, non-toxic ingredients like citrus, herbs, and essential oils. These are safe for furniture and pets. They deter cats without harmful chemicals. Always check labels for allergens to ensure safety for your cat and household.



### How Effective Are Cat Deterrent Sprays On Indoor Furniture?



Cat deterrent sprays are highly effective indoors. They discourage scratching, marking, and chewing by creating an unpleasant scent. Consistent use trains cats to avoid treated areas. Results vary by cat but generally show significant behavior improvement within weeks.



### Can Cat Repellent Sprays Be Used On All Furniture Types?



Yes, most sprays are safe on wood, fabric, leather, and rugs. Test a small area first to avoid discoloration. Natural formulas minimize damage risk. Read manufacturer instructions to ensure compatibility with your furniture surfaces.



### How Often Should I Apply Cat Repellent Spray On Furniture?



Reapply sprays every few days or after cleaning to maintain effectiveness. Some formulas last longer, but regular application helps reinforce boundaries. Follow product guidelines for best results and avoid overuse that might irritate pets.



### Will Cat Deterrent Sprays Stop My Cat From Urine Marking?



Certain sprays, like Nature's Miracle Advanced Platinum, reduce urine marking by discouraging scent marking behaviors. They work best combined with cleaning and behavioral training. While not a cure-all, these sprays effectively reduce repetitive spraying indoors.



### Are Natural Cat Repellents Better Than Chemical Ones?



Natural repellents are safer for pets, children, and furniture. They use plant-based ingredients that cats dislike but won’t harm them. Chemical repellents may be more potent but risk toxicity. For long-term use, natural sprays are generally recommended.
