## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Best For Cat Scratch Sofa Protectors?



Sisal and heavy-duty adhesive tapes are ideal materials. They withstand cat claws and protect furniture effectively. Natural sisal offers durability and a texture cats love. Adhesive tapes are easy to apply and remove without residue. Both options help maintain sofa appearance and longevity.



### How Do Adhesive Cat Scratch Protectors Work?



Adhesive protectors create a barrier cats dislike scratching. They stick firmly on sofa corners, edges, and surfaces. The texture deters cats by feeling uncomfortable under their claws. Most are easy to apply and remove without damaging furniture. This method prevents scratches while preserving your sofa.



### Are Twist Pins Necessary For Scratch Protectors?



Twist pins add extra security to protectors, especially on fabric sofas. They keep the protector firmly in place against cat scratching. Pins prevent slipping or peeling caused by active cats. Using pins enhances durability and protection but is optional for adhesive-only protectors.



### Can Scratch Protectors Damage My Sofa?



High-quality protectors use gentle adhesives that don’t damage sofas. Most are designed for easy removal without leaving residue or marks. Using twist pins is safe if done carefully. Always follow product instructions to avoid fabric damage. Protectors effectively shield sofas from claw damage without harm.



### What Size Protector Fits Most Sofas?



Protector sizes vary, but 17x12 inches and larger are common. Choose size based on sofa cushions and vulnerable areas. Larger covers protect more surface but must fit well to avoid peeling. Many kits include multiple pieces for corners, arms, and backs.



Measure your sofa for best fit.
