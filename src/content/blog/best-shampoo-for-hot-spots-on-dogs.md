## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Soothe Hot Spots On Dogs?



Medicated shampoos often contain chlorhexidine, oatmeal, tea tree oil, and hydrocortisone. These ingredients reduce itching, inflammation, and bacterial infections effectively.



### How Often Should I Use Hot Spot Shampoo On My Dog?



Use the shampoo 2-3 times a week or as directed by your vet. Overuse can dry out the skin and worsen irritation.



### Can Hot Spot Shampoos Prevent Future Skin Infections?



Yes, regular use of antiseptic and antifungal shampoos can prevent bacterial and yeast infections. Consistent care improves skin health and reduces recurrence.



### Are Natural Ingredients Effective For Dog Hot Spots?



Natural ingredients like tea tree oil and oatmeal help soothe skin and reduce itching. They work well but may need vet advice for severe cases.



### Is It Safe To Use Human Shampoo For Dog Hot Spots?



No, human shampoos can irritate a dog’s sensitive skin. Use vet-approved shampoos formulated specifically for dogs to ensure safety and effectiveness.



### What Signs Indicate A Dog Needs Medicated Hot Spot Shampoo?



Signs include excessive licking, redness, scabs, and hair loss. If these appear, medicated shampoo can help control infection and soothe discomfort.
