Select puppy formulas with balanced nutrients, like Royal Canin German Shepherd Puppy or Bully Max Puppy Food. These support healthy growth and development.



### Can High-calorie Dog Food Benefit Active German Shepherd Pitbulls?



Yes, high-calorie foods like Bully Max Pro 2X provide energy and promote muscle building for active dogs and working breeds.

Lamb-based formulas, like Bully Max 25/11, are gentle and ideal for sensitive stomachs. Grain-free options reduce allergy risks.

### Are Large Kibble Sizes Better For German Shepherd Pitbull Mixes?



Large kibble encourages chewing, improving digestion. Bully Max and Royal Canin offer breed-specific kibble sizes suited for this mix.

Choosing the best dog food for your German Shepherd Pitbull mix helps keep them strong and healthy. This mix needs food rich in protein and good fats to support their muscle and energy. Foods like Bully Max and Royal Canin offer balanced nutrition with quality ingredients.
