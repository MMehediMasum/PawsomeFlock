## Feeding Tips

Feeding your dog the right food is important for its health. Cesar dog food offers tasty meals for small dogs. Here are tips to feed your dog properly.

Following these tips helps your dog enjoy its food and stay healthy.

### Portion Control

Give your dog the right amount of Cesar dog food to avoid overeating. Check the package for daily feeding guidelines based on your dog’s weight.

Adjust portions if your dog is less active or gaining weight. Small dogs usually need smaller portions spread over the day.

* Use a measuring cup for accuracy
* Feed smaller meals twice or three times daily
* Watch your dog’s weight and energy levels
* Ask your vet for advice if unsure

### Transitioning Foods

Change your dog’s food slowly to avoid stomach upset. Mix Cesar dog food with the old food over several days.

Start with a small amount of Cesar food and increase it daily. This helps your dog adjust to the new taste and ingredients.

* Day 1-2: 25% Cesar, 75% old food
* Day 3-4: 50% Cesar, 50% old food
* Day 5-6: 75% Cesar, 25% old food
* Day 7: 100% Cesar dog food

### Storage Recommendations

Keep Cesar dog food fresh by storing it properly. Store unopened cans in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.

After opening, refrigerate any unused food. Use it within two days to keep it safe and tasty for your dog.

* Seal opened cans tightly before refrigerating
* Do not freeze wet dog food cans
* Check expiration dates before feeding
* Clean your dog’s bowl after each meal
