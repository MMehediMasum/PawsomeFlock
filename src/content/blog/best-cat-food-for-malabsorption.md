**Cats with malabsorption need special diets to help them absorb nutrients better. Choosing the right cat food supports their digestion and overall health.**



Malabsorption in cats means their bodies cannot properly absorb nutrients from food. This can cause weight loss, diarrhea, and low energy. A good cat food for malabsorption must be easy to digest and rich in essential nutrients. Foods with high protein, limited ingredients, and added vitamins help improve nutrient absorption.



Some dry cat foods focus on sensitive stomachs, while others avoid grains or allergens. Brands like IAMS, Purina, and ORIJEN offer formulas that support digestion and promote healthy weight. Feeding the right food helps your cat feel better and stay active. Always pick a diet designed to meet your cat’s specific digestive needs.
