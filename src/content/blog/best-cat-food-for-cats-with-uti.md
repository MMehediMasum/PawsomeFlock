**Cats with urinary tract infections (UTIs) need special care, especially in their diet. Choosing the right cat food can help ease their discomfort and support healing.**



UTIs cause pain and frequent urination in cats. Feeding them food designed for urinary health helps reduce infection risk and promotes bladder health. Many options exist, including wet and dry foods, supplements, and treats. These products contain ingredients that support the urinary system, like cranberries and added moisture.



Some foods also control hairballs, which can worsen urinary problems. Antibiotics may be needed alongside diet changes for infections. Selecting the best cat food with urinary support helps cats recover faster and stay healthy. This guide covers top-rated cat foods and supplements to manage UTIs effectively.
