## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do Squirrel-proof Bird Feeders Work?



Squirrel-proof bird feeders use weight-activated mechanisms or designs that deter squirrels. These feeders close access to seeds when a squirrel's weight triggers the mechanism. Some feeders have collapsible perches or cages, preventing squirrels from reaching the seeds while allowing birds to feed comfortably.



### Which Bird Species Benefit From Squirrel-proof Feeders?



Squirrel-proof feeders attract a variety of bird species, including cardinals, finches, chickadees, and blue jays. These feeders provide a safe feeding environment by keeping squirrels at bay. This ensures that wild birds can enjoy the seeds without competition from squirrels.



### Are Metal Bird Feeders More Durable?



Yes, metal bird feeders are more durable than plastic ones. They withstand weather conditions and resist chewing by squirrels. Metal feeders often have protective coatings, enhancing their longevity. Their robust design ensures they remain functional and attractive in outdoor settings for extended periods.



### What Is The Seed Capacity Of These Feeders?



The seed capacity of these squirrel-proof feeders ranges from 1. 3 to 5 pounds. Larger capacity feeders require less frequent refilling. This is convenient for bird enthusiasts who want to minimize maintenance while ensuring a continuous supply of seeds for visiting birds.



### How Easy Is It To Clean Squirrel-proof Feeders?



Squirrel-proof bird feeders are designed for easy cleaning. Many models have removable perches and components, simplifying the cleaning process. Regular cleaning is essential to maintain hygiene and prevent mold or bacteria growth, ensuring a healthy feeding environment for birds.



### Do Weight-activated Perches Really Deter Squirrels?



Yes, weight-activated perches effectively deter squirrels by closing access to seeds when a squirrel lands. These perches adjust to bird weight, allowing access only to lighter birds. This mechanism ensures that only birds, not squirrels, can feed from the bird feeder.
