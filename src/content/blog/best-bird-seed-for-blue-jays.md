Some mixes also include fruits and nuts that Blue Jays enjoy. Feeding them the right seed encourages natural behaviors and keeps them coming back.



Quality bird seed blends like Kaytee Nut & Fruit Wild Bird Seed or Armstrong Royal Jubilee Jays Blend offer balanced nutrition. You can find options in various bag sizes, perfect for any feeder. Offering the best seed creates a welcoming space for Blue Jays and other wild birds in your garden or backyard.
