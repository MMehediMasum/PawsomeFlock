## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Shock Collar Waterproof For Dogs?



Waterproof shock collars have sealed electronics and water-resistant materials. This protects them from rain, splashes, and swimming. IPX7 or IP67 ratings ensure collars work even when submerged temporarily. Waterproofing increases durability and reliability during outdoor training sessions or wet weather.



### Are These Shock Collars Safe For All Dog Sizes?



Yes, most collars listed suit dogs from 5 to 120 pounds. They offer adjustable stimulation levels to fit small, medium, and large breeds. Safety features like gradual shock intensity and beep or vibration modes help customize training without harm.



### How Far Can The Remote Control Operate These Collars?



Remote ranges vary from 2,600 to 4,200 feet depending on the model. This allows owners to train dogs at a distance in open spaces. A longer range enhances control and effectiveness during outdoor activities or off-leash training.



### What Training Modes Do These Dog Shock Collars Offer?



Common training modes include beep, vibration, safe shock, and light modes. These modes enable humane, non-harmful correction options. Multiple modes allow owners to choose the best method based on their dog's sensitivity and training needs.



### How Long Do The Rechargeable Batteries Last In These Collars?



Battery life typically ranges from several days to over a week per charge. Rechargeable batteries provide convenience and cost savings over disposable ones. Fast charging and low battery alerts ensure collars are ready for consistent use.



### Can These Collars Train Two Dogs Simultaneously?



Some models support two dogs with one remote. This feature allows controlling two collars independently with separate settings. It’s ideal for multi-dog households to train both dogs efficiently and safely at the same time.
