## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Litter Type For Catlink Litter Boxes?



Clumping clay litter works best with CATLINK boxes. It clumps tightly for easy scooping. Avoid non-clumping or crystal litters, as they may clog the automatic system or reduce odor control.



### Can Multiple Cats Use The Catlink Automatic Litter Box?



Yes, CATLINK litter boxes are designed for multiple cats. The large capacity and self-cleaning feature handle litter waste efficiently for 2 or more cats without frequent manual cleaning.



### How Does Catlink Control Odor In Litter Boxes?



CATLINK uses double or triple odor removal technology. It combines ventilation, activated carbon filters, and negative ion sterilization to keep the litter box smelling fresh and reduce bacteria.



### Are Catlink Trash Bags Necessary For Litter Maintenance?

### Is The Catlink Ramp Useful For Elderly Or Disabled Cats?

### Can I Monitor My Cat’s Health Using Catlink Litter Boxes?



Yes, some CATLINK models include health monitoring via app. They track usage patterns and alert owners to potential health issues early.
