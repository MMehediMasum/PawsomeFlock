Weruva B. F. F. OMG and Wellness CORE Signature Selects provide high protein and natural ingredients. These support Savannah cats’ active lifestyle and health.



### Are Grain-free Wet Foods Suitable For Savannah Cats?



Yes, grain-free options like Wellness CORE Signature Selects help with digestion and reduce allergies, ideal for sensitive Savannah cats.



### How Important Is Variety In Wet Food For Savannah Cats?



Variety packs like Purina Fancy Feast and Blue Buffalo Tastefuls keep cats interested and ensure balanced nutrition through diverse flavors.

Smaller cans or twin-packs, like Sheba Perfect Portions or IAMS Perfect Portions, help control portions and reduce food waste for Savannah cats.
