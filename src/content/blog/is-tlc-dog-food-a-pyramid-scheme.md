## Pyramid Scheme Characteristics

Pyramid schemes are business models that focus on recruiting people. They promise high returns by enrolling others into the scheme.

These schemes often disguise themselves as legitimate businesses. Knowing their traits helps protect you from scams.

### How Pyramid Schemes Work

Pyramid schemes rely on recruiting new members to make money. Each member pays a fee to join and recruits others.

The money from new recruits pays the earlier members. This process continues until no new recruits join.

* Members earn mainly by bringing in new members
* Sales of actual products are minimal or fake
* Growth depends on constant recruitment
* Early members make money, later ones lose money

### Common Red Flags

Many signs help spot pyramid schemes. Watch for promises that seem too good to be true.

* High upfront fees to join
* Focus on recruiting over selling products
* Vague or complicated payment plans
* Pressure to recruit quickly
* Little or no real product value

### Legal Definitions

Pyramid schemes are illegal in many countries. Laws protect consumers from these scams.

Authorities define pyramid schemes as plans where earnings depend mainly on recruitment. Selling real products is not the main focus.

* Illegal to operate or promote pyramid schemes
* Enforcement by consumer protection agencies
* Penalties include fines and jail time
* Legal business models require real product sales
