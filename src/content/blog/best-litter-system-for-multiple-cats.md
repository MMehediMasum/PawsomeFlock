Using the right litter pads and refills also helps maintain hygiene. Options like the Purina Tidy Cats Breeze system and PetSafe ScoopFree offer advanced features for busy cat owners. Selecting a system that fits your cats’ needs and your lifestyle makes daily care simpler and more pleasant. This guide covers top litter systems suited for homes with several cats.
