## Feeding Recommendations

Feeding your dog Framers Dog Food correctly can make a big difference in their health and happiness. Understanding the right portion sizes and how to switch their diet smoothly helps avoid digestive issues and keeps your dog energetic. Let’s look at practical tips that can guide you through feeding Framers Dog Food the right way.

### Portion Sizes

Portion sizes depend on your dog's weight, age, and activity level. Framers Dog Food usually provides a feeding chart on the packaging—use it as a starting point but adjust based on your dog's needs.

For example, a 20-pound dog might need about 1 to 1.5 cups per day, split into two meals. If your dog is very active or a growing puppy, increase the amount slightly to match their energy needs.

Watch your dog’s weight and energy. If they gain too much weight, reduce the portions. If they seem hungry or lose weight, add a little more.

### Transitioning Tips

Sudden diet changes can upset your dog’s stomach. Instead, mix Framers Dog Food with their current food gradually over 7 to 10 days.

* Start with 25% Framers and 75% old food for the first 2-3 days.
* Next, use 50% Framers and 50% old food for another 2-3 days.
* Then, 75% Framers and 25% old food for the following 2-3 days.
* Finally, switch to 100% Framers Dog Food.

This slow transition helps your dog’s digestive system adjust smoothly. Have you noticed how some dogs react differently to new food? Keeping a close eye during this period can save you from unnecessary vet visits.
