## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Squirrel Proof For Cardinals?



Squirrel proof feeders use weight-activated perches that close access when squirrels land. This design protects seeds from squirrels while allowing cardinals and blue jays to feed freely. Metal and gravity protection enhance durability and effectiveness outdoors.



### How Much Seed Capacity Is Ideal For Blue Jay Feeders?



A seed capacity of 3 to 6 pounds is ideal. This size supports multiple birds like cardinals and blue jays. Larger capacity feeders reduce refill frequency, perfect for outdoor use and attracting wild birds consistently.



### Which Materials Are Best For Durable Bird Feeders Outdoors?



Heavy-duty metal is the best choice for outdoor feeders. It resists weather, chewing, and rust. Metal feeders also support weight-activated mechanisms and are easy to clean, attracting cardinals, blue jays, and finches effectively.



### How Do Weight-activated Perches Work In Bird Feeders?



Weight-activated perches detect heavy animals like squirrels. When triggered, they close seed access, preventing squirrel feeding. This mechanism allows smaller birds like cardinals and blue jays to feed safely without competition or seed loss.



### Can These Feeders Attract Multiple Bird Species Simultaneously?



Yes, feeders with multiple ports attract cardinals, blue jays, finches, and sparrows simultaneously. Multiple feeding spots reduce crowding and encourage diverse bird visits. Weatherproof designs ensure consistent attraction in various climates.



### What Maintenance Is Needed For Metal Squirrel Proof Bird Feeders?



Regular cleaning and refilling keep feeders in top condition. Remove old seeds and debris to prevent mold. Inspect weight mechanisms for smooth operation. Metal feeders require minimal maintenance but benefit from occasional checks for rust or damage.
