This makes scooping easier and keeps the litter box fresh longer. Some litters also help prevent tracking, so litter stays in the box and not on your floors.



Many products use natural ingredients and offer dust-free formulas, which benefit both cats and owners. This guide covers top cat litter options designed specifically for male cats, helping you find a product that meets your needs and keeps your feline friend happy.
