## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Hummingbird Feeder Bee Proof?



Bee proof feeders have built-in bee guards and ant moats. These prevent bees and ants from accessing the nectar. Multiple feeding ports also help reduce crowding. Together, these features keep hummingbirds safe and feeders clean, ensuring less insect interference and a better feeding experience.



### How Do Ant Moats Protect Hummingbird Feeders?



Ant moats are water-filled barriers placed above feeding ports. They stop ants from reaching the nectar by creating a physical water barrier. This prevents ants from invading the feeder, keeping the nectar clean and accessible only to hummingbirds, maintaining a healthy feeding environment.



### Are Glass Or Plastic Feeders Better For Durability?



Glass feeders are more durable and easier to clean. Plastic feeders are lightweight and less fragile but may degrade over time. Glass models often resist mold better. Choose based on your outdoor conditions and cleaning preference for long-lasting use and optimal hummingbird attraction.



### How Often Should I Clean A Bee Proof Hummingbird Feeder?



Clean feeders every 3 to 5 days in warm weather. Regular cleaning prevents mold, bacteria, and insect buildup. Use warm water and a cleaning brush to reach all parts. This keeps nectar fresh and hummingbirds healthy, ensuring consistent feeder use.



### Can Hummingbird Feeders Attract Other Wildlife Or Pests?



Yes, feeders can attract bees, ants, wasps, and occasionally squirrels. Bee proof feeders reduce insect visits by blocking access. Ant moats and bee guards help minimize pest problems. Proper placement and maintenance further limit unwanted visitors while attracting hummingbirds.



### What Nectar Solution Is Best For Bee Proof Feeders?



A simple nectar mix is four parts water to one part white granulated sugar. Avoid honey or artificial sweeteners as they harm birds. Boil water to dissolve sugar, then cool before filling feeders. This solution is safe, nutritious, and attracts hummingbirds effectively.
