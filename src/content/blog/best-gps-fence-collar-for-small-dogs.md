Some collars like Halo Collar 4 and PetSafe Guardian GPS 2. 0 require subscriptions for GPS services. Others, like Dogtra GPS Fence, work without subscriptions. Subscription models often provide real-time tracking and app features, while no-subscription models focus on basic boundary alerts.



### Are Gps Fence Collars Waterproof For Outdoor Use?



Most collars listed, including Dogtra GPS Fence and PYRODUM Wireless Dog Fence, feature waterproof ratings like IPX7 or IPX9K. This ensures durability in rain or wet conditions, making them suitable for outdoor use with small dogs.



### Can Gps Fence Collars Provide Training Functions?



Yes, collars like Halo Collar 4 and LOTYP 2-in-1 offer training modes such as beep, vibration, and static corrections. These features help reinforce boundary training and improve dog behavior safely and effectively.



### What Is The Adjustable Range Of Wireless Dog Fences?



Ranges vary widely; for example, PATPET offers 33 to 9999 yards, while others like PYRODUM have 25 to 999 yards. Smaller ranges suit small yards, while larger ranges fit bigger outdoor spaces. Choose based on your property size.



### Are Gps Fences Suitable For Dogs Under 15 Pounds?



Yes, collars like Halo Collar 4 and PetSafe Guardian GPS 2. 0 are designed specifically for dogs 10-15 lbs and above. Ensure the collar is lightweight and adjustable for comfort and effectiveness on small dogs.
