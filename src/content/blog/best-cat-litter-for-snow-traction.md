**Snow and ice make outdoor paths slippery and dangerous. Using cat litter for snow traction offers a simple way to improve grip.**



Cat litter works well on icy surfaces because it provides extra traction. Many types of cat litter have properties like clumping, low dust, and odor control. Some are plant-based and lightweight, while others use crystals or pine for natural grip.



Choosing the right cat litter depends on your needs and pet’s comfort. Products like SnowPaww Casssava and Purina Tidy Cats offer tight clumping and low tracking. Crystal litters, like PetSafe ScoopFree, reduce dust and keep areas fresh. Natural options like Feline Pine provide a chemical-free alternative. Using cat litter on snowy paths can prevent slips and keep your home safe during winter.
