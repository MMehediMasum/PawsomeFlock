## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Litter Box Suits Maine Coon Cats Best?



Maine Coons need extra-large litter boxes for comfort. Boxes around 28" L x 20" W provide ample space. XXL or XXXL sizes prevent crowding and allow easy movement during use. Choose high-sided or enclosed designs to contain litter scatter effectively.



### Are Stainless Steel Litter Boxes Better For Maine Coons?



Yes, stainless steel boxes are durable and rust-resistant. They resist odors and are easy to clean. Their non-stick surface prevents urine absorption, keeping hygiene optimal. Stainless steel is ideal for large cats like Maine Coons and multi-cat households.



### How Does An Enclosed Litter Box Help Maine Coons?



Enclosed boxes control odors and reduce litter scatter. They provide privacy, which many cats prefer. High sides and lids help contain urine and minimize mess. Enclosed designs often include charcoal filters for added odor control.



### What Features Prevent Urine Leakage In Large Cat Litter Boxes?



Look for 3-layer leak-proof bottoms and sealed edges. Stainless steel boxes often have anti-leak layers. Removable lids with tight seals also help. These features keep floors clean and reduce unpleasant odors in multi-cat homes.



### Is An Open-top Or Covered Litter Box Better For Maine Coons?



Open-top boxes offer easy access and ventilation. Covered boxes control odors and contain mess. Maine Coons can use either, but large sizes with high sides are essential. Choose based on your cat’s preference and household needs.



### How Do Included Accessories Improve Litter Box Maintenance?



Included scoops and mats simplify cleaning and reduce tracking. Metal scoops are durable and effective for clumping litters. Mats catch litter from paws, keeping floors tidy. Accessories enhance convenience, especially in multi-cat or large cat households.
