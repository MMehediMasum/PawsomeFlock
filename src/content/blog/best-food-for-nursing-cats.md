## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Food For Nursing Cats?



Royal Canin Mother & Babycat and Nulo Freestyle are top choices. They offer high protein, essential nutrients, and easy digestion. These foods support milk production and kitten growth effectively.



### Can Dry Food Support Nursing Cats Adequately?



Yes, Royal Canin Mother & Babycat dry formula provides balanced nutrition for nursing cats. It contains vital vitamins and minerals to maintain energy and health during lactation.



### Are Wet Foods Better For Nursing Cats?



Wet foods like Tiki Cat Aloha Friends and Blue Buffalo Baby BLUE provide hydration and high moisture content. They are palatable and aid digestion, making them ideal for nursing cats.



### Should I Give Supplements To Nursing Cats?



Supplements like Lexelium Nursing Supplement boost lactation and recovery. They support muscle and mental development in kittens and nursing cats when combined with balanced food.



### How Important Is Protein For Nursing Cats?



High protein is crucial for nursing cats. Foods like Nulo Freestyle and Tiki Cat Baby packs have high protein levels that support milk production and kitten growth efficiently.



### Can I Feed Adult Wet Food To Nursing Cats?



Adult wet foods such as Sheba Perfect Portions can be fed occasionally. However, kitten or nursing-specific formulas better meet the increased nutrient demands during lactation.
