## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Cats With Sensitive Stomachs?



Ingredients like chicken, salmon, and natural fibers aid digestion. Grain-free formulas reduce irritation. These promote gentle digestion and nutrient absorption for sensitive cats.



### Are Grain-free Canned Foods Better For Sensitive Cats?



Yes, grain-free foods often reduce allergic reactions. They contain limited fillers and more protein, which supports digestion and skin health in sensitive cats.



### How Do These Wet Foods Support Skin Sensitivity?



They include omega fatty acids and vitamins. These nutrients soothe skin inflammation and promote healthy fur. This benefits cats with both stomach and skin issues.



### Can Variety Packs Improve My Cat’s Digestion?



Variety packs provide different protein sources and flavors. This prevents food boredom and helps identify sensitive ingredients. It can improve digestion and overall health.



### Are Natural Ingredients Important In Sensitive Cat Foods?



Yes, natural ingredients minimize artificial additives and preservatives. They ensure better nutrient quality and reduce stomach upset. This enhances your cat’s digestive comfort and well-being.
