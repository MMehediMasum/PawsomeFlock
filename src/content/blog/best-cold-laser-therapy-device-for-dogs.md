### Can Cold Laser Therapy Devices Treat Arthritis In Dogs?



Yes, these devices effectively reduce arthritis pain and stiffness in dogs. They enhance joint mobility by decreasing inflammation. Regular use promotes tissue repair and comfort. Many pet owners report improved activity levels in arthritic dogs.



### Are Handheld Cold Laser Devices Safe For Home Use?



Yes, handheld cold laser devices are safe when used as directed. They emit low-level infrared light that doesn’t harm tissue. Most devices come with protective covers and instructions. Always follow recommended treatment times for best results.



### What Wavelengths Are Best For Pet Laser Therapy?



Wavelengths between 650nm and 980nm are ideal for pet therapy. These penetrate skin to target muscles and joints effectively. Devices like LLLT and Dawfek use combinations of these wavelengths. This range maximizes pain relief and healing in pets.



### How Long Should I Use Cold Laser Therapy On My Dog?



Treatment sessions usually last 5 to 15 minutes per area. Frequency depends on your dog’s condition and device guidelines. Most pets benefit from 2-3 sessions weekly. Consistency is key to achieving lasting pain relief and healing.
