## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Litter Box Ideal For Multiple Cats?



An ideal litter box for multiple cats offers large capacity, automatic cleaning, odor control, and safety sensors. App control and easy waste disposal enhance convenience. These features reduce maintenance and keep your home fresh and hygienic for all cats.



### How Does App Control Improve Automatic Litter Boxes?



App control allows remote monitoring and management of the litter box. You receive alerts for cleaning, waste levels, and maintenance. This feature ensures timely care, improves convenience, and keeps the litter box functioning efficiently for multiple cats.



### Are Safety Sensors Important In Automatic Litter Boxes?



Yes, safety sensors prevent the cleaning mechanism from operating while cats are inside. They protect pets from injury and ensure safe operation. This feature is essential for multi-cat households to avoid accidents and maintain pet safety.



### Can Automatic Litter Boxes Handle Odor For Multiple Cats?



Automatic litter boxes often include odor control systems like sealed waste compartments and deodorization features. These keep the area fresh by minimizing unpleasant smells. Proper odor management is crucial for homes with multiple cats.



### What Types Of Litter Liners Are Best For Self-cleaning Boxes?



Durable, tear-resistant liners designed for automatic boxes are best. They simplify waste removal and maintain cleanliness. Many models include multiple rolls of liners, making maintenance easier and more hygienic for multi-cat use.



### How Large Should A Litter Box Be For Multiple Cats?



A large capacity litter box, typically 65L or more, is recommended for multiple cats. This size accommodates frequent use and reduces cleaning frequency. Extra-large boxes ensure comfort and cleanliness for all cats.
