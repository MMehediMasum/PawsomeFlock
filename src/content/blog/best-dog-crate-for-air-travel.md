## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Dog Crate Is Best For Air Travel?



Choose a crate that fits your dog comfortably with room to stand and turn. Measure your dog’s height and length. Airline-approved crates like the Petmate Vari Dog Kennel in 36 inches are ideal for large dogs during air travel.



### Are Plastic Dog Crates Airline Approved?



Yes, many plastic dog crates such as Petmate Vari and SportPet Designs are airline approved. They meet IATA standards for safety, ventilation, and durability. Always check specific airline requirements before traveling.



### How To Make A Dog Crate Comfortable For Flights?



Add soft bedding and familiar toys inside the crate. Ensure proper ventilation and secure all doors. Avoid food or water close to departure to prevent spills during air travel.



### Can Soft-sided Carriers Be Used For Air Travel?



Soft-sided carriers like the Henkelion Small Dog Carrier are TSA approved for small pets under 15 lbs. They are lightweight and collapsible but usually for cabin use, not cargo.



### Do Rolling Plastic Kennels Help During Travel?



Yes, rolling kennels such as SportPet Designs and OLIXIS models ease transportation through airports. They offer stability and reduce strain while moving large or heavy crates. Rolling features improve travel convenience.



### Are Double-door Crates Better For Traveling Dogs?



Double-door crates like Amazon Basics Travel Dog Kennel offer easier access and ventilation. They allow flexible placement of food or water bowls. These crates are convenient for both home and travel use.
