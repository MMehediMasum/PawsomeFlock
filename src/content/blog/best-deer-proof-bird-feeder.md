## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Deer Proof And Squirrel Resistant?



Deer proof feeders have weight-activated perches and metal construction. These features prevent deer and squirrels from accessing seeds. Durable materials like metal and chew-proof designs enhance resistance. Hanging feeders also reduce ground access. This keeps your bird feeder safe and focused on wild birds only.



### How Does Weight-activated Perch Protect Bird Feeders?



Weight-activated perches close feeding ports when heavy animals land. This stops squirrels and deer from eating seeds. Only lighter birds can feed comfortably. It’s an effective mechanical barrier that doesn’t harm wildlife. This feature improves seed preservation and bird feeding experience.



### What Capacity Is Ideal For Outdoor Squirrel Proof Feeders?



A capacity between 3 to 5 pounds suits most outdoor bird feeders. Larger capacity means less frequent refilling. It supports feeding multiple birds simultaneously. Metal feeders with 3-5 lb capacity balance durability and convenience well. Choose based on your local bird population and feeding needs.



### Are Metal Bird Feeders Better For Deer And Squirrel Proofing?



Yes, metal feeders are chew-proof and rust-resistant. They withstand weather and animal damage longer than plastic. Metal mesh designs also deter squirrels effectively. These feeders are ideal for outdoor use and heavy seed loads. Metal feeders offer durability and reliable protection.



### Can These Feeders Attract Specific Bird Species Like Finches Or Cardinals?



Yes, feeders with multiple ports and mesh designs attract finches, cardinals, and blue jays. Weight-activated perches allow smaller birds easy access. Seed selection also plays a role. These feeders are designed to support a variety of wild birds, enhancing your backyard birdwatching experience.
