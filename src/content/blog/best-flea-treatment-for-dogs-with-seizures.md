## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Flea Treatments Are Safe For Dogs With Seizures?



PetArmor CAPACTION and Capstar (nitenpyram) are fast-acting oral flea treatments safe for dogs with seizures. They start killing fleas within 30 minutes and have minimal neurological side effects. Always consult your vet before using flea treatments on dogs with seizure history.



### How Quickly Do Oral Flea Treatments Work On Dogs?



Oral flea treatments like PetArmor CAPACTION and Capstar begin killing fleas within 30 minutes of administration. This fast action helps relieve your dog quickly and prevents flea infestations. They are effective for dogs over 2 pounds, depending on the product dosage.



### Can Topical Flea Treatments Trigger Seizures In Dogs?



Some topical flea treatments may cause neurological reactions in sensitive dogs. Products like FRONTLINE Plus and Amazon Basics topical treatments are generally safe but should be used cautiously. Always consult your vet if your dog has a seizure history before applying topical flea medications.



### Which Flea Treatments Suit Different Dog Weight Categories?



Products like FRONTLINE Plus and Amazon Basics offer flea treatments specific to dog weight ranges: small (5-22 lbs), medium (23-44 lbs), and large (45-88 lbs). Choose treatments based on your dog's weight to ensure safety and effectiveness, especially for dogs prone to seizures.



### Are Flea Treatments Waterproof And Long-lasting?



Many flea treatments like PetArmor Plus and TevraPet Activate II are waterproof and provide protection for 3-4 months. This ensures continuous flea and tick prevention even after bathing or swimming, making them convenient for active dogs with seizure concerns.
