**Choosing the right low sodium dog treats helps support heart health in dogs with heart murmurs. These treats keep sodium low while offering good nutrition and taste.**



Dogs with heart murmurs need snacks that don’t strain their heart. High sodium can cause fluid buildup and worsen symptoms. Low sodium treats protect their heart while still rewarding good behavior. Many brands now offer healthy options made with simple, quality ingredients.



From freeze-dried chicken hearts to baked pumpkin snacks, these treats suit sensitive hearts and picky eaters. Some treats also avoid common allergens like corn, soy, and gluten. Selecting treats with low protein and phosphorus supports overall health too. This guide covers the best low sodium dog treats that keep your dog happy and safe.
