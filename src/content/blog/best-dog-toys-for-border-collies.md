Herding balls stimulate Border Collies' herding instincts and provide exercise. They enhance agility and mental sharpness. Using balls like Race&Herd or FOMAOGO helps your dog stay active. They also offer physical and mental challenges, crucial for this intelligent breed.

Yes, durable toys are essential for Border Collies. They have strong jaws and high energy. Toys like Outdoggy's double-layered balls withstand rough play. This ensures longevity and safety. Durable toys prevent frequent replacements and keep your pet engaged longer.



### Can Border Collies Play With Squeaky Toys?



Yes, Border Collies enjoy squeaky toys like Letsmeet's. These toys provide auditory stimulation and are great for training. They help reduce boredom and anxiety. Squeaky toys also enhance a Border Collie's playtime experience by keeping them entertained and active.



### How Do Interactive Toys Help Border Collies?



Interactive toys, like QDAN's, keep Border Collies mentally and physically active. They challenge the dog's intelligence and instincts. These toys encourage bonding and reduce destructive behavior. Interactive play is crucial for a Border Collie's well-being and happiness.
