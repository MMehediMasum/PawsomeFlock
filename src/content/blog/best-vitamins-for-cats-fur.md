Cats need certain vitamins to maintain soft, smooth fur and healthy skin. Omegas like Omega-3 and Omega-6 support coat shine and reduce dryness. Vitamins C and E help soothe skin and protect cells from damage. Biotin strengthens fur and helps prevent hair loss.



Some supplements combine these nutrients in tasty chews or liquid drops for easy feeding. Other vitamins support overall health, improving your cat’s immune system and digestion. Choosing the right vitamin mix promotes less shedding and fewer skin issues. Regular use can help your cat look and feel better. Understanding the best vitamins for your cat’s fur is important for long-term health and happiness.
