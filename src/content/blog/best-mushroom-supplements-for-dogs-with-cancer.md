Turkey Tail, Reishi, Lion’s Mane, and Shiitake are top mushrooms for dogs with cancer. They enhance immunity, support cellular health, and provide antioxidants. These mushrooms help reduce tumor growth and improve cognitive and joint function.



### How Do Mushroom Supplements Support A Dog’s Immune System?

Yes, most mushroom supplements are safe when given as directed. Choose organic, vet-formulated products without fillers or additives. Always consult your vet before starting any new supplement for cancer care.

### Can Mushroom Supplements Improve A Dog’s Energy And Joint Health?



Yes, mushrooms like Reishi and Turkey Tail reduce inflammation and support joint function. They help increase energy levels and mobility in dogs with cancer or aging issues.
