### How Does A Wireless Invisible Fence Work?



A wireless invisible fence creates a circular boundary using a central transmitter. The dog wears a collar that emits a tone or static correction when they approach the boundary. This trains them to stay within the designated area, offering a safe and effective containment solution.



### Are Waterproof Collars Included In These Systems?



Yes, most wireless fences include waterproof collars. For instance, the PetSafe Original Wireless Electric Fence features a waterproof collar. This ensures durability and reliability in various weather conditions, allowing your dog to stay safe and comfortable outdoors.



### Can Wireless Fences Be Used For Multiple Dogs?



Yes, many wireless fences support multiple dogs. Systems like the MIMOFPET Wireless Dog Fence can manage up to two dogs. Additional collars can be purchased separately, allowing you to expand the system as needed for your furry family members.



### Do These Systems Require Professional Installation?



No, most wireless fences are DIY-friendly and don't require professional installation. They typically involve a simple setup process. With no wires to bury, these systems are perfect for quick installation and easy relocation, making them ideal for travel or temporary setups.
