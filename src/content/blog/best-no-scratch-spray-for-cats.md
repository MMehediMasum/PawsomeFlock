## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A No Scratch Spray Effective For Cats?



An effective no scratch spray uses natural, non-toxic ingredients to deter cats. It discourages scratching without harming furniture or pets. Consistent use trains cats to avoid treated areas, protecting your home while ensuring your cat's safety and comfort.



### Are These Sprays Safe For Indoor And Outdoor Use?



Yes, most sprays like Nature's Miracle and JAMBOS are safe for both indoor and outdoor use. They contain non-toxic formulas suitable for furniture, curtains, and outdoor surfaces, making them versatile for all environments your cat frequents.



### How Do No Scratch Sprays Protect Furniture From Cats?



No scratch sprays emit scents cats dislike, discouraging scratching behavior. When sprayed on furniture or fabrics, cats avoid those areas, preventing damage. Regular application reinforces this behavior, helping maintain your home’s condition while keeping cats comfortable.



### Can These Sprays Be Used For Kittens As Well?



Yes, sprays like SmartyKat and QINGQIYUE are safe and gentle for kittens. Their non-toxic, alcohol-free formulas ensure kittens won't be harmed. Early training with these sprays helps kittens learn to avoid scratching inappropriate surfaces.



### How Often Should I Apply Cat Deterrent Sprays?



Apply sprays every few days or after cleaning the treated area. Frequent application maintains the scent, reinforcing the no-scratch message. For best results, follow each product’s instructions on frequency to ensure consistent protection.



### Do Natural Ingredients In Sprays Affect Cats' Health?



No, natural ingredients in these sprays are non-toxic and safe for cats. They avoid harmful chemicals, reducing health risks. Products like MGSDL and NaturVet use safe botanicals to protect furniture without compromising your cat’s well-being.
