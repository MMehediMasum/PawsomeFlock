Yes, no-mess blends like Kaytee Wild Bird Ultimate reduce hull waste. They attract finches, chickadees, and woodpeckers without mess. These blends make feeding easier and keep your yard tidy.

### How Large Are Typical Wild Bird Seed Bags For Michigan?



Seed bags range from 4 to 20 pounds. Smaller bags like Audubon Park’s 4-pound are good for casual feeders. Larger bags like Wagner's 20-pound offer better value for regular birdwatchers.



### Is Shell-free Bird Seed Better For Michigan Feeders?



Shell-free seeds like Morning Song Clean & Free reduce mess and attract a variety of birds. They are easier to clean up and prevent seed waste. This makes feeding more enjoyable and efficient.
