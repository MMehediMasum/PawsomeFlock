Yes, products like Benebone Wishbone and SPOT Bam-Bones Plus are designed for aggressive chewers. They are made from durable materials to withstand strong chewing and prevent choking hazards.



### Which Flavors Do Lab Puppies Prefer In Chew Bones?



Lab puppies often prefer flavors like bacon, chicken, turkey, and peanut butter. Brands such as Nylabone and Benebone offer these popular flavors to encourage chewing and reduce teething discomfort.



### How Do Chew Bones Help With Puppy Teething?



Chew bones soothe sore gums and reduce teething pain. They also promote healthy teeth development and prevent destructive chewing behaviors in puppies.



### Are Rawhide-free Chew Options Better For Puppies?



Rawhide-free chews like Canine Naturals Chicken and Rice are easier to digest and safer. They reduce choking risks and allergic reactions, making them ideal for sensitive puppies.



### Can Chew Bones Improve A Puppy's Dental Health?



Yes, chew bones like Benebone Dental Chews help clean teeth and massage gums. They reduce plaque buildup and support overall oral hygiene in puppies.
