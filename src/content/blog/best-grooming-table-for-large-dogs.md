## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Grooming Table Size For Large Dogs?



For large dogs, a grooming table around 43 to 46 inches is ideal. It provides ample space for comfort and safety during grooming sessions. Tables like SHELANDY 46-in and KANIS 49" models offer sturdy surfaces and proper height for large breeds.



### How Important Is Height Adjustability In Grooming Tables?



Height adjustability is crucial for ergonomic grooming. It reduces strain on your back and allows grooming at a comfortable level. Most heavy-duty tables like KANIS and LEIBOU offer adjustable heights from 22 to 39 inches, accommodating different dog sizes and groomer preferences.



### Are Foldable Grooming Tables Practical For Large Dogs?



Yes, foldable grooming tables like ynhunreth 46'' and Lyromix 36'' provide easy storage and portability. Despite being foldable, they maintain strong weight capacities up to 330 lbs, making them practical and durable for large dogs at home or on the go.



### What Features Ensure Safety On Grooming Tables For Large Dogs?



Safety features include non-slip tabletops, sturdy frames, grooming arms, and nooses. Tables like ROOMTEC and Yaheetech have anti-slip surfaces and adjustable arms to secure dogs safely during grooming. Heavy-duty construction also prevents wobbling or tipping.



### How Much Weight Can Large Dog Grooming Tables Support?



Most large dog grooming tables support between 260 to 350 lbs. For example, SHELANDY and 43. 6" Pet Grooming Tables hold up to 350 lbs, ensuring they can safely accommodate the heaviest large breed dogs during grooming sessions.



### Which Grooming Table Accessories Improve Grooming Efficiency?



Accessories like adjustable grooming arms, tool organizers, and mesh trays enhance efficiency. Tables such as KANIS and Yaheetech include these features, helping keep grooming tools organized and dogs securely restrained, making grooming faster and more comfortable.
