## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Raw Dog Food For Puppies?



The best raw dog food for puppies includes options like Primal Kibble in The Raw and Open Farm Freeze Dried. These provide high protein, whole ingredients, and are crafted for puppy growth. They support healthy development with raw protein and essential nutrients.



### Are Freeze Dried Raw Foods Safe For Puppies?



Yes, freeze dried raw foods like Stella & Chewy's and Dr. Marty’s Nature’s Blend are safe for puppies. They retain nutrients, are easy to store, and provide balanced meals with meat, organs, and bone. Always follow feeding guidelines for best results.



### How Does Raw Food Benefit Puppy Growth?



Raw food benefits puppy growth by providing natural proteins, fats, and nutrients. Ingredients like real chicken, salmon, and whole organic produce support strong muscles, healthy skin, and a robust immune system. It mimics a puppy’s natural diet for optimal development.



### Can Raw Food Be Used As A Meal Topper?



Yes, products like Open Farm Freeze Dried and Stella & Chewy’s Freeze Dried Raw Meal Mixers work great as meal toppers. They enhance flavor and nutrition when added to dry kibble, encouraging picky eaters and boosting overall diet quality.



### Are Grain-free Raw Puppy Foods Better?



Grain-free raw puppy foods, such as Instinct Raw Boost Grain Free, are ideal for puppies with grain sensitivities. They focus on high protein from real meat without fillers, promoting better digestion and healthy weight management for sensitive pups.



### How To Choose Raw Dog Food For Puppies?



Choose raw dog food with high-quality proteins, like chicken or salmon, whole ingredients, and no artificial additives. Look for reputable brands like Merrick Backcountry and Primal Kibble, ensuring balanced nutrition tailored for puppies’ growth stages and dietary needs.
