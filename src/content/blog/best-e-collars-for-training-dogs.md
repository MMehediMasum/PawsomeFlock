Most collars like Jugbow offer ranges from 3300 to 4200 feet. Longer range means better control during outdoor training sessions. Choose based on your training environment size and dog activity level.



### Are These E-collars Waterproof And Rechargeable?



Yes, all featured collars are waterproof (IPX7 or IPX8) and rechargeable. This ensures durability in wet conditions and convenient power management for frequent use.



### How Many Training Modes Do These Dog Collars Have?



These collars offer multiple modes including beep, vibration, and shock. Modes range from 3 to 4 or more, allowing customized training for different dog behaviors and sizes.



### Can These Collars Train Multiple Dogs Simultaneously?



Yes, some models like Jugbow and Bousnic support training two dogs at once. They come with dual remotes and adjustable settings for each dog’s needs.



### Are These Collars Safe For All Dog Breeds And Sizes?



These collars suit a wide range of breeds and sizes, from small to large dogs. Adjustable shock levels ensure training remains safe and humane for every dog.



### What Features Improve Nighttime Training With E-collars?



Several collars include LED lights or night lights for visibility. This feature aids safe training in low-light conditions and helps locate your dog easily.
