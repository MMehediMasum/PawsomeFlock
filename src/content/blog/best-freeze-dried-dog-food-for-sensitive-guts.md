## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Freeze Dried Dog Food Good For Sensitive Guts?



Freeze dried dog food retains nutrients and avoids common allergens. It’s gentle on digestion and free from artificial additives. This helps dogs with sensitive stomachs tolerate meals better and reduces upset or allergic reactions.



### Are Homemade Freeze Dried Dog Food Recipes Safe?



Yes, homemade recipes are safe when using vet-approved ingredients. Proper freeze drying preserves nutrients and prevents bacteria growth. Always follow trusted guides and maintain cleanliness to ensure your dog’s health and safety.



### How Does Freeze Dried Dog Food Compare To Canned Food?



Freeze dried food is lighter, nutrient-rich, and lasts longer than canned food. It requires rehydration and often contains fewer preservatives. This makes it ideal for sensitive dogs needing gentle digestion and high-quality nutrition.



### Can Freeze Dried Dog Food Improve My Dog’s Digestion?



Yes, freeze dried food is easily digestible and often grain-free. It supports gut health by reducing allergens and fillers. This can lead to fewer digestive issues and better nutrient absorption in sensitive dogs.



### What Ingredients Should I Avoid In Freeze Dried Dog Food?



Avoid artificial preservatives, fillers, and common allergens like corn, soy, and wheat. Also, steer clear of high-protein animal by-products. Choose natural, whole-food ingredients to support sensitive digestion and overall health.



### How Do I Rehydrate Freeze Dried Dog Food Properly?



Use warm water to rehydrate freeze dried food, following package instructions. Let it soak for 5-10 minutes until soft. Proper rehydration improves texture and makes it easier for sensitive dogs to digest.
