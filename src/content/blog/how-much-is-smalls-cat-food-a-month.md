## How To Calculate Monthly Expenses

Calculating your monthly expenses for Smalls cat food helps you plan your budget better and avoid surprises. It’s not just about the price on the bag but also how much your cat actually eats daily. Knowing these details gives you control over your spending and ensures your cat stays well-fed without overspending.

### Portion Sizes Per Meal

Start by checking the recommended portion size on the Smalls cat food packaging. Portion sizes usually depend on your cat’s weight and age. For example, a typical portion might be 4 ounces per meal for an average adult cat.

If you’ve noticed your cat finishes quickly or leaves food behind, adjust the portion size accordingly. Tracking this for a few days helps you get an accurate daily amount. This step is crucial because overfeeding means more cost, while underfeeding affects your cat’s health.

### Number Of Meals Per Day

Most cats eat two meals a day, but some prefer three or more smaller meals. Decide how many meals you will feed your cat daily and multiply the portion size by that number. For instance, if your cat eats 4 ounces per meal and you feed twice a day, that’s 8 ounces daily.

This simple math helps you estimate the total daily food your cat consumes. Multiply that by 30 to get the monthly amount in ounces or grams. This approach gives a clear picture of how much food you’ll need to buy each month.

### Adjusting For Treats And Extras

Don’t forget to include treats, supplements, or any additional snacks in your calculation. Treats can add up quickly and impact your monthly spending on cat food. If your cat enjoys a few treats daily, estimate their quantity and add it to the total food amount.

For example, if you give 1 ounce of treats per day, add 30 ounces for the month. Adjusting for these extras prevents underestimating your budget. Have you noticed how small treats can suddenly increase your monthly pet expenses?
