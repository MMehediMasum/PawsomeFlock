## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Cage Is Best For A Pet Squirrel?



A cage at least 45 inches tall with multiple levels is ideal. It provides enough space for climbing and playing. Options like the HABUTWAY 45''H or Yaheetech 69-inch cages offer ample room and ventilation for squirrel comfort and activity.



### Are Metal Cages Better For Squirrels Than Plastic Ones?



Yes, metal cages are more durable and chew-resistant. They provide better ventilation and are easier to clean. Metal cages like YINTATECH 52-inch or Gingbau foldable traps ensure safety and longevity for active squirrels, preventing escapes and damage.



### How Many Levels Should A Squirrel Cage Have?



Two to four levels are recommended for squirrels. Multiple tiers encourage climbing and exercise. Products like the Yaheetech 3-level cage or HABUTWAY 4-tier cage create a stimulating environment that keeps squirrels healthy and entertained.



### What Features Make A Cage Safe For Squirrels?



Look for tight bar spacing (around 1/2 inch) to prevent escapes. Secure doors, chew-proof materials, and included accessories like water bottles and food bowls enhance safety. Models like the Deluxe Double Unit cages meet these safety standards well.



### Can Squirrel Cages Be Used For Other Small Animals?



Yes, many cages are versatile. They suit chinchillas, ferrets, guinea pigs, and rabbits too. Cages like HABUTWAY and YINTATECH models offer adaptable designs with ramps and multiple levels for various small pets.



### Are Humane Live Traps Effective For Catching Squirrels?



Yes, humane traps like Gingbau and Anyhall are designed to safely capture squirrels without harm. They are easy to set up and relocate squirrels responsibly, ideal for temporary capture or relocation needs.
