## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Truly Squirrel Resistant?



A squirrel resistant feeder uses weight-activated perches or metal cages to block squirrels. Durable materials like metal prevent chewing. Locking lids keep squirrels from accessing seeds. These features ensure birds get food without interference from squirrels.



### How Much Seed Capacity Do Squirrel Proof Feeders Have?



Most squirrel proof feeders hold between 1. 3 to 5 pounds of seed. Larger capacities reduce frequent refilling. For example, some models offer up to 5LB capacity, ideal for heavy bird traffic and longer feeding periods.



### Are Metal Bird Feeders Better Against Squirrels?



Yes, metal feeders are more durable and chew-proof. They resist damage from squirrels’ teeth. Metal mesh and solid metal designs provide long-lasting protection and stability for outdoor use.



### Can Squirrel Proof Feeders Attract Specific Bird Species?



Yes, feeders with adjustable ports and perches attract finches, cardinals, chickadees, and blue jays. The design allows access for wild birds while deterring squirrels effectively.



### How Do Locking Lids Improve Feeder Security?



Locking lids prevent squirrels from opening the feeder and stealing seeds. They add an extra layer of protection. This feature keeps seeds secure and reduces waste.



### Is It Easy To Clean Squirrel Resistant Bird Feeders?



Most squirrel proof feeders have removable parts and easy-to-clean metal surfaces. This simplifies maintenance and prevents mold or seed buildup. Regular cleaning ensures bird health and feeder longevity.
