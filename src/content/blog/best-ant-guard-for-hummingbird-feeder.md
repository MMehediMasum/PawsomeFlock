## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Ant Guard For Hummingbird Feeders?



The best ant guard effectively keeps ants away and fits most feeders. Durable plastic or glass moats with red color attract hummingbirds. Products like Ant Moat sets or Perky-Pet glass guards are highly rated for reliability and ease of use.



### How Do Ant Moats Prevent Ants On Feeders?



Ant moats create a water barrier that ants cannot cross. They hang above the feeder and hold water, blocking ants from reaching nectar. This method is safe for hummingbirds and helps maintain a clean feeding area without harmful chemicals.



### Can All Ant Guards Fit Every Hummingbird Feeder?



Most ant guards are designed to fit a wide range of feeders. Adjustable hooks and standard sizes, like those in 2 or 4 packs, ensure compatibility. However, always check product dimensions to confirm fit with your specific feeder model.



### Are Glass Ant Moats Better Than Plastic Ones?



Glass ant moats are durable, rustproof, and easy to clean, but heavier. Plastic moats are lightweight and often more affordable. Both materials effectively prevent ants; choice depends on personal preference and feeder setup.



### How Often Should I Refill The Water In An Ant Moat?



Refill the ant moat water every few days or when it evaporates. Keeping water fresh ensures continuous protection against ants. Regular cleaning prevents mold and keeps the feeder environment healthy for hummingbirds.



### Do Ant Guards Affect Hummingbird Feeding Behavior?



Properly designed ant guards do not disturb hummingbirds. They allow easy access to nectar while blocking ants. Bright red colors attract hummingbirds, so ant guards can enhance feeder usability without causing stress to birds.
