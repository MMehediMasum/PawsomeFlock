**Budgies love tasty treats that keep them happy and healthy. Choosing the best treats helps improve their diet and mood.**



Budgies need a mix of seeds, fruits, and special snacks to stay strong. Millet sprays, fruit kabobs, and crunchy sticks offer variety and nutrition. Natural millet treats provide energy and help keep beaks busy. Fruit kabobs with real carrot, cherry, and cranberry add vitamins and fun.



Crunch sticks with honey, egg, and apple give extra protein and flavor. Treats like parrot grains and snack blocks support budgies’ natural eating habits. Always pick treats made from safe, natural ingredients for your pet’s well-being. Offering different treats keeps your budgie curious and active. This guide covers top options to make treat time enjoyable and healthy for your feathered friend.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Treats For Budgies?



Budgies enjoy a variety of treats that offer nutritional benefits. Millet sprays like Living World Millet Spray and Jmxu's Spray Millet are popular. They provide natural and healthy options. Fruits and grains in treats like Wild Harvest Fruit Kabobs and Kaytee Food From The Wild offer essential vitamins and minerals.



### Are Millet Sprays Good For Budgies?



Yes, millet sprays are excellent for budgies. They are natural and easy to digest. Options like Birds LOVE Millet Tips & Tails and Jmxu's Spray Millet provide essential nutrients. These sprays also encourage foraging behavior, keeping budgies mentally stimulated.



### Can Budgies Eat Fruit Kabobs?



Yes, budgies can enjoy fruit kabobs. Wild Harvest Fruit Kabobs are made with real fruits like carrot, cherry, and cranberry. These kabobs offer a tasty and nutritious snack. The edible skewer adds extra fun, encouraging natural foraging instincts in budgies.



### What Are Mixed Grain Treats For Budgies?



Mixed grain treats are nutrient-rich snacks for budgies. KATUMO Bird Treats and Vitakraft Crunch Sticks offer grains and seeds. These treats provide essential vitamins and minerals. They are perfect for adding variety to your budgie's diet while promoting healthy eating habits.



### Are Treat Sticks Safe For Budgies?



Yes, treat sticks are safe and beneficial for budgies. Options like Vitakraft Crunch Sticks and A&E Cage Company Smakers offer nutritious ingredients. These treats promote natural foraging and chewing behavior. They are a great way to keep budgies entertained and healthy.



### How Often Should Budgies Get Treats?



Budgies should receive treats in moderation. Offering treats 2-3 times a week is recommended. Treats like Zoo Med Fruit Bird Banquet Block provide essential nutrients. They should complement a balanced diet. Always ensure fresh water and a variety of foods are available.



## Conclusion



Choosing the best treats for your budgie helps keep it happy and healthy. Millet sprays, like Living World or Jmxu’s natural sun-dried millet, are favorites among many birds. Fruit kabobs with real carrot, cherry, and cranberry offer a tasty and nutritious option.



Crunch sticks, such as those from Vitakraft and KATUMO, provide fun and flavor in one treat. Treats like Kaytee Food From The Wild and Zoo Med’s Fruit Bird Banquet Block add variety and important nutrients. Always offer treats in moderation to avoid overfeeding.
