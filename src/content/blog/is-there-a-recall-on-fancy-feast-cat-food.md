## Symptoms Of Contaminated Cat Food

Symptoms of contaminated cat food can appear quickly after your cat eats it. Cats are sensitive to harmful substances in their food. Detecting signs early can help protect your pet from serious health problems.

Contaminated food often causes digestive and behavioral changes. Watch for unusual symptoms. These signs indicate your cat may have consumed unsafe food.

### Signs Of Illness In Cats

* Vomiting or frequent retching
* Diarrhea or loose stools
* Loss of appetite or refusal to eat
* Excessive drooling or pawing at the mouth
* Lethargy or weakness
* Abdominal pain or bloating
* Excessive thirst or urination
* Unusual behavior such as hiding or aggression

### When To Contact A Veterinarian

Seek veterinary care immediately if symptoms worsen or last more than 24 hours.

Call your vet if your cat shows:

* Severe vomiting or diarrhea
* Blood in vomit or stools
* Signs of dehydration like dry gums or sunken eyes
* Difficulty breathing or persistent coughing
* Sudden collapse or seizures

Early treatment improves recovery chances. Keep your cat safe by acting quickly.
