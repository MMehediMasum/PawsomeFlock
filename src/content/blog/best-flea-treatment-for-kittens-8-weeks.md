## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Flea Treatment For Kittens?



The best flea treatment depends on your kitten's weight and age. Options include Advantage II and Frontline Plus. Both are effective for kittens over 8 weeks. Always consult your vet before starting treatment.



### How Often Should I Apply Flea Treatments?



Most flea treatments for kittens should be applied monthly. Brands like Advantage and Frontline recommend monthly application. This ensures continuous protection from fleas and ticks.



### Are Flea Treatments Safe For Kittens?



Yes, most flea treatments are safe for kittens over 8 weeks old. Always choose a product suitable for your kitten's weight. Consult your vet to ensure safety.



### Can Kittens Under 2 Lbs Use Flea Treatments?



Not all treatments are safe for kittens under 2 lbs. Brands like Advantage II start at 2 lbs. Always consult your vet for the best option for smaller kittens.



### Do Flea Treatments Prevent Ticks As Well?



Many flea treatments also protect against ticks. Products like Frontline Plus offer dual protection. Always check the label to ensure tick prevention.
