**Cats with stinky poop need special food to help reduce odor and improve digestion. Choosing the right diet can make litter box cleanup easier and keep your home fresh.**



Bad-smelling cat poop often means poor digestion or food intolerance. Feeding your cat high-quality, natural ingredients supports a healthy gut and less odor. Foods with real chicken, pumpkin, and probiotics help balance digestion and reduce smell. Grain-free options can also improve stool quality for sensitive cats.



Some cat litters include odor-fighting additives that work with good food to keep the litter box fresh. This post covers top cat foods and litter products designed to tackle stinky poop. These choices promote better digestion, reduce odor, and make life easier for both you and your cat.
