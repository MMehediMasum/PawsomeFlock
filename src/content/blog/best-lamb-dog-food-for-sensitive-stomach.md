## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Lamb Dog Food Good For Sensitive Stomachs?



Lamb dog food is gentle and easy to digest. It contains fewer allergens, reducing stomach upset and skin issues. Lamb protein supports dogs with food sensitivities, making it ideal for sensitive stomachs and promoting overall digestive health.



### Is Grain-free Lamb Dog Food Better For Sensitive Dogs?



Grain-free lamb dog food avoids common allergens like wheat and corn. It helps reduce digestive problems and skin irritations in sensitive dogs. However, consult your vet before switching, as some dogs may benefit from limited grains for fiber and nutrients.



### Can Puppies With Sensitive Stomachs Eat Lamb Dog Food?



Yes, lamb dog food designed for puppies supports sensitive stomachs with gentle ingredients. It provides essential nutrients for growth while minimizing digestive upset. Choose formulas specifically made for puppies to ensure balanced nutrition and easy digestion.



### How Do Probiotics In Lamb Dog Food Help Digestion?



Probiotics promote healthy gut bacteria, improving digestion and nutrient absorption. They reduce diarrhea and gas in dogs with sensitive stomachs. Many lamb dog foods include prebiotics and probiotics to support a balanced digestive system and enhance overall gut health.



### Are Wet Lamb Dog Foods Better For Sensitive Stomachs?



Wet lamb dog foods have higher moisture, aiding hydration and easier digestion. They are often more palatable, encouraging eating in picky or sensitive dogs. Wet options can soothe upset stomachs and provide gentle nutrition, complementing dry lamb dog food diets effectively.
