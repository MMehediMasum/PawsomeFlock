Some packs come in convenient sizes from 4 to 18 pounds. Seeds without fillers like red millet or cracked corn work best. Look for options that prevent seed growth and keep birds safe. Feeding cardinals with quality seed encourages them to visit often and stay longer. This guide covers top cardinal bird seed choices to help you pick the right one for your feeder.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Good Cardinal Bird Seed Mix?



A good cardinal bird seed mix includes sunflower and safflower seeds. These seeds attract cardinals and provide essential nutrients. Look for blends with minimal fillers to ensure quality. A high-quality mix can help cardinals thrive in your backyard. Consider options like Audubon Park or Kaytee for reliable choices.



### Why Choose Safflower Seeds For Cardinals?



Safflower seeds are a favorite among cardinals. They offer high energy and nutrition. Safflower seeds also deter squirrels, making them ideal for feeders. Many blends, like Happy Wings, include safflower to attract cardinals. It's a smart choice for bird enthusiasts.



### How Do I Attract Cardinals To My Feeder?



To attract cardinals, use seed blends with sunflower and safflower seeds. Place feeders in quiet, sheltered areas. Cardinals prefer feeders with larger perches. Try products like Wagner's or Lyric blends. Regularly clean feeders to maintain a healthy environment.



### Are Seed Blends Safe For Other Birds?



Yes, most seed blends attract a variety of birds, not just cardinals. Blends like Kaytee Ultimate Birder's Blend are ideal for multiple species. These blends include nutrients suitable for grosbeaks, nuthatches, and more. Ensure feeders are accessible to different bird sizes for best results.



### What Is "no Grow" Bird Seed?



"No grow" bird seed prevents unwanted germination under feeders. This type of seed is heat-treated to stop growth. Happy Wings offers a "no grow" mix with sunflower and safflower seeds. It's perfect for maintaining a tidy garden. This option keeps your yard neat while attracting cardinals.



## Conclusion



Choosing the best cardinal bird seed helps attract these bright, beautiful birds to your yard. Seeds like sunflower, safflower, and peanut pieces are favorites. Blends from brands such as Kaytee, Wagner's, and Audubon Park offer great options. These mixes provide the right nutrition and taste cardinals love.



A good seed mix also supports other wild birds, making your feeder a lively spot. Remember to pick fresh, clean seed to keep birds healthy. Changing seeds with the seasons can also keep cardinals coming back. Feeding cardinals is simple when you have the right seed.



Enjoy watching these colorful birds visit your garden often. A well-chosen seed mix brings nature closer to home.
