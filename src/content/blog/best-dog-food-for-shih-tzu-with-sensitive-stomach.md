**Choosing the best dog food for a Shih Tzu with a sensitive stomach is important. Proper nutrition helps keep your pet healthy and comfortable.**



Shih Tzus with sensitive stomachs need gentle, easy-to-digest foods. Ingredients like salmon, rice, and limited grains can reduce stomach upset. Foods designed for small breeds support their unique needs and size. Brands like Royal Canin, Hill's Science Diet, and Purina offer options tailored for sensitive digestion.



Wet and dry foods both work well, depending on your dog’s preferences. Look for formulas with no fillers, artificial colors, or strong additives. Feeding the right food helps prevent vomiting, diarrhea, and discomfort. This guide covers top choices to keep your Shih Tzu happy and well-nourished.
