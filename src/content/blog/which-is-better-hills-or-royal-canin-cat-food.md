## Brand Histories

Understanding the histories of Hills and Royal Canin can help you see why these brands have earned trust among pet owners. Their backgrounds reveal their commitment to pet health and the science behind their cat food formulas. Let’s look closer at what shaped these brands into leaders in pet nutrition.

### Hills Background

Hills was founded in 1948 by a veterinarian who wanted better nutrition for pets recovering from illness. This focus on therapeutic diets quickly expanded into a broader range of science-based pet foods. They are well known for their Prescription Diet and Science Diet lines, which target specific health needs.

What makes Hills stand out is their close collaboration with vets and pet nutritionists. This helps ensure their products are not only tasty but also support cats’ overall health. If you’ve ever talked to a vet, chances are they’ve recommended Hills for managing health issues like kidney problems or weight control.

### Royal Canin Background

Royal Canin began in 1968 with a strong focus on breed-specific and condition-specific nutrition. The founder was a veterinarian who believed that each cat's needs were unique based on their breed, size, and health status. This philosophy drives their extensive research and product variety today.

Royal Canin invests heavily in scientific research and clinical trials to fine-tune their formulas. Their foods often target very specific health concerns or life stages, which shows in their detailed ingredient choices. If you want tailored nutrition for your cat, Royal Canin’s approach might appeal to you.

## Ingredient Quality

Ingredient quality is a key factor when choosing between Hills and Royal Canin cat food. The health and happiness of your cat depend heavily on what goes into their meals. Understanding the differences in ingredients can help you decide which brand aligns better with your cat’s needs and your expectations.

### Protein Sources

Both Hills and Royal Canin emphasize protein, but their sources differ. Hills often uses high-quality animal proteins like chicken and lamb, which provide essential amino acids. Royal Canin tends to include a mix of animal and plant proteins, which can vary in digestibility and nutritional value.

Think about your cat’s sensitivity to certain proteins. Have you noticed if your cat prefers one flavor or type over another? This could hint at how well they tolerate the protein sources in their food.

### Additives And Fillers

Fillers can dilute the nutritional value of cat food. Hills typically avoids excessive fillers and focuses on meaningful ingredients. Royal Canin sometimes includes grains and other fillers to balance texture and cost, but this might not suit cats with grain sensitivities.

Look closely at the ingredient list. Are there unnecessary additives that don’t contribute to your cat’s health? Less can often mean better for cats with delicate stomachs.

### Nutritional Balance

Maintaining the right nutritional balance is crucial for your cat’s overall well-being. Hills formulates its recipes to support specific health needs, such as urinary health or weight management. Royal Canin also offers tailored formulas but often relies more on precise nutrient ratios than whole food ingredients.

Ask yourself: Does your cat require specialized nutrition? Your choice might lean towards the brand that targets those specific needs with the best ingredient profile.
