## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Nectar Brand For Hummingbirds?



Kaytee ElectroNectar and Perky-Pet are top-rated brands. They offer ready-to-use, clear, and natural nectar formulas. These brands attract hummingbirds effectively and are easy to use in feeders.



### How Do I Prepare Hummingbird Nectar From Powder?



Mix powder with water according to package instructions. Stir well until fully dissolved. Most powders make 64 to 96 ounces of nectar. No boiling is usually required for these easy mixes.



### Are Natural Hummingbird Nectars Better Than Artificial Ones?



Yes, natural nectars like BIRDBROS and Sapphire Labs use pure cane sugar without dyes. They are safer and more appealing to hummingbirds. Artificial additives can harm birds or deter them from feeders.



### How Long Does Hummingbird Nectar Stay Fresh?



Nectar typically stays fresh for 3 to 5 days in warm weather. Products with Nectar Defender, like Sapphire Labs, last longer by preventing mold. Regular cleaning of feeders also helps maintain freshness.



### Can I Leave Nectar Out Year-round For Hummingbirds?



It’s best to offer nectar during hummingbird active seasons, usually spring to fall. Leaving nectar out year-round can spoil quickly and attract unwanted insects or mold. Clean feeders regularly to keep birds healthy.



### What Makes A Hummingbird Nectar Concentrate Convenient?



Concentrates like Perky-Pet 32 oz allow easy mixing to create up to 96 ounces of nectar. They save storage space, reduce spills, and offer longer shelf life compared to ready-to-use nectar.
