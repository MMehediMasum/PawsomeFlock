## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Seed Mix For Bluebirds?



Kaytee Wild Bird Seed & Mealworm Seed Cake is ideal. It combines seeds and mealworms, attracting bluebirds and other species. This mix provides balanced nutrition and encourages frequent visits to your feeder. It’s perfect for attracting bluebirds, chickadees, and woodpeckers.



### Are Mealworms Good Food For Bluebirds?



Yes, mealworms are a high-protein favorite for bluebirds. Brands like Kaytee and I LOVE WORMS offer dried mealworms that boost energy. Mealworms also support growth and breeding, making them essential during nesting seasons for bluebirds and other wild birds.



### Can Bluebirds Eat Suet Nuggets Safely?



Absolutely, bluebirds enjoy suet nuggets, especially in winter. C&S Wild Bird Bluebird Suet Nuggets provide fats and nutrients. These nuggets are easy to digest and attract bluebirds, robins, and chickadees. Use suet feeders like the Kingsyard Bluebird Feeder for best results.



### What Fruits And Nuts Attract Bluebirds?



Kaytee Nut & Fruit Wild Bird Seed blends nuts and berries that appeal to bluebirds. Raisins, peanuts, and blueberries offer essential vitamins and energy. This mix encourages a variety of birds to your yard, enhancing your bird-watching experience.



### How Do I Keep Bluebird Feeders Clean And Safe?



Regular cleaning is key. Wash feeders weekly with mild soap and water. Avoid moldy or wet food like spoiled suet or seeds. Use no-mess blends like Kaytee Seed & Suet No Mess to reduce waste and maintain bird health.
