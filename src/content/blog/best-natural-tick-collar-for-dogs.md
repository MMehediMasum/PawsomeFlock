## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Natural Tick Collars Safe For Dogs?



Natural tick collars use plant-based oils and ingredients. They avoid harsh chemicals, reducing skin irritation and toxicity risks. These collars offer gentle but effective pest control suitable for dogs of all sizes and ages, including puppies.



### How Long Does Natural Tick Collar Protection Last?



Protection duration varies by brand but typically ranges from 4 to 8 months. Some collars offer up to 16 months. Longevity depends on waterproof features and the natural oils' potency within the collar.



### Can Natural Tick Collars Fit All Dog Sizes?



Most natural tick collars are adjustable to fit small, medium, and large dogs. Adjustable sizing ensures comfort and effectiveness. Always check the collar’s length and fit before purchase for best results.



### Are Natural Tick Collars Waterproof?



Many natural tick collars feature waterproof designs. This helps maintain effectiveness during rain or bathing. Waterproof collars prevent loss of active ingredients and ensure continuous pest protection.



### Do Natural Tick Collars Repel Other Pests Besides Ticks?



Yes, many natural collars repel fleas, mosquitoes, and other biting insects. Essential oils like citronella and eucalyptus provide broad-spectrum pest control. This helps keep dogs comfortable and protected outdoors.



### How Quickly Do Natural Tick Collars Start Working?



Natural tick collars usually begin repelling ticks within 24 to 48 hours. Full protection develops as active ingredients disperse evenly. Consistent wear ensures ongoing defense against ticks and fleas.
