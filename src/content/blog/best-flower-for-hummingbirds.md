## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Flowers Attract Hummingbirds The Most?



Hummingbirds love brightly colored, tubular flowers like trumpet vine, penstemon, and zinnia. These flowers provide easy access to nectar, attracting hummingbirds effectively.



### Are Wildflower Seed Mixes Good For Hummingbirds?



Yes, wildflower seed mixes with varieties like butterfly milkweed and cosmos offer nectar-rich blooms. They support hummingbirds and other pollinators throughout the season.



### How Do Trumpet Vine Seeds Help Hummingbird Gardens?



Trumpet vine produces vibrant, trumpet-shaped flowers ideal for hummingbirds. These seeds grow hardy plants that attract and feed hummingbirds naturally.



### Can I Plant Perennial Flowers For Hummingbirds?



Absolutely. Perennials like red penstemon and butterfly milkweed provide consistent nectar sources year after year, creating a sustainable hummingbird habitat.



### What Is The Best Seed Mix For Butterfly And Hummingbird Gardens?



Look for mixes with 20+ non-GMO annual and perennial species, such as wildflower and butterfly-hummingbird blends. They ensure diverse blooms and attract pollinators.



### How Do Resealable Seed Packages Benefit Gardeners?



Resealable packages keep seeds fresh and prevent moisture damage. They also allow easy storage, ensuring seeds remain viable for future planting seasons.
