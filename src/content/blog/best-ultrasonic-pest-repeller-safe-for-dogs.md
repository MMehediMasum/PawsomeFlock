## Frequently Asked Questions



### Are Ultrasonic Pest Repellers Safe For Dogs At Home?



Yes, ultrasonic pest repellers emit sound frequencies that are harmless to dogs. They target pests like rodents and insects without affecting pets. Always choose models labeled pet-safe to ensure no discomfort or health risks for your dogs during use.



### How Effective Are Ultrasonic Pest Repellers Against Rodents?



Ultrasonic pest repellers disrupt rodents’ communication and navigation using high-frequency sound. Many users report fewer rodent sightings after consistent use. However, effectiveness varies by pest species and environment, so combining methods may yield the best results.



### Can Ultrasonic Pest Repellers Repel Multiple Insect Types?



Yes, these devices repel various insects such as mosquitoes, ants, spiders, and cockroaches. Multi-mode models allow customization for different pests. This versatility makes them practical for homes with diverse pest problems.



### Do Ultrasonic Pest Repellers Work In Large Spaces?



Ultrasonic waves have limited range and may not cover large areas effectively. For bigger spaces, multiple units or combined pest control methods are recommended. Proper placement enhances coverage and repelling efficiency.



### How Energy-efficient Are Ultrasonic Pest Repellers?



Most ultrasonic pest repellers consume very low power, often less than 5 watts. They plug into standard outlets and run continuously without significantly increasing electricity bills. This makes them cost-effective and eco-friendly pest control options.
