Some brands add probiotics to help digestion and improve appetite. Options like Royal Canin Savor Selective and Blue Buffalo Tastefuls offer tasty recipes with natural ingredients.



Others, such as Stella & Chewy’s and I and love and you, focus on high protein and grain-free formulas. Affordable choices like Purina Friskies and Meow Mix provide variety and flavor.
