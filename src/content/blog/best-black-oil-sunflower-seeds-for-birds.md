## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Black Oil Sunflower Seeds Best For Birds?



Black oil sunflower seeds have thin shells and high oil content. Birds find them easy to crack and nutritious. They attract many species like cardinals, blue jays, and woodpeckers. Their non-GMO, USA-grown quality ensures healthy feeding for wild birds.



### How Do I Choose Quality Black Oil Sunflower Seeds?



Look for seeds that are fresh, clean, and non-GMO. Choose trusted brands like Wagner’s or Old Potters. Bags should be properly sealed to maintain freshness. Consider weight options from 4 to 50 pounds based on your feeding needs.



### Which Birds Prefer Black Oil Sunflower Seeds?



Northern cardinals, blue jays, and hairy woodpeckers favor black oil sunflower seeds. These seeds provide high energy and essential fats. Many backyard birds also enjoy them. Using these seeds helps attract a diverse variety of wild birds.



### Are Black Oil Sunflower Seeds Safe For All Wild Birds?



Yes, black oil sunflower seeds are safe and healthy for most wild birds. They provide essential nutrients and energy. Non-GMO seeds grown on small farms ensure no harmful additives. Always provide fresh seeds and clean feeders for bird safety.



### How Should I Store Black Oil Sunflower Seeds?



Store seeds in a cool, dry place away from moisture. Use airtight containers to maintain freshness and prevent mold. Avoid direct sunlight and extreme temperatures. Proper storage extends seed life and keeps birds healthy during feeding seasons.
