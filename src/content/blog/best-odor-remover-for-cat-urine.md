## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Enzymatic Cleaners Effective For Cat Urine?



Enzymatic cleaners break down urine molecules using natural enzymes. This removes stains and odors completely. Products like Rocco & Roxie and Nature’s Miracle use this technology. They prevent cats from re-marking areas by eliminating the source of the smell.



### Can Odor Removers Work On Old Cat Urine Stains?



Yes, many enzymatic removers like Resolve Urine Destroyer are designed for old, stubborn stains. They penetrate deeply to break down dried urine crystals. Consistent application may be needed for best results on severe or set-in stains.



### Are These Odor Removers Safe For Carpets And Upholstery?



Most listed products, such as Pet Odor and Stain Eliminator, are safe for carpets and upholstery. They use non-toxic, pet-friendly ingredients. Always test a small area first to ensure no discoloration or damage occurs.



### How Often Should I Apply Odor Remover To Cat Urine Spots?



Apply enzymatic odor removers immediately after spotting urine. For tough odors, reapply every few hours until smell disappears. Regular use helps fully break down urine and prevents recurring odors.



### Do Citrus-based Cleaners Like Angry Orange Work Well For Cat Urine?



Yes, citrus-based cleaners like Angry Orange contain enzymes plus natural citrus oils. They effectively remove urine odors while leaving a fresh scent. Citrus oils also discourage cats from marking treated areas again.



### Can These Products Eliminate Urine Odors From Hardwood Floors?



Yes, enzyme-based sprays such as KINZUA Urine B-Gone are safe and effective on hardwood floors. They clean urine without damaging wood finish. Use recommended amounts and wipe excess moisture to protect flooring.
