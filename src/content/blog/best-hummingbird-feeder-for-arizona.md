## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Hummingbird Feeder For Arizona's Climate?



The best feeder for Arizona is durable, easy to clean, and has built-in ant and bee guards. Glass feeders with ant moats work well here, like the Perky-Pet 9111-1SR, which resists heat and deters pests effectively.



### How Do Built-in Bee Guards Benefit Hummingbird Feeders?



Built-in bee guards prevent bees and wasps from accessing the nectar. This keeps the feeder cleaner and reduces competition for hummingbirds. It also helps maintain a safe feeding environment for birds, especially in warm climates like Arizona.



### Why Choose A Glass Hummingbird Feeder Over Plastic?



Glass feeders are more durable and resist fading in strong sunlight. They are easier to clean and don’t retain odors. Glass models like the WOSIBO or FEED GARDEN feeders last longer in Arizona’s harsh sun and maintain nectar purity.



### How Important Is An Ant Moat On Hummingbird Feeders?



An ant moat is crucial to keep ants away from the nectar. It creates a water barrier that ants cannot cross. Feeders with built-in ant moats, like the Jealoeur and Fchstaer models, are ideal for outdoor use in Arizona gardens.



### What Size Hummingbird Feeder Is Ideal For Arizona?



A feeder between 16 to 32 ounces is ideal. Larger feeders like the LUJII 36 oz hold more nectar, reducing refill frequency. Choose a size based on local hummingbird activity and how often you want to refill.
