**Cats with urinary tract infections need special care, including the right cat litter. Choosing the best litter helps monitor health and keeps your cat comfortable.**



Urinary tract infections (UTIs) cause discomfort and health risks for cats. Certain cat litters can help detect and manage these infections early. Health-indicating litters change color when they detect abnormal urine, alerting you to possible problems. Non-clumping litters with silica crystals absorb moisture well and control odor, keeping your cat’s environment clean.



Some litters also come with supplements or test kits to support urinary health. Using the right litter along with proper diet and supplements improves your cat’s well-being. This guide covers top cat litters and products designed to support cats with UTIs, helping you make informed choices for your furry friend.
