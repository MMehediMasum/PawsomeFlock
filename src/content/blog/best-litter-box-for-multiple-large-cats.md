## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Litter Box Size Suits Multiple Large Cats Best?



A jumbo or XXL litter box with high sides is ideal. It offers ample space for big cats and reduces mess. Look for boxes measuring around 28" to 31" in length for comfort and cleanliness.



### Are Stainless Steel Litter Boxes Better For Large Cats?



Yes, stainless steel boxes are durable and easy to clean. They resist odors, scratches, and urine stains, making them perfect for multiple large cats. Their non-stick surface also prevents litter from clumping and sticking.



### How Do High-sided Litter Boxes Prevent Litter Scatter?



High sides act as a barrier, keeping litter inside the box. They reduce scatter and splashing during digging and covering. This feature is crucial for large or multiple cats who tend to be more vigorous.



### Is An Enclosed Litter Box Good For Multiple Cats?



Enclosed boxes provide privacy and help contain odors. However, ensure the size is extra-large to accommodate multiple large cats comfortably. Proper ventilation is also essential to maintain freshness inside.



### What Features Make Litter Boxes Easy To Clean?



Non-stick surfaces, removable lids, and included scoops simplify cleaning. Stainless steel and high-quality plastic materials prevent urine leakage. These features ensure quick maintenance and a hygienic environment for multiple cats.
