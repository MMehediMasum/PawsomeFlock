## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Enzymatic Cleaners Effective For Pet Stains?



Enzymatic cleaners break down proteins in pet urine and feces. This stops odor and removes stains deeply. Products like Rocco & Roxie and Nature’s Miracle use enzymes for thorough cleaning and odor elimination. They are safe and work well on carpets, upholstery, and hard floors.



### How To Choose The Best Stain Remover For Dogs?



Look for enzyme-based formulas that target both stains and odors. Check if the product is safe for pets and surfaces. Consider scent preferences and the severity of messes. Popular choices include Nature’s Miracle and Resolve, which handle everyday and severe stains effectively.



### Can These Removers Eliminate Old Pet Stains Completely?



Yes, many enzyme cleaners like Nature’s Miracle Advanced and OxiClean are designed for old stains. They break down deep-set proteins causing odors. Consistent application may be needed for tough, aged stains. Always follow product instructions for best results.



### Are These Odor Removers Safe For Pets And Children?



Most products like OUT! PetCare and Rocco & Roxie use non-toxic ingredients. They are safe when used as directed. Avoid direct contact with pets during application. Always ventilate rooms and store cleaners out of reach to ensure safety.



### How Often Should I Use Pet Stain Removers?



Use stain removers immediately after accidents for best results. For persistent odors, reapply every few days until fully gone. Regular cleaning helps maintain odor-free and clean surfaces. Products like Nature’s Miracle Everyday Mess formula are ideal for frequent use.



### Do These Products Work On All Surfaces?



Yes, enzyme-based removers like Resolve and Pet Odor Eliminator work on carpets, upholstery, hardwood, and tile. Check product labels for specific surface recommendations. Test on a small area first to avoid damage. They effectively remove stains and odors across different materials.
