Finding the best cat litter for less tracking improves both your cat’s comfort and your home’s cleanliness. Some litters are designed to clump tightly, so less dust and granules stick to your cat’s paws. This means fewer particles spread outside the litter box.



Many options also offer odor control and low dust, making them healthier for pets and people. From clay-based to natural wood pellets, each type has unique benefits. This guide reviews top low-tracking cat litters like Purina Tidy Cats Tidy Feet, Fresh Step Clean Paws, and Dr. Elsey’s, helping you pick the right one for your home and cats.
