## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Cats With Sensitive Stomachs?



Cats with sensitive stomachs benefit from limited ingredients like rice, chicken, turkey, and lamb. Avoid fillers, artificial additives, and grains that cause irritation. Probiotics and fiber also support digestion and gut health, found in formulas like Solid Gold Sensitive Stomach and Hill's Science Diet.



### How Does Purina Cat Chow Gentle Aid Digestion?



Purina Cat Chow Gentle uses a gentle formula with natural fibers and prebiotics. It supports healthy digestion and soothes sensitive stomachs. The recipe also includes essential nutrients for skin health, making it ideal for cats with both stomach and skin sensitivities.



### Is Grain-free Cat Food Better For Sensitive Stomachs?



Grain-free cat food can reduce digestive issues for some cats. Products like Solid Gold Sensitive Stomach offer grain-free options with high-quality protein and probiotics. However, not all cats need grain-free diets, so consult your vet before switching foods.



### Can Wet Food Help Cats With Sensitive Stomachs?



Yes, wet food like Hill's Science Diet Sensitive Stomach & Skin wet variety provides hydration and easier digestion. It often contains fewer fillers and more moisture, which can soothe sensitive stomachs and improve nutrient absorption.



### How Often Should I Feed Sensitive Stomach Cat Food?



Feed cats with sensitive stomachs 2-3 small meals daily. This prevents overeating and reduces digestive stress. Consistent feeding times with sensitive formulas like Purina Pro Plan and Blue Buffalo help maintain gut health and reduce stomach upset.



### Are Probiotics Important In Sensitive Stomach Cat Food?



Probiotics are vital for gut health and digestion. They balance intestinal flora and reduce inflammation in sensitive stomachs. Brands like Solid Gold and Blue Buffalo include probiotics to support healthy digestion and improve nutrient absorption in cats.
