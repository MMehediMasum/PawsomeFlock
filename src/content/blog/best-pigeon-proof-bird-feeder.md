## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Pigeon Proof?



A pigeon proof bird feeder uses cages or weight-activated perches to block pigeons. It allows smaller birds to feed safely. Metal mesh and heavy-duty materials prevent pigeon access and damage. These features ensure seed is reserved for smaller wild birds like finches and cardinals.



### How Do Weight-activated Squirrel Proof Feeders Work?



Weight-activated feeders close feeding ports when heavy birds or squirrels land. This mechanism keeps larger pests away while allowing smaller birds to feed. It helps protect seeds from being eaten or wasted, ensuring only desired birds like finches and chickadees can access the food.



### Which Materials Are Best For Durable Bird Feeders?



Heavy-duty metals like copper or steel offer long-lasting durability. Recycled plastic with metal mesh is also rugged and weatherproof. These materials resist chewing and harsh weather. They keep feeders functional and attractive for years, protecting seeds from pests like squirrels and pigeons.



### How Large Should A Bird Feeder Capacity Be?



A 3 to 5-pound seed capacity is ideal for most outdoor feeders. It supports multiple birds and reduces frequent refills. Larger capacities suit active birdwatchers and busy feeding stations. Ensure the feeder design matches seed type and bird species to optimize feeding efficiency.



### Can Pigeon Proof Feeders Deter Other Pests Too?



Yes, many pigeon proof feeders also block squirrels and grackles. Features like metal cages and weight-activated perches prevent multiple pests from accessing seeds. This multi-pest protection keeps bird feeders cleaner and more efficient, attracting only desired wild birds.
