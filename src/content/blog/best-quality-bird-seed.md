Larger bags suit regular feeding and save money. The right seed mix also reduces waste and mess. Feeding birds supports local wildlife and brings natural beauty to your garden. Choosing quality seed ensures birds stay healthy and active. This guide covers top bird seed options for your feeding needs and bird types.
