### Breed And Size Impact

The breed and size of your dog play a major role in grooming costs. Larger dogs naturally require more time and products, which increases the price. Breeds with thick or long coats, like Golden Retrievers or Poodles, often need more attention than short-haired breeds.

Have you noticed your dog’s breed affects how often they need grooming? Smaller breeds like Chihuahuas usually cost less to groom, but some small dogs with dense coats can still be pricey. It’s about the amount of work needed, not just size.

### Coat Condition And Style

The current condition of your dog’s coat can add to grooming time and cost. Matted or dirty fur requires extra care and can take longer to clean and cut. Groomers might charge more if your dog needs a thorough detangling or deep cleaning before styling.

Choosing a specific haircut style also impacts the price. Simple trims cost less, but detailed cuts or breed-specific styles take skill and time. Think about how much effort you want your groomer to put in when budgeting.

### Additional Services And Add-ons

Extra services like nail trimming, teeth brushing, or flea treatments usually add to the overall grooming price. Some owners opt for specialty shampoos or conditioners, which can also raise costs. Pet Supplies Plus offers these add-ons, so you can customize your dog’s grooming experience.
