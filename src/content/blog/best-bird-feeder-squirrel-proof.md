### What Makes A Bird Feeder Squirrel Proof?



A squirrel proof bird feeder uses weight-activated perches or gravity protection. This design blocks squirrels from accessing the seed while allowing birds to feed safely.



### How Much Seed Capacity Do These Feeders Offer?



Seed capacities vary from 1. 3 pounds to 5 pounds. Larger capacities reduce refilling frequency and attract more wild birds.



### Are Metal Mesh Feeders Better For Durability?



Yes, metal mesh feeders are durable and weather-resistant. They also prevent squirrels from chewing through, ensuring long-lasting outdoor use.



### Which Bird Species Do These Feeders Attract?



These feeders attract finches, cardinals, chickadees, and blue jays. Their design suits small to medium wild birds.



### Do Weight-activated Perches Effectively Deter Squirrels?



Yes, weight-activated perches close access when squirrels land. This mechanism effectively prevents squirrels from stealing birdseed.



### Can These Feeders Be Hung Outdoors Easily?



Yes, all listed feeders come with hanging hooks or loops. They are convenient for outdoor placement in gardens or backyards.
