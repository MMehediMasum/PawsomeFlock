## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Hummingbird Feeder Leak-proof?



A leak-proof hummingbird feeder has sealed feeding ports and a secure base. This prevents nectar spills and waste. Materials like glass or quality plastic enhance durability and stop leaks. Built-in ant moats also help maintain cleanliness and protect nectar from insects.



### How Does An Ant Moat Protect Hummingbird Feeders?



An ant moat is a water-filled barrier around the feeder’s hook or base. It stops ants from reaching the nectar. This feature keeps the feeder clean and prevents ants from contaminating the nectar, ensuring a safe feeding environment for hummingbirds.



### Are Bee Guards Important On Hummingbird Feeders?



Yes, bee guards prevent bees from accessing the nectar. This reduces competition and contamination. It also protects hummingbirds from aggressive bees. Feeders with built-in bee guards offer a safer, cleaner feeding spot for birds and maintain nectar quality longer.



### How Easy Is It To Clean Leak-proof Feeders?



Leak-proof feeders often have wide mouths and detachable parts. This design allows for easy cleaning and refilling. Regular cleaning prevents mold and bacteria. It ensures healthy nectar for hummingbirds and prolongs feeder life.



### What Size Hummingbird Feeder Is Ideal Outdoors?



A feeder between 15 to 26 ounces is ideal for outdoor use. It holds enough nectar for multiple visits. Larger feeders reduce refill frequency. Choose based on your hummingbird activity and garden size.



### Can Glass Feeders Withstand Outdoor Conditions?



Yes, glass feeders are durable and weather-resistant. They resist UV damage better than plastic. Glass is also easier to clean and doesn't retain odors. Proper hanging and handling prevent breakage, making glass feeders a great outdoor choice.
