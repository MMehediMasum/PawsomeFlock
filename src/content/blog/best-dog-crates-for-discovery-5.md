## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Dog Crate Fits A Discovery 5 Best?



A 42-inch crate suits medium-large dogs like those in the Discovery 5 category. It offers ample space for comfort. Models like the Precision Pet Products 42 Inch crate are ideal for pets 70-90 lbs, ensuring secure and roomy housing.



### Are Wire Dog Crates Better Than Collapsible Ones?



Wire crates provide sturdy ventilation and visibility, while collapsible crates offer portability. Choose wire crates like the Midwest iCrate for home use. Opt for collapsible crates such as EHEYCIGA for travel and convenience.



### How Secure Are Dog Crates With 5-point Locking Systems?



Five-point locking systems enhance safety by preventing accidental openings. Crates like Precision Pet Products feature these locks. This system ensures your dog stays secure during travel or at home.



### Can Dog Crates Double As Furniture?



Yes, some crates like BOINN’s 42-inch model serve as furniture. They blend functionality with aesthetics, offering storage and stylish home décor. These crates have removable trays and wheels for easy cleaning and mobility.



### What Features Make Outdoor Dog Crates Durable?



Outdoor crates need weather-resistant materials like galvanized steel roofs and secure locks. The Backyard Discovery Waggin’ Retreat offers these features. It provides safe, comfortable shelter against various weather conditions for your dog.



### Are Airline-approved Dog Crates Suitable For Home Use?



Airline-approved crates like Petmate Sky Kennel are sturdy and ventilated. They are safe for both travel and home use. Their compact design suits small to medium dogs, ensuring comfort and security indoors or in transit.
