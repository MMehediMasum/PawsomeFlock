Rechargeable toys like lasers and feather wands offer endless fun. Track toys with balls can keep them busy for hours. Motion-activated toys also provide excitement and exercise. These toys not only entertain but also help maintain your cat's physical and mental well-being. Explore these options to ensure your cat stays engaged and content while you're away.
