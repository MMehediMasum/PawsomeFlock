## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Cat Door Fits Large Exterior Doors?



For large exterior doors, choose cat doors like Depets Large (9. 9" x 9. 2") or CEESC Extra Large (11" x 9. 8"). They accommodate cats and small dogs up to 25 lbs. These sizes fit door circumferences under 23 inches, ensuring easy pet access and weatherproof protection.



### How Does 4-way Locking Cat Door Enhance Security?



A 4-way locking system allows locking the flap fully open, fully closed, or one-way entry/exit. This feature provides control over pet movement, prevents unwanted animals from entering, and improves security for exterior doors. Models like Depets and CEESC offer this locking convenience.



### Are Weatherproof Cat Doors Suitable For All Climates?



Yes, weatherproof cat doors like CEESC and PetSafe are designed to withstand rain, wind, and temperature changes. They feature insulated flaps and durable materials to keep your home energy-efficient and your pets comfortable, making them ideal for various outdoor weather conditions.



### Which Cat Door Is Best For Small To Medium Pets?



For small to medium pets, options like PetSafe Doggie Door (up to 15 lbs) and goTeamghjkl Cat Door (circumference < 15. 75") are ideal. They offer secure, weatherproof designs with easy installation for interior and exterior doors, perfect for cats and small dogs.



### Can Cat Doors Be Installed On Walls Or Sliding Doors?



Yes, certain models like PetSafe and Thin Cat Door are versatile for installation on walls, sliding doors, or standard doors. They come with adjustable frames and weatherproof features to fit various home structures and ensure pet convenience and safety.
