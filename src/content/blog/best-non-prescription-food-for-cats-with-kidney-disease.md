Brands like Hill’s, Forza10, and Dave’s Pet Food offer formulas designed for kidney support. Wet and dry varieties cater to different preferences and needs. Some include added vitamins and minerals to boost health. Always monitor your cat’s response to new food and consult your vet regularly. Feeding the right food can help your cat live more comfortably despite kidney issues.
