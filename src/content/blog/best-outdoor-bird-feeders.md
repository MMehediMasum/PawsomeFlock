Outdoor bird feeders come in many styles and sizes. Some have metal roofs to protect seeds from rain. Others include weight-activated perches that keep squirrels away. Large capacity feeders mean fewer refills. A feeder with mesh sides lets small birds easily access seeds.



Some models combine seed and suet feeding options for different bird types. Durable materials like metal resist chewing and weather damage. Hanging designs save space and keep birds safe from ground predators. Picking the right feeder depends on the birds you want to attract and your outdoor space. This guide covers top feeders that balance function, durability, and bird friendliness for your backyard.
