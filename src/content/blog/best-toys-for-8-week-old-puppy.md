**Choosing the best toys for an 8-week-old puppy helps your new friend grow healthy and happy. Puppies need safe, fun toys to chew, play, and learn.**

Interactive toys, like treat dispensers and puzzle balls, encourage mental growth and stop boredom. Plush toys provide comfort and fun without making a mess. Selecting the right size and texture is important for safety and enjoyment. These toys support healthy chewing habits and prevent destructive behavior. Giving your puppy a variety of toys creates a happy, active, and well-adjusted dog.
