## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Wireless Fence For Big Dogs?



The best wireless fence offers a wide coverage area and strong, waterproof collars. Models like MIMOFPET and Dogtra provide reliable containment and multiple training modes suitable for large dogs.



### How Do Wireless Fences Keep Big Dogs Safe?



Wireless fences use a boundary signal and correction collar. When dogs approach the boundary, they receive a warning tone or gentle static correction to prevent escape.



### Can Wireless Fences Cover Large Outdoor Areas?



Yes, some models like MIMOFPET cover up to 856 acres, ideal for big dogs with large yards or farms needing extensive boundaries.



### Are Wireless Fences Suitable For Stubborn Big Dogs?



Yes, systems like PetSafe Stay & Play offer adjustable settings and strong corrections to train even stubborn big dogs effectively and safely.



### How Waterproof Are Wireless Fence Collars For Big Dogs?



Most collars, such as Dogtra and MIMOFPET, are IPX9K waterproof. They withstand rain, swimming, and outdoor conditions without damage.



### Do Wireless Dog Fences Require Monthly Subscriptions?



No, most wireless fences like Dogtra and PetSafe operate without monthly fees, providing a one-time purchase solution for big dog containment.
