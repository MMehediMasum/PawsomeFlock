## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Vitamins Are Best For Senior Cats' Joint Health?



Supplements with glucosamine, MSM, and omega fatty acids support joint flexibility. Products like Cat Hip & Joint Health Chews help reduce arthritis discomfort in senior cats effectively.



### How Do Vitamins Improve Senior Cats' Skin And Coat?



Vitamins E, B-complex, zinc, and taurine nourish skin and promote shiny fur. Multivitamins like INABA Churu and Nutri-Vet help maintain healthy skin and coat in aging cats.



### Can Multivitamins Boost Senior Cats' Immune Systems?



Yes, antioxidants like vitamin C, selenium, and zinc strengthen immunity. Liquid drops and daily chews provide essential immune support for older cats to stay healthy.



### Are Lickable Vitamin Treats Good For Senior Cats?



Lickable treats like INABA Churu combine taste with nutrients. They provide vitamins and taurine in a tasty form, making supplementation easy for picky senior cats.



### What Vitamins Support Senior Cats' Heart Health?



Taurine, B12, folic acid, and L-arginine are crucial for heart function. Products like Life Extension Cat Mix and 15 in 1 Multivitamin Drops support cardiovascular health in senior cats.



### How Often Should Senior Cats Receive Vitamin Supplements?



Daily supplementation is recommended for consistent nutrient support. Follow product instructions and consult your vet to tailor vitamin frequency for your cat’s specific needs.
