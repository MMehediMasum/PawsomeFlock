## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Bird Seed "no Mess" And Why Choose It?



No mess bird seed is husk-free or dehulled to reduce waste. It prevents shell scatter and keeps feeders clean. Choosing it saves time on cleanup and attracts birds without attracting pests like squirrels or rodents.



### Which Birds Are Attracted To Kaytee No Mess Seed Blends?



Kaytee no mess blends attract cardinals, finches, chickadees, woodpeckers, juncos, grosbeaks, and sparrows. These blends suit many colorful songbirds and city-dwelling birds. They provide a balanced diet tailored to different bird species.



### How Does The Blueberry Flavor Benefit Wild Birds?



Blueberry-flavored seed blends provide natural antioxidants and vitamins. They appeal to birds’ taste preferences, encouraging feeding. This flavor enhances bird health and encourages more frequent visits to your feeder.



### Is No Mess Bird Seed Suitable For Outdoor Feeders?



Yes, no mess seed is ideal for outdoor feeders. It reduces hull debris and seed waste on the ground. This keeps feeding areas cleaner and more attractive to wild birds.



### Can No Mess Bird Seed Deter Unwanted Pests?



No mess seed reduces leftover shells that attract pests like squirrels and rats. It helps maintain a cleaner feeding area. However, some pests may still visit, so additional deterrents might be needed.



### How Much No Mess Bird Seed Should I Buy For Regular Feeding?



Buying 5 to 15 pounds of no mess seed suits most backyard feeders. This quantity balances freshness and supply. Larger bags like 10-15 pounds offer value and longer feeding periods without spoilage.
