## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Cat Foods Help With Weight Loss And Sensitive Stomach?



IAMS Proactive Health and Hill's Science Diet offer weight control with gentle digestion support. Purina ONE and Solid Gold also provide formulas for sensitive stomachs and weight management, combining natural ingredients and probiotics for healthy digestion and reduced irritation.



### Are Grain-free Cat Foods Better For Sensitive Stomachs?



Grain-free options like Solid Gold can be gentler on sensitive stomachs. They avoid common allergens and include probiotics for gut health, improving digestion and reducing discomfort in cats prone to food sensitivities or allergies.



### Which Cat Food Brands Focus On Skin And Stomach Sensitivity?



Purina ONE Sensitive Stomach and IAMS Proactive Health Adult Sensitive Digestion specifically target skin and stomach issues. These formulas contain natural ingredients, balanced nutrients, and gentle proteins to support skin health and reduce digestive upset.



### How Do Probiotics In Cat Food Aid Digestion?



Probiotics improve gut flora balance, enhancing nutrient absorption and reducing digestive issues. Foods like Solid Gold include probiotics to support healthy digestion and alleviate symptoms related to sensitive stomachs in cats.



### Can Indoor Cats Benefit From Weight Control Cat Food?



Yes, indoor weight control cat foods like Purina ONE Indoor Dry Cat Food help prevent obesity. They contain fewer calories and balanced nutrients tailored for less active indoor cats to maintain a healthy weight.



### What Ingredients Should I Avoid For Sensitive Stomach Cats?



Avoid artificial preservatives, fillers, and common allergens like corn, soy, and wheat. Choose foods with natural proteins, fiber, and probiotics such as those found in IAMS and Purina ONE formulas for better digestion and weight management.
