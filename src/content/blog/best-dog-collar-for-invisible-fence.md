## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Collar For Invisible Fence?



The best collar for invisible fences depends on your dog's size and behavior. Options include nylon replacement collars, GPS wireless systems, and the PetSafe Stubborn Dog Fence Collar. Each offers unique features like waterproofing, adjustable sizes, and various alert modes to ensure effective containment.



### Are Replacement Collars Compatible With Most Brands?



Yes, many replacement collars are compatible with most invisible fence brands. They often fit popular brands like PetSafe and Invisible Fence. Always check compatibility with your specific system before purchasing to ensure a proper fit and effective performance.



### How Does A Gps Dog Fence Collar Work?



A GPS dog fence collar uses satellite positioning to create a virtual boundary. When your dog approaches the boundary, the collar emits alerts like beeps or vibrations. This technology is effective for large areas, providing a customizable and portable solution for pet containment.



### Is The Petsafe Stubborn Dog Collar Waterproof?



Yes, the PetSafe Stubborn Dog Collar is waterproof. It's designed for outdoor use, making it suitable for various weather conditions. This feature ensures your pet stays safe and contained, even in wet environments.



### What Are The Features Of An Electric Dog Fence?



Electric dog fences offer features like adjustable boundaries, waterproof collars, and multiple alert modes. These systems provide secure containment without visible barriers. They are ideal for outdoor areas, ensuring pets remain within designated boundaries while offering flexibility and convenience.
