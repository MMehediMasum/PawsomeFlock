Some foods also help reduce hairballs and support easy litter box cleanup. Selecting the right food prevents common health issues and keeps your cat active. This guide covers top options like Purina ONE, Hill’s Science Diet, IAMS, and Blue Buffalo. Each choice fits different needs, from sensitive stomachs to weight management. Feeding the right food helps your cat enjoy their golden years comfortably.
