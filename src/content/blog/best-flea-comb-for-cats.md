## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Flea Comb Effective For Cats?



An effective flea comb has fine, closely spaced teeth to trap fleas and eggs. Stainless steel teeth are durable and easy to clean. A comfortable handle ensures easy grooming. The comb should suit your cat’s hair type for best results.



### How Often Should I Use A Flea Comb On My Cat?



Use a flea comb daily during flea season or if your cat shows signs of fleas. Regular combing helps remove fleas, eggs, and debris early. It also monitors flea infestations and supports your cat’s overall hygiene.



### Can Flea Combs Remove Ticks From Cats?



Yes, flea combs with fine teeth can help remove small ticks. However, larger ticks might require special tick removal tools. Always check your cat’s skin carefully and consult a vet if ticks persist.



### Are Stainless Steel Flea Combs Better Than Plastic Ones?



Stainless steel combs are more durable and resistant to bending. They clean easily and last longer than plastic combs. Stainless steel teeth are better at catching fleas and eggs, making them a preferred choice for many pet owners.



### What Features Should I Look For In A Flea Comb?



Look for fine, closely spaced teeth made of stainless steel. A comfortable, non-slip handle enhances grip during grooming. Some combs offer self-cleaning or dual-sided designs for convenience. Choose a size and style suitable for your cat’s fur length.



### Can Flea Combs Help With Pet Hair Removal Too?



Yes, flea combs can effectively remove loose hair and dander. Their fine teeth catch shedding fur during grooming sessions. This helps reduce hairballs and keeps your cat’s coat clean and healthy.
