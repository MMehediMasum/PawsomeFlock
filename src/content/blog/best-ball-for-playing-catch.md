## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Ball For Playing Catch With Kids?



The best ball for kids is soft, lightweight, and easy to catch. Foam or sticky balls like Ayeboovi or EVERICH sets are ideal. They reduce injury risk and improve hand-eye coordination, perfect for ages 3-10 in outdoor play.



### Are Foam Baseballs Good For Indoor Catch Games?



Yes, foam baseballs like Franklin Sports foam balls are perfect for indoor catch. They are soft, lightweight, and safe, minimizing damage risks. These balls allow kids to practice hitting and catching indoors comfortably.



### Which Toss And Catch Sets Are Suitable For All Ages?



Sets like the Higoala and Champion Sports are great for all ages. They include paddles and balls that develop coordination and are easy to use outdoors. These sets support family play, backyard, beach, or camping fun.



### How Do Sticky Balls Improve Toss And Catch Games?



Sticky balls, such as those in EVERICH sets, enhance catching by sticking to paddles. This feature helps beginners catch easily and builds confidence. Sticky balls also encourage interactive play, making outdoor games more engaging for kids.



### What Size Toss And Catch Balls Are Best For Young Kids?



Smaller balls around 1. 8 to 2. 5 inches, like Hanaive’s replacement balls, are best for young kids. They are easier to handle and catch. These sizes promote skill development without overwhelming younger children during outdoor play.
