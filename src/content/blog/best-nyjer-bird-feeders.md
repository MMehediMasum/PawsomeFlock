## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Squirrel Proof?



Squirrel-proof feeders like the Gtongoko use metal tubes and design features to block squirrels. This protects the Nyjer seed and ensures birds get the food without interruptions.



### How Much Seed Do Typical Nyjer Feeders Hold?



Most Nyjer feeders, such as the Kingsyard and iBorn models, hold around 2 to 2. 5 pounds of seed. This capacity suits small birds and reduces frequent refilling.



### Why Choose Metal Mesh Over Plastic Feeders?



Metal mesh feeders offer durability and chew-proof protection. They also provide better ventilation to keep Nyjer seed fresh and prevent mold growth.



### How Do Weatherproof Domes Benefit Nyjer Feeders?



Weatherproof domes shield the seed from rain and snow. This keeps seeds dry, prolongs their freshness, and attracts finches reliably.



### What Are The Best Features For Attracting Finches?



Look for feeders with multiple ports and perches, like 12 to 16 openings. This allows several finches to feed simultaneously, enhancing birdwatching enjoyment.



### How Easy Is Cleaning These Nyjer Bird Feeders?



Most metal tube feeders have removable trays and parts for easy cleaning. Regular cleaning prevents seed mold and keeps birds healthy.
