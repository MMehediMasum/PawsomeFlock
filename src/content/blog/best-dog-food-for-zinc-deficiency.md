### What Are The Signs Of Zinc Deficiency In Dogs?



Zinc deficiency in dogs can cause hair loss, skin lesions, and slow wound healing. Dogs may also show poor appetite and lethargy. Identifying these signs early helps in providing proper zinc supplementation and preventing further health issues.

Dog foods like Forza10 Dermo Allergy and IAMS Advanced Health Skin & Coat contain nutrients supporting zinc absorption. These foods promote healthy skin and coat while addressing zinc deficiency symptoms effectively.



### Are Zinc Supplements Safe For All Dog Breeds?
