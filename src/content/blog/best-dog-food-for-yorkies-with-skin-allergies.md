## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Yorkies With Skin Allergies?



Ingredients like salmon, rice, and sweet potatoes reduce allergens. Limited ingredient diets avoid common irritants. Omega-3 fatty acids support healthy skin and coat. These components soothe sensitive skin and improve digestion in Yorkies.



### How To Choose Dog Food For Sensitive Yorkie Skin?



Look for limited ingredient, grain-free formulas with natural proteins. Avoid fillers, artificial additives, and common allergens. Check for omega fatty acids to support skin health. Small breed-specific recipes ensure proper nutrition for Yorkies.



### Can Hill's Science Diet Improve Yorkie Skin Allergies?



Yes, Hill's Science Diet Sensitive Stomach & Skin supports skin health. It contains chicken, prebiotic fiber, and antioxidants. These ingredients help reduce irritation and improve digestion in Yorkies with allergies.



### Is Salmon-based Dog Food Good For Yorkies' Skin?



Salmon provides essential omega-3 fatty acids that reduce inflammation. It helps soothe itching and promotes a shiny coat. Many sensitive skin formulas use salmon as a primary protein source for Yorkies.



### Why Choose Grain-free Dog Food For Yorkies With Allergies?



Grain-free diets minimize exposure to common allergens like wheat and corn. They reduce digestive upset and skin irritation. Grain-free options often use novel proteins and carbohydrates suited for sensitive Yorkie skin.
