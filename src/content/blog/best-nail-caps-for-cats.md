## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Nail Caps For Cats To Prevent Scratching?



The best nail caps for cats are made from soft, durable vinyl resin. They protect furniture and skin while being easy to apply. Brands like VICTHY and Soft Claws offer long-lasting, veterinarian-developed caps in multiple colors and sizes for medium cats.



### How Do I Apply Cat Nail Caps Properly?



Apply cat nail caps by first trimming the cat’s nails. Use the included adhesive and applicator to attach the caps securely. Wait a few minutes for the glue to dry. Follow product instructions carefully for safe and effective use.



### Are Cat Nail Caps Safe For My Pet?



Yes, cat nail caps are safe when used correctly. They are made from non-toxic materials and designed to protect cats and their owners. Always follow application instructions and monitor your cat to ensure comfort and safety.



### How Long Do Cat Nail Caps Last On Average?



Cat nail caps typically last 4 to 6 weeks. They fall off naturally as the cat’s nails grow. Regular replacement is needed to maintain protection. Products like Soft Claws and VICTHY offer durable options with long-lasting adhesive.



### Can Cat Nail Caps Help With Furniture Damage?



Yes, cat nail caps significantly reduce furniture scratches. They cover sharp claws, preventing damage to wood, fabric, and skin. Using caps from brands like VICTHY or Soft Claws offers a humane, stylish way to protect your home.



### What Sizes And Colors Are Available For Cat Nail Caps?



Cat nail caps come in various sizes, typically small to medium for cats weighing 9-13 lbs. Color options range from natural to vibrant shades like pink sparkle, red, blue, and multicolor packs, allowing customization and style for your pet.
