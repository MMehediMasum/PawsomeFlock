## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Clumping Litter For Automatic Litter Boxes?



Purina Tidy Cats and ARM & HAMMER Clump & Seal are top choices. They offer strong odor control and easy maintenance. These litters work well with self-cleaning boxes, keeping them efficient and fresh for longer periods. They clump tightly, making scooping simple.



### Can I Use Natural Litter In Automatic Litter Boxes?



Yes, PETKIT Mixed Cat Litter is 100% natural, flushable, and dust-free. It clumps fast and dissolves in water, making it eco-friendly. This litter suits automatic boxes designed for clumping litters, providing easy cleanup without harming your pets or plumbing.



### How Does Odor Control Vary Between Clumping Litters?



Odor control differs by formula. ARM & HAMMER Clump & Seal uses activated charcoal for superior odor blocking. Fresh Step Multi-Cat also offers long-lasting odor control. Both reduce smells effectively, essential for automatic litter boxes in multi-cat households.



### Are Low-dust Litters Better For Automatic Litter Boxes?



Yes, low-dust litters like PETKIT and Fresh Step minimize dust buildup inside the box. This reduces sensor malfunctions and keeps the environment cleaner. Low dust is also healthier for cats’ respiratory systems and improves overall litter box performance.



### Which Litter Is Best For Multi-cat Automatic Litter Boxes?



ARM & HAMMER Clump & Seal Platinum and Fresh Step Multi-Cat formulas are ideal. They provide strong clumping and odor control designed for multiple cats. These litters maintain freshness and simplify cleaning in busy households with automatic litter boxes.



### What Features Should I Look For In Clumping Litter?



Choose litter with fast clumping, strong odor control, low dust, and compatibility with your litter box model. Features like flushability or natural ingredients add value. These qualities ensure easy cleaning, reduce mess, and keep your automatic litter box functioning smoothly.
