## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Squirrel Proof?



Squirrel proof feeders have weight-activated perches or cages blocking squirrels. They prevent squirrels from accessing birdseed, protecting feeders. Metal construction also resists chewing. These features ensure birds get the food without squirrel interference.



### How To Choose The Best Large Bird Feeder?



Consider seed capacity, material durability, and weather resistance. Choose feeders with multiple feeding ports and easy refill options. Squirrel proof features add value. Size depends on bird types and feeding frequency.



### Which Bird Feeders Attract Finches And Cardinals Best?



Feeders with metal mesh or dual compartments work well. Look for feeders designed with circular perches and weatherproof domes. These attract finches, cardinals, chickadees, and other wild birds effectively.



### Are Metal Bird Feeders Better Than Plastic Ones?



Metal feeders are more durable and chew-proof. They resist rust and weather damage longer than plastic. Metal feeders also offer better protection against squirrels. Plastic feeders may crack or degrade faster outdoors.



### How Often Should I Refill A Large Bird Feeder?



Refill when seed runs low to keep birds visiting regularly. For large feeders, check every few days during peak feeding seasons. Frequent refills ensure fresh seeds and reduce waste or mold growth.



### Can Dual-seed Compartments Attract More Bird Species?



Yes, dual compartments allow offering different seed types simultaneously. This attracts diverse birds like cardinals, titmice, and nuthatches. It enhances feeding variety and birdwatching enjoyment.
