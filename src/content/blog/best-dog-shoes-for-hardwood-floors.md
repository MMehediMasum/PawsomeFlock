## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Dog Shoes On Hardwood Floors?



Dog shoes prevent slipping and protect paws on hardwood floors. They provide extra grip and reduce injuries from slipping. Shoes also stop dogs from licking or damaging their paws, keeping them clean and healthy. They are especially helpful for senior or active dogs.



### How Do Non-slip Dog Socks Improve Traction Indoors?



Non-slip dog socks feature rubber grips that increase floor traction. They reduce slipping and sliding on smooth hardwood surfaces. These socks are lightweight and flexible, allowing natural movement. They also cushion paws and prevent damage from friction or cold floors.



### Can Dog Shoes Protect Paws From Hot Or Cold Pavement?



Yes, dog shoes shield paws from extreme temperatures. They insulate against hot pavement in summer and cold or snow in winter. Shoes prevent burns, cracks, and irritation. Many models have breathable, waterproof materials for all-season comfort and protection.



### Are Dog Socks Better Than Traditional Dog Boots?



Dog socks offer lightweight, breathable protection with added grip. They are easier to put on and more comfortable for indoor use. Traditional boots provide more robust outdoor protection but can be bulkier. Socks are ideal for hardwood floors and light outdoor activities.



### How Do I Choose The Right Size Dog Shoes?



Measure your dog’s paw width and length accurately. Check each brand’s sizing chart for best fit. Shoes should be snug but not tight to avoid discomfort. Proper fit ensures maximum grip, comfort, and prevents shoes from slipping off.



### Can Dog Shoes Prevent Paw Licking And Injuries?



Yes, dog shoes act as a physical barrier against licking. They protect wounds or irritated areas from further damage. Shoes also reduce paw wear from rough surfaces. This helps speed recovery and maintains paw health.
