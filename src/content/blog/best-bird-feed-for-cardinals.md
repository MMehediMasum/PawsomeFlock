Choosing the best bird feed for cardinals means knowing their favorite seeds. Cardinals prefer sunflower seeds, safflower seeds, and peanuts. These seeds provide essential nutrients and energy. Several high-quality blends combine these ingredients to meet cardinals’ needs. Products like Audubon Park Cardinal Blend and Kaytee Cardinal Wild Bird Seed offer great options.



Some mixes also include fruit flavors or other seeds for variety. Using a good blend keeps cardinals healthy and active around your feeder. A clean feeder and fresh food also matter for their safety. Feeding cardinals can be a simple joy with the right seed mix on hand.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Seeds Are Best For Feeding Cardinals?



Sunflower seeds, safflower seeds, and red millet are ideal for cardinals. Blends like Audubon Park and Kaytee offer these seeds. These mixes attract cardinals while supporting their dietary needs with high energy and nutrients.



### How To Choose Bird Food For Outside Cardinal Feeders?



Select blends with large seeds like sunflower and safflower. Avoid small seeds that cardinals struggle to eat. Products like Wagner’s Cardinal Blend and Happy Wings mix offer optimal nutrition and seed size for outdoor feeders.



### Can Cardinal Blends Attract Other Wild Birds Too?



Yes, blends like Kaytee Ultimate Birder’s Blend attract grosbeaks, nuthatches, and woodpeckers. These mixes contain diverse seeds and nuts, benefiting multiple species while keeping cardinals satisfied.



### Why Avoid Seeds With Fillers In Cardinal Food?



Fillers like millet or cracked corn offer little nutrition and can waste seed. High-quality blends such as Freebird Premium Songbird Blend focus on nutritious seeds that cardinals prefer, ensuring less waste and more bird visits.



### How Often Should I Refill Cardinal Bird Feeders?



Refill feeders when seeds run low, typically every 3-5 days. Regular refilling keeps cardinals returning and prevents mold or spoilage. Clean feeders often to maintain seed quality and bird health.
