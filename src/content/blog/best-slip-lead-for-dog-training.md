## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Slip Leads Effective For Dog Training?



Slip leads are effective as they combine a collar and leash in one. They offer control without causing discomfort. The design helps discourage pulling by applying gentle pressure. This makes them perfect for training, ensuring better obedience and focus during walks.



### Are Slip Leads Suitable For All Dog Sizes?



Yes, slip leads are available for all dog sizes. From small to large breeds, there are options for everyone. It's important to choose the right diameter and length for your dog's size. This ensures comfort and effectiveness during training sessions.



### How Do Slip Leads Prevent Pulling?



Slip leads prevent pulling by using a loop system. When a dog pulls, the loop tightens gently around its neck. This discourages the behavior, promoting better leash manners. The pressure is released when the dog stops pulling, reinforcing positive behavior.



### Why Choose A Padded Handle Slip Lead?



A padded handle slip lead offers extra comfort for handlers. It reduces hand strain during long walks or training sessions. This feature is especially beneficial for strong pullers. The padding provides a better grip, ensuring more control and comfort.



### Are Reflective Slip Leads Beneficial For Night Walks?



Yes, reflective slip leads are highly beneficial for night walks. They enhance visibility in low-light conditions. This increases safety for both dogs and handlers. Reflective threads ensure that you and your dog are easily seen by passing vehicles.
