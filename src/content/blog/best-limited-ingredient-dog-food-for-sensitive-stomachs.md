Dogs with sensitive stomachs need simple, gentle diets that avoid common allergens and irritants. Limited ingredient dog foods contain fewer components, making it easier to identify and avoid triggers. Products like Blue Buffalo Basics and Natural Balance offer recipes with single protein sources and easy-to-digest ingredients.



Wet and dry options, such as salmon, turkey, chicken, and lamb, provide balanced nutrition without extra fillers. These foods help soothe upset stomachs, improve digestion, and support healthy skin and coat. Selecting the right limited ingredient diet can improve your dog’s quality of life by minimizing discomfort and promoting better nutrient absorption.
