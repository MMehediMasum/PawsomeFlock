## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Range Of These Remote Control Shock Collars?



Most collars offer a remote range between 3300 to 4200 feet. This long range allows effective training even in large outdoor areas. It ensures you maintain control and communicate commands clearly from a distance, ideal for various dog breeds and sizes.



### Are These Shock Collars Waterproof And Durable?



Yes, all featured shock collars are IPX7 waterproof rated. This means they withstand rain, splashes, and brief submersion. Their durable build supports outdoor use in various weather conditions, making them reliable for active dogs and different training environments.



### How Many Training Modes Do These Collars Offer?



These collars typically feature 3 to 4 modes, including beep, vibration, shock, and sometimes ultrasonic. Multiple modes allow customizable training suited to your dog’s temperament. This flexibility helps ensure humane and effective behavior correction.



### Can These Collars Be Used For Multiple Dogs?



Certain models support training two dogs simultaneously with one remote. This feature is perfect for owners with multiple pets. Separate controls help train each dog individually without interference, saving time and effort.



### Are The Collars Rechargeable And Easy To Maintain?



Yes, all collars come with rechargeable batteries. Charging is convenient via USB, reducing the need for frequent battery replacements. This ensures consistent performance and lowers maintenance effort for long-term use.



### What Dog Sizes Are Compatible With These Shock Collars?



These collars are suitable for dogs weighing between 5 to 150 pounds. Adjustable settings accommodate small, medium, and large breeds. This wide compatibility makes them versatile training tools for various dog sizes and ages.
