## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Relieve Dog Allergies In Medicated Shampoos?



Medicated shampoos often contain chlorhexidine, hydrocortisone, oatmeal, and antifungal agents. These ingredients soothe itching, reduce inflammation, and fight infections. They help alleviate symptoms of allergies, hot spots, and yeast infections effectively.



### How Often Should I Use Medicated Shampoo For My Allergic Dog?



Use medicated shampoo 1-3 times weekly or as directed by your vet. Frequent bathing helps control itching and skin irritation but avoid overuse to prevent dryness.



### Can Medicated Shampoos Prevent Dog Skin Infections From Allergies?



Yes, medicated shampoos reduce bacteria, fungi, and parasites causing infections. Regular use helps prevent flare-ups and promotes healthy skin and coat in allergic dogs.



### Are Medicated Shampoos Safe For Puppies And Cats With Allergies?



Many are formulated to be gentle and safe for puppies and cats. Always check product labels for age suitability and consult your vet before use.



### What Signs Show A Medicated Shampoo Is Working On My Dog?



Look for reduced redness, less scratching, and improved coat condition. Your dog should feel more comfortable with fewer allergy symptoms within a few uses.



### Can Medicated Shampoos Help With Hot Spots And Yeast Infections?



Yes, shampoos with antifungal and antibacterial properties effectively treat hot spots and yeast infections. They soothe irritated skin and promote faster healing.
