Many cages come with accessories such as perches and feeding trays to meet your bird’s needs. Selecting a cage that fits your space and your bird’s lifestyle supports their health and happiness. ## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Cage Is Ideal For A Cockatiel?



A cage at least 20 inches wide, 20 inches deep, and 24 inches tall suits cockatiels. Larger cages like Yaheetech’s 52 to 69-inch models offer ample space for flying and playing, promoting cockatiel health and happiness.



### Are Rolling Stands Beneficial For Bird Cages?



Yes, rolling stands provide easy mobility for cleaning and relocating cages. Many Yaheetech cages include detachable rolling stands, enhancing convenience without compromising stability or space for cockatiels.



### How Important Is Cage Material For Cockatiel Safety?



Material is crucial; wrought iron or steel cages like Yaheetech’s ensure durability and non-toxicity. These materials prevent rust and withstand chewing, keeping cockatiels safe and secure inside their environment.



### Do Open-top Cages Suit Cockatiels?



Open-top cages are great for interaction and climbing. Yaheetech’s open-top designs allow easy access for playtime and socializing, making them popular choices for cockatiel owners seeking engagement.



### What Features Improve Cockatiel Cage Comfort?



Features like spacious interiors, perches, playtops, and easy access doors enhance comfort. Yaheetech cages often include these, plus removable trays for easy cleaning, ensuring a cozy and hygienic home for your cockatiel.



### Can Large Cages Accommodate Multiple Cockatiels?



Yes, larger Yaheetech cages (52-69 inches) comfortably house multiple cockatiels. They provide enough space to reduce stress and territorial disputes, promoting a harmonious living environment for several birds.
