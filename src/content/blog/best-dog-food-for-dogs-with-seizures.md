## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Dogs With Seizures In Supplements?



Supplements with Ashwagandha, Valerian, Chamomile, and Turmeric support seizure control. L-Taurine and Milk Thistle aid nervous system health and detoxification, calming dogs naturally.



### Is Grain-free Dog Food Better For Seizure-prone Dogs?



Grain-free dog food reduces inflammation and allergens that may trigger seizures. High protein and low carb formulas support brain health and steady energy levels.



### How Does L-tryptophan Benefit Dogs With Epilepsy?



L-tryptophan boosts serotonin production, promoting calmness and reducing seizure-related anxiety. It helps regulate mood and nervous system function in epileptic dogs.



### Can Freeze-dried Raw Food Improve Seizure Management?



Freeze-dried raw food offers antioxidants, amino acids, and prebiotics that support brain health. It aids in reducing seizure frequency with natural nutrition.



### Are Natural Herbal Aids Safe For Seizure Control In Pets?



Natural herbs like Blue Vervain and Valerian are generally safe and calming. Always consult a vet before starting herbal seizure supplements for dogs or cats.



### What Role Does Protein Play In Seizure Dog Food?



High protein content supports brain function and muscle maintenance. It helps maintain healthy weight and energy in dogs prone to seizures.

Choosing the right food and supplements can help manage your dog’s seizures better. Natural ingredients like ashwagandha, valerian, and chamomile support calmness and nervous system health. High protein, grain-free foods with low carbs also help maintain your dog’s strength and energy.



Products like Wellness CORE, ORIJEN Senior, and Ketona offer good nutrition designed for special needs. Supplements such as Seizure Guardian and Seizure Support provide extra relief from twitching and anxiety. Always watch how your dog responds to new foods or supplements.
