## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Bird Cage Is Best For African Grey Parrots?



A cage at least 36 inches wide and 48 inches tall is ideal. Larger cages like the Yaheetech 69-inch provide ample space for movement and comfort.



### How Important Is A Rolling Stand For Large Bird Cages?



Rolling stands offer easy mobility for cleaning and relocation. Most large cages, such as the BOINN and ZENY models, include sturdy rolling stands for convenience.



### Are Play Tops Beneficial For Parrots In Cages?



Yes, play tops encourage exercise and mental stimulation. Many cages like the Yaheetech and VINGLI include play tops with ladders and perches.



### Which Cage Features Are Best For Small To Medium Parrots?



Look for cages with multiple feeding doors, secure locks, and adequate space. The Yaheetech 61-inch and YITAHOME 62-inch cages offer these features for small to medium birds.



### How Do Wrought Iron Cages Benefit Parrot Owners?



Wrought iron cages provide durability, chew resistance, and easy cleaning. Models like Yaheetech and HSM use wrought iron, ensuring long-lasting, safe habitats for parrots.



### What Cage Size Suits Multiple Parrot Species Like Conures And Cockatiels?



Extra-large cages, around 60 to 70 inches tall, accommodate multiple birds comfortably. The Yaheetech 63-inch and VINGLI 65-inch cages offer spacious designs for mixed species.
