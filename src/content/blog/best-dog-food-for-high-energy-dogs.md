## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Protein Levels Are Ideal For High Energy Dogs?



High energy dogs need dog food with at least 26% to 33% protein. This supports muscle repair and sustained energy. Products like VICTOR and Diamond Naturals offer protein-rich recipes ideal for active dogs and puppies.



### Are Grain-free Dog Foods Better For Active Dogs?



Grain-free dog foods can be beneficial for dogs with sensitivities. They often include high-quality proteins and fats. VICTOR’s grain-free options provide 33% protein, supporting energy without grains that may cause allergies.



### How Important Is Fat Content In High Energy Dog Food?



Fat is essential for energy in active dogs. Foods like VICTOR Realtree Edge contain around 22% fat, fueling endurance and muscle function. Higher fat content helps maintain stamina during prolonged activity.



### Can High Protein Dog Food Help With Muscle Building?



Yes, high protein dog foods support muscle growth and maintenance. Bully Max, for example, offers high protein and fat, aiding muscle gain and weight management in active dogs and puppies.



### What Ingredients Ensure A Dog Food Is Good For Active Dogs?



Look for real meat meals like beef, chicken, pork, or fish. Avoid fillers like peas or potatoes. Foods such as Diamond Performance use meat-first recipes with no legumes, ensuring quality protein and energy.



### Is Gluten-free Dog Food Beneficial For High Energy Dogs?



Gluten-free options reduce the risk of allergies and improve digestion. Many VICTOR products are gluten-free, combining high protein and fat to support active dogs’ energy and joint health.
