## Adjusting Portions For Different Life Stages

Feeding the right amount of Hill's Prescription Diet is important for your dog's health. Different life stages need different portions. Adjusting the food amount helps your dog stay healthy and strong.

Age, size, and activity level affect how much food your dog needs. It is best to follow guidelines and watch your dog's condition.

### Puppies And Growing Dogs

Puppies need more food per pound than adult dogs. Their bodies grow fast and need extra nutrition. Feed small meals often to support healthy growth.

Use the feeding chart on the dog food bag as a guide. Increase portions as your puppy grows but watch their weight closely.

* Feed 3 to 4 meals daily
* Adjust portions every few weeks
* Monitor weight and body condition

### Adult Dogs

Adult dogs need balanced portions to maintain their weight. Overfeeding can cause obesity, while underfeeding can lead to weight loss.

Feed according to your dog's weight and activity level. Divide the daily portion into two meals for better digestion.

* Feed 2 meals per day
* Adjust food based on activity and weight
* Regularly check your dog’s body condition

### Senior Dogs

Senior dogs often need fewer calories but more nutrients. Their metabolism slows down, and they may be less active.

Reduce portion size slightly and choose food that supports joint and organ health. Watch for changes in weight and appetite.

* Feed smaller, more frequent meals
* Use portions based on weight and health
* Consult your vet for special needs

## Monitoring Your Dog’s Weight And Health

Feeding your dog the right amount of Hill's Prescription Diet helps keep them healthy. Watch your dog’s weight and health closely. Adjust food as needed to avoid problems.

Regular checks can help you spot signs of overfeeding or underfeeding early. This keeps your dog happy and fit.

### Signs Of Overfeeding

Overfeeding can cause weight gain and health issues. Look for extra fat around the ribs and belly. Your dog may seem less active or tired.

* Weight increases quickly
* Fat pads appear over ribs or hips
* Dog seems lazy or less playful
* Breathing may become harder

### Signs Of Underfeeding

Underfeeding can lead to weight loss and low energy. Your dog may look thin with ribs showing. They might seem weak or less interested in activities.

* Weight drops noticeably
* Ribs and spine are easy to see
* Low energy and tiredness
* Less interest in food or play

### When To Consult Your Veterinarian

If you see signs of overfeeding or underfeeding, talk to your vet. Sudden changes in weight or health need professional advice. Your vet can adjust your dog’s diet safely.

Regular vet visits help keep your dog’s diet on track. They can check your dog’s health and suggest the right food amount.
