## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make The Best Hummingbird Feeders?



The best feeders have ant moats, bee guards, and multiple feeding ports. They are leak-proof, easy to clean, and durable. Glass or high-quality plastic materials ensure safety and longevity. These features attract hummingbirds while keeping pests away for a pleasant feeding experience.



### How Do Ant Moats Benefit Hummingbird Feeders?



Ant moats create a water barrier that prevents ants from reaching the nectar. This keeps the nectar clean and uncontaminated. Ant moats reduce maintenance and increase feeder efficiency. Most top-rated feeders include built-in ant moats for hassle-free outdoor use.



### Are Bee Guards Necessary On Hummingbird Feeders?



Yes, bee guards prevent bees and wasps from accessing the nectar. They protect hummingbirds by minimizing competition and stings. Bee guards also reduce nectar wastage and contamination. Many quality feeders come with built-in bee guards for safer bird feeding.



### What Size Hummingbird Feeder Is Ideal For A Garden?



A feeder between 16 to 32 ounces is ideal for most gardens. It holds enough nectar to attract many hummingbirds without frequent refills. Larger feeders suit areas with high bird traffic, while smaller feeders are easier to clean and refill.



### How Often Should I Clean My Hummingbird Feeder?



Clean feeders every 3 to 5 days in warm weather to prevent mold and bacteria. Use warm water and mild soap or a cleaning brush. Regular cleaning keeps nectar fresh and hummingbirds healthy. Some feeders come with cleaning brushes for convenience.



### Can Glass Feeders Offer Better Bird Safety Than Plastic?



Yes, glass feeders are non-toxic and easier to clean thoroughly. They resist scratches that harbor bacteria. Glass feeders often have a classic look that enhances garden decor. However, durable plastic feeders provide lightweight, shatter-resistant alternatives.
