## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Hummingbird Food For Feeders?



The best hummingbird food is a 4:1 water-to-sugar ratio nectar. Ready-to-use options like Kaytee ElectroNectar are convenient and safe. Avoid dyes and preservatives to keep hummingbirds healthy. Natural formulas like Hummer’s Galore are also highly recommended.



### How Do I Prepare Hummingbird Nectar At Home?



Mix four parts water with one part white granulated sugar. Boil to dissolve sugar and kill bacteria. Let it cool before filling feeders. Avoid honey or artificial sweeteners as they harm hummingbirds. Ready-to-mix packets like Hummer’s Galore simplify this process.



### How Long Does Homemade Hummingbird Nectar Last?



Homemade nectar lasts 3 to 5 days in warm weather. In cooler temperatures, it can last up to a week. Replace nectar frequently to prevent mold and fermentation. Store feeders in the shade to extend nectar freshness.



### Are Colored Hummingbird Nectars Safe For Birds?



Colored nectars with red dyes can be harmful to hummingbirds. Choose clear, dye-free nectars like Perky-Pet’s clear concentrate. Natural nectar attracts birds without risking their health. Avoid red food coloring to ensure safe feeding.



### Can I Use Store-bought Hummingbird Nectar?



Yes, store-bought nectars like Perky-Pet and Kaytee are convenient and safe. Ensure they contain no preservatives or dyes. They provide the correct sugar concentration and are ready to use. These options save time and attract more hummingbirds.
