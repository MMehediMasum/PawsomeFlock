## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Litter Box Features Prevent Cats From Peeing Outside?



High sides, splash guards, and odor control filters help contain litter and urine. Products like YOILES splash guards and Nature’s Miracle hooded boxes reduce mess and discourage outside peeing by offering privacy and cleanliness.



### How Does Cat Litter Attractant Aid Litter Training?



Attractants like Fresh Step training powder encourage cats to use the litter box. They contain natural scents that draw cats in, helping kittens and adults develop good habits and avoid peeing outside the box.



### Which Litter Controls Odor Effectively For Outdoor Pee Issues?



Rocco & Roxie odor eliminators and Dr. Elsey’s clumping litter offer superior urine odor control. These products neutralize smells naturally and keep litter boxes fresh, reducing the chance cats avoid the box due to bad odors.



### Are High-sided Litter Boxes Better For Cats Who Pee Outside?



Yes, high-sided boxes like Amazon Basics or IRIS USA models prevent urine splashes. They contain messes and provide cats with a secure space, making them ideal for cats prone to peeing outside the box.



### Can Scooping Tools Improve Litter Box Cleanliness?



Absolutely, scooping tools like Arm & Hammer’s sifting scoops help remove waste quickly. Keeping the box clean encourages cats to use it consistently, reducing accidents outside the box.



### What Litter Types Suit Multi-cat Homes With Pee Problems?



Non-clumping litters like Purina Tidy Cats Breeze XL manage odor and moisture well. Combined with large, easy-clean boxes, they keep multi-cat homes fresher and discourage cats from peeing outside the box.
