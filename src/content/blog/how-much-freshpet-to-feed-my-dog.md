## Factors Influencing Freshpet Portions

Feeding your dog the right amount of Freshpet food is important for their health. Different dogs need different portion sizes. Understanding the factors that influence these portions helps you feed your dog correctly.

This guide covers key factors that affect how much Freshpet food your dog should eat every day.

### Dog's Weight And Size

The size and weight of your dog are the main reasons for portion differences. Bigger dogs need more food to fuel their bodies. Smaller dogs need less food to stay healthy.

Use your dog’s weight to estimate the right Freshpet portion. Overfeeding can cause weight gain, and underfeeding can lead to malnutrition.

* Small dogs (under 20 lbs) usually need less than one cup per day
* Medium dogs (20-50 lbs) need around one to two cups per day
* Large dogs (over 50 lbs) may need two cups or more per day

### Activity Level

Active dogs burn more energy and need larger portions to stay strong. Dogs that exercise daily or work outside need more calories. Less active dogs need fewer calories to avoid weight gain.

Adjust the amount of Freshpet food based on how much your dog moves each day.

* High activity dogs need 20-30% more food
* Moderate activity dogs need standard portions
* Low activity dogs may need smaller portions

### Age And Health

Age affects how much your dog should eat. Puppies need more food for growth. Senior dogs often need fewer calories because they are less active. Health problems can also change food needs.

Always check with your vet if your dog has health issues. They can help set the right Freshpet portion size.

* Puppies need frequent, smaller meals with more calories
* Adult dogs need balanced portions for maintenance
* Senior dogs may require smaller or special diet portions

## Common Feeding Mistakes

### Overfeeding Risks

Giving your dog too much Freshpet can cause weight gain. Overfeeding can lead to obesity, which puts stress on joints and organs. It may also cause digestive problems.

Overfeeding can make your dog feel tired and less active. This can reduce their quality of life and increase health risks.

* Obesity and related health problems
* Stomach upset and diarrhea
* Lower energy and activity levels
* Increased risk of diabetes and heart issues

### Ignoring Dog's Signals

Dogs give signs when they are full or still hungry. Ignoring these signals can cause feeding problems. Some dogs may eat quickly and want more food even if they are full.

Watch your dog's body language and behavior during meals. Stop feeding if your dog turns away, slows down, or leaves food behind.

* Look for signs like pushing food away
* Notice if your dog seems bored or distracted
* Feed smaller portions if your dog leaves food
* Adjust feeding amounts based on activity and weight
