## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why Does My Dog Choose To Sleep On My Shoes?

Dogs sleep on shoes because they smell like their owner. The scent provides comfort, security, and a sense of closeness. Shoes carry your unique smell, which calms your dog and helps reduce anxiety when you are not around.

### Is It Normal For Dogs To Sleep On Shoes?

Yes, it is normal behavior. Dogs use scent to bond with their humans. Sleeping on shoes helps them feel safe and connected. This habit shows affection and trust toward you.

### Can Sleeping On Shoes Harm My Dog?

Generally, sleeping on shoes is harmless. However, shoes can carry dirt, chemicals, or bacteria. Ensure your shoes are clean to avoid any potential health risks to your dog.

### How Can I Discourage My Dog From Sleeping On Shoes?

Provide a cozy bed with your scent to attract your dog. Use positive reinforcement when they use their bed. Keep shoes out of reach or stored in closed spaces to reduce temptation.
