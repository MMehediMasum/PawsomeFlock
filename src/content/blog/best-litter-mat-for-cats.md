## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Litter Mat Is Best For Small Spaces?



A compact litter mat around 23. 6"x14. 6" works well in small spaces. It fits easily and prevents litter scatter effectively.



### How Do Double-layer Litter Mats Trap Cat Litter?



Double-layer mats use a honeycomb design to catch litter between layers. This prevents mess and keeps floors clean longer.



### Are Waterproof Litter Mats Necessary For Indoor Cats?



Yes, waterproof mats protect floors from urine and spills. They are easy to clean and maintain hygiene.



### Which Litter Mats Are Easiest To Clean?



Mats like Pieviev and Gorilla Grip are washable and quick to clean. Their materials resist stains and odors.



### Do Large Litter Mats Reduce Floor Mess Better?



Large mats, such as 35"x24" or bigger, cover more area. They trap more litter and keep floors cleaner.



### Are Soft Litter Mats Comfortable For Cats’ Paws?



Yes, mats like Drymate and Pieviev Chenille are soft and gentle. They encourage cats to use the litter box comfortably.
