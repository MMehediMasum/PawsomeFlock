## Feeding Guidelines And Tips

Feeding your dog Purina True Instinct properly ensures they get the full benefit of this high-quality food. Understanding the right portion sizes and how to switch your dog’s diet smoothly can make a big difference in their health and happiness. Let’s look closely at practical tips that help you feed True Instinct confidently.

### Portion Sizes

Finding the correct portion size depends on your dog's weight, age, and activity level. Purina provides feeding charts on their packaging, but remember, these are starting points—not strict rules.

If your dog is very active, they may need slightly more food. Less active dogs or those prone to weight gain might require smaller portions. Keep an eye on their body condition and adjust portions accordingly.

| Dog Weight (lbs) | Daily Portion (cups) |
| --- | --- |
| 10 - 20 | 1 - 1.5 |
| 20 - 40 | 1.5 - 2.5 |
| 40 - 60 | 2.5 - 3.5 |
| 60+ | 3.5+ |

Are you measuring your dog’s food each time? Using a standard measuring cup helps maintain consistency and prevent overfeeding.

### Transitioning To True Instinct

Switching your dog’s food too quickly can cause upset stomachs and digestive issues. A gradual transition over 7-10 days is best.

Start by mixing 25% Purina True Instinct with 75% of the old food. Every few days, increase the True Instinct portion while reducing the old food. By day 7 or 10, your dog should be eating only True Instinct.

Have you noticed any changes in your dog’s stool or energy during the switch? These signs help you know if the transition is going well or if it needs to slow down.

Keeping fresh water available and feeding at regular times also supports digestion during the change. Your dog will thank you with better digestion and more energy.
