## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Millet Type For Budgies?



Spray millet is the best type for budgies. It is natural, sun-dried, and highly palatable. Budgies love nibbling on millet sprays, making it a perfect treat and supplement for their diet.



### How Often Should I Feed Millet To My Budgie?



Feed millet as an occasional treat, about once or twice a week. It provides extra nutrients but should not replace a balanced diet. Overfeeding millet can cause obesity and nutritional imbalance.



### Are Millet Sprays Safe For All Pet Birds?



Yes, millet sprays are safe for budgies, canaries, finches, cockatiels, and lovebirds. They are natural, non-GMO, and free from harmful additives. Always offer in moderation as a healthy snack.



### Can Millet Improve My Budgie’s Health?



Millet is rich in carbohydrates and fiber, supporting energy and digestion. It also encourages natural foraging behavior. Including millet in your budgie’s diet promotes mental stimulation and overall well-being.



### What Is The Nutritional Value Of Millet For Budgies?



Millet provides essential carbohydrates, fiber, and trace minerals. It is low in fat and easy to digest. These nutrients help maintain your budgie’s energy levels and digestive health.



### How Should I Store Millet To Keep It Fresh?



Store millet in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Avoid moisture and direct sunlight to maintain freshness. Proper storage prevents mold and keeps millet tasty for your budgie.
