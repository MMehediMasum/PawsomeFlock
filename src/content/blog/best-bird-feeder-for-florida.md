## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Ideal For Florida's Climate?



An ideal Florida bird feeder is weatherproof and rust-resistant. It withstands heat, humidity, and rain while attracting local wild birds. Metal and durable plastic feeders are great choices for longevity and easy cleaning in Florida’s varied weather.



### How Do Squirrel-proof Bird Feeders Work In Florida?



Squirrel-proof feeders use locking lids or weight-activated perches to block squirrels. These features prevent seed theft, ensuring birds get food. Many Florida feeders have chew-proof metal cages to resist persistent squirrels and protect birdseed effectively.



### Which Bird Feeders Attract Florida’s Native Wild Birds?



Feeders with sunflower seeds, suet cages, or cherry-flavored food attract cardinals, finches, and chickadees. Designs like tray, tube, or double suet cages are popular. Large capacity feeders are preferred for frequent refills in Florida’s active bird communities.



### How Often Should Florida Bird Feeders Be Cleaned?



Clean feeders every two weeks to prevent mold and bacteria. Frequent cleaning keeps birds healthy and food fresh. Use mild soap and water, rinse well, and dry before refilling to maintain hygiene in Florida’s warm climate.



### What Seed Types Work Best In Florida Bird Feeders?



Sunflower seeds, safflower, and Wagner's cherry-flavored wild bird food attract many species. These seeds resist mold in humid Florida and appeal to cardinals, finches, and chickadees. Using quality seed ensures frequent bird visits and feeder success.
