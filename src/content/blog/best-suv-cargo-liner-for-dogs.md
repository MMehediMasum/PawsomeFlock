## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Cargo Liner Dog-friendly?



A dog-friendly cargo liner should be waterproof, non-slip, and scratchproof. It should have bumper flap protectors to prevent damage. Universal fit is essential for easy installation in SUVs, sedans, and vans. Features like side flaps and easy cleaning options enhance its suitability for pet owners.



### How Do I Install A Suv Cargo Liner?



Installing a cargo liner is straightforward. Spread it out in your vehicle's trunk. Ensure the bumper flap protector is positioned correctly. Use any provided straps or fasteners to secure it in place. Adjust it to cover all necessary areas, and ensure it's non-slip for safety.



### Are Cargo Liners Waterproof?



Yes, most cargo liners are waterproof. They prevent moisture from seeping through, protecting your vehicle's interior. This feature is crucial for pet owners, as it shields the car from spills, wet paws, and accidents. Always check product specifications to confirm waterproof capabilities.



### How Do You Clean A Cargo Liner?



Cleaning a cargo liner is simple. Remove it from your vehicle and shake off loose dirt. Use a damp cloth or sponge with mild detergent for stains. Rinse with water and let it air dry. Some liners are machine washable; check the product label for instructions.



### Do Cargo Liners Fit All Suvs?



Most cargo liners offer universal fit, suitable for various SUVs, sedans, and vans. They are designed to cover large trunk areas. However, always measure your vehicle's cargo space and compare it with the liner's dimensions to ensure a perfect fit.



Adjustable straps enhance compatibility.
