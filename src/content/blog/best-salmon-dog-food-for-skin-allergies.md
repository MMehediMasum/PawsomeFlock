## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Salmon Dog Food Good For Skin Allergies?



Salmon is rich in omega-3 fatty acids that reduce inflammation. These nutrients soothe itchy, irritated skin and improve coat health. Salmon-based dog foods often avoid common allergens like chicken or beef, making them ideal for sensitive dogs with skin allergies.



### Are Grain-free Salmon Dog Foods Better For Allergy-prone Dogs?



Grain-free salmon dog foods can help reduce allergic reactions in sensitive dogs. Grains sometimes trigger skin problems, so avoiding them helps. Many salmon formulas also include limited ingredients, which simplifies digestion and supports skin and stomach health.
