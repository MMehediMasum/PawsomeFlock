The ideal pole height for Bird Buddy feeders ranges from 74 to 109 inches. This height keeps feeders safe and visible for birds. Adjustable poles like Smart Bird Feeder Pole offer flexibility for different garden setups and bird species.

Squirrel baffles prevent squirrels from reaching the feeders by creating a physical barrier. Poles with squirrel-proof baffles, such as the Flanagan 92 Inch Bird House Pole, help keep feeders safe. This ensures birds get the food without interference from pests.

Bases with 5 prongs, such as the Bird Feeder Pole Kit, offer enhanced stability. They anchor the pole firmly in the ground, reducing tipping risk. This is crucial for supporting heavy feeders and deterring squirrels.
