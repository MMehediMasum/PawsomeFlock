## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Litter Box Is Best For Large Cats?



A large litter box with at least 65L capacity suits big cats. It offers ample space for comfort and reduces mess. Models like the 2026 Self Cleaning Litter Box provide extra-large trays ideal for multiple large cats.



### How Does App Control Improve Litter Box Use?



App control allows remote monitoring and cleaning scheduling. It helps track usage, adjust settings, and receive alerts. This makes maintenance easier and keeps the litter box clean for multiple cats, enhancing convenience and hygiene.



### Are Self-cleaning Litter Boxes Safe For Multiple Cats?



Yes, modern self-cleaning litter boxes have safety sensors and dual protection features. These prevent accidents during automatic cleaning and handle waste efficiently for multiple cats without odor or mess.



### How Effective Is Odor Control In These Litter Boxes?



Advanced models use negative ion sterilization and deodorization. This technology neutralizes odors quickly, keeping the area fresh. It’s especially useful in homes with multiple large cats to maintain a clean environment.



### What Maintenance Is Required For Self-cleaning Litter Boxes?



Maintenance involves refilling litter, emptying waste bins, and occasional cleaning. Most models include removable liners and trash bags for easy disposal. Regular upkeep ensures long-lasting performance and hygiene.



### Can Self-cleaning Litter Boxes Track Cat Health?



Some litter boxes feature weight tracking and health monitoring. They detect changes in cat behavior or weight via app alerts. This helps owners monitor multiple cats’ health conveniently and proactively.
