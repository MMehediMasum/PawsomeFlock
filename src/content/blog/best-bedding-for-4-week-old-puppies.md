## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Bedding Size For 4 Week Old Puppies?



For 4 week old puppies, small to medium-sized bedding works best. Sizes like 13x19 or 23x15 inches offer comfort and security. These dimensions fit well in crates or pens, providing ample space without overwhelming the puppy’s small size.



### Which Material Is Safest For Puppy Bedding?



Soft, breathable materials like fleece and flannel are safest for puppies. They provide warmth and comfort while being gentle on sensitive skin. Look for washable, hypoallergenic fabrics to maintain hygiene and prevent irritation or allergies.



### How Often Should Puppy Bedding Be Washed?



Puppy bedding should be washed at least once a week. Frequent cleaning prevents odors, bacteria, and allergens. Use pet-safe detergents and avoid harsh chemicals to keep the bedding safe and fresh for your puppy.



### Are Waterproof Or Pee-proof Pads Necessary For Puppies?



Yes, waterproof or pee-proof pads are essential for young puppies. They protect bedding and furniture from accidents. Reusable, washable pads like dog urine pads improve hygiene and simplify cleanup during house training.



### Can Puppy Bedding Double As A Teething Toy?



Some soft blankets, like fleece ones, can be used for teething. They provide comfort and distraction but should be supervised. Avoid bedding with loose threads or small parts that puppies could swallow.



### What Bedding Colors Or Prints Appeal To Puppies?



Bright, vivid colors and playful paw prints are popular for puppy bedding. These designs stimulate puppies visually and add a cute, cozy vibe to their sleeping area. Choose reversible options for variety and style.
