## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Good Nyjer Seed Bird Feeder?



A good Nyjer seed feeder is durable, squirrel-proof, and has multiple feeding ports. It should allow easy refilling and cleaning. Metal mesh designs with weatherproof roofs protect seeds and attract finches effectively. Features like built-in perches enhance bird comfort and feeding experience.



### How To Prevent Squirrels From Stealing Nyjer Seeds?



Choose feeders with chew-proof metal construction and squirrel-proof designs. Features like weighted perches or cages help deter squirrels. Hanging feeders away from branches reduce access. Using baffles or squirrel guards also protects seeds efficiently, ensuring finches get uninterrupted feeding.



### How Much Nyjer Seed Does A Typical Feeder Hold?



Capacity varies, commonly between 2 to 10 pounds. Most tube feeders hold around 2 to 2. 5 pounds, ideal for small flocks. Larger feeders with weatherproof trays can store up to 10 pounds. Choose capacity based on bird activity and refilling convenience.



### How To Clean And Maintain Nyjer Seed Feeders?



Regularly clean feeders with warm soapy water to prevent mold and bacteria. Rinse thoroughly and dry before refilling. Remove old seeds and inspect for damage. Metal mesh feeders should be checked for rust or chew marks to ensure longevity and bird safety.



### Why Choose Metal Mesh Feeders For Nyjer Seeds?



Metal mesh feeders provide excellent ventilation, preventing seed spoilage. They are chew-proof and durable against squirrels and weather. Mesh size suits Nyjer seeds, allowing easy access for finches. The sturdy design supports multiple feeding ports and perches for bird comfort.
