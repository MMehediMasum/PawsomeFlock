Wet foods like Tiki Cat Aloha Friends and Blue Buffalo True Solutions provide high moisture. This helps keep cats hydrated and eases constipation.



### Can Pumpkin In Cat Food Relieve Constipation?



Yes, pumpkin is rich in fiber and moisture. It softens stool and promotes healthy digestion, effectively relieving constipation in cats.
