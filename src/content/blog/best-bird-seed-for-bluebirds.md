Some blends include fruits like berries to add flavor and vitamins. Using the right bird seed supports bluebirds’ health and encourages them to visit often. This guide covers top seed options like Wagner’s Farmer’s Delight and Kaytee’s mealworms to help you choose the best food for bluebirds.
