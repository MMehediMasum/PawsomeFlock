## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Calm Cats In These Chews?



These chews contain calming ingredients like melatonin, ashwagandha, GABA, hemp, pheromones, and chamomile. They reduce anxiety and stress naturally. Some also include glucosamine for joint support. These blends promote relaxation and reduce destructive behavior in cats during travel or vet visits.



### Are These Calming Chews Safe For All Cats?



Yes, most calming chews are safe for adult cats when used as directed. However, consult a vet before use, especially for pregnant, nursing, or medicated cats. Always follow dosage instructions to avoid side effects and ensure your cat’s health and safety.



### How Long Do Calming Chews Take To Work?



Calming chews typically take 30 minutes to 2 hours to show effects. Timing varies by ingredient and cat’s metabolism. For best results, give the chews before stressful events like travel, grooming, or vet visits. Consistent use may improve overall anxiety management.



### Can Calming Chews Help With Separation Anxiety?



Yes, calming chews containing pheromones, melatonin, or herbal extracts can reduce separation anxiety in cats. They help soothe nervousness and prevent destructive behaviors when cats are alone. Pair chews with environmental enrichment for best calming effects.



### Do Calming Chews Have Side Effects For Cats?



Side effects are rare but can include mild drowsiness, digestive upset, or allergic reactions. Always monitor your cat after giving chews. Discontinue use and consult a vet if adverse symptoms appear. Proper dosage minimizes risks and promotes safe anxiety relief.



### Which Calming Chews Are Best For Travel And Vet Visits?



Chews with melatonin, hemp, or ashwagandha are ideal for travel and vet visits. Products like Rocco & Roxie and Sentry Calming Chews offer fast-acting relief. These treats help reduce stress, making handling and examinations easier for both cat and owner.
