## Feeding Tips

Feeding your dog treats like Wiggles And Wags should be done thoughtfully to keep your furry friend happy and healthy. Knowing how much to give and how to introduce these treats can make a big difference in your dog's digestion and overall well-being.

### Serving Size Guidelines

Start by checking the package for recommended serving sizes based on your dog’s weight. Overfeeding treats can lead to unwanted weight gain or upset stomachs.

For example, if your dog weighs 20 pounds, stick to the suggested number of treats per day rather than guessing. Treats should never replace a balanced meal but act as occasional rewards.

* Keep treats under 10% of your dog’s daily calorie intake.
* Break larger treats into smaller pieces for training or frequent rewards.
* Always have fresh water available after treat time.

### Introducing New Treats

Introduce Wiggles And Wags treats gradually to see how your dog reacts. Sudden changes in diet can cause digestive issues.

Start by offering a small piece and observe for any signs of allergies or discomfort. Watch for itching, vomiting, or diarrhea over the next 24 hours.

If everything looks good, slowly increase the amount over a week. This helps your dog’s system adjust and ensures the treats are well tolerated.

Have you noticed how some dogs prefer certain flavors? Offering a variety can keep treat time exciting and help you spot what your dog enjoys most.
