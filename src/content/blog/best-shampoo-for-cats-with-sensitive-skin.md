## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Are Safe For Cats With Sensitive Skin?



Safe ingredients include oatmeal, shea butter, aloe vera, and fragrance-free formulas. Avoid harsh chemicals and artificial dyes to prevent irritation.



### How Often Should I Bathe A Cat With Sensitive Skin?



Bathing once every 3 to 4 weeks is ideal. Overbathing can dry out sensitive skin and cause more itching.



### Can Hypoallergenic Shampoos Relieve Cat Itching And Dryness?



Yes, hypoallergenic shampoos soothe irritation and moisturize dry skin. They reduce itching without causing allergic reactions or discomfort.



### Are Medicated Shampoos Safe For Cats With Allergies?



Medicated shampoos are safe when used as directed by a vet. They target infections, itching, and skin conditions effectively.



### What Makes Burt’s Bees Shampoo Good For Sensitive Cats?



It contains shea butter and honey that gently moisturize and soothe dry, itchy skin. It’s hypoallergenic and cruelty-free.



### Can Flea Shampoos Be Used On Sensitive Skin Cats?



Yes, but only gentle formulas like Adams Plus designed for sensitive skin. Always follow product instructions and vet advice.
