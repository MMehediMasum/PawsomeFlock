## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Squirrel-proof?



Squirrel-proof bird feeders use weight-activated perches or cages to block squirrels. They ensure only birds access the seeds. This prevents seed waste and damage caused by squirrels, protecting your bird feed and attracting more birds.



### How Does Weight-activated Perch Technology Work?



Weight-activated perches close feeding ports when heavy animals, like squirrels, land on them. Birds are lighter and keep access open. This smart design keeps squirrels away while allowing birds like finches and cardinals to feed safely.



### Which Seed Capacity Is Ideal For Anti-squirrel Feeders?



Seed capacity between 3 to 5 pounds is ideal. It balances frequent refills with enough supply for various bird species. Larger capacity feeders reduce maintenance while keeping seeds fresh and protected from squirrels.



### Are Metal Mesh Feeders More Durable Against Squirrels?



Yes, metal mesh feeders offer strong protection against squirrel damage. They resist chewing and scratching. Metal feeders last longer outdoors and provide excellent ventilation, helping keep seeds dry and fresh.



### Can Collapsible Perches Improve Squirrel-proof Effectiveness?



Collapsible or flipper perches tip or close when squirrels land. This design discourages squirrels from feeding and jumping on feeders. It’s a clever, mechanical way to keep squirrels off without harming birds.



### How Do Gravity Protection Feeders Stop Squirrels?



Gravity protection feeders close feeding ports when weight triggers a mechanism. Squirrels are too heavy, so ports close. Birds remain unaffected due to their light weight, ensuring seed access while preventing squirrel theft.
