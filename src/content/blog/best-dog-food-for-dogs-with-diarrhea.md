## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Dog Food Is Best For Dogs With Diarrhea?



Hill's Science Diet Sensitive Stomach & Skin is ideal for dogs with diarrhea. It supports digestion and soothes the stomach with gentle ingredients. This food helps restore digestive balance and improve stool consistency quickly.



### Are Bland Diets Effective For Dogs With Diarrhea?



Yes, bland diets like chicken and rice are gentle on the stomach. Products like KOHA Limited Ingredient Bland Diet help ease digestion and reduce irritation in dogs with diarrhea. These diets are easy to digest and promote recovery.



### Can Pumpkin Supplements Help Dogs With Diarrhea?



Pumpkin supplements, such as Fruitables Pumpkin Digestive Supplement, provide natural fiber. This fiber supports healthy digestion and helps regulate bowel movements. Pumpkin is a safe, gentle remedy for mild diarrhea in dogs.



### Should I Use Probiotics For My Dog's Diarrhea?



Probiotics like Purina Pro Plan FortiFlora help restore healthy gut bacteria. They improve digestion and strengthen the immune system. Probiotics are recommended alongside a bland diet for better diarrhea management.



### Is Wet Or Dry Dog Food Better For Diarrhea?



Wet dog food like Blue Buffalo True Solutions Digestive Care is easier to digest and hydrates dogs. Dry foods like Hill’s Perfect Digestion support digestion but may be harder to digest when diarrhea is present. Wet food is often preferred during diarrhea.



### How Long Should I Feed Special Food For Diarrhea?



Feed sensitive or bland diets for 3 to 5 days or until stools normalize. If diarrhea persists beyond one week, consult your veterinarian for further evaluation and treatment.
