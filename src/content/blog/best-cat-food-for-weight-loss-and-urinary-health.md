Some brands like IAMS and Purina ONE offer urinary health formulas without prescriptions. However, veterinary diets like Blue Buffalo Natural Veterinary require a prescription for safety and proper health management.



### Which Ingredients Support Weight Control In Cat Food?



High protein, low fat, and fiber-rich ingredients aid weight control. Ingredients like chicken, turkey, and natural fibers promote fullness and muscle maintenance while reducing calorie intake in cat foods.



### How Does Wet Food Benefit Cats With Urinary Issues?



Wet food increases hydration, diluting urine and reducing urinary crystals. Brands like Hill’s Science Diet wet formulas help maintain urinary tract health and support weight management through moisture-rich meals.



### Is High Protein Important For Weight Management Cat Food?



Yes, high protein supports muscle mass while reducing fat. Purina ONE High Protein formulas help cats burn fat and stay lean, making protein essential in weight control diets.
