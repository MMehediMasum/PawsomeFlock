Some nets include features like drawstrings and zippers for easy use and secure coverage. Using durable, heavy-duty materials ensures protection against strong birds and pests. Installing netting also helps reduce the need for harmful chemicals, promoting a natural garden. This guide covers top bird netting options that combine protection, ease, and value, helping you choose the best netting for your fruit trees.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Netting Is Best For Fruit Trees?



A 10x10 ft or larger netting size suits most fruit trees. It covers branches fully and prevents bird access. Larger nets like 25x25 ft work well for bigger trees or multiple plants. Choose based on tree size and garden space for effective protection.



### How Does Drawstring And Zipper Improve Bird Netting?



Drawstrings and zippers offer easy installation and secure closure. They prevent birds and insects from entering through openings. Zippers allow quick access for harvesting or pruning. Drawstrings tighten the net around trunks for a snug fit. These features enhance usability and protection.



### Which Mesh Size Is Ideal To Keep Out Pests?



A 1/2" to 3/4" mesh size effectively blocks birds, insects, and small animals. Smaller mesh stops cicadas, bugs, and squirrels. Larger mesh may let tiny pests through. Choose mesh size based on the pest type threatening your fruit trees.



### Can Bird Netting Protect Against Deer And Squirrels?



Yes, heavy-duty netting with strong mesh can deter deer and squirrels. Netting like Feitore’s 7x100 ft is reusable and designed for multiple animals. Secure installation with ties or staples improves protection. Proper netting reduces damage and fruit loss from these animals.



### Are Fruit Tree Netting Bags Reusable And Durable?



Most fruit tree netting bags are made from nylon or strong mesh for durability. Many come with reinforced edges and weather-resistant features. Proper care and storage extend lifespan. Reusable bags provide cost-effective, long-term garden protection from pests and birds.



### How To Install Bird Netting For Fruit Trees Effectively?



Install netting by draping it over trees, securing edges with drawstrings or staples. Use zippers for easy access points. Ensure no gaps remain for pests to enter. Proper tension prevents sagging and damage. Follow product instructions for best results.
