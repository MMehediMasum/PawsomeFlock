## Tips For Feeding Fancy Feast

Feeding Fancy Feast wet food to your cat can be a rewarding experience when done right. ### Portion Control

Overfeeding is a common mistake that can lead to weight gain and health problems in cats. Check the feeding guidelines on the Fancy Feast package, but remember these are general recommendations. Your cat’s activity level, age, and health may require adjustments.

Try dividing the daily amount into smaller meals throughout the day. This keeps your cat satisfied without overeating. Have you noticed if your cat finishes the food or leaves some behind? That’s a good sign to tweak portions.

### Transitioning Foods

Switching your cat to Fancy Feast wet food should be done gradually to avoid digestive upset. Start by mixing a small amount of Fancy Feast with their current food. Slowly increase the Fancy Feast portion over 7-10 days.

Watch your cat closely during this period. Any signs of vomiting or diarrhea mean you may need to slow down the transition. Your patience here helps your cat accept new flavors and textures without stress.

### Storage And Freshness

Fancy Feast wet food tastes best and stays safest when stored properly. Once opened, cover the can with plastic wrap or use a sealed container. Refrigerate it immediately and use within 2-3 days.

Always check the food before serving. If it smells off or looks different, don’t risk your cat’s health. Keeping food fresh not only protects your pet but also maintains the flavor they love.
