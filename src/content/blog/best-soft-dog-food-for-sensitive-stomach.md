## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Dogs With Sensitive Stomachs?



Ingredients like real chicken, pumpkin, salmon, and rice soothe sensitive stomachs. These foods are gentle and easy to digest, reducing discomfort and promoting better digestion in dogs with sensitive stomachs.



### Which Soft Dog Food Is Best For Sensitive Skin?



Purina Pro Plan and Hill's Science Diet formulas support sensitive skin and stomachs. They contain limited ingredients and essential nutrients to reduce irritation and improve skin health.



### Are Grain-free Wet Dog Foods Good For Sensitive Stomachs?



Yes, grain-free wet dog foods like Blue Buffalo Basics are ideal. They reduce allergens and digestive issues, making them suitable for dogs with sensitive stomachs and skin.



### How Does Pumpkin Benefit Dogs With Digestion Problems?



Pumpkin is rich in fiber and helps regulate digestion. It can relieve both constipation and diarrhea, making it a common ingredient in sensitive stomach dog foods.



### Can Small Breed Dogs Benefit From Sensitive Stomach Formulas?



Absolutely, Hill's Science Diet offers small breed options. These foods are tailored to smaller dogs’ needs, supporting digestion and skin health effectively.



### How Often Should I Feed Soft Dog Food For Sensitive Stomachs?



Feed sensitive stomach dog food twice daily or as recommended by your vet. Consistent feeding helps maintain digestive balance and reduces stomach upset.
