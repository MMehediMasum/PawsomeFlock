## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Cat Carrier Fits Two Cats Comfortably?



A carrier around 24"x16"x16" offers ample space for two cats. It allows room to move and rest during travel. Models like PEGIC Extra Large or PetMasion XL fit medium to large cats well.



### Are Soft-sided Or Hard-sided Carriers Better For Two Cats?



Soft-sided carriers provide flexibility and comfort, ideal for calm cats. Hard-sided carriers offer more protection and structure, good for active cats. Choose based on your cats’ temperament and travel needs.



### Can I Use These Carriers For Air Travel With Two Cats?



Many carriers listed, like Oeko-TEX TSA Approved bags, meet airline regulations. Check individual airline rules before flying. Soft-sided and collapsible designs often comply with cabin size limits.



### How Do Double Compartment Carriers Benefit Two Cats?



Double compartments keep cats separated, reducing stress and preventing fights. Groxkox Double Cat Carrier offers zippered compartments for safe, comfortable travel. It also helps manage litter and food separately.



### Are These Carriers Suitable For Outdoor Travel And Hiking?



Yes, carriers like Groxkox Double Carrier and PEGIC are designed for outdoor use. They offer ventilation, sturdy construction, and portability. Perfect for picnics, hikes, and road trips with two cats.



### What Weight Limit Should I Consider For A Two-cat Carrier?



Choose a carrier supporting at least 20-24 lbs for two average cats. Models like Bejibear and PetMasion support up to 20-24 lbs combined. Always check the product weight capacity before buying.
