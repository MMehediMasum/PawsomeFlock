## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Litter Box Ideal For High Peeing Cats?



A litter box with high sides prevents urine splashes and leaks. It also offers ample space for big cats. Materials like stainless steel ensure durability and easy cleaning. Enclosed designs control odors effectively, keeping your home fresh.



### How Does Stainless Steel Benefit Cat Litter Boxes?



Stainless steel is durable, rust-resistant, and easy to clean. It prevents urine absorption, reducing odors and bacteria buildup. This material is perfect for high peeing cats as it handles messes efficiently and maintains hygiene longer than plastic alternatives.



### Why Choose An Enclosed Litter Box For Big Cats?



Enclosed litter boxes contain odors and messes better. They provide privacy and reduce litter scatter. High-sided enclosures prevent urine leaks and splashes. They are especially useful for large or multiple cats needing extra space.



### Are High-sided Litter Boxes Better For Multiple Cats?



Yes, high-sided boxes minimize urine spills and litter scatter. They provide ample room for multiple cats to use comfortably. These boxes help maintain cleanliness and reduce odor, making them suitable for multi-cat households.



### What Accessories Improve Litter Box Cleanliness For Big Cats?



Accessories like litter mats, scoops, and deodorizer filters enhance cleanliness. Mats catch stray litter, reducing mess. Scoops simplify waste removal. Charcoal filters control odors efficiently, keeping the litter area fresh and hygienic.



### How Do Lids Help In Managing Cat Litter Odor?



Lids trap odors inside the box, preventing them from spreading. They also reduce litter scatter and provide cats with privacy. Lidded boxes are easier to maintain in odor control, especially with high peeing or large cats.
