## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do Ultrasonic Bark Deterrents Work?



Ultrasonic bark deterrents emit high-frequency sounds that are inaudible to humans. These sounds interrupt a dog's barking, capturing their attention. Over time, dogs associate the sound with stopping their barking. These devices are effective, humane, and safe for all dog sizes.



### Are Dog Bark Deterrents Safe For Pets?



Yes, dog bark deterrents are designed to be safe and humane. They use ultrasonic technology, which doesn't harm dogs. The sound emitted is simply a distraction. It's a non-invasive method to control barking and is suitable for indoor and outdoor use.



### Can I Use Bark Deterrents Outdoors?



Absolutely, many bark deterrents are designed for both indoor and outdoor use. They often have weatherproof features, making them durable in various conditions. This versatility makes them ideal for controlling barking from neighbor's dogs in outdoor settings.



### What Range Do Bark Deterrents Cover?



Most bark deterrents cover a range of 23 to 75 feet, depending on the model. This range is effective for both indoor and outdoor use. It's important to choose a device with a range that suits your specific needs for optimal results.



### Are Bark Deterrent Devices Easy To Use?



Yes, bark deterrent devices are user-friendly and require minimal setup. Most devices are portable and rechargeable, allowing for convenient use anywhere. Simply turn the device on and position it where needed. They often come with multiple modes for customized training.
