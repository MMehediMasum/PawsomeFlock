## Feeding Recommendations

Feeding your puppy the right amount of Purina Puppy Chow helps support healthy growth. Proper feeding ensures your puppy gets enough nutrients without overeating. Understanding portion sizes, how to switch foods, and feeding frequency keeps your puppy happy and healthy.

### Portion Sizes

Portion sizes depend on your puppy’s weight and age. Purina Puppy Chow packaging offers a feeding chart for guidance. Use a measuring cup to give exact amounts. Avoid guessing portions to prevent overfeeding or underfeeding. Adjust portions as your puppy grows and gains weight.

### Transitioning To Purina Puppy Chow

Switch to Purina Puppy Chow gradually. Mix small amounts with your puppy’s current food for 5 to 7 days. Increase Purina Puppy Chow and reduce old food daily. This helps avoid stomach upset. Watch your puppy for signs of discomfort during the transition.

### Frequency Of Feeding

Puppies need multiple meals each day. Feed puppies under six months old three to four times daily. After six months, reduce to two meals per day. Keep feeding times consistent. Regular feeding supports digestion and energy levels throughout the day.
