## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Raw Bones For Aggressive Chewers?



The best raw bones for aggressive chewers include beef marrow bones and beef shank bones. These are tough, natural, and long-lasting. Brands like K9 Connoisseur and The Country Butcher offer USA-made options designed to withstand heavy chewing and keep dogs entertained safely.



### Are Raw Marrow Bones Safe For Large Dogs?



Yes, raw marrow bones are safe for large dogs when supervised. They provide essential nutrients and promote dental health. Choose appropriately sized bones, such as 4-inch or larger beef marrow bones, to prevent choking and ensure a satisfying chew experience for big breeds.



### How Do Raw Bones Benefit Dog Dental Health?



Raw bones help reduce plaque and tartar buildup naturally. Chewing stimulates saliva production, which cleans teeth and gums. The act of gnawing strengthens jaw muscles and promotes healthier teeth. Raw marrow bones are especially effective due to their texture and nutrient content.



### Can Small Dogs Safely Chew Raw Bones?



Small dogs can chew raw bones designed for their size, like Dynamo Bones for dogs up to 50 pounds. Always choose smaller, appropriately sized bones to avoid choking hazards and supervise chewing to ensure safety. Small raw bones provide mental stimulation and dental benefits.



### What Makes Usa-made Raw Bones Superior?



USA-made raw bones follow strict quality and safety standards. They are often sourced from trusted farms ensuring natural, hormone-free beef. This guarantees high-quality, fresh, and safe products for your dog. Brands like Raw Paws Pet and The Country Butcher prioritize American-made ingredients and processing.



### How Long Do Raw Bones Last For Dogs?



Raw bones can last from several minutes to hours, depending on the dog's chewing strength. Large, dense bones like beef shanks last longer for aggressive chewers. Always monitor your dog to prevent bone splintering and replace bones when they become too small or worn.
