### What Ingredients Help Cats Stay Calm In These Treats?



These treats often include melatonin, ashwagandha, GABA, and calming pheromones. They work together to reduce anxiety and stress in cats naturally.



### Are These Calming Treats Safe For Cats To Eat Daily?



Yes, most calming treats are formulated to be safe for daily use. Always follow the recommended dosage on the package and consult your vet for long-term use.



### How Do Calming Treats Help With Travel Anxiety In Cats?



Calming treats reduce nervousness and promote relaxation during travel. They help cats stay calm in unfamiliar environments like cars or vet visits.



### Can Calming Treats Reduce Destructive Behavior In Cats?



Yes, calming treats may curb destructive behavior caused by stress and separation anxiety. They help cats feel more relaxed and secure at home.



### Which Calming Treats Are Best For Cats With Sensitive Stomachs?



Grain-free options like Get Naked Cat Calming Treats are ideal for sensitive stomachs. They use natural, gentle ingredients to avoid digestive upset.



### How Long Before An Event Should I Give Calming Treats?



Administer calming treats 30 to 60 minutes before stressful events. This timing helps the active ingredients work effectively to reduce anxiety symptoms.
