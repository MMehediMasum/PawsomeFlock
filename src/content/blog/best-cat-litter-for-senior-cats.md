Lightweight and unscented options reduce stress for aging cats. Odor control matters because senior cats may use the litter box more frequently.



Easy scooping and low tracking keep the area clean and safe. This guide covers top products like Dr. Elsey’s Ultra Unscented, Precious Cat Senior Litter, and Purina Tidy Cats Lightweight. Each offers features designed for older cats’ needs, improving their comfort and well-being.
