## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Seeds Attract Cardinals The Most?



Sunflower and safflower seeds attract cardinals best. These seeds are high in fat and easy to eat. Cardinal blends often include these for optimal appeal.



### Are Cardinal Seed Blends Suitable For Other Birds?



Yes, cardinal blends attract finches, woodpeckers, and nuthatches. Many blends combine sunflower, millet, and peanut pieces for diverse bird species.



### How Much Seed Should I Offer Cardinals Daily?



Offer about 1/4 to 1/2 cup of seed daily. Fresh seed encourages regular visits and supports healthy feeding habits.



### Can I Use Cardinal Seed Mixes Year-round?



Yes, cardinal seed blends are suitable year-round. They provide essential nutrients and energy regardless of the season.



### Do Cardinal Seeds Contain Fillers Or Grow Seeds?



High-quality blends like Happy Wings avoid fillers and grow seeds. This ensures cleaner feeding and reduces unwanted plant growth near feeders.



### What Is The Best Cardinal Seed Size For Outside Feeders?



Medium-sized seeds like sunflower chips and safflower are best. They fit well in outside feeders and are easy for cardinals to handle.
