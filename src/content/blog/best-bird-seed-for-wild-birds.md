**Choosing the best bird seed helps attract more wild birds to your yard. Different seeds suit different bird types and seasons.**



Wild birds need food that matches their natural diet to stay healthy and active. Seeds with nuts, fruits, and sunflower provide energy and nutrition. Some blends focus on local birds by using regional ingredients. Others mix many types to welcome a variety of birds.



For example, Wagner’s Farmer’s Delight with cherry flavor offers a tasty treat. Kaytee’s Nut & Fruit blend includes peanuts and dried fruit to attract fruit-loving birds. Audubon Park’s premium seed contains safflower and sunflower for strong nutrition. Using the right seed mix supports wild birds and keeps your feeder busy all year. Choosing quality seed means happy, healthy birds visiting your yard more often.
