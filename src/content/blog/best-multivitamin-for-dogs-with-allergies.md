Glucosamine and chondroitin support joint health and mobility. They also reduce inflammation, which can help dogs with allergies feel more comfortable and active.



### Can Probiotics Improve My Dog's Allergy Symptoms?



Yes, probiotics boost gut health and immunity. A healthy gut can reduce allergic reactions and improve your dog's skin condition.



### Why Is Omega Fish Oil Important For Dogs With Allergies?



Omega fish oil contains fatty acids that soothe skin inflammation. It promotes a shiny coat and reduces itching caused by allergies.

Multivitamins with vitamins E, A, and Omega-3 nourish skin and coat. They help reduce dryness, itching, and improve overall skin health in allergic dogs.
