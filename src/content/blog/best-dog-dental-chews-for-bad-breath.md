## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Reduce Dog Bad Breath In Dental Chews?



Natural ingredients like parsley, mint, and seaweed are common. These help fight bacteria and freshen breath effectively. Many chews also contain enzymes that break down plaque and tartar, improving oral hygiene naturally.



### How Often Should Dogs Chew Dental Sticks For Fresh Breath?



Daily chewing is ideal to maintain fresh breath and clean teeth. Most products recommend one chew per day based on dog size. Consistency helps reduce plaque buildup and controls bad breath over time.



### Are Grain-free Dental Chews Better For Dogs’ Oral Health?



Grain-free chews reduce allergy risks and improve digestion for sensitive dogs. They often use natural ingredients that support oral health without fillers. Many owners find these chews effective for freshening breath and cleaning teeth.



### Can Dental Wipes Replace Chews For Dog Bad Breath?



Dental wipes help remove plaque and freshen breath but need regular use. They are a good supplement but not a full replacement for chewing action. Combining wipes with chews offers better oral hygiene results.



### Which Dental Chew Sizes Suit Small And Medium Dogs Best?



Small dogs benefit from smaller, softer chews like ProDen PlaqueOff or WHIMZEES S size. Medium dogs do well with medium-sized chews like WHIMZEES Stix M or PURINA DENTALIFE. Size ensures safe chewing and effective cleaning.
