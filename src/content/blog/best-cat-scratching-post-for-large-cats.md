## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Scratching Post For Large Cats?



The best scratching posts for large cats are tall, sturdy, and made with durable sisal rope. Products like MidWest Homes Feline Nuvo Grand Forte and Sheripet 45" tall post provide stability and enough height for full-body stretching.



### How Tall Should A Cat Scratching Post Be For Large Cats?



A scratching post for large cats should be at least 30 to 45 inches tall. Taller posts like PELUOMOZ 36-inch or Sheripet 45-inch allow cats to fully stretch and engage naturally.



### Are Sisal Rope Scratching Posts Better For Big Cats?



Yes, sisal rope is durable and withstands heavy scratching from large cats. Posts like SmartCat Ultimate and Ahomdoo Heavy Duty use thick sisal for long-lasting use and claw maintenance.



### Can Scratching Posts Protect Furniture From Large Cats?



High-quality scratching posts attract cats away from furniture. Wide-base posts like MidWest Homes Feline Nuvo Grand Forte provide stability, making furniture less likely to be scratched.



### Do Scratching Posts With Toys Attract Large Cats More?



Yes, hanging toys like balls and mice increase engagement. Posts such as Ahomdoo and MECOOL include interactive toys, encouraging cats to scratch and play, reducing boredom.



### How To Choose A Scratching Post For Multiple Large Cats?



Choose a tall, heavy-duty post with a wide base and durable materials. MidWest Homes Feline Nuvo Grand Forte and Cat Craft Bobcat are ideal for multi-cat households.
