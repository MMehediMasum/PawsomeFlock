Brands like Purina, VICTOR, Blue Buffalo, and Hill's Science Diet offer specially formulated diets. These options include natural ingredients like chicken and brown rice to aid weight loss while ensuring your dog gets essential nutrients. Selecting the right food helps your furry friend stay active and happy. Explore these options to find the best fit for your senior dog's weight management needs.
