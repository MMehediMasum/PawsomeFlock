## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Make Cat Deterrent Sprays Effective?



Effective sprays use natural, non-toxic ingredients like citrus, peppermint, and eucalyptus oils. These scents repel cats without harm. Many sprays also include odor neutralizers to stop urine smells and prevent repeat marking, ensuring safe use indoors and outdoors.



### How Do Cat Deterrent Sprays Stop Furniture Scratching?



These sprays emit scents cats dislike, discouraging scratching behavior. When applied to sofas, rugs, or curtains, the spray trains cats to avoid these areas. Consistent use helps reinforce boundaries while protecting your furniture naturally and humanely.



### Can Cat Deterrent Sprays Be Used Indoors And Outdoors?



Yes, most sprays are formulated for both indoor and outdoor use. They protect furniture, gardens, and walls from cat urine marking and scratching. Always check product labels for specific usage instructions to ensure safety and effectiveness in various environments.



### Are Cat Deterrent Sprays Safe For Kittens And Adult Cats?



Most sprays use safe, non-toxic natural ingredients suitable for kittens and adult cats. They are designed to deter without causing harm or stress. However, always test a small area first and monitor your pet’s reaction to ensure comfort.



### How Long Do Cat Deterrent Sprays Remain Effective?



Effectiveness typically lasts from several hours up to a few days, depending on the formula and surface. Regular reapplication is necessary, especially on porous surfaces or outdoor areas, to maintain protection and prevent cats from returning to marked spots.



### Do Cat Deterrent Sprays Eliminate Urine Odor Completely?



Many sprays neutralize urine odors while deterring cats from re-marking. Products like Bodhi Dog Cat No More Marking! Spray specifically target and break down strong smells. This dual action helps keep your home and garden fresh and clean.
