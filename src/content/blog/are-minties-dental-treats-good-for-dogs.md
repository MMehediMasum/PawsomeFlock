## Potential Risks And Side Effects

Minties dental treats can help clean dogs' teeth. But some dogs may face risks from eating them. It is important to know these risks before giving Minties to your dog.

### Allergic Reactions

Some dogs may be allergic to ingredients in Minties treats. Allergic reactions can include itching, swelling, or redness.

If your dog shows signs of allergy, stop giving the treat and see a vet. Allergies can cause serious problems if ignored.

### Digestive Issues

Eating Minties may cause upset stomach in some dogs. Signs include vomiting, diarrhea, or gas.

Too many treats or eating too fast may lead to these problems. Always follow feeding instructions carefully.

* Vomiting
* Diarrhea
* Excess gas
* Loss of appetite

### Choking Hazards

Minties treats can be hard and chewy. Small dogs or dogs that eat quickly may choke on pieces.

Always watch your dog while they eat Minties. Break the treat into smaller pieces for small dogs.

* Hard texture can block airways
* Quick eating increases choking risk
* Supervise your dog during treat time
* Cut treats for small or young dogs

## How To Use Minties Safely

Watch your dog while they eat Minties. This helps you stop choking or chewing problems quickly.

* Stay near your dog during treat time
* Remove any large leftover pieces
