## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Cat Scratcher Ideal For Nail Filing?



An ideal cat scratcher has a rough texture like sisal or corrugated cardboard. It helps file nails naturally while protecting furniture. Toys or catnip enhance engagement. Size and shape should fit your cat’s preference for vertical or horizontal scratching.



### How Do Wall-mounted Scratchers Benefit Indoor Cats?



Wall-mounted scratchers save floor space and cater to cats’ natural vertical scratching instincts. They help cats stretch and file nails effectively. These scratchers also keep claws healthy and prevent damage to walls or furniture by providing a designated spot.



### Can Cat Scratchers With Nail Files Replace Traditional Grooming Tools?



Yes, cat scratchers with built-in nail files help maintain healthy nails naturally. They reduce the need for frequent clipping. However, occasional grooming may still be necessary for complete nail care, especially for indoor cats with less activity.



### Are Cardboard Scratchers Effective For Cats’ Nail Filing?



Cardboard scratchers are highly effective for filing nails gently. Their corrugated texture allows cats to scratch and shed old nail layers. They are affordable, lightweight, and often infused with catnip to increase interest and use.



### How Do Interactive Toys On Scratchers Enhance Nail Care?



Interactive toys like hanging balls encourage cats to use scratchers more often. Increased use results in better nail filing and exercise. Toys also reduce boredom and promote mental stimulation, keeping cats engaged and nails well-maintained.



### What Size And Shape Scratcher Suits Small Or Kitten Cats Best?



Small or kitten cats prefer compact scratchers around 18-22 inches. L-shaped or vertical posts suit their scratching style and strength. These sizes ensure comfort and encourage regular use, helping kittens develop healthy nail habits early.
