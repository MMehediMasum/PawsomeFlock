## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Truly Squirrel Proof?



A squirrel proof bird feeder uses weight-activated mechanisms or cages to block squirrels. It stops squirrels from accessing the seed, ensuring birds get the food. Durable metal construction also prevents chewing. This design protects birdseed and keeps squirrels away effectively.



### How Do Metal Bird Feeders Benefit Outdoor Use?



Metal bird feeders resist weather damage like rain, snow, and sun exposure. They are chew-proof and last longer than plastic feeders. Metal feeders also provide sturdy support for heavy seed loads. Their durability makes them ideal for outdoor bird feeding.



### What Is The Ideal Seed Capacity For Squirrel Proof Feeders?



A seed capacity between 3 to 5 pounds is optimal. It holds enough seed for multiple bird visits without frequent refills. Larger capacity feeders attract more birds and reduce maintenance time. Capacity depends on your bird-watching needs and feeder size.



### How Do Weight-activated Springs Deter Squirrels?



Weight-activated springs close feeding ports when heavy animals like squirrels land. Birds, being lighter, can feed without triggering the mechanism. This selective action keeps squirrels from stealing seed while allowing birds easy access.



### Which Birds Are Attracted To Squirrel Proof Feeders?



Squirrel proof feeders attract birds like cardinals, finches, sparrows, chickadees, and blue jays. The feeder’s design accommodates various bird sizes. Seed type and feeder ports are tailored for these common wild birds.



### Can Squirrel Proof Feeders Withstand Harsh Weather Conditions?



Yes, many squirrel proof feeders feature rain and snow protection. Metal construction and covered ports prevent seed spoilage. Weather-resistant feeders ensure consistent feeding during all seasons. This keeps birds fed year-round.
