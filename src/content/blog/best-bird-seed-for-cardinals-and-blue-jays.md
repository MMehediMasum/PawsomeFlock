Choosing the best bird seed for cardinals and blue jays means understanding their favorite foods. Cardinals love sunflower seeds, safflower, and peanuts, while blue jays enjoy corn, sunflower seeds, and peanuts. Mixing these seeds creates a blend that appeals to both species.



Quality seed blends like Kaytee Nut & Fruit or Armstrong Royal Jubilee Jays provide essential nutrients. Offering fresh, clean seed in an outside feeder invites more birds to visit. Watching these colorful birds up close brings joy and supports local wildlife. This guide covers top bird seed options to attract and nourish cardinals and blue jays in your yard.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Bird Seeds Attract Cardinals Most Effectively?



Seeds like sunflower, safflower, and peanuts attract cardinals well. Brands like Kaytee Nut & Fruit and Wagner’s Cardinal Blend use these seeds. These blends provide essential nutrients and appeal to cardinals’ preferences, ensuring consistent visits to your feeder.



### Which Seed Blends Are Best For Blue Jays?



Blue jays prefer whole corn, black oil sunflower, and peanuts. Armstrong Wild Bird Food Royal Jubilee Jays Blend contains these. It’s ideal for outside feeders, offering a mix that supports blue jays’ dietary needs and attracts them reliably.



### Can A Single Bird Seed Mix Attract Both Cardinals And Blue Jays?



Yes, blends like Kaytee Wild Bird Ultimate Birder’s Blend are designed for multiple species. They include sunflower, peanut pieces, and fruit, appealing to cardinals, blue jays, and other wild birds.



### How Often Should I Refill My Bird Feeder With These Seeds?



Refill feeders when seed levels drop below half. This keeps seeds fresh and prevents spoilage. Regular refilling encourages consistent visits from cardinals and blue jays.



### Are Flavored Bird Seeds Better For Attracting Cardinals?



Flavored seeds, like Wagner's Cherry Flavor, can be appealing but are not necessary. Natural seed blends typically attract cardinals effectively while offering balanced nutrition.



### What Feeder Types Work Best With These Cardinal And Blue Jay Seeds?



Platform and hopper feeders work well. They accommodate larger seeds like sunflower and peanuts. These feeder types allow cardinals and blue jays easy access to their preferred seeds.
