**Choosing the best cat litter for stray cats helps keep them comfortable and your space clean. Stray cats need litter that controls odor and is gentle on their paws.**



Stray cats often face tough conditions, so providing good litter improves their hygiene and health. The right cat litter clumps well, reduces dust, and controls odor for a fresher environment. Lightweight or low-dust options make cleaning easier and protect sensitive noses.



Some litters have natural ingredients, which are safer for cats. For stray cats, durability and ease of use matter. Using mats or enclosures can catch stray litter and keep the area tidy. Selecting the best litter and accessories supports stray cats’ well-being while maintaining your home’s cleanliness. This guide covers top cat litter choices that meet these needs effectively.
