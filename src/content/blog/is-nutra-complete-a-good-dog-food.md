## Feeding Guidelines

Feeding your dog the right amount of food is important for its health. Nutra Complete offers clear guidelines to help you feed your dog properly.

Following these feeding tips helps your dog get all needed nutrients without overeating or losing weight.

### Recommended Dosage

The amount of Nutra Complete you give depends on your dog’s weight and age. Always check the package for specific instructions.

* Small dogs (up to 20 lbs): ½ to 1 cup per day
* Medium dogs (21-50 lbs): 1 to 2 cups per day
* Large dogs (51-90 lbs): 2 to 3 cups per day
* Extra-large dogs (over 90 lbs): 3 cups or more per day

Divide the daily amount into two meals for best digestion.

### Transitioning Tips

Switch your dog’s food slowly to avoid stomach upset. Mix Nutra Complete with the old food over several days.

* Day 1-2: 25% Nutra Complete, 75% old food
* Day 3-4: 50% Nutra Complete, 50% old food
* Day 5-6: 75% Nutra Complete, 25% old food
* Day 7: 100% Nutra Complete

Watch your dog’s reactions during this time. Stop if you see signs of discomfort.

### Potential Side Effects

Some dogs may have mild side effects when starting Nutra Complete. These usually go away after a few days.

* Loose stools or diarrhea
* Minor vomiting
* Gas or bloating
* Allergic reactions like itching or redness

If side effects last more than a week, talk to your vet for advice.
