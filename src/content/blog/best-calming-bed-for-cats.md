### What Features Make A Cat Bed Calming And Soothing?



Calming cat beds use soft faux fur, donut shapes, and raised edges. These features provide comfort and a sense of security. They reduce anxiety and promote restful sleep for indoor cats and small pets.



### Are These Calming Beds Suitable For Cats Up To 15 Lbs?



Yes, most calming beds listed fit cats and small dogs up to 15 pounds. Sizes typically range around 20 inches, ideal for small to medium pets. Always check product dimensions before purchasing.



### Can I Machine Wash These Calming Cat Beds Safely?



Yes, the beds mentioned are machine washable. Use gentle cycles and mild detergent. Regular washing keeps the bed clean and maintains the plush texture for comfort.



### Do These Beds Have Anti-slip Bottoms For Safety?



Most beds feature anti-slip bottoms. This prevents sliding on smooth floors, ensuring your cat’s safety. Anti-slip design adds stability when your pet moves or curls up.



### Which Bed Size Is Best For Small And Medium Cats?



A 20-inch diameter bed suits small and medium cats perfectly. It provides enough room to curl comfortably while maintaining the cozy, enclosed feeling cats love.



### Are These Calming Beds Also Suitable For Puppies And Small Dogs?



Yes, many calming beds are designed for both cats and small dogs. Their size and soft materials cater well to small pets needing comfort and anxiety relief.
