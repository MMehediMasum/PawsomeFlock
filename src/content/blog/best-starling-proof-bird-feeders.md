## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Starling-proof?



Starling-proof feeders have adjustable grille sizes or metal cages that prevent large starlings from accessing seeds. They allow smaller birds to feed comfortably while keeping aggressive starlings away, ensuring a peaceful feeding environment for songbirds.



### How Do Squirrel-proof Bird Feeders Work?



Squirrel-proof feeders use metal cages, weight-sensitive mechanisms, or flexible grille adjustments. These features block squirrels from reaching seeds, protecting bird food and preventing feeder damage. They ensure birds get their feed without interference from squirrels.



### Why Choose Caged Suet Feeders For Starlings?



Caged suet feeders limit access to suet cakes, preventing starlings from dominating feeding spots. The cages allow smaller birds to feed safely and reduce waste. They also often include trays and weatherproof designs for outdoor durability.



### How Important Is Seed Capacity In Bird Feeders?



Seed capacity affects how often you refill feeders. Larger capacities, like 2-4 lbs, are ideal for frequent bird visitors and reduce maintenance. Choose feeders with appropriate capacity to suit your local bird population and feeding habits.



### Are Metal Bird Feeders Better For Outdoor Use?



Metal feeders are durable, chew and rust-proof, and withstand various weather conditions. They offer long-lasting protection against squirrels and starlings. Metal cages and tubes also enable easy cleaning and maintenance, keeping feeders hygienic.



### Can I Attract Small Birds With Starling-proof Feeders?



Yes, starling-proof feeders with narrow grilles or cages are designed to attract small birds like finches, chickadees, and cardinals. They prevent larger birds from dominating, ensuring small birds have safe access to seeds and suet.
