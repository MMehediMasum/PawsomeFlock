## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Cat Litter Works Best With Litter Robot?



Dr. Elsey's Ultra UnScented and Purina Tidy Cats are top choices. They clump well and control odors effectively. These litters are low dust and compatible with Litter Robot sensors. Always check for low tracking and dust to keep your Litter Robot functioning smoothly.



### How Does Odor Control Vary Among These Litters?



Arm & Hammer Clump & Seal and Fresh Step offer superior odor control. They use activated charcoal and strong clumping agents. Purina Tidy Cats formulas also provide effective odor management. Selecting a litter with odor control helps maintain a fresh-smelling environment for multi-cat households.



### Are Scented Litters Suitable For Litter Robot?



Scented litters like Arm & Hammer Forever Fresh offer freshness with essential oils. However, some cats prefer unscented options like Dr. Elsey's. Scented litters may affect Litter Robot sensors, so use with caution. Choose based on your cat’s preference and sensitivity to fragrances.



### Can I Use Litter Box Liners With Litter Robot?



Yes, heavy-duty liners designed for automatic boxes fit well. They help keep the waste drawer clean and make maintenance easier. Liners should be extra thick and durable to avoid tearing. Using liners can reduce odor and simplify cleaning routines.



### Which Litter Is Best For Multi-cat Homes With Litter Robot?



Fresh Step Multi-Cat and Purina Tidy Cats Multi-Cat formulas excel in odor control and clumping. They handle higher waste volumes efficiently. These litters reduce dust and tracking, making them ideal for multi-cat households using Litter Robot. Regular cleaning complements their performance.



### How Important Is Low Dust Litter For Litter Robot?



Low dust litters like Purina Tidy Cats Lightweight protect the Litter Robot’s sensors and mechanics. Dust buildup can cause malfunctions and requires frequent cleaning. Choosing low dust formulas ensures longer device life and healthier air quality for you and your cat.
