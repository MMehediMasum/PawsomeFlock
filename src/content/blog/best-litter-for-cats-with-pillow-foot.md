## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Litter For Cats With Pillow Feet?



Purina Tidy Cats Tidy Feet and Cat's Pride Max Power are excellent for cats with pillow feet. They offer low tracking and gentle textures. These litters minimize litter sticking to paws, keeping your cat comfortable and your home cleaner.



### How Does Low Tracking Cat Litter Benefit Cats With Pillow Feet?



Low tracking litter reduces litter granules stuck on cats' soft paws. It prevents discomfort and mess around your home. Products like Purina Tidy Cats Tidy Feet and Fresh Step Clean Paws are designed for this purpose, ensuring easy cleanup and paw comfort.



### Are Scented Litters Safe For Cats With Sensitive Paws?



Scented litters like Purina Tidy Cats Clean Linen are generally safe but may irritate very sensitive paws. Unscented options such as Dr. Elsey's Ultra Unscented are better for sensitive cats. Always observe your cat’s reaction when switching litters.



### Which Cat Litter Offers The Best Odor Control For Pillow Foot Cats?



ARM & HAMMER Clump & Seal SLIDE and Cat's Pride Max Power provide superior odor control. Both keep odors fresh up to 10 days without harsh chemicals. This benefits cats with pillow feet by maintaining a clean litter environment.



### Can Lightweight Clumping Litter Be Good For Cats With Pillow Feet?



Yes, lightweight clumping litters like Cat's Pride Premium Lightweight are easier on soft paws. They reduce litter tracking and provide effective clumping and odor control. This combination keeps cats comfortable and homes tidy.
