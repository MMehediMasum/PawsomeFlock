Supplements like Shed-X Liquid and Zesty Paws Omega-3 Blend provide essential fatty acids. These nourish skin cells, reduce inflammation, and boost fur quality. Regular use helps relieve dryness and itching, supporting a shiny, healthy coat and minimizing excessive shedding in dogs.

Yes, fish oil is rich in omega-3 fatty acids that support skin hydration and coat health. It reduces inflammation and dryness, which are common causes of shedding. Adding fish oil supplements or foods like Diamond Naturals with wild-caught salmon improves fur strength and shine.



### Are Natural Dog Foods Better For Shedding Control?



Natural dog foods like Blue Buffalo True Solutions and Nutrish Real Chicken & Veggies offer wholesome ingredients. They provide balanced nutrition with antioxidants and omega fatty acids that improve skin health. Such foods reduce shedding by maintaining a healthy coat and preventing irritation.



### How Quickly Do Shedding Supplements Work On Dogs?
