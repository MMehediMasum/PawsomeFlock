Feeding cardinals properly supports their health and bright red color. Your backyard can become a favorite spot for these beautiful birds with the best food choices.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Seeds Are Best For Feeding Cardinals?



Cardinals prefer sunflower, safflower, and nyjer seeds. These seeds are found in blends like Kaytee Cardinal Wild Bird Seed and Wagner's Cardinal Blend. They provide essential nutrients and attract cardinals to your feeder consistently.



### How Does Safflower Benefit Cardinals?



Safflower seeds are high in fat and protein. Cardinals enjoy them, and they help maintain healthy feathers and energy. Blends like Happy Wings Cardinal Blend contain safflower to boost cardinals’ nutrition and keep them visiting your feeder.



### Are Flavored Bird Foods Safe For Cardinals?



Yes, flavored foods like Wagner's Farmer's Delight with cherry flavor are safe. They add variety and attract cardinals without harmful additives. Always choose blends designed for wild birds to ensure safety and nutrition.



### Which Bird Food Mix Attracts Multiple Species Including Cardinals?



Kaytee Wild Bird Ultimate Birder’s Blend is ideal. It attracts cardinals, grosbeaks, nuthatches, and woodpeckers. This mix provides diverse seeds catering to various birds, enhancing your backyard birdwatching experience.



### Can Cardinal Cakes Supplement Regular Seed Blends?



Yes, Kaytee Cardinal Cake offers extra nutrition. It contains high-energy ingredients that cardinals love. Use it alongside seed blends to provide variety and support cardinals through colder months or breeding season.



### How Much Bird Food Should I Buy For Cardinals?



Buy in sizes like 4 to 18 pounds to ensure freshness. Smaller bags like Audubon Park Cardinal Blend are good for short-term use, while larger bags like Lyric Cardinal Wild Bird Seed suit frequent feeders.
