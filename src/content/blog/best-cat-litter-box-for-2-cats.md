## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Litter Box Is Best For Two Cats?



An extra-large litter box with high sides works best for two cats. It provides enough space and reduces litter scatter, keeping the area clean and comfortable for multi-cat households.



### Are Stainless Steel Litter Boxes Better For Multiple Cats?



Yes, stainless steel litter boxes are durable, rust-resistant, and easy to clean. They resist odors and bacteria, making them ideal for homes with two or more cats.



### How Do High-sided Litter Boxes Prevent Urine Leakage?



High-sided litter boxes contain urine and litter effectively. They prevent spills and keep surrounding floors clean, especially important when used by two cats.



### Do Enclosed Litter Boxes Reduce Litter Scatter For Two Cats?



Yes, enclosed litter boxes with lids and scatter shields minimize litter tracking. They help contain messes, making them perfect for multi-cat environments.



### What Accessories Come With These Two-pack Cat Litter Boxes?



Most two-pack stainless steel litter boxes include scoops, mats, and lids. These accessories enhance hygiene and simplify cleaning for homes with multiple cats.
