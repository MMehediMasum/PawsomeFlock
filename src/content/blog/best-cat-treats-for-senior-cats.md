## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Treats For Senior Cats With Joint Issues?



Treats like Cat Hip & Joint Health Crunchy & Creamy Chews and Nutramax Cosequin support joint health. They contain glucosamine, chondroitin, and omega-3s. These ingredients help reduce inflammation and improve mobility in senior cats. Ideal for cats with arthritis or stiffness.



### Are Lickable Treats Good For Senior Cats?



Yes, lickable treats like INABA Churu and Hartz Delectables Bisque are excellent for senior cats. They are easy to consume and hydrating. Plus, they contain vitamins like taurine, E, and C that support overall health. Perfect for cats with dental issues or reduced appetite.



### How Do Grain-free Treats Benefit Senior Cats?



Grain-free treats like Blue Buffalo Wilderness reduce digestive issues in senior cats. They are rich in protein and avoid fillers. This helps maintain healthy weight and energy levels. Ideal for cats with food sensitivities or allergies.



### Can Treats Improve Senior Cat’s Skin And Coat?



Yes, treats with omega-3s and vitamins, such as Nutramax Cosequin, support skin and coat health. These nutrients reduce dryness and promote shine. Regular supplementation helps senior cats maintain a healthy, glossy coat.



### Are Senior Cat Treat Variety Packs Worth Buying?



Variety packs like Hartz Delectables Stew and Senior Lickable Wet Treats offer multiple flavors. They keep senior cats interested in treats. Also, they provide balanced nutrition with vitamins and minerals. Great for picky eaters or cats needing dietary variety.



### How Often Should I Give Treats To Senior Cats?



Limit treats to 5-10% of your senior cat’s daily calories. Overfeeding can cause weight gain and health issues. Use treats for training, bonding, or supplementing nutrition. Always consult your vet for personalized recommendations.
