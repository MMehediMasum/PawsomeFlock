## Tips For Transitioning To New Dog Food

Katherine Heigl recommends feeding dogs high-quality food that supports their health. Changing your dog’s food can be tricky. It needs to be done slowly to avoid stomach problems.

Here are tips to help your dog adjust to new food easily. Follow these steps to keep your dog happy and healthy during the change.

### Start With Small Amounts

Begin by mixing a small amount of the new food with your dog’s current food. This helps the dog get used to the new taste and texture slowly.

* Mix 25% new food with 75% old food
* Feed this mix for 2 to 3 days
* Watch your dog’s reaction to the new food

### Gradually Increase New Food

Slowly add more new food while reducing the old food. This helps your dog’s digestive system adjust without problems.

* Change to 50% new and 50% old food after 3 days
* Then move to 75% new and 25% old food
* Keep each stage for about 3 days

### Watch For Signs Of Discomfort

During the transition, look for signs like vomiting, diarrhea, or less appetite. These could mean your dog needs a slower change or a different food.

* Check your dog’s stool for changes
* Notice any vomiting or upset stomach
* Observe if your dog refuses to eat

### Keep Fresh Water Available

Make sure your dog always has fresh water. New food might change their thirst. Water helps digestion and keeps your dog healthy.

* Refill water bowl often
* Use clean bowls every day
* Encourage your dog to drink more if needed

### Maintain A Regular Feeding Schedule

Feed your dog at the same times daily. A routine helps your dog accept the new food easier and keeps digestion steady.

* Feed twice a day at regular times
* Avoid extra snacks during transition
* Keep portion sizes consistent
