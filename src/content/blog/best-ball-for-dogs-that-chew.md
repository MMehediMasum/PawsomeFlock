### Are Squeaky Balls Safe For Dogs That Chew Aggressively?



Yes, squeaky balls like DISSKNIC and EASTBLUE are made from non-toxic, durable rubber. They withstand aggressive chewing while providing fun interactive play. Always supervise your dog to prevent accidental swallowing of small parts. These toys help reduce anxiety and promote active play.



### Which Balls Float For Water Play With Dogs?



Chuckit! Ultra Ball floats and is perfect for water fetch games. Made from durable rubber, it suits dogs weighing 20-60 lbs. Its buoyancy makes it great for outdoor water activities, ensuring your dog can safely retrieve the ball in pools, lakes, or the ocean.



### What Size Ball Is Best For Medium To Large Dogs?



Balls sized 2. 5 to 5. 5 inches, like Chew King and Monster K9 balls, suit medium and large dogs. These sizes prevent choking hazards and allow easy gripping. Choose the size based on your dog’s breed and chewing strength for optimal safety and fun.



### Can These Balls Help Relieve Dog Anxiety?



Yes, interactive chew toys like DISSKNIC balls help reduce anxiety. Chewing releases calming endorphins and distracts dogs from stress. Durable, squeaky balls provide mental stimulation and physical activity, promoting relaxation and preventing destructive behaviors in anxious dogs.



### How Many Balls Come In Typical Chew Ball Packs?



Most packs contain 2 to 4 balls, such as Chew King and Kimee sets. Multiple balls ensure you always have a backup during playtime. This variety keeps dogs engaged and allows for longer, uninterrupted fetch or chew sessions.
