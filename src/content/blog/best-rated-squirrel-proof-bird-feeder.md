## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Truly Squirrel Proof?



A squirrel-proof bird feeder uses weight-activated perches or gravity protection to block squirrels. Metal and chew-proof designs prevent damage. These features ensure only birds access the seeds, keeping squirrels away effectively.



### How Much Seed Capacity Do These Feeders Offer?



Seed capacities vary from 1. 3 to 5 pounds. Larger capacities like 3 to 5 pounds reduce refilling frequency. Choose based on your bird feeding needs and how often you want to refill.



### Are Metal Mesh Feeders Easier To Clean?



Yes, metal mesh feeders are easier to clean and maintain. Their open design allows better airflow, reducing mold and seed spoilage. They are durable and resistant to squirrel chewing.



### Do Squirrel-proof Feeders Support Various Bird Species?



Yes, these feeders accommodate small birds like finches and chickadees. Features like multiple feeding ports and removable perches attract diverse wild birds. They balance squirrel protection with bird accessibility.



### How Do Gravity Protection Feeders Work?



Gravity protection feeders close access ports when a squirrel’s weight is detected. This stops squirrels from reaching seeds. Birds, being lighter, can feed without triggering the mechanism.



### Can These Feeders Be Hung Outdoors Safely?



Yes, all listed feeders are designed for outdoor hanging. They feature durable, weather-resistant materials that withstand outdoor conditions. Hanging them in sheltered spots improves longevity and bird safety.
