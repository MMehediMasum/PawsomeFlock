Use sturdy hooks or branches to hold feeders like Nature’s Way Hanging Glass Hummingbird Feeder or iBorn Bee Proof Hummingbird Feeders.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### How High Should I Hang A Hummingbird Feeder?



Hang the feeder 4 to 6 feet above the ground. This height protects from predators and allows easy bird access. Choose a visible spot near flowers to attract hummingbirds quickly.



### What Is The Best Location To Hang A Feeder?



Place the feeder in a shaded area to keep nectar fresh longer. Avoid windy spots to prevent spills. Near hummingbird-friendly plants is ideal for natural attraction.



### How Do I Prevent Ants From Reaching The Feeder?



Use feeders with built-in ant moats or add water-filled ant barriers. Regularly clean the feeder and surrounding area to discourage ants. This keeps nectar fresh and hummingbirds safe.



### Can I Hang Multiple Feeders Together?



Yes, but space them 10 to 15 feet apart. This reduces competition and territorial behavior among hummingbirds. Multiple feeders increase feeding opportunities and attract more birds.



### How Often Should I Clean And Refill The Feeder?



Clean and refill every 3 to 5 days, more often in hot weather. Use warm soapy water to prevent mold and bacteria. Fresh nectar ensures hummingbird health and feeding activity.
