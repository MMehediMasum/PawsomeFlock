Products like Hill’s Science Diet Oral Care and Royal Canin Dental Care focus on dental health. Treats like Purina DentaLife and Greenies support cleaner teeth between meals. Nutritional balance matters too. Foods with real chicken and gentle ingredients keep seniors strong. This guide covers the best cat food and supplements for older cats with dental issues, helping you make smart choices for your furry friend’s wellbeing.
