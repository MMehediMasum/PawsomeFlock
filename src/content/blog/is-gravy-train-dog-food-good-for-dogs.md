## Feeding Tips And Recommendations

Feeding your dog with Gravy Train dog food requires care and attention. Proper feeding helps your dog stay healthy and happy. Follow these tips to make the most out of this food.

### Portion Control

Feed your dog the right amount of Gravy Train based on its weight and age. Overfeeding can cause weight gain and health problems. Use the feeding guide on the package as a starting point. Adjust portions depending on your dog's activity level and metabolism.

* Measure food with a standard cup or scoop.
* Divide daily portions into two or more meals.
* Watch for signs of hunger or fullness.

### Combining With Other Foods

Mixing Gravy Train with other dog foods can add variety and nutrition. Combine wet and dry food for texture and taste. Add small amounts of fresh vegetables or lean meats for extra nutrients.

* Introduce new foods slowly to avoid upset stomach.
* Keep the total food quantity balanced to prevent overeating.
* Consult a vet for safe food combinations.

### Monitoring Dog’s Health

Check your dog’s health regularly while feeding Gravy Train. Look for changes in energy, coat quality, and digestion. Healthy stools and consistent energy levels show good tolerance to the food.

* Watch for allergies or digestive issues like vomiting or diarrhea.
* Visit the vet if you notice unusual symptoms.
* Adjust feeding habits based on your dog’s health feedback.
