## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Dog Food Is Best For Yorkies With Sensitive Stomachs?



Hill's Science Diet Sensitive Stomach & Skin and Purina Pro Plan Sensitive Skin and Stomach are top choices. These formulas support digestion and skin health, using gentle ingredients like chicken or salmon and rice, ideal for Yorkies with sensitive stomachs.



### Are Grain-free Options Good For Yorkies' Sensitive Stomachs?



Grain-free diets like Blue Buffalo Basics can help if your Yorkie is allergic to grains. They use limited ingredients to reduce digestive upset. However, always consult your vet before switching to grain-free dog food for sensitive stomachs.



### How Does Hill’s Science Diet Support Sensitive Skin And Stomach?



Hill’s Science Diet uses prebiotic fibers and balanced nutrients to promote gut health and reduce irritation. Their recipes contain high-quality proteins and essential fatty acids, which help improve digestion and soothe sensitive skin in small breed dogs like Yorkies.



### Can Purina Pro Plan Help With Yorkie Digestive Issues?



Yes, Purina Pro Plan Sensitive Skin and Stomach formulas use salmon and rice to aid digestion. They include probiotics and antioxidants that support a healthy immune system and reduce digestive discomfort for sensitive small breed dogs.



### Is Wet Food Better For Yorkies With Sensitive Stomachs?



Wet food, like Hill’s Science Diet Sensitive Stomach & Skin Loaf, offers hydration and gentle textures. It is easier to digest and can be soothing for Yorkies with sensitive stomachs, especially if they have trouble chewing dry kibble.



### What Ingredients Should I Avoid In Sensitive Stomach Dog Food?



Avoid artificial additives, fillers, and common allergens like corn, soy, and wheat. Opt for simple, high-quality ingredients such as chicken, lamb, salmon, and rice to minimize digestive upset in Yorkies with sensitive stomachs.
