## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Bird Feeder Squirrel Proof For Cardinals?



Squirrel proof feeders use weight-activated perches or gravity mechanisms. These close access when squirrels land, allowing only lighter birds like cardinals to feed safely.



### How Much Seed Capacity Is Ideal For Squirrel Proof Feeders?



A 3 to 5-pound seed capacity is perfect. It reduces frequent refills and supports multiple birds, including cardinals, without attracting too many squirrels.



### Are Metal Bird Feeders Better For Outdoor Squirrel Protection?



Yes, metal feeders are durable and chew-proof. They withstand squirrel damage and harsh weather, making them ideal for long-lasting outdoor use.



### How Do Weight-activated Perches Work On Bird Feeders?



Weight-activated perches close feeding ports under heavy weight. This prevents squirrels from eating seeds while allowing small birds like cardinals to feed freely.



### Can Squirrel Proof Feeders Attract Multiple Bird Species?



Yes, these feeders attract cardinals, finches, chickadees, and blue jays. Their design supports various birds while keeping squirrels away effectively.



### Is It Easy To Clean Squirrel Proof Bird Feeders?



Most squirrel proof feeders have removable parts and simple designs. This makes them easy to clean regularly and maintain bird health.
