### How Does A Swivel Bin Pooper Scooper Work?



Swivel bin pooper scoopers, like the FUSBUNTY model, have a rotating waste bin attached to the rake. This design allows easy collection and disposal without touching the waste. It’s practical for large and small dogs, improving hygiene and convenience during cleanup.



### Are Stainless Steel Pooper Scoopers Durable?



Yes, stainless steel pooper scoopers such as the MXF and TOOGE models are highly durable. They resist rust and bending, making them perfect for heavy-duty use on various surfaces like grass and gravel. They also offer long-lasting performance for large dogs.



### What Size Pooper Scooper Suits Large Dogs?



A pooper scooper with a long handle (around 32 to 40 inches) and a wide tray works best for large dogs. Models like Pupzle and iEnjoyed offer large capacity bins and sturdy construction, reducing strain and speeding up waste removal.



### Can Pooper Scoopers Be Used On Different Terrains?



Yes, many pooper scoopers, including UPSKY and MXF, are designed for grass, dirt, and gravel. Their strong springs and wide trays make picking up waste easy on various surfaces, ideal for outdoor yards and parks.



### Do Pooper Scoopers Come With Waste Bags?



Some pooper scoopers, like FUSBUNTY and Arm & Hammer, include scented or heavy-duty waste bags. These bags improve odor control and make disposal cleaner and quicker, adding value to your pet waste cleanup routine.
