### Which Flea Treatment Is Best For Large Cats Over 9 Lbs?



Advantage II Large Cat, TevraPet Actispot II, and Cheristin are ideal for cats over 9 lbs. They offer effective flea control with easy application and vet recommendations.



### Can Oral Flea Treatments Be Used Safely On Kittens?



Oral treatments like CAPSTAR are safe for cats over 2 lbs, but always check the age and weight restrictions. Consult your vet before treating kittens to ensure safety.



### How Often Should I Apply Flea Treatment To My Cat?



Most topical treatments provide protection for 1 to 4 months, so apply monthly or as recommended. Oral treatments like CAPSTAR are usually given daily during infestations for quick control.



### Do Flea Treatments Also Prevent Ticks On Cats?



Products like FRONTLINE Plus, PetArmor Plus, and TevraPet FirstAct Plus protect against both fleas and ticks. They offer comprehensive pest prevention to keep your cat healthy.
