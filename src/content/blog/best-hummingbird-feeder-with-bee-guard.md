### What Is The Best Hummingbird Feeder With Bee Guard?



The Perky-Pet 203CPBN-2 is a top choice. It features durable glass, built-in bee guards, and an ant moat. This feeder protects nectar from bees and ants, ensuring hummingbirds feed safely and comfortably.



### How Do Bee Guards Work On Hummingbird Feeders?



Bee guards cover the feeding ports to block bees while allowing hummingbirds access. They prevent nectar theft and reduce contamination. Most feeders include these guards to keep your feeder bee-free and hummingbird-friendly.



### Are Ant Moats Necessary In Hummingbird Feeders?



Yes, ant moats are essential. They hold water to block ants from reaching nectar. Many feeders, like FEED GARDEN and Kingsyard, combine bee guards with ant moats for optimal protection.



### How Often Should I Clean A Hummingbird Feeder With Bee Guard?



Clean feeders every 3-5 days in warm weather. This prevents mold and bacteria growth. Use warm soapy water and brushes included in packs like FEED GARDEN for thorough cleaning.



### Can I Use Replacement Bee Guards For My Feeder?



Yes, replacement bee guards like Perky-Pet 205Y fit most feeders. They keep the feeder functional and protected. Regularly replacing worn guards ensures continuous bee prevention.
