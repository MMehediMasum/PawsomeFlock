Foods like Stella & Chewy's Freeze-Dried Raw Beef Dinner support digestion and reduce grass eating. Pawstruck Air Dried Dog Food offers high protein and limited ingredients for sensitive dogs. These options promote gut health and minimize grass cravings naturally.
