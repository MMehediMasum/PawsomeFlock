## Feeding Tips And Recommendations

Feeding your dog Blue Buffalo wet food requires attention to portion sizes, smooth transitions, and proper storage. These tips help keep your dog healthy and happy. They also ensure the food stays fresh and tasty for your pet.

### Portion Sizes

Follow the feeding guidelines on the Blue Buffalo packaging. The amount depends on your dog's weight, age, and activity level. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain. Underfeeding may cause nutritional gaps.

* Measure portions carefully with a kitchen scale or measuring cup.
* Split daily portions into two or more meals for better digestion.
* Adjust portions as your dog’s weight or activity changes.

### Transitioning Foods

Switch to Blue Buffalo wet food slowly to avoid stomach upset. Gradually mix it with your dog's current food over 7 to 10 days. Increase the wet food amount each day. Watch for any signs of discomfort or allergies.

* Start with 25% Blue Buffalo and 75% old food.
* Increase to 50% Blue Buffalo by day four.
* Reach 100% Blue Buffalo by the end of the transition.

### Storage Guidelines

Keep opened Blue Buffalo wet food fresh by storing it correctly. Refrigerate unused portions immediately. Use airtight containers to prevent spoilage and odors. Serve food at room temperature for better taste.

* Use leftover food within 2 to 3 days.
* Do not leave wet food out for more than 2 hours.
* Check the expiration date before feeding.
