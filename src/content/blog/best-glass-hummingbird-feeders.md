## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make These Glass Hummingbird Feeders Best?



These feeders offer glass durability, multiple feeding ports, built-in ant moats, and bee guards. They also have perches and easy-to-clean designs, attracting more hummingbirds safely.



### How Do Ant Moats Benefit Glass Hummingbird Feeders?



Ant moats prevent ants from reaching nectar by creating water barriers. This keeps nectar fresh and uncontaminated, ensuring hummingbirds have a clean feeding source.



### Are These Feeders Easy To Clean And Refill?



Yes, most have wide mouths and two-part bases. This design simplifies refilling and thorough cleaning, reducing mold growth and maintaining nectar quality.



### Which Feeder Sizes Suit Different Garden Spaces?



Feeders range from 10 to 32 ounces. Smaller feeders suit limited spaces, while larger ones support more hummingbirds and require less frequent refilling.



### How Do Bee Guards Protect Hummingbird Feeders?



Bee guards block bees and wasps from accessing nectar. This prevents insect interference, making feeding safer and more enjoyable for hummingbirds.



### Can Glass Feeders Withstand Outdoor Weather Conditions?



Yes, these glass feeders are designed for outdoor use. They resist weather damage and maintain nectar quality, ensuring long-lasting performance in gardens and yards.
