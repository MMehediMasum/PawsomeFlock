## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Bow Tie Collar Fits Small Dogs Best?



Small dogs typically need collars sized XS or S. Measure your dog’s neck before buying. Adjustable collars ensure a better fit and comfort.



### Are Bow Tie Collars Comfortable For Small Dogs?



Yes, soft and adjustable bow tie collars offer comfort. Materials like velvet or cotton prevent irritation and allow easy movement.



### Can Bow Tie Collars Be Detached Or Removed Easily?



Many bow tie collars feature detachable bows for versatility. Quick-release buckles make removal safe and convenient for small dogs.



### Which Materials Are Best For Small Dog Bow Tie Collars?



Soft fabrics like velvet, cotton, and polyester blends are ideal. These materials are gentle on fur and durable for daily wear.



### Do Bow Tie Collars Come With Matching Leashes?



Some sets include matching leashes and accessories. These coordinated sets enhance style and convenience during walks or outings.



### How To Clean And Maintain Bow Tie Dog Collars?



Hand wash or spot clean with mild soap. Air dry to maintain shape and color, ensuring long-lasting collar quality.
