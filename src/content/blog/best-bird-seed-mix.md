Their mixes are popular for attracting a variety of colorful songbirds, including cardinals, finches, and chickadees. Choose a blend with nuts and fruits for added nutrition.

Yes, many bird seed mixes attract cardinals. Kaytee Wild Bird Ultimate No Mess Seed is ideal for cardinals. It contains a mix of seeds and nuts preferred by cardinals. The blend also attracts other colorful songbirds, enhancing your backyard birdwatching experience.



### Are No-mess Bird Seeds Worth It?



No-mess bird seeds are worth it for easy cleanup. They leave no shells or waste. Morning Song Clean & Free Shell Free Bird Food is a great option. This type of seed mix reduces mess under feeders and attracts a wide variety of birds.



### How To Choose Bird Seed For Finches?



Choose a bird seed mix specifically for finches to attract them effectively. Pennington Pride Wild Finch Blend is designed for finches. It contains small seeds that finches love. This blend is perfect for year-round feeding and attracts a variety of musical and colorful birds.



### Why Add Fruit To Bird Seed Mixes?



Adding fruit to bird seed mixes offers extra nutrition. Birds enjoy the natural sugars and vitamins in fruits. Wagner's and Kaytee offer mixes with fruits like cherries. These mixes attract fruit-loving birds like grosbeaks and woodpeckers, providing a balanced diet and enhancing birdwatching.
