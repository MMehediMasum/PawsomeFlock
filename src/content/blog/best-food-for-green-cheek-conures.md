## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Diet For Green Cheek Conures?



A balanced diet of pellets, fresh fruits, and vegetables is ideal. Kaytee Forti-Diet Pro Health and Sunseed Vita Prima are top pellet choices. These provide essential nutrients for vibrant feathers and good health. Avoid excessive seeds to prevent obesity and nutritional gaps.



### Are Pellets Better Than Seed Mixes For Conures?



Yes, pellets offer balanced nutrition and reduce selective eating. Seed mixes like Higgins Sunburst are tasty but lack full vitamins. Pellets such as ZuPreem FruitBlend ensure consistent nutrient intake. Mix seeds occasionally for variety but prioritize pellets daily.



### How Often Should I Feed Pellets To My Conure?



Feed pellets twice daily, covering about 60-70% of their diet. Fresh pellets like Kaytee Fiesta support overall health and digestion. Supplement with fresh fruits and veggies to enrich their meals. Avoid overfeeding to maintain a healthy weight and activity level.



### Can Green Cheek Conures Eat Fruits And Vegetables?



Yes, fresh fruits and vegetables are vital for vitamins and fiber. Safe options include apples, carrots, and leafy greens. Avoid avocado, onion, and chocolate as they are toxic. Combine fresh produce with premium pellets like KikiBirds for best nutrition.



### What Makes Kaytee Forti-diet Pro Health Special For Conures?



Kaytee Forti-Diet Pro Health contains fortified vitamins and minerals tailored for conures. It supports skin, feathers, digestion, and heart health. The formula includes safflower and egg-cite options for variety. This diet promotes longevity and vibrant plumage.



### Is Zupreem Fruitblend Suitable For Green Cheek Conures?



Yes, ZuPreem FruitBlend is a nutritious pellet with added fruit flavors. It provides core nutrition and is designed for medium birds like conures. The pellets encourage healthy eating habits and reduce selective feeding. Ideal for daily maintenance and overall wellness.
