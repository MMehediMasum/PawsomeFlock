## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Natural Chicken Jerky Ideal For Dog Treats?



Natural chicken jerky uses only one ingredient: pure chicken. It contains no fillers or chemicals. This makes it safe, healthy, and easily digestible for dogs.



### Are Bully Sticks Good For Aggressive Chewers?



Yes, bully sticks are durable and natural. They satisfy aggressive chewers while promoting dental health. They are fully digestible and made from 100% beef pizzle.



### How Long Do Bully Sticks Last For Medium Dogs?



Bully sticks typically last 20-30 minutes for medium dogs. Thickness and chew intensity influence longevity. Thicker sticks last longer and offer better dental benefits.



### Why Choose Grass-fed Beef Bully Sticks For Puppies?



Grass-fed beef bully sticks are natural and chemical-free. They ensure better nutrition and safety for puppies. These treats support healthy teeth and gums in young dogs.



### Can I Use A Food Dehydrator For Dog Treats?



Yes, a food dehydrator preserves nutrients and flavor. It efficiently dries meats and fruits into healthy, long-lasting dog treats. Stackable racks increase drying capacity.
