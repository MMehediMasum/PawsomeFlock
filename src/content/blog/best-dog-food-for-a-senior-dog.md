### How Does Protein Content Affect Senior Dog Food?



High-quality protein helps maintain muscle mass in senior dogs. Purina ONE High Protein formulas provide essential amino acids for healthy aging and vitality.



### Are Small Breed Senior Dog Foods Different?



Yes, small breed senior foods have smaller kibble size and tailored nutrition. Blue Buffalo and Hill's Science Diet offer options designed for small and mini breeds.



### Can Wet Food Benefit Senior Dogs?
