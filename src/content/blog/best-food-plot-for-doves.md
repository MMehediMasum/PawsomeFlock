Easy-to-plant and fast-growing options save time and effort. Feeding doves with quality seeds encourages them to return often. You can find mixes designed for different acre sizes, from small backyard plots to larger fields. Selecting a well-balanced seed mix helps create a thriving food plot that benefits both doves and other wildlife.
