## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Cat Litter For Hedgehogs?



The best cat litter for hedgehogs is natural, dust-free, and non-toxic. Wood pellets or paper-based litter like Kaytee Wood Pellets are ideal. Avoid clumping clay litters as they can harm hedgehogs’ sensitive feet and respiratory systems. Always choose absorbent and odor-controlling options.



### Can Hedgehogs Use Clumping Cat Litter Safely?



Clumping cat litters like Dr. Elsey's Ultra UnScented are generally not recommended for hedgehogs. They can cause ingestion issues and irritate skin. Instead, use natural, pellet-based litters or paper bedding, which are safer and easier to clean for hedgehog habitats.



### How Often Should I Change Hedgehog Litter?



Change hedgehog litter at least once a week to maintain hygiene. Spot clean daily to remove waste and odors. Using absorbent litters like Nature’s Logic Pine Pellets helps reduce odor and moisture, keeping your hedgehog’s environment fresh and healthy.



### Are Disposable Pee Pads Good For Hedgehogs?



Disposable pee pads, like kathson 100Pcs Rabbit Pee Pads, are useful for hedgehog cages. They absorb urine and reduce mess. However, they should be changed frequently to prevent odors and keep the cage dry. Combine them with safe litter for best results.



### Is Pine Pellet Litter Safe For Hedgehogs?



Yes, pine pellet litter such as Nature’s Logic 100% Natural Pine Pellets is safe for hedgehogs. It is dust-free, biodegradable, and controls odor well. Ensure the pellets are plain without added fragrances or chemicals to avoid respiratory irritation for your pet.



### Can I Use Multi-cat Litter For Hedgehogs?



Multi-cat litters like WORLD'S BEST CAT LITTER are usually not recommended for hedgehogs. They may contain chemicals or fragrances harmful to small animals. Opt for natural, unscented, and dust-free options designed for small pets instead to ensure safety and comfort.
