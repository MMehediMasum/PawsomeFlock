## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of A Raw Food Diet For Cats?



A raw food diet provides natural nutrients and enzymes cats need. It supports healthy skin, coat, digestion, and strong muscles. Raw diets often reduce allergies and improve energy levels. Feeding freeze-dried or freeze-fresh options like Vital Essentials or Stella & Chewy’s ensures high quality and balanced nutrition.



### Are Freeze-dried Raw Cat Foods Safe And Nutritious?



Yes, freeze-dried raw cat foods like Vital Essentials and Nature’s Diet are safe. They retain essential nutrients through gentle processing. This method preserves protein, vitamins, and minerals, making them a convenient raw diet alternative. Always check ingredients for quality and avoid fillers or grains.



### How Do Raw Food Toppers Enhance My Cat’s Diet?



Raw food toppers like Instinct Raw Boost Mixers add flavor and nutrition. They enrich dry or wet food with protein, healthy fats, and functional ingredients. Toppers improve palatability and encourage picky eaters. They support digestion, immune health, and weight management naturally.



### Can Raw Diets Help Cats With Food Allergies?



Yes, raw diets with limited ingredients like Instinct Limited Ingredient Diet reduce allergy risks. They avoid grains, fillers, and artificial additives that trigger reactions. Using single-protein treats or meals can identify and manage sensitivities effectively. Always consult a vet for allergy diagnosis.



### What Makes Nature’s Diet Ultimate Raw Different From Other Raw Foods?



Nature’s Diet Ultimate Raw combines freeze-dried raw whitefish, bone broth, and superfoods. This blend delivers balanced nutrition with high-quality protein and essential nutrients. It supports hydration and joint health. Its unique formula offers variety and functional benefits beyond standard raw diets.



### Is Raw Coated Kibble A Good Option For Cats?



Raw coated kibble, like Instinct Original Dry Cat Food, blends raw nutrition with convenience. The kibble is coated with freeze-dried raw pieces, providing extra protein and flavor. It supports high energy needs and grain-free diets. This option suits cats transitioning to raw or with busy owners.
