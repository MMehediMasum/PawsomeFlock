## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is A Cat Calming Diffuser And How Does It Work?



A cat calming diffuser releases synthetic pheromones that mimic natural cat signals. These pheromones reduce anxiety, stress, and aggressive behavior. They create a calming environment, helping cats feel safe and relaxed. Diffusers are easy to use and plug into power outlets for continuous release.



### Which Diffuser Is Best For Multi-cat Households?



FELIWAY MultiCat diffuser is ideal for multi-cat homes. It reduces conflicts, tension, and signs of stress among cats. Its formula targets social harmony, making it effective in households with several cats. This diffuser promotes peaceful coexistence and reduces fighting and spraying behaviors.



### How Long Does A Calming Diffuser Last?



Most calming diffuser refills, like the 48ml bottles, last around 30 days. Continuous use is recommended for lasting results. Some products offer timing functions to optimize use. Regular replacement ensures the diffuser maintains its effectiveness in reducing cat stress and anxiety.



### Can Calming Diffusers Stop Cats From Scratching And Spraying?



Yes, calming diffusers help reduce scratching and spraying by lowering stress levels. They target behaviors linked to anxiety and territorial disputes. While not a complete solution, they significantly decrease these behaviors when used consistently with other training methods.



### Are Cat Calming Diffusers Safe For Kittens And Adult Cats?



Cat calming diffusers are safe for both kittens and adult cats. They use natural pheromones without harmful chemicals. Always follow product instructions and consult a vet if your cat has health concerns. These diffusers are designed to support emotional well-being without side effects.



### How Do I Choose The Right Diffuser Refill Size?



Choose refill size based on your home size and number of cats. A 48ml bottle typically covers an average room for 30 days. Larger households or bigger areas may require multiple diffusers or frequent refills. Check product details for coverage recommendations to ensure effectiveness.
