## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Litter Box For Multiple Cats?



The best litter box for multiple cats is large, durable, and easy to clean. Options like the PetPivot Self-Cleaning or Cumrige Automatic Litter Box handle multiple cats efficiently. They reduce odor and minimize litter tracking, keeping your home cleaner.



### How Does A Self-cleaning Litter Box Help Multiple Cats?



Self-cleaning litter boxes automatically remove waste after each use. This keeps the box clean and odor-free, which is essential for multiple cats. Models like TAYACICY and PetPivot reduce maintenance time and ensure a hygienic environment for all cats.



### Are Hooded Litter Boxes Better For Multiple Cats?



Hooded litter boxes provide privacy and control odor for multiple cats. The Amazon Basics No-Mess Hooded Litter Box and Catit Airsift Jumbo Hooded Litter Pan are popular choices. They help contain litter scatter and reduce smells effectively.



### What Size Litter Box Is Ideal For Multiple Cats?



Extra-large litter boxes are ideal for multiple cats. Boxes like the Stainless Steel Litter Box with Lid and IRIS USA Large Cat Litter Boxes offer ample space. Larger size helps prevent crowding and encourages cats to use the box comfortably.



### Can Litter Mats Reduce Mess For Multiple Cats?



Yes, litter mats catch stray litter and reduce mess. Several models, including PetPivot and self-cleaning boxes, come with litter mats. They help keep floors clean and reduce the need for frequent sweeping or vacuuming.
