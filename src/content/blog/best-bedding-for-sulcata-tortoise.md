## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Substrate For Sulcata Tortoise Bedding?



Coconut fiber substrates like ZeeDix and Riare are ideal for Sulcata tortoise bedding. They retain moisture well and are natural. These substrates support burrowing and help maintain humidity, which Sulcatas need for comfort and health.



### How Often Should I Change Sulcata Tortoise Bedding?



Change bedding every 2 to 4 weeks or when soiled. Frequent cleaning prevents bacteria buildup. Spot clean daily to remove waste and keep the enclosure fresh and healthy for your tortoise.



### Can I Use Coconut Fiber For Sulcata Tortoise Burrowing?



Yes, coconut fiber substrates such as Zilla and Legigo chips are excellent for burrowing. They are soft, natural, and allow easy digging. This mimics the tortoise’s natural environment and promotes natural behavior.
