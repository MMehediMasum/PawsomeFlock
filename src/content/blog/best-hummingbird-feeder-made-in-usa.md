## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make These Hummingbird Feeders Usa-made?



These feeders are crafted with high-quality glass and durable materials. They include ant moats, bee guards, and multiple feeding ports. Each design focuses on ease of cleaning and leak-proof performance. Handmade or assembled in the USA ensures quality and supports local manufacturing.



### How Do Ant Moats Benefit Hummingbird Feeders?



Ant moats prevent ants from reaching nectar, keeping it fresh and uncontaminated. This feature enhances feeding efficiency and reduces maintenance. Most USA-made feeders include built-in ant moats, ensuring a pest-free experience. It improves feeder longevity and provides a healthier environment for hummingbirds.



### Are These Feeders Easy To Clean And Refill?



Yes, these glass feeders feature wide openings for easy cleaning and refilling. Many designs are leak-proof, preventing sticky messes. The glass construction resists staining and odors. Regular cleaning helps maintain nectar quality and attracts more hummingbirds to your garden.



### How Do Bee Guards Improve Hummingbird Feeding?



Bee guards protect nectar from bees and wasps without blocking hummingbirds. This reduces competition and keeps nectar cleaner longer. USA-made feeders with bee guards ensure hummingbirds feed comfortably and safely. It also minimizes the need for frequent cleaning caused by bee activity.



### What Nectar Capacity Suits Different Garden Sizes?



Feeders range from 10 to 32 ounces to fit various garden needs. Smaller feeders (10-16 oz) are ideal for limited spaces or fewer birds. Larger feeders (25-32 oz) support more hummingbirds and reduce refilling frequency. Choose based on bird activity and garden size for optimal results.
