## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Window Perch For Large Cats?



The AMOSIJOY and Zakkart models are ideal for large cats. They feature sturdy wood and metal frames that support heavier weights. These perches provide spacious, comfortable beds perfect for big cats to lounge safely by the window.



### How Do Suction Cup Window Perches Work For Cats?



Suction cup perches attach securely to glass surfaces using strong suction cups. Models like AMOSIJOY’s cordless perch offer easy installation and stability. They are great for renters or when you want a perch without drilling holes in walls or windowsills.



### Are Orthopedic Cat Window Perches Beneficial?



Yes, orthopedic perches like Zakkart’s bolster design support cats' joints. They provide comfort and reduce pressure on hips and elbows. These perches help older or arthritic cats relax while enjoying sunbathing and window views.



### Can Window Perches Be Installed Without Drilling?



Yes, some perches like AMOSIJOY’s cordless cat hammock use suction cups for installation. These options allow easy mounting and removal without damaging windows or walls. They are perfect for temporary setups or apartment living.



### Which Window Perch Is Easiest To Clean?



Perches with removable and washable covers, such as K&H Pet Products’ Kitty Sill, are easiest to maintain. Waterproof or flannel covers also help keep the perch clean and odor-free. Regular cleaning ensures a healthy resting spot for your cat.



### What Features Make A Cat Window Perch Durable?



Durable perches have solid metal frames and premium hardwood bases. Strong materials prevent sagging and support weight securely. Models like Zakkart and AMOSIJOY combine wood and metal for long-lasting use and stability.
