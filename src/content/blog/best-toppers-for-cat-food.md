## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Using Freeze-dried Cat Food Toppers?



Freeze-dried toppers retain nutrients and flavor without additives. They boost your cat's diet with natural proteins and vitamins. These toppers improve palatability and encourage hydration. They are grain-free, gluten-free, and filler-free, making them ideal for sensitive cats.



### How Do Broth Toppers Improve My Cat’s Hydration?



Broth toppers add moisture to dry food, helping cats stay hydrated. They are flavorful and encourage cats to drink more. Broth toppers also provide essential nutrients like vitamins and minerals. Using them supports urinary tract health and overall well-being.



### Are Grain-free Cat Food Toppers Better For Cats?



Grain-free toppers reduce allergy risks and digestive issues in cats. They focus on high-quality proteins and natural ingredients. These toppers support healthy digestion and skin. Grain-free options are ideal for cats with food sensitivities or dietary restrictions.



### Can Cat Food Toppers Enhance Picky Eaters’ Appetite?



Yes, toppers add flavor and texture to dull food, enticing picky cats. They make meals more exciting with tasty ingredients like real chicken or seafood. Toppers improve food palatability without extra calories. This encourages regular eating and balanced nutrition.



### What Functional Ingredients Support My Cat’s Health In Toppers?



Functional ingredients like taurine, vitamins, and minerals boost immunity and digestion. Ingredients such as quail egg yolk promote skin and coat health. Probiotics and fiber aid gut health and nutrient absorption. These toppers offer targeted benefits beyond basic nutrition.



### How Often Should I Use Cat Food Toppers In Meals?



Use toppers daily or several times a week to maintain interest and nutrition. They complement regular cat food without replacing it. Moderation ensures balanced calorie intake and avoids digestive upset. Adjust frequency based on your cat’s preferences and dietary needs.
