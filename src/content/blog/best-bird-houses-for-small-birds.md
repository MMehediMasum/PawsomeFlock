## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Entrance Hole Is Best For Small Birdhouses?



An entrance hole of about 1 to 1-1/8 inches suits small birds like wrens and chickadees. This size keeps predators out and allows easy access for small birds to nest safely.



### Which Materials Are Best For Durable Outdoor Birdhouses?



Cedar and recycled plastic are ideal for outdoor birdhouses. Cedar resists rot and insect damage, while recycled plastic is waterproof and low maintenance, ensuring longevity and safety for birds.



### How Do Predator Guards Protect Small Birdhouses?



Predator guards block access to nests by animals like squirrels and raccoons. Copper guards or metal plates around entrance holes prevent predators from reaching eggs and chicks, enhancing bird safety.



### Why Are Ventilation And Drainage Important In Birdhouses?



Proper ventilation prevents overheating inside the birdhouse. Drainage holes keep nests dry by allowing rainwater to escape, creating a healthy environment for small birds.



### Can Birdhouses Accommodate Multiple Bird Families?



Some birdhouses have multiple compartments or holes, supporting several small bird families simultaneously. This setup encourages diverse bird activity and efficient use of space in your garden.
