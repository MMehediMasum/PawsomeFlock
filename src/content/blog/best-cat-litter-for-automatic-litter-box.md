**Choosing the best cat litter for an automatic litter box helps keep your home clean and your cat happy. Not all litters work well with self-cleaning systems.**



Automatic litter boxes need clumping, low-dust litter to function properly. Strong odor control is also important to keep the area fresh. Some litters may clog the sensors or reduce cleaning efficiency. Cats prefer soft, dust-free litter that feels comfortable on their paws.



This guide covers top litters that match features of popular automatic boxes like Cumrige, KITPLUS, and Fumoi. These litters support app-controlled, large-capacity, and safety-protected models. Using the right litter extends the life of your device and keeps your cat healthy. Find the best options to enjoy a cleaner, easier cat care routine.
