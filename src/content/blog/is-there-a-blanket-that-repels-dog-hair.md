### Best Fabrics For Repelling Hair

* Microfiber: Tight weave and smooth texture repel hair effectively.

Durability matters for pet owners. Some fabrics wear out quickly with regular washing. Microfiber holds up well and resists tears. Canvas is very strong but may feel rough.

Maintenance affects how easy a blanket is to clean. Leather wipes clean but can crack over time. Microfiber and nylon machine wash easily and dry fast. Avoid fabrics that trap hair deep inside, like fleece or wool.

## Diy Solutions For Fur Resistance

Dealing with dog hair on blankets can feel like a never-ending battle. Luckily, you don’t have to buy expensive specialty blankets to fight this issue. There are simple DIY solutions you can try at home that help reduce fur cling and keep your blankets cleaner for longer.

### Home Treatments To Reduce Hair Cling

One easy way to make your blankets less attractive to dog hair is by treating the fabric yourself. Rubbing a dryer sheet over the surface before use can reduce static, which helps repel loose fur. You can also toss a few dryer sheets in the dryer along with your blanket to keep fibers smooth and less sticky.

Another trick is to lightly spray your blankets with a mix of water and fabric softener. This softens fibers and lowers static electricity, making it harder for hair to stick. Have you noticed how your hairbrush picks up less fuzz after a quick rinse in fabric softener? The same idea works here.

### Easy Cleaning Hacks

Regular cleaning can make a huge difference. Use a lint roller or sticky tape to quickly remove hair from your blanket before it builds up. For stubborn fur, a rubber glove slightly dampened and rubbed over the fabric can grab hair in seconds.

Vacuuming your blankets with a brush attachment also helps pull out embedded hairs. Try to do this weekly if your dog sheds a lot. You might be surprised how much less hair ends up on your furniture when you keep your blankets fur-free.

### Homemade Fabric Sprays And Their Effectiveness

Making your own anti-static spray is simple and budget-friendly. Mix equal parts water and white vinegar in a spray bottle and add a few drops of essential oil if you want a nice scent. Lightly mist your blankets before use; the vinegar reduces static without leaving any strong smell once dry.

Another option is a solution of water and a small amount of hair conditioner. Shake well and spray lightly on the fabric to soften fibers and repel hair. Keep in mind that these sprays work best when applied sparingly to avoid dampness.

Have you tried any homemade sprays before? Sometimes the simplest ingredients can make a big difference in [keeping your favorite blanket free from dog](https://pawsomeflock.com/what-to-put-on-couch-to-keep-dogs-off/) hair.

## Maintaining A Fur-free Blanket

Keeping your blanket free from dog hair can feel like a never-ending task, especially if your furry friend loves to snuggle. However, with the right approach, you can maintain a clean, hair-free blanket that stays fresh longer. It’s all about consistent care and using smart techniques to stop fur from settling in.

### Regular Cleaning Techniques

Washing your blanket regularly is crucial. Use a lint roller or a pet hair remover brush before each wash to pick up loose hairs. When washing, choose a gentle cycle and add a half cup of white vinegar to the rinse cycle; it helps loosen pet hair and reduces static cling.

Dry your blanket with dryer sheets or wool dryer balls to reduce hair sticking during drying. Avoid fabric softeners as they can make hair cling more. How often do you wash your pet’s bedding? More frequent cleaning prevents fur buildup and keeps your blanket feeling softer.

### Storage Tips To Prevent Fur Build-up

Where you store your blanket matters. Keep it in a sealed container or a zippered bag when not in use to block fur and dust from settling. If you have a designated pet area, consider using a blanket cover that can be washed separately.

Try to keep your pet’s sleeping area clean by brushing your dog before they lie down. This simple step can reduce the amount of hair transferred to your blanket. Have you noticed how much less hair sticks after a good brushing session?

### Tools That Help Keep Blankets Hair-free

Investing in the right tools makes a big difference. A high-quality lint roller or pet hair remover glove quickly lifts fur without damaging the fabric. Vacuum cleaners with pet hair attachments are especially effective for deep cleaning.

Sticky tape and rubber brooms also work well on certain blanket materials. Experiment with different tools to find what fits your blanket type and daily routine. Which tool has surprised you the most in tackling stubborn dog hair?
