### What Is Aafco?

AAFCO stands for the Association of American Feed Control Officials. It is a group that sets guidelines for pet food.

These guidelines help ensure that pet food is safe and nutritious. AAFCO does not regulate pet food, but its standards are important.

AAFCO approval means the food meets basic nutritional standards. This is important for your dog’s health.
