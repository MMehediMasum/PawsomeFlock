## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Cat Brush For Long Hair Cats?



The best cat brush for long hair cats removes loose fur and mats gently. Look for soft silicone or slicker brushes designed for long coats. Brands like Aumuca and Swihauk offer effective grooming tools that reduce shedding and prevent painful pulling.



### How Often Should I Brush My Long-haired Cat?



Brush your long-haired cat at least 3-4 times a week. Frequent brushing prevents mats and tangles, reduces shedding, and keeps fur healthy. Using self-cleaning brushes like Dipoo or Swihauk makes grooming easier and more efficient.



### Can I Use One Brush For Short And Long-haired Cats?



Yes, some brushes like Aumuca and SleekEZ work well for both short and long-haired cats. They offer versatile grooming with soft pins or silicone bristles that gently remove loose fur from all coat types.



### How Do Self-cleaning Cat Brushes Work?



Self-cleaning brushes have bristles that retract or release fur with a button press. This feature makes cleaning the brush quick and hygienic. Brushes like Dipoo and Swihauk include this handy function, saving time during grooming sessions.



### Are Deshedding Brushes Safe For Cats’ Skin?



Yes, quality deshedding brushes are skin-friendly and designed to avoid irritation. Look for rounded pins or soft silicone like in Garstor or Aumuca brushes. These tools massage the skin while removing loose undercoat safely.



### What Brush Helps With Matted Fur On Long-haired Cats?



For matted fur, use grooming combos with metal combs or rakes like Docrok or Maxpower Planet. These tools detangle and demat gently without hurting your cat. Regular use prevents painful mats and keeps your cat’s coat healthy.
